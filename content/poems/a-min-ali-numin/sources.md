# Umar ibn Abi Rabi'a · أَمِن آلِ نُعمٍ

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari recites the long ra'iyya. The original-clock interval is 3.50–549.50 s, with natural margins at both ends. There are 74 different bayts, each delivered once in order. The reference diwan has 75. Between our 34 and 35 al-Nahari skips «فبتّ قرير العين أُعطيت حاجتي / أقبّل فاها في الخلاء فأكثر». It is on printed p. 97. No other diwan bayt is omitted. The short repeated strings near some full-transcript boundaries belong to overlapping recognition windows. ## Witnesses and commentary

- Muhammad Muhyi al-Din Abd al-Hamid, *Sharh Diwan Umar ibn Abi Rabi'a al-Makhzumi*, no. 1, printed pp. 92–103. The incipit and the preceding biographical pagination are discontinuous in the scan. Printed p. 92 is scan leaf 5. The final bowl, rope and cloudy water are on p. 103, which also begins the next poem. The notes on pp. 93–96 explain the messages, the travellers’ exposure, the unguarded saddle and the night watch. Pages 97–101 explain the mouth, the women’s clothing, the shield and the farewell. Pages 101–103 explain the lean camel, well, small trough and improvised tackle. These explanations underlie the original Arabic and English commentary. [Opening, p. 92](https://archive.org/details/ibnabirbea3h/ibnabirbea3h/page/n5/mode/2up) · [Close, p. 103](https://archive.org/details/ibnabirbea3h/ibnabirbea3h/page/n16/mode/2up)
- Ibn Maymun, *Muntaha al-talab min ash'ar al-Arab*, pp. 150–152, preserves a long parallel text. Its «فقلت كذاك الحب» after our 30 and «فبت قرير العين» after our 34 are absent from the performance. It lacks our 50 and 59, among other differences of wording and order. Its 74 bayts are a different set from the 74 in this recording. ## Readings in the performance

Abd al-Hamid brackets both the unperformed bayt on p. 97 and the younger sister’s gift of clothes on p. 100, noting their absence from his base copy. Al-Nahari skips the first and sings the second (our 53). On p. 102 the editor glosses the web’s «خام» as an untanned hide.

The scrolling studio text is independently visible throughout the recording. The metre is tawil, with ra as rawiy. The opening and the night-watch verses carry the long four-foot half. The displayed halves follow the delivery even where syntax runs across the break, as with «مشية / الحباب» and «وأنظر / إليهم».

At 2.2 both full recognisers, isolated Cohere and widened Audar hear «فمبلغ», with the half-segment acoustic comparison also favouring the m sound. The card, all three written witnesses and the syntax give «فتبلغَ عذراً». The subjunctive verb answers «لم تقل في جوابها» and keeps the second-person subject of the preceding half. Abd al-Hamid explains that the traveller kept his purpose from anyone who asked. An accusative participle «فمُبلغَ» with this object has no governor here. The edition therefore retains the verb and the word card identifies the apparent spoken substitution. At 3.1 the spoken «تهيم» keeps the self-address. The diwan prints «أهيم» and records «تهيم» and «نهيم» below it. At 5.2 the wordplay is «نهى ذا النُّهى», checked on p. 93. At 7.2 the direct object in «والبغضَ يظهر» parallels «يسرّ لي الشحناء». Ibn Maymun has «للبغض مظهر». At 19.1 the card has «جشّمني السرى», as do the parallel classical texts. The word-only comparison now favours «جشّمني» over the early draft’s «جشّمتني» by 11.5 points. The implicit mover is longing. At 20.1 the editor prefers the sense of imminent danger for «على شفا» to the older suggestion that it means the end of the day. At 22.2 «مُعوِر» describes the unattended saddle, and at 26.1 «قُمير» is the diminutive.

The studio and running voice give «أهوى غيوبه» (26.1), «وخُفّض عني الصوت … وشخصي» (27), and «بمخفوض التحية» (28.2). Ibn Maymun has «ونفّضت عني النوم … ولكنّي» and «بمرفوع التحية». At 30.1 the card gives «إن هُنّا» against Ibn Maymun’s «إذ هنّا». Nu'm addresses the visitor in «ما مكثتَ» at 34.2. At 35.2 «ليلي» has the first-person ending, and at 36.1 «ومجلسٍ» continues «من ملهىً». The mouth is «مُقبّل» (37.1), while Ibn Maymun gives «مفلّج». The recorded comparisons at 38–39 are hailstones, daisies and «إلى ظبية», as in the principal commentary. [Diwan, pp. 95–98](https://archive.org/details/ibnabirbea3h/ibnabirbea3h/page/n8/mode/2up)

At 48.2 Nu’m says «وأن ترحبا صدراً بما كنت أحصر». Both full transcripts, the isolated half and the studio card at 349 s give «صدراً». The three collated witnesses read «سِرباً». Abd al-Hamid explains it as the mind or disposition and cites the expression for someone easy in mind. The spoken «صدراً» makes a closely related contrast between her sisters’ breadth of chest and her own constriction. [Studio card, 349 s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LnfJSCieUJ8&t=349s) · [Diwan, p. 99, n. 6](https://archive.org/details/ibnabirbea3h/ibnabirbea3h/page/n12/mode/2up) · [Ibn Maymun, p. 151](https://shamela.ws/book/732/151) · [Khizana, 5/319](https://shamela.ws/book/12732/2215)

The sisters’ two cloaks at 50 and the profile of the departing woman at 59 belong to the principal text and the performance. At 61.1 the envy is of «أهل العامرية» collectively. Ibn Maymun has «بعل العامرية». In the closing journey the reciter says «عنس» (62.1), «طرف الأرجاء» (65.2), «فقمت إلى» (67.1), «كقاب الشبر» (71.2) and «والأديم المضفّر» (73.2). The other long witnesses also preserve readings such as «حرف»، «شرف الأرجاء»، «فطافت به»، «كقيد الشبر» and «والجديل». At 74.1 the diwan and the performance give «وما ردّ شربها», where the two anthologies have «صدّ». [Diwan, pp. 99–103](https://archive.org/details/ibnabirbea3h/ibnabirbea3h/page/n12/mode/2up)

The isolated halves also recover the case ending of «قليلاً» (16.1) and «في العشاء» (25.2). All three printed witnesses have «قليل» and «بالعشاء». In 51.2, 59.1 and 70.1 the recording gives «للأمر»، «حين أعرضت» and «وأنني». Abd al-Hamid has «بالأمر»، «حيث أعرضت» and «وأني». The omitted bayt on p. 97 puts his numbering one ahead of the performance from our 35 onward. [Diwan, pp. 94–96](https://archive.org/details/ibnabirbea3h/ibnabirbea3h/page/n7/mode/2up) · [pp. 100–103](https://archive.org/details/ibnabirbea3h/ibnabirbea3h/page/n13/mode/2up)

At 74.2 the two continuous transcriptions recover «عن الريّ مطروق من الماء أكدر». Audar’s fresh closing clip joins the heard sounds across the first boundary («عن الريم طرق من الماء أكدر»), preserving the sequence through the final adjective. Cohere on that same 12-second original-edge crop loops unrelated «الحنجية…». The diwan prints the complete half and its note explains both «مطروق» and «أكدر». Ibn Maymun p. 152 and al-Baghdadi 5/321 give the same close. All four words following «عن الريّ» belong to the poem. [Diwan, p. 103](https://archive.org/details/ibnabirbea3h/ibnabirbea3h/page/n16/mode/2up) · [Ibn Maymun, p. 152](https://shamela.ws/book/732/152) · [Al-Baghdadi, 5/321](https://shamela.ws/book/12732/2217)
