# كِليني لِهَمٍّ يا أُمَيمَةَ ناصِبِ

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari, 10 April 2024. The source runs 313.795 s. The voice enters after the initial 0–3.232 s quiet stretch. The final rhyme on «مذاهبي» dies away before the 311.060–313.795 s tail. Keep **2.73–311.60 s**, a continuous 308.87 s. The two full-source transcriptions, the changing bayt cards and all 58 aligned halves show the 29 bayts once, in the order of the diwan. The initial vocative belongs to the poem. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fUr-cCHjgn0)

## Text and commentary

- Al-Nabigha, *Diwan*, ed. Muhammad Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim, Dar al-Ma'arif, poem 3, **pp. 40–48**. This is the base for the extent and numbering. The headnote attributes the account of the poet fleeing an informer at al-Nu'man’s court to Abu Ubayda, and records disagreement over the name of the Ghassanid patron in the genealogy. We follow its opening identification, Amr ibn al-Harith. Pp. 40–41 explain Umayma’s name, the stars’ herdsman and the recall of a straying worry. Pp. 42–44 move from Ghassanid ancestry to the birds’ sight-lines, posture and expectation of food. Pp. 45–46 give two traditions for the Day of Halima, the sparks from stone and the image of a pregnant camel’s spurting urine. Pp. 47–48 supply the Christian feast, the garment pegs, two accounts of the shoulders’ colour and the gift in the last bayt. The nine page images used are retained in local research. [Opening, p. 40](https://archive.org/details/Dnabkh/page/n39/mode/1up) · [Battle imagery, p. 46](https://archive.org/details/Dnabkh/page/n45/mode/1up) · [Close, p. 48](https://archive.org/details/Dnabkh/page/n47/mode/1up)
- Al-A'lam al-Shantamari, *Ash'ar al-shu'ara' al-sitta al-jahiliyyin*, al-Nabigha, poem 3. The complete 29-bayt text appears between the headings for poems 3 and 4 on electronic p. 34. Its electronic «قذ» at 8 and «المشاحب» at 26 are corrected by the scan, the cards and the sense. In 22.2 compare its «إبزاغ» with the scan’s «إيزاغ» and the independent whole-take transcription. [Complete second witness](https://shamela.ws/book/5449/34)
- Shawqi Dayf, *Tarikh al-adab al-arabi*, I, *al-Asr al-jahili*, pp. 282–284, quotes the bird sequence, interprets the star-herdsman as dawn and discusses why the first flocks guide later ones. His account associates the opening grief with captives from the poet’s tribe. The introduction attributes the different flight account to its source. The sharh of 6–7 also uses Ibn Qutayba’s explanation of the two graves as a challenge to live up to one’s ancestry. Al-Mu'allimi sets the wording in *al-Ma'ani al-kabir*, p. 1015, alongside Asim ibn Ayyub’s commentary. [Dayf, p. 282](https://shamela.ws/book/11996/278) · [P. 283](https://shamela.ws/book/11996/279) · [Ibn Qutayba quotation and parallel](https://shamela.ws/book/14381/10690)
- The verse about the notches in the swords is an instance of praise that briefly sounds like an exception to praise. The sharh retains the physical defect on the blades. Its meaning depends on their history of repeated use before the poem identifies them as heirlooms. Likewise the blade rims and the marks on the brows in 18 and the two possible colours of the shoulders in 27 are explained from the diwan’s own commentary. [P. 44](https://archive.org/details/Dnabkh/page/n43/mode/1up) · [P. 48](https://archive.org/details/Dnabkh/page/n47/mode/1up)

Tawil, with a kasra on the rhyme consonant ba. In the opening sadr «كِليني | لِهَمٍّ يا | أُمَيمَـ | ـةَ ناصِبِي» scans فعولن مفاعيلن فعولُ مفاعلن. In 15.2 «بِهِنَّ | كُلومٌ بَيْـ | ـنَ دامٍ | وَجالِبِي» gives فعولُ مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. Bayts 6–7 carry one conditional across the intervening rhyme, and 10–15 sustain the birds as witnesses until the poet turns from their wings to the wounded horses below. No additional introductory or closing verse from another ode enters the count.

## Decisions against the recording

Comparisons used the aligned half and the same surrounding words. The difference below is the winning margin in the compact-window run. Two forms which the romaniser represents alike receive the same score. The full local score file and labelled studio frames preserve the underlying evidence.

| Half | Reading and reason |
| --- | --- |
| 5.2 | حُسنُ ظنّ, the only knowledge he has, can be construed as a nominative substitute. The vocalic comparison against حُسنَ gives only +1.48. The scan at p. 41 supports the nominative. |
| 9.1 | دُنيا gives the close degree of kinship. P. 42 explains the form with an initial kasra and also discusses the untanwined form with damma. In the aligned half دُنيا leads دِنياً by 5.67. This compares the whole forms, including their different endings. |
| 10.1 | The diwan has في الجيش. Both full transcriptions and the independent half give بالجيش. These spellings contain different numbers of written words, so the comparison score cannot decide between them. |
| 12.1 | عيونها, +17.66 over عيونهن. The plural birds take a collective feminine singular pronoun in the verse, also confirmed by the isolated half and both text witnesses. |
| 14.2 | Both books and the studio have الكواثب, the horses’ withers. The isolated half agrees and scores +5.06 over the الكواكب suggested by whole-take recognition. The stars belong in the opening, whereas the spears rest above the horses here. |
| 16.1 | The source card at 165 s prints للموت and repeats الموت from the following half. The scan prints للطعن. The narrow Cohere half and the full take support the latter. It leads للطعان by 7.91 and للموت by 24.25. |
| 17.1 | Both printed witnesses and the studio card have singular المنية. Both complete transcriptions and the separately cut half render the plural المنيات. The compact word comparison favours the singular by 4.80. The plural, which also scans and fits the reciprocal verb, is retained with a reader note. The quantity of the chanted vowel is a point for listening review. |
| 22.1 | The two full engines and the isolated half expand the first vowel to ساكناته. The scoring cannot distinguish the two lengths. The wording on the source card at 231 s and the gloss in the diwan, p. 46, both give سكناته, the necks where the heads rest. The first vowel’s length remains uncertain. |
| 22.2 | The phrase كإيزاغ المخاض, the spurting of a pregnant she-camel, is written with yāʾ on p. 46. Al-Shantamari’s electronic text has كإبزاغ, with bāʾ. The aligned half gives the first reading an 11.65 lead, and its isolated transcription has no bāʾ. |
| 23.2 | من الناس, +27.09 over من الجود. Both full engines and the isolated half have الناس. The studio card and both editions have الجود. The reading changes the scope of غيرهم and is explained on the word card. |
| 24.1 | The reciter’s vowel is long enough for both engines and the separate clip to write محلاتهم. Both books and the source card at 252 s give محلتهم. The comparison gives identical scores. The edition keeps the transmitted singular, with the possible plural noted, since the vowel is sung in a position that permits lengthening. |

## Half-verse verification

Of 58 separately transcribed halves, 14 showed a difference. Widened Audar independently transcribed each. The table identifies the words affected, including cases in which the wider clip picked up a sound across a pause.

| Halves | Resolution |
| --- | --- |
| 1.1, 2.1, 2.2 | The engines split أميمة ناصب across the sustained vocative. Both words are on the opening card and on p. 40. The second run restores «تطاول حتى قلت ليس بمنقض» and «يرعى النجوم بآيب», including the herdsman’s return. |
| 5.2, 7.2 | The held rhyme of بصاحب is expanded to بصاحبه in the short and wide clips. The unpossessed noun is written on the card and p. 41. Both engines divide ليلتمسن into shorter words. The conditional in 6 requires its affirmative answer, printed with the light emphatic nun on p. 42. |
| 8.1, 8.2 | Extra consonants/heard articles at the cut come from the join غزت كتائبُ. Both whole takes and the card put كتائب at the opening of the second half as the subject of غزت. |
| 13.2, 15.2 | The recognisers split أوّل into unstable words. The card and the diwan agree on «أوّل غالب». The first 15.2 clip returned nothing. Widened Audar recognises «بهن كلوم بين دام وجالب» after picking up part of the preceding sound. This is the complete wounds half of p. 43. |
| 16.2, 17.2 | The wider 16.2 restores the complete «إلى الموت إرقال الجمال المصاعب». In 17.2 the recognisers move the joined mim from «بأيديهمُ» to «بيض». The source card, both books and the individually timed words preserve the pronoun and the white blades. |
| 21.1 | The second transcript restores المضاعف نسجه but renders السلوقي as السلوك. The doubled weave and the Saluqi mail are glossed together on p. 46 and written on the plate at 219 s. |
| 28.1, 28.2 | The two recognisers give بعدهم or شرّ أبعده in place of «شرّ بعده», and ضربات لازب for «ضربة لازب». The two parallel halves on the 297 s card and p. 48 distinguish a good turn from a stroke of ill fortune thought permanent. |
