# The prison verses attributed to Salih ibn Abd al-Quddus

## Performance and extent

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed introduces the poet by name and adds «وتروى لغيره». His voice starts around 20.51 seconds. A pause from 24.09 to 25.67 separates that spoken attribution from the poem. The master keeps 25.17–123.03 on the original clock, through the final البلوى and its decay. All nine bayts of Abd Allah al-Khatib's no. 39 are present in his order. The opening and the dream verse «فإن حسنت» scan in tawil. The rhyme falls on final long ā in الشكوى, الموتى, الدنيا and عجلى, an example Yaqut quotes in discussing the maqsur rhyme. ## Editions, attributions and commentary

- Abd Allah al-Khatib, *Salih ibn Abd al-Quddus al-Basri*, Baghdad, Dar Manshurat al-Basri, 1967, no. 39, p. 137, apparatus continued on p. 138. All nine bayts stand together here. Al-Khatib specifies sources for 1–5, 2–8 and 8–9, and lists the anonymous witnesses as well. This is an editor's collection of overlapping passages. A witness to a particular group of bayts does not independently establish the other groups. [Text and apparatus](https://archive.org/details/saleh-bin-abdolghoddus/page/n136/mode/2up)
- Al-Sharif al-Murtada, *Amali*, ed. Muhammad Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim, I, pp. 145–146, gives 2–8 as Salih's prison verse. His editor supplies the opening in a note. Al-Ma'arri himself, in *Risalat al-ghufran*, p. 142, assigns the two bayts corresponding to 2–3 to a son of Salih imprisoned for heresy. His second bayt has an enquiring visitor in place of the gaoler. For 7.2 al-Murtada's comparison with Ali ibn al-Jahm allows «فنُغشى» as an independent assertion that visitors do come. The marginal commentator instead construes it as the consequence of a negative. Our translation follows the latter and reports the former. [Al-Ma'arri](https://shamela.ws/book/1589/137) · [P. 145 and attribution note](https://shamela.ws/book/20937/174) · [P. 146 and grammatical note](https://shamela.ws/book/20937/175)
- Ibn Qutayba, *Uyun al-akhbar*, I, p. 152, gives five verses without an author's name, including «كشف المصيبة» and «إذا جاءنا السجان». *Al-Mahasin wa-l-addad*, p. 71, attributes the four lines corresponding to 2–5 to Abd Allah ibn Mu'awiya ibn Abd Allah ibn Ja'far. The new rubric «وقال آخر» before the next quotation explicitly separates its version of 8 from that attribution. Safi al-Din al-Halabi, *Uns al-masjun*, pp. 132–133, no. 340, introduces a three-bayt selection anonymously. [Ibn Qutayba](https://shamela.ws/book/23790/147) · [Abd Allah ibn Mu'awiya attribution](https://shamela.ws/book/10511/61) · [Al-Halabi's source apparatus](https://shamela.ws/book/120146/125) · [Anonymous extract](https://shamela.ws/book/120146/126)
- Al-Mas'udi, *Muruj al-dhahab*, §2609 (IV, p. 256 in the edition cited by al-Halabi’s editor), puts versions of **1–3** in al-Fadl ibn Yahya’s mouth after his imprisonment with his father. He introduces the lines with «قال الفضل بن يحيى يذكر ما هما فيه». Ibn al-Imad, *Shadharat al-dhahab*, II, p. 424, transmits a different account through Ibn al-Ahdal. Al-Fadl recites the same three in prison and the account expressly assigns the words to Abu al-Atahiya. Both versions open with a collective appeal to God and end with the gaoler entering from the world outside. These reports establish a tradition about the opening group. The nine-line edition follows al-Khatib under the attribution al-Saeed announces. [Al-Mas'udi, Barmakid passage](https://github.com/OpenITI/0350AH/blob/master/data/0346Mascudi/0346Mascudi.MurujDhahab/0346Mascudi.MurujDhahab.JK010344-ara1.completed#L24463-L24468) · [Ibn al-Imad, II, p. 424](https://shamela.ws/book/12398/814)
- Ibn al-Mu'tazz, *Tabaqat al-shuʿaraʾ*, p. 91, under Salih, quotes the last pair with «يبكي» for «يأوي». This independently locates the ninth bayt which is absent from al-Murtada's sequence. The ninth line completes al-Khatib's collection and the recording. Al-Zirikli, *al-Aʿlam*, III, p. 192, supplies the poet's name, Basran background and approximate death date. Ibn al-Mu'tazz and al-Murtada tell the prosecution story differently. The profile gives the accusation as an accusation and the date as approximate in prose. ## Recording readings and clip verification

The opening is «كشف المضرّة», as in al-Khatib. At 3–4 the reader has the gaoler, «عجبنا» and «ونفرح بالرؤيا», as in al-Murtada. The eased spellings «تهدا / يهدا» at 6.2 follow al-Khatib and the rhyme. The aligned word-window scores for 1.2 are −15.70 for المضرّة and −35.27 for المصيبة. At 7.2 the three similar-sounding forms mean “be feared / be visited / visit”. Both transcriptions support the final visit verb with ghayn.
