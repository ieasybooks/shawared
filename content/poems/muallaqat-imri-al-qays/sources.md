## Attribution and title

- **Imru' al-Qays ibn Hujr.** Ibrahim's edition of the diwan prints it as poem 1 and lists it (p. 367) as the first poem in al-A'lam's recension, the third in al-Tusi's (al-Mufaddal's, read with Ibn al-A'rabi), the first in al-Sukkari's and Ibn al-Nahhas's, the second in al-Batalyawsi's and the forty-fifth in Abu Sahl's, and as the first of the Mu'allaqat of Abu Sa'id al-Darir, Ibn al-Anbari, Abu Ja'far al-Nahhas and al-Zawzani, of al-Tibrizi's ten and of Abu Zayd al-Qurashi's *Jamhara*. Ibn al-Anbari, al-Zawzani and al-Tibrizi all comment on it as his. Ibn Sallam (*Tabaqat Fuhul al-Shu'ara'*, vol. 1, p. 55, shamela 6738/54) and Ibn Rashiq (*al-'Umda*, vol. 1, p. 218, shamela 10909/200) cite its features and its opening as his.
- **Two reports on parts of it**, both in Ibrahim's notes (p. 367): al-Batalyawsi from Ibn al-Kalbi, «أعراب كلب ينشدون هذه القصيدة لابن خِذام»; and Ibn Hazm's *Jamharat al-Ansab* (p. 426) from Ibn al-Kalbi, that the Bedouin of Kalb, asked what Ibn Himam wept over, recited «خمسة أبيات متصلة من أول: قفا نبك من ذكرى حبيب ومنزل، ويقولون: إن بقيتها لامرئ القيس». The two copies of Ibn al-Kalbi's report differ in the name (ابن خِذام in al-Batalyawsi, ابن حِمام in Ibn Hazm as Ibrahim prints him) and in the extent (the whole poem, or its first five abyat). No other source I read gives the poem to anyone else, and the intro now mentions the report (the drafter's intro left it out). The poet's other poem in the app, «تطاول ليلك», is the disputed one; this is not.
- **The wolf passage (abyat 49–52)** is the part the old authorities doubted: Ibn al-Anbari, p. 80: «وروى بعض الرواة هاهنا أربعة أبيات، وذكر أنها من هذه القصيدة خالفه فيها سائر الرواة، وزعموا أنها لتأبط شرا», and p. 82: «فهذه الأبيات الأربعة رواها بعض الرواة في قصيدة امرئ القيس، وزعم الأصمعي وأبو عبيدة وغيرهما أنها ليست منها»; al-Zawzani (Dar Ihya' print, p. 61, shamela 11253/72): «لم يروِ جمهور الأئمة هذه الأبيات الأربعة في هذه القصيدة وزعموا أنها لتأبط شرًّا»; al-Tusi, as Ibrahim quotes him (p. 372): «وتُروى هذه الأبيات الثلاثة لتأبط شرًّا»; Ibrahim's main text lacks them. The recording has them, the sharh and intro say whose they may be, and they stay under Imru' al-Qays as every commentary prints them.
- **Abyat 3–4** are also flagged as additions: Ibn al-Anbari, p. 23, on bayt 3: «وروى هذا البيت أبو عبيدة. وقال الأصمعي: هو منحول لا يعرف، وقال: الأعراب يروونه فيها»; al-Tibrizi (Muniriyya print, p. 6): «وهذا البيت وما بعده مما يزاد في هذه القصيدة، قال الأصمعي: والأعراب ترويهما»; the critical edition of al-Zawzani lacks both, and the Dar Ihya' print marks the glosses on them «هذا الشرح ليس للزَّوزَني».
- **Title.** «معلقة امرئ القيس» is the name its editions use: the running head of every page of al-Tibrizi in Muhyi al-Din's edition is «(١) معلقة امرئ القيس بن حجر»; the Muniriyya al-Tibrizi and the Dar Ihya' al-Zawzani head it «معلقة امرئ القيس»; Ibrahim calls it «الأولى من المعلقات السبع». It is not only an editor's heading: Nasr al-Hurini uses the name in his own text, «مطلع مُعلَّقة امرئ القَيْس» (*al-Matali' al-Nasriyya*, p. 275, shamela 97799/267), and the owner's brief names «معلقة امرئ القيس» as an example of a known name. The English title is "The Mu'allaqa of Imru' al-Qays".

- **Al-Zawzani**, شرح القصائد السبع, ed. Bilal al-Khalil and Ahmad Abd al-Hamid (Durrat al-Ghawwas, Cairo, 2019), a critical edition from several manuscripts: scan archive.org `Zawzani_Sharh` (leaf = printed page + 4), pp. 85–174, read in full from the page images (`zawzani.txt`), with the editors' notes on the readings that matter here (pp. 95, 117, 119, 124, 133, 163). 79 abyat: it lacks 3 and 4, and its bayt 45 is 47.1 joined to 48.2 («فيا لك من ليل كأن نجومه / بأمراس كتان إلى صم جندل»), as Ibrahim says of al-Zawzani (p. 372: «جعله ملفقًا من صدر هذا البيت وعجز تاليه»). ## How the recording's text stands to the editions

The reciter says al-Zawzani's text, with these departures:

- **Abyat 3–4** and **bayt 47 and 48 as two abyat**, as in Ibn al-Anbari, al-Tibrizi and Ibrahim (al-Zawzani's critical edition lacks 3–4 and joins 47/48).
- **28.1 «تَمشي»** (Ibrahim; al-A'lam and al-Batalyawsi; al-Zawzani's manuscript ح) for al-Zawzani's «أَمشي». His editors think it is what he meant (p. 119 n. 3: «ح: «تمشي». وكذا هي عند الأعلم ١٤، والبطليوسي ١/٣٩. وكأنها مراد الزوزني»), and his commentary reads «فأخرجتها من خدرها وهي تمشي» (Dar Ihya' print).
- **58.1 «يَزِلُّ الغُلامُ الخِفُّ»** (Ibn al-Anbari p. 87, al-Tibrizi p. 110, read again on the page image; al-Zawzani's «ويروى») for «يُزِلُّ الغُلامَ الخِفَّ».
- **67.2 «يَنضَح»** (al-Tibrizi, Muhyi al-Din's edition p. 116 on the page image, and the Muniriyya print; al-Zawzani's Dar Ihya' print) for «يُنضَح».
- **75.2 «الكَنَهبَل»**: al-Zawzani's critical edition prints «الكَنَهبُل» (p. 168), and his commentary allows both («بضم الباء وفتحها»). (65.1 «الجَزع» is not a departure: al-Zawzani's critical edition prints «الجَزْع» in the bayt and the gloss, p. 160; only the Dar Ihya' print has «الجِزع».)
- **11.2 «عَجَبا»** without tanwin, as al-Zawzani's critical edition prints it (p. 96) and as al-Zawzani and al-Tibrizi explain it (the alif stands for the ya of «عجبي»); Ibn al-Anbari's print (p. 33) and the Dar Ihya' print have «عجبًا». Not scored: a tanwin would merge into the mim that follows, and both transcribers' «عجبا من» fits either.
- **69.2 «تَسَفَّلِ»**: in no edition I read but al-Zawzani's manuscript ح and the Dar Ihya' print; the critical edition's note (p. 163): «وتواتر الناس على نسبتها إلى الزوزني خطأً، ولم تأتِ بها رواية». Word note gives «تسهّل».
- **52.2 «يَهزِلِ»**: every edition I read has «يُهزَلِ»; al-Qurashi's *Jamhara* has «يَهزِل». Word note gives both, with Lisan's gloss.
