# يا عين جودي بالدموع

Osama al-Nahari, published 25 October 2023. Keep 5.10–143.39 s of the 145.043 s original. The first word is يا and the last is القوارح. All twenty bayts of the Beirut recension are recited in order, with no repeated half or internal cut. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9MABIZAnUTU)

## Printed texts and commentary

- *Diwan al-Khansa'*, Dar Sadir, Beirut, pp. 21–23, under «حنين والهة». Count seven bayts on p. 21, nine on p. 22 and four on p. 23. This is the extent indicated by `abyat_in_full`. It begins with the eye, proceeds from the grave to the brother’s public acts, and puts the horse-teeth comparison last. [Opening](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-khansa/page/n20/mode/1up) · [Middle](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-khansa/page/n21/mode/1up) · [Close](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-khansa/page/n22/mode/1up)
- *Diwan al-Khansa'*, commentary of Abu al-Abbas Ahmad ibn Yahya Tha'lab, ed. Anwar Abu Suwaylim (Amman, Dar Ammar, 1988), no. 42, pp. 328–344. The heading explicitly calls this another elegy for Sakhr and gives مجزوء الكامل. There are **25 bayts under 24 printed numerals**. Both فكأنما أم الزمان at the bottom of p. 334 and فنساؤنا يندبن at the top of p. 335 bear the number 15. The last is numbered 24 before the rubric of no. 43. The five additional verses in this recension open إن البكاء، إذ غاب مدرهنا، وتعذرت، تذري السوافي and والآخذ الحمد. Its horse-teeth verse is no. 11. Its final four move from setting the broken bone to forgiving an offence, giving camels and choosing restraint. [Rubric](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n327/mode/1up) · [First 15](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n333/mode/1up) · [Second 15](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n334/mode/1up) · [No. 24 and next poem](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n343/mode/1up)
- At recorded 7, Sadir explains المهاصر through the lion breaking its prey and glosses الممانح as المعطاء, the generous giver. Tha'lab’s base at printed no. 21 has المهيض من المصاهر والمانح. Abu Suwaylim’s note 5 also transmits الكسير من المهاصر والمانح with the same explanations of the grappler and the giver. At recorded 8, Tha'lab’s no. 23 gives «الواهب العيس العتاق مع الخناذيذ السوابح», then explicitly transmits Abu Yusuf’s «الواهب المائة الهجان من الخناذيذ» and explains that the horses were the means of capturing the camels. The translation follows that explanation. At 9, Sadir’s ملح is eating a man’s food. Tha'lab also records kinship, shared nursing or familiarity. [Sadir’s glosses](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-khansa/page/n20/mode/1up) · [Tha'lab, pp. 340–342](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n339/mode/1up) · [Abu Yusuf’s reading](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n341/mode/1up)
- At 15 Sadir calls the هادية the woman who goes first. Tha'lab’s text has هادئة and explains the other mourners going quiet or sleeping, while his apparatus supplies هادية. At 16 the bereaved camel raises its head and refuses water. At 17 Sadir glosses شعثت as scattering or becoming dishevelled and explains لا ينين as never flagging. Its النوائح gives the women mourners. Tha'lab’s base has شعثاً and النوابح, the barking dogs, and cites شعثت in the apparatus. The paired translations and both explanations follow the recorded recension. [Sadir, pp. 22–23](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-khansa/page/n21/mode/1up) · [Tha'lab, pp. 335–336](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n334/mode/1up)

## Metre and recited halves

The opening gives *yā ʿayni jū / dī bid-dumū | ʿil-mustahil / lātis-sawāfiḥ*. The first three feet have the shape مستفعلن, the syncopated form of متفاعلن. The fourth has an extra final syllable, ترفيل. At 11, *dhākal-ladhī / kunnā bihī | nashfil-mirā / ḍa minal-jawāniḥ* has the same measure, with an unsyncopated final متفاعلاتن. The rawiy is ح. Where a word straddles the feet of the two halves it is divided at that point in the Arabic. The title supplies the whole word الدموع. The English puts a complete word where an English reader can understand it.

## Recording decisions

The photographed words of the original video at 25.21 s give إذ ثوى, at 32.30 s رمساً and هوج النوافح, at 73.50 s حين يبغي الحلمَ and at 108.32 s بعد هادية النوائح. At 121.16 s the card reads شعثت شواحب لا ينين / إذا ونى ليل النوائح. These confirm which recension the reciter followed where the two printings differ. The original continuous Cohere and Audar transcripts join the divided words. Isolated half clips often assign a shared word and its last consonant wholly to one side. The enlarged Audar clips also sometimes pick up a neighbouring syllable. Each flagged address appears below. Scores use equal word counts, the MMS model and 0.30 s of context. The Arabic prose and scores, including alternatives outside the flags, are worked against the pages above in `media/research/ya-ayni-judi`.

At 10.2, يبغي scores −25.21 against يبغُ −30.52 and both transcripts give the full verb. At 15.2 the joined genitive هادِيَةِ النَّوائِح and an indefinite هادِيَةٍ نَوائِح sound alike when the nūn of tanwin assimilates to the following nūn. The CTC spelling preference (−28.77 / −22.03) cannot decide the construction. Sadir’s gloss and the continuous Audar wording support the leader of the mourners. At 16.1 the isolated Cohere gives the fully vocalised يَحْنُنَّ. The video at 114.28 s and the photographed Sadir p. 22 both place damma on the first nūn. Tha'lab's edition at p. 336 vocalises a different form, يُحَنِّنَّ. Ibn al-Qatta' gives يَحِنّ for the camel's cry, and al-Ru'ayni distinguishes the ordinary kasra of longing from the damma in حنّ عنه, “turn away from him”. We keep Sadir's performed vocalisation and its meaning in this particular bayt, where حنين والهة explicitly describes the sound. Across the complete half the performed يَحنُنَّ scores −19.96, the usual يَحنِنَّ −24.23 and Tha'lab’s يُحَنِّنَّ −35.50. A trial with fatḥa, يَحنَنَّ, gives −19.85. The acoustic score alone cannot separate fatḥa and ḍamma here. The vocalised clip, Sadir and the video agree on ḍamma. The word card gives the ordinary dictionary form. [Sadir, p. 22](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-khansa/page/n21/mode/1up) · [Tha'lab, p. 336](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n335/mode/1up) · [Ibn al-Qatta', I, p. 249](https://shamela.ws/book/12050/271) · [Al-Ru'ayni, p. 111](https://shamela.ws/book/6982/77)

At 4.1 Sadir and the caption give رَمساً (−20.78). Tha'lab’s base has أَمسى (−25.98) and his apparatus also transmits رمساً. At 4.2 the construct هوجُ النَّوافح (−44.72) and Tha'lab’s indefinite هوجٌ نوافح (−31.39) both sound *hūju n-nawāfiḥ* in liaison and pause. The strong spelling preference supplies no independent distinction here. Sadir, the caption and continuous Cohere identify the construct. At 7.2 وَالمُمانِح scores −43.28 against the trial with final ʿayn, وَالمُمانِع (−50.80). The print, the caption and the rhyme require ḥāʾ. [Tha'lab, p. 329](https://archive.org/details/KnSSa_TH/page/n328/mode/1up) · [Sadir, p. 21](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-khansa/page/n20/mode/1up)

At 16.2 والهةٍ and والهةً tie at −18.75. حنين والهةٍ is a genitive construction.
