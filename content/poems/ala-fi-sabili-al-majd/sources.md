# أَلا في سَبيلِ المَجدِ ما أَنا فاعِلُ

Osama al-Nahari recites the entire poem, **41 bayts** in diwan order, each half once. Published on 30 July 2022, the video credits the reader and displays the opening bayt on a fixed image. That image cannot settle the wording of later verses. The keep is **1.120–322.020 s** of a 323.744 s recording, or **320.900 s**, from before «ألا» through the release of «كوامل». The full recording has the long night ride and the final argument from danger to moderation. The first two catalogue matches under Ibn al-Wardi reuse phrases from this poem and open with different verses. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FedzfiCBTZU)

## Readings and interpretation

At **2.1** the recording and the two full books have «خفية», a secret one has fathomed. Al-Jirawi gives «حقيقة». At **11** al-Jirawi has «وأيّ جوادٍ», changing a direct identification with a thoroughbred into an exclamation. At **13** his «فوق السماكين» places the speaker above the stars, whereas the recitation puts him **between** them, «بين السماكين», as in the full diwan. The corresponding equal-word acoustic gaps favour «خفية» by 14.66, «وإنّي جوادٌ» by 7.23 and «بين» by 18.02. At **2.2** «يُخيَّب» leads «يَخيب» by 2.63. The two passives make the informer and the petitioner parallel.

In **3** the enlarged lines show two separately dotted nūns in «أنّني» in both halves of the Beirut diwan and the *Shuruh*. Both continuous transcriptions and both final Cohere halves give the same word. The extra syllable opens the third foot in each half. The isolated model prefers the shortened comparison «أنّي» by 3.75 and 3.63 in the two halves. Al-Tabrizi and al-Batalyawsi explain the two superlatives together. Hatred and departure usually mark the extremes of estrangement, but the speaker calls them its slightest degrees. [Beirut, p. 193](https://archive.org/details/3487pdf_202001/page/n192/mode/1up) · [*Shuruh*, II, p. 520](https://archive.org/details/4034pdf/page/n546/mode/1up)

At **7.2** the nominative «ضَوؤُها» has its medial hamza on wāw, as in Beirut p. 193. In **18** al-Batalyawsi explains that yesterday envies the present day. In the second half the nominative «الأصائلُ» makes late afternoon envy the hours before dawn. Those rivals can even possess his company within a single day. [Beirut, p. 193](https://archive.org/details/3487pdf_202001/page/n192/mode/1up) · [*Shuruh*, II, pp. 530–531](https://archive.org/details/4034pdf/page/n556/mode/1up)

In **21–24** the repeated conditions have one eventual answer, «فيا موت زر». Hatim and Quss are the respective objects of accusations made by Madir and Baqil. The faint Suha then makes the same sort of accusation against the sun. In **23.2** the stars are the object «الشهبَ» and the pebbles and rocks are the delayed subject. The acoustic comparison favours the accusative by 5.03. In **26–31** the light markings of one horse resemble silver anklets. A dark mount later supplies the second of two simultaneous nights, the one without a single star. Al-Tabrizi allows a desert for the sea traversed in 31, but prefers night because «التبلّج», the appearance of dawn, is its only shore. In **32** «لم تَصْحُ» comes from صحا, to sober up, a reading favoured by 2.59 over the form from صحّ. The changing features of the night supply its human disposition. [The proverbial speakers](https://archive.org/details/4034pdf/page/n561/mode/1up) · [Horses and sea](https://archive.org/details/3487pdf_202001/page/n194/mode/1up) · [Night and the Pleiades](https://archive.org/details/3487pdf_202001/page/n195/mode/1up)

At **40** both continuous passes give «فإن كنت تبغي العزّ». Beirut prints it. The *Shuruh* lemma is «وإن كنت تهوى العيش» and its apparatus expressly attributes «فإن كنت تبغي العز» to *al-Tanwir*. Its other reading «فإن كنت تبغي العيش» also explains why «العز» matters. The prudence of the last two bayts costs a man some of the glory he claimed at the beginning. The recording's four-word phrase leads those two alternatives by 40.27 and 17.27. [*Shuruh*, II, p. 552](https://archive.org/details/4034pdf/page/n578/mode/1up)
