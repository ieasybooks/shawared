# عَرَفَ الهَوى في نَظرَتي فَنَهاني

## The recording and its limits

Osama Al-Waaedh recites al-Barudi’s ghazal on his own channel, uploaded 27 May 2023. The edit keeps 2.08–146.92 s of the 148.77 s source. The voice begins at about 2.59 s and the last sustained vowel dies away at about 146.34 s. The single interval preserves the pauses between verses. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Py9c3ewV4N8)

Fourteen verses, each once. In the diwan’s numbering they are 1–8 and 10–15. He passes directly from the women on the saddles to the lions and gazelles, omitting «أغوينني فتبعت شيطان الهوى». He ends at «غصون البان», before the diwan’s last verse «فاخلع عذارك واغتنم زمن الصبا». The introduction to the preceding short poem about al-Sabi belongs to that poem. Ma'ruf sets this one apart with a fresh «وقال» and gives it no title or dated occasion. The reference to Abraq al-Hannan belongs to the poem’s desert scene.

## Text and commentary

- Mahmud Sami al-Barudi, *Diwan al-Barudi*, vol. 2, edited and annotated by Muhammad Shafiq Ma'ruf, Babtain Foundation with the Egyptian General Book Organisation. Printed pp. 729–734, scan leaves 730–735. The volume names Ma'ruf on its cover. His text, lexical notes and explanations on these pages are the basis of the edition and both commentaries. Page 734 prints the final two verses and the next poem, so the total is sixteen. [Volume cover](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-117931/page/n0/mode/1up) · [Opening, p. 729](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-117931/page/n730/mode/1up) · [Ending, p. 734](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-117931/page/n735/mode/1up)
- *Diwan al-Barudi*, Dar al-Awda, Beirut, 1998, pp. 685–688. The page images have all sixteen verses in the same order and abridge the commentary. Hindawi’s *Diwan Mahmud Sami al-Barudi*, p. 287, prints the sixteen together under «من الكامل» in the nun section. The difference between «بعدما» and Hindawi’s «بعد ما» at 7.1 is spacing. These resets transmit the same poem. [Dar al-Awda, pp. 685–688](https://archive.org/details/20191210_20191210_1135/page/n684/mode/1up) · [Hindawi, p. 287](https://archive.org/details/DewanAlbarody/page/n287/mode/1up)
- The Abu Dhabi Poetry Encyclopedia, poem 42843, supplies a digital concordance. Old Shamela book 4423, database pp. 403–404, comes from that project. Its «حساً» at our 10.2 loses the dots of the shin. All the inspected page images have «حشاً» and Ma'ruf explains the pounding heart under the hand. The modern Abu Dhabi entry also reads «حشاً». The bayt-by-bayt comparison and the scan images are in `media/research/arafa-al-hawa/`. [Abu Dhabi, poem 42843](https://poetry.dct.gov.ae/poems/42843-عرف-الهوى-في-نظرتي-فنهاني)
- For 11.2, Yaqut’s *Mu'jam al-buldan*, vol. 1, p. 67, gives «أبرق الحنّان» as a water source of the Banu Fazara and expressly doubles the nun. Ma'ruf explains how the flash leads the eye to the cloud, then the rain to pasture and the gathering of the tribes. His gloss on «غصون البان» identifies the women’s figures behind the curtains. [Yaqut, «أبرق الحنّان»](https://shamela.ws/book/23735/62) · [Ma'ruf, pp. 733–734](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-117931/page/n734/mode/1up)

## Metre and rhyme

Complete kamil. Using u for a short syllable and – for a long one, the opening scans **uu–u– / ––u– / uu––** in the sadr and **––u– / uu–u– / uu––** in the ajuz. The two halves share the shortened ending in the rhymed opening. Verse 8 gives **uu–u– / uu–u– / uu–u–** and **––u– / ––u– / –––**. The latter line illustrates both the uncontracted *mutafa'ilun* and *idmar*, which contracts the opening pair of short syllables into one long syllable. The rhyme consonant is nun with kasra after long alif. The sustained i after the nun in «الأظعانِ» and «البانِ» is the sound of the rhyme. The ya in «فرعاني» and «كتماني» also belongs to the first-person ending.

## Performance readings and verification

Cohere transcribed all 28 half-lines individually. The first pass flagged ten clips. Rechecking the extended line endings added 10.1, making eleven. Both complete-file transcripts, the overlapping source windows and a second transcription of each flagged half with 0.35 s of padding were checked against the app audio and the editions. Scores below are differences of summed alignment log probabilities for equal-word-count candidates. Times in this section refer to the 144.84 s app recording.

| Half | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| 1.1 | The short clip gives «نضرتي». Both full transcripts, the overlapping opening and the wider Audar clip give «نظرتي». The score favours the spelling with ض by 5.35, but the aligner represents ظ as plain z. There is also a closure and release in this reader’s «فانظر» at 13.1. The wording about a glance, which Ma'ruf explains, stands. |
| 2.1 | «فوشابها» joins the sounds across the word boundary. Both full takes separate the verb and its complement, «فوشى بها». |
| 3.1 | «لوعتينا» is a misdivision of the sustained ending. Full takes and overlapping windows give the singular «لوعة». The inflected singular scores 14.24 above the dual «لوعتين». One complete take gives «أكاذب», the other and the overlapping window «أكذّب». The widened Audar clip also gives «أكاذب» and loses the end of the half. The score ties. The chanted a lasts about 0.43 s (23.96–24.39), much like the short a before the undisputed doubled d in «تردّد» (about 119.95–120.36). Length in this position cannot establish an added alif. We follow the printed verb for denying what the tears testify to. |
| 4.2 and 6.1 | Both short clips generated the same unrelated spoken credit. The full recording and the overlapping source windows have the two different verse texts in their proper places. Wider Audar clips recover both, with «الأضعانين» at 4.2 and loss of the final i of «قلبي» in writing at 6.1. At 4.2 several transcripts spell «الأضعان», a dental-consonant confusion in «الأظعان», the camel litters. At 6.1 the vowel after the k in «فعليكَ» matches this reciter’s «لعلّكَ» at 13.1. At 52.46–52.52 s its first two formants are approximately 575–601 and 1627–1775 Hz. The masculine reading also scores 1.93 above «فعليكِ» and is the form in the reciter’s written caption. The greeting can thus continue the address to his friend. Ma'ruf’s gloss understands a greeting to the departing woman. |
| 9.1 and 9.2 | The full takes give «بادرة النوى» and «أنّ الأسود». The narrow recogniser adds a final m to the first half and a prefixed w to the next. The wider Audar clips write «النواة» and also prefix w to «أنّ». The complete takes and the source window beginning at the second half support «أنّ». «النوى» is separation. «أنّ» without the w scores 6.22 higher and supplies the object of «أعلم». |
| 10.1 | After the release of the final syllable was extended to 98.78 s, the short clip supplied an unrelated sentence. Both full takes contain «رحلوا فأيّة عبرة مسفوحة». The overlapping window carries the line from «الغزلان» through «ويد تضمّ» in order, and the wider Audar clip recovers all four words. |
| 12.1 | The short clip supplied unrelated words. Two overlapping source windows between them give «يستن في عرض الغمام كأنه», as do the complete takes and the wider Audar clip. The racing subject is the lightning. |
| 13.1 | The narrow clip supplies plural «ركابهم». Both complete takes, the overlapping window and the wider Audar clip give singular «ركابه», which wins by 5.22. The possessive refers to the lightning and its train of rainclouds. |
| 14.2 | Both full takes, the overlapping window and the wider Audar clip give «هُدب الخدود». The two-word reading with final d scores 5.15 above the reading with r, and d alone wins by 3.82. All three printed texts have «الخدور». Their curtains supply the loose threads above the imagined figures of the women. The text keeps «الخدور» and both word cards record the slip. |

Other checks concerned the recordings’ consonants and inflections. At 2.2 «كِتماني» wins by 1.98. At 5.1 isolated «فَقَتُ» scores 3.81 above «فقدتُ», but the bordering full-source window has «فقدت الحلم», losing one’s composure, as in all editions. At 7.1 «يرجع» wins by 3.09 over the imperative. At 9.2 «الغِزلان» wins by 2.27. At 10.2 an overlapping window explicitly gives «وَيَدٍ», coordinated with «عبرةٍ», where case scoring ties. Both complete takes give «حشا», although «حسا» scores 2.31 higher. At 11.2 one full take shortens «منّا» to «من» and the two scores tie. The other full take, an overlapping window and Ma'ruf have «منّا».

The verifier reported no extra word at a clip edge. At the very end, the final vowel of «البانِ» stays voiced through roughly 144.25 s in the app audio. The final release is at about 144.28 s.
