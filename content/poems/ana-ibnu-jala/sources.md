# أَنا اِبنُ جَلا وَطَلّاعُ الثَّنايا

## Recording and extent

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed, موقع الشنكبوتية, 31 August 2009. The 137.462 s original includes the messengers’ story, their employers’ taunting verse «إن بداهتي وجراء حولي», and the introduction to Suhaym’s answer. Only the answer is sung as the poem. A single source cut, 53.75–128.60 s, gives 74.85 s. The source-voice windows put the first onset at 54.12 s and the end of the last voiced group at 128.09 s. [Original recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OvyvMBN5l9U)

The film recites verses 1–11 of *al-Asma'iyyat* no. 1 once each. The printed opening is followed by the prose anecdote and the challengers’ verse before the editor resumes the numbering with 2. Here the introductory story is already over when Suhaym says «أنا». The last line is his own «فإن قناتنا مشظ شظاها». Al-Hajjaj’s celebrated use of the incipit in a speech is part of its reception. A quotation by a later poet cannot establish the text or length of Suhaym’s reply.

## Witnesses and commentary

- Al-Asma'i, *al-Asma'iyyat*, ed. Ahmad Muhammad Shakir and Abd al-Salam Harun, no. 1, pp. 17–20. The transmitter from Riyah gives the names al-Akhwas and al-Ubayrid. The glosses explain the interval in «غِبّ», stalking in «يدّري» and a second milking in «عُلالة». The printed sequence underlies `abyat_in_full`. [Opening](https://shamela.ws/book/6905/3) · [Verses 3–7](https://shamela.ws/book/6905/5) · [Verses 8–11](https://shamela.ws/book/6905/6)
- Ibn Sallam al-Jumahi, *Tabaqat fuhul al-shu'ara'*, ed. Mahmud Muhammad Shakir, II, pp. 579–580, paragraph 775. His four lines attest the author and the rivalry but transmit «ألم تر أنني في حميري» and «وماذا يغمز الأعداء مني». [Four-line quotation](https://shamela.ws/book/6738/559)
- Abd al-Qadir al-Baghdadi, *Khizanat al-adab*, I, pp. 257–261, discusses the idiom in the opening, the turban, the mountain passes, the two challengers, and the camel ages. Al-Suyuti’s *Sharh shawahid al-Mughni*, I, pp. 459–460, gives the longer quotation and explicitly identifies the dotted ذ of «نجّذني». Both longer sequences omit the performed «سأحيا ما حييت» from the verse text and insert three between «كريم الخال» and the final spear image. Those additions name Qatan, Zayd, Salma and Hammam. Al-Suyuti’s editor supplies «سأحيا» in a note. The three additional verses are absent from the recording. [Al-Baghdadi](https://shamela.ws/book/12732/258) · [Al-Suyuti](https://shamela.ws/book/17724/457)
- Al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, III, p. 79, supplies the full lineage and the approximate death date, 60 AH (680 CE). The entry distinguishes Suhaym ibn Wathil of Riyah from Suhaym, the poet attached to Banu al-Hashas. [Biographical entry](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/2163)

## Metre and rhyme

Wafir with nun as *rawiy*. A dash is long and u is short. The foot مفاعلتن may undergo عصب, giving `u---`. The final فعولن is `u--`.

| Recorded bayt | Sadr | Ajuz |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 «أنا ابن جلا … متى أضع العمامة» | `u-uu- / u--- / u--` | `u-uu- / u-uu- / u--` |
| 6 «وماذا يدّري … وقد جاوزت» | `u--- / u-uu- / u--` | `u--- / u--- / u--` |

The alif of «أنا» drops before the initial wasl of «ابن» in connected recitation. The closing long vowel of «تعرفوني» completes the same last foot as «الأربعينِ» with its rhyme release.
