# مُثَلَّثُ قُطرُبٍ

## Recording

- أسامة الواعظ, produced by استوديو القمة: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_qXLo4wTk9E (uploaded 2022-09-17, 12:34), «نظم مثلث قطرب للبهنسي مع شرحه لابن زريق - إلقاء: أسامة الواعظ».
- **Audio:** kept 8.15–751.3 s of the download (the silence before the first word and after the last cut), gain −5.5 dB, giving −20.1 LUFS with a peak of −6.8 dBTP.
- **What is recited:** both texts the title names, interleaved: al-Bahnasi's opening stanza; then 37 triplet stanzas, each followed by a stanza of Ibn Zurayq's verse commentary on it; al-Bahnasi's closing stanza; and Ibn Zurayq's five closing stanzas. All 162 abyat, in the starting text's order, none left out. He says the last two (161–162) twice, and the timeline plays them twice.
- **How the text is set:** the poem is majzu' rajaz in stanzas of four short lines; each line is a half here, so a stanza is two abyat. Al-Bahnasi's triplet stanzas are 3–4, 7–8, 11–12 … 147–148 (every fourth pair), Ibn Zurayq's commentary stanzas the pairs after them.

## Who wrote what

- **The triplet poem (نظم مثلث قطرب)** is al-Bahnasi's: al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 7, p. 95 n. 1 (shamela 12286/6220, `alam-7-95.txt`): «ناظمها، وهو سديد الدين المهلبي البهنسي، المتوفى سنة ٦٨٥ يقول في ختامها…»; Sezgin, *Tarikh al-Turath* (Arabic), vol. 1, p. 101 (shamela 143165/97, `sezgin-1-101.txt`): «نظم مثلثات قطرب» لعبد الوهاب بن الحسن بن بركات البهنسي المهلبي… مربّع من ٣٢ رباعية على وزن الرجز. Hajji Khalifa (*Kashf al-Zunun*, Furqan ed., vol. 6, p. 249, `kashf-6-249.txt`) gives it to Qutrub himself; al-Zirikli corrects that from the closing stanza (bayt 152 here).
- **The verse commentary** is by Muhammad ibn Ali ibn Ibrahim ibn Zurayq: Sezgin, vol. 1, p. 103 (d. 977/1569, after Hadiyyat al-Arifin; MSS Ambrosiana 351/59, Berlin 7079–7080); *al-A'lam*, vol. 7, p. 95 n. 1, citing the Ambrosiana catalogue: «نظم لمحمد بن علي بن زريق في شرح قصيدة المثلثات»; the *Khizanat al-Turath* manuscript catalogue (shamela 5678/57566, `khizana-58-566.txt`) gives d. 803. *Mu'jam al-Shu'ara' al-Arab* (shamela 2114/1885, `mujam-al-shuara-1884.txt`) reads the closing the same way and says of Ahmad al-Makhalibi, the dedicatee: «رجل مغمور لم نعثر له على ترجمة». He is not Ibn Zurayq al-Baghdadi, the poet of «لا تعذليه».
- **Seven stanzas** of the recited text are in neither edition of the poem: لقى, رشا, زجاج, منّة, قرى, ظلم, قطر (abyat 119–134 and 139–150, with their commentary). The starting text gives no separate author for them; they are kept under the poem's poet.
- **The poet id** `al-bahnasi` follows al-Zirikli's heading, «البَهْنَسي» (*al-A'lam*, vol. 4, p. 182, shamela 12286/3512, `alam-4-182.txt`). The poet file gives his name as there, «عبد الوهاب بن الحسن المهلبي البهنسي», with the death year al-Zirikli adopts (685, from al-Asnawi and Ibn Qadi Shuhba; *al-Shadharat* has 686). The rest of the bio: Ibn Qadi Shuhba, *Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya*, vol. 2, p. 183 (shamela 6736/456: judge of Misr and Cairo in Rajab 680, Cairo and the Delta taken from him in Jumada II 681); al-Subki, *Tabaqat al-Shafi'iyya al-Kubra*, vol. 8, p. 317 (shamela 6739/2992: the anecdote of «من بنى أربعتك على الكسر», and the name «عبد الوهاب بن الحسين»); al-Dhahabi, *Tarikh al-Islam*, vol. 51, p. 250 (shamela 12397/23548). Kashf's editors (vol. 6, p. 249 nn. 4–5) and al-Zirikli take Sadid al-Din of the manuscripts and the judge Wajih al-Din for one man.

## Editions

- **Starting text:** poetry.coiod.com, «يا مولعا بالغضب والهجر والتجنب (نظم مثلث قطرب) لـ سديد الدين البهنسي مع شرحه لابن زريق» (2021; `coiod/lines.txt`): the combined text, fully voweled, 162 abyat. It is the only text found of Ibn Zurayq's commentary and of the seven extra stanzas, and the reciter follows it word for word; its tashkeel was put in the house style.
- **al-Muhallabi (al-Bahnasi), *al-Muthallathat al-Lughawiyya = Sharh Nazm Muthallath Qutrub***, ed. Jamil Abd Allah Uwayda (1428/2007): shamela's repository book 37030 as OpenITI `0685SadidDinMuhallabi.MuthallathatLughawiyya` (`openiti-raw/text.txt`), printed pp. 8–37. The author's own prose commentary on his poem, opening «نظمت مثلث قطرب في قصيدة قلتها أبياتا على حروف المعجم، وهي ثلاثون نوعا… ليسهل حفظه»: the opening stanza, thirty triplet stanzas (one for each letter in turn, and عمر), and the closing stanza, with a gloss and a proof verse for each meaning. Unvoweled. It has no commentary by Ibn Zurayq. (The machine-voweled copy in `openiti/` was not used.)
- **Cheikho's print, «شرح مثلثات قطرب»**, in *al-Bulgha fi Shudhur al-Lugha*, ed. A. Haffner and L. Cheikho (Beirut, 1914), printed pp. 181–186: scan archive.org `20230111_20230111_1710`, leaves 181–186, read as page images. The poem (marked ق), voweled on its triplet words, with a different verse commentary (marked ش) by an unnamed author; 29 stanzas (no عرف) and a closing stanza.
- **Lexicons:** Ibn Malik, *Ikmal al-I'lam bi-Tathlith al-Kalam* (shamela 7047, `ikmal.txt`), the triplets dictionary; Ibn Manzur, *Lisan al-Arab* (shamela 1687): سهم, vol. 12, pp. 309–310; عمر, vol. 4, p. 602 (`lisan-4-602.txt`).
- **Also seen:** Fathi al-Khuli, *Qutuf Lughawiyya*, pp. 152 ff. (archive.org `20220628_20220628_0417`), which prints the poem with yet another verse commentary; checked at 12.1 and 15.1 only.

`abyat_in_full` is 162, the combined text as the starting text has it, all of it recited. Al-Bahnasi's poem alone is 32 stanzas (64 of these abyat) in Uwayda's edition, and the recited text has 39 stanzas of his poem (78 abyat) and 42 of Ibn Zurayq's (84 abyat, with his five closing stanzas).

## Meter and harakat

- **Meter:** majzu' al-rajaz. Every half was scanned with a rajaz check (feet مستفعلن, مفاعلن, مفتعلن, فعِلَتن; the last may be مفعولن or فعولن, and the rhymes on ـام take an extra long syllable).
