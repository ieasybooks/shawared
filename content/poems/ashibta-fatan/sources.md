# أَشِبتَ فَتىً وَقَبلَ ضُحى المَشيبِ

## Recording and text

Khalid al-Sharafi recites 27 bayts in order for Alqimmah Studio. The studio credits Ihab al-Shabatat and published the performance on 19 April 2024. The source audio runs 288.533 seconds. The edit retains 2.55–286.05 seconds, including the decay of the last rhyme. Each half is said once. [Studio recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q5NJpVFDbm4)

On 23 November 2014 al-Shabatat posted all 27 bayts under his own name on Manabir Thaqafiyya as «تنهيدة بعد أول شيبة», followed by twelve numbered glosses. The later publication on his al-Diwan account and the studio use the incipit as title and preserve the same order. The signed forum post and its decoded text are in `media/research/ashibta-fatan/`. After the initial HTTP 403, the independent reader recovered the complete al-Diwan entry at `media/research/ashibta-fatan/aldiwan-complete.txt`. The word collation of both authorial versions against this edition is in `media/work/shabatat/ashibta-fatan-collation.txt`. [Author's earlier text and glosses](https://www.mnaabr.com/vb/showthread.php?t=15169) · [Author's al-Diwan entry 118594](https://www.aldiwan.net/poem118594.html)

The transcript of the local WebM was compared with the studio's captions, sampled every three seconds under `media/research/video/q5NJpVFDbm4/`. The studio shows both halves and, in several places, a smaller gloss underneath them. At approximately 21, 144, 153, 165, 174, 207, 237 and 246 seconds the lower captions clarify the pulse, the tailbone, the children's fragrance, pallor, the yellow of the Quranic description, sides or directions, unseen things and the essence of a thing. Both authorial versions and the studio give 27 bayts. No verse was omitted.

## Scansion and readings

The metre is wafir and the rhyme consonant is bāʾ. In 1.1 the divisions are a-shib-ta-fa-tan / wa-qab-la-du-hal / ma-shi-bi, two runs of مفاعلتن and a closing فعولن. In 14.1 both of the long feet are contracted, ma-a-si-na / ka-an-qa-in / ta-ba-hat. The two openings «كُرهاً» and «كَرهاً» at 2 are distinguished both in the poet's posting and on the card. At 8.1 «قد» belongs on screen with the first half although its verb opens the second, as «في» does in 23.1.

- **12.2.** The later al-Diwan entry gives «مِن بدْئِها». The early post has «بدْءِها». The modern seat in «بَدئِها» reflects the genitive after «مِن». Both spellings represent the same reading.
- **14.2.** The studio prints «عَجْبُ» and explains it with عجب الذنب. The full later al-Diwan text likewise prints «عَجْبُ الحروبِ». The early forum text vowels it «عَجَبُ» in the verse, while the poet's gloss refers explicitly to the surviving bone. The separate Cohere clip supplies a sukun, and a full-half variant test favours «عَجْب» over «عَجَب» by 7.93 points (−63.19 against −71.12). The interpretation and the word card follow the noun for the tailbone.
- **19.1.** The full-recording recogniser ran the consonants of «الحَقَّ دُرٌّ» together and produced «الحق قدر». Both authorial versions and the studio have the pearl, whose scattering is the next half's subject. The scores alone are close (−67.30 for «دُرّ» and −68.89 for «قَدَر»). Both extra clip transcriptions recover the initial d sound of دُرّ after الحق. Cohere restores the separate «دُرٌ» in a complete-bayt pass.
- **20.2.** The poet explains الجُنوب as the sides or directions, plural of جَنب. He explicitly identifies the children evoked by «شذانا» at 15.1 and the sense “weak, foolish” of «اللَّغوب» at 24.2. These three glosses govern the translations.
- **21–22, 25 and 27.** Retain «مُؤق العين»، «الرَّحل تَقطُر»، «ضَريب» and the plural «أُسد» as printed. The poet glosses ضريب as a peer. The English consequently keeps the bond between mourner and mourned and closes with the roar of lions.

The Arabic and English explanations were written for this edition. The interpretation of Mary, the bone of resurrection and the colour visible to onlookers follows the poet's numbered notes. The shaking of the trunk in 21 recalls Mary's palm. ## Clip verification

The 283.50 s track measures −20.0 LUFS after a −7.5 dB gain. The first word begins at 0.44 s and the last ends at 279.16 s of the app track. The independent half-by-half pass flagged 8 of 54 clips and 21 of 243 tokens. There were no edge insertions. Audar was run on wider half-clips, and both Cohere and Audar were run on the eight complete bayts affected by differences or weak word alignments. The comparison is in `media/work/q5NJpVFDbm4/clip-review.txt`, with the complete-bayt transcripts in the neighbouring `flag-context-cohere/` and `flag-context-audar/` directories.

| Half | Clip evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| 12.1 | Cohere joins «التي ما» as «اليتيمة». Audar separates the words in the short clip and both models return «التي ما بدت» in the complete bayt. The negative «ما» governs the verb in 12.2. |
| 14.1 | The short clips attach the comparative kāf to «مآسينا» and divide the following bird name. With the complete bayt Audar gives «مآسينا كعنقاء تباهت». The poet glosses the mythical bird, and both authorial texts print the simile. |
| 17.1, 18.1 | Cohere adds a possessive to «وجوه» and joins «تفيض ناساً» in writing. Audar separates the intended words in both half-clips. Both full-bayt passes also give the bare plural “faces” and the mills pouring out people. |
| 19.1 | The clip turns «الحقّ دُرّ» into one graphic word. Audar separates the pearl from «الحق» but appends a consonant to it. Full-bayt Cohere recovers «أنّ الحقّ دُرٌّ تناثر», which also agrees with the two authorial publications and the studio card. |
| 23.2 | Both recognisers divide «ذُرى هَوَسي لِشمس» as if the first pronoun belonged to «ذرى» and «وسيل» were a new word. Audar’s wider half also includes the preceding «في», which belongs to 23.1. The poet prints «ذُرى هَوَسي» in both publications and glosses «ذُرى» and «غُيوب» separately. The studio card agrees. The complete-half acoustic score is −36.62 for «ذرى هوسي لشمس» against −43.55 for «ذراها وسيل شمس», a 6.93-point advantage. This keeps the first-person longing and the object it reaches towards. |
| 24.1, 24.2 | The isolated 24.1 produces an unrelated string about the earth. Audar and both complete-bayt passes recover the question about uncovering a veil. Cohere splits the noun «كُنهاً» into two short words at 24.2. Audar restores the single word, explicitly glossed by the author as essence. |

The four weak alignments were «هلِ» in 24.1 (0.335), «فكم» and «من» in 26.1 (0.347 and 0.209), and «وكم» in 26.2 (0.319). All are audible to the independent complete-bayt models. The 26.1 and 26.2 half-clips also reproduce the repeated construction exactly.
