# صَحا القَلبُ عَن سَلمى وَأَقصَرَ باطِلُه

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari, on his own channel, uploaded 15 May 2023. The original is 444.941 s. The single keep is 9.00–443.78 s, giving 434.78 s. The source-voice audit places the opening at 9.62 s and the last voiced group through 443.32 s. The reciter closes on «جاهله». The uploader displays one half at a time. The original cards, the two full-take transcriptions and the alignment account for 94 consecutive halves, with no repeated bayt. [Original recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-WzElzsG9U)

The edition counts **47** after al-A'lam al-Shantamari’s third poem of Zuhayr in *Ash'ar al-shu'ara' al-sitta al-jahiliyyin*. His heading names Hisn ibn Hudhayfa. The first page contains 1–11, the next 12–47. The last two are part of this version and of the recording. They have a separate attribution history. [Poem 3, opening](https://shamela.ws/book/5449/49) · [Continuation and close](https://shamela.ws/book/5449/50)

## Witnesses and commentary

- Tha'lab, *Sharh shi'r Zuhayr ibn Abi Sulma*, ed. Fakhr al-Din Qabawa, poem 7, pp. 101–115. The scanned pages supply the text and scholia. The headnote gives the dispute with al-Nu'man. Hisn granted protection to a man the king wanted. The demand that he surrender the fugitive develops into a test of what the chief and his allies can withstand. The place-names and first rains are treated on pp. 102–104. The horse, the wild asses, the handling of the bridle and the parting of the jack from his herd occupy pp. 104–110. The praise of Hisn follows on pp. 110–115. [Scanned edition](https://archive.org/details/3918pdf)
- The two editions transmit some material in a different order. Recording 1–9 = Tha'lab 1–9. Recording 10, 11 = Tha'lab 11, 10. Recording 12–29 = Tha'lab 12–29. Recording 30–36 = Tha'lab 34–40, then 37–40 = Tha'lab 30–33. Recording 41–45 = Tha'lab 41–45. Tha'lab’s numbered text ends with the sands of Alij. Qabawa prints the two household-quarrel verses of recording 46–47 in the apparatus on p. 115 and records competing attributions, including Khawwat ibn Jubayr. Other unperformed praise verses quoted in the notes on p. 113 include «ترى الجند والأعراب يغشون بابه» and «فلو لم يكن في كفّه غير نفسه». They are outside the 47-verse base. [Tha'lab, printed p. 113](https://archive.org/details/3918pdf/page/n112/mode/1up) · [Apparatus, p. 115](https://archive.org/details/3918pdf/page/n114/mode/1up)
- Abu Ubayd al-Qasim ibn Sallam, *al-Gharib al-musannaf*, I, p. 357, quotes «وأهل خباء» under Khawwat’s name and glosses «أنا آجله» as «جالبه». The closing verses turn from praise of Hisn to a quarrel told in the first person. The translation follows Abu Ubayd’s gloss so the reader knows who caused the trouble before the culprit turns up to ask what happened. [Lexical testimony](https://shamela.ws/book/1750/339)
- Al-Suyuti, *Sharh shawahid al-Mughni*, II, pp. 940–942, quotes the passage about the women who reproach Hisn and about the petitioner. He explains وَعُرِّيَ as a metaphor for leaving youthful pastimes, the visitors who seek gifts, the daily giving and the competing readings of الصريم. The raincloud of verse 30 picks up the pasture rain of verse 8 and the squall of verse 24. Tha'lab’s gloss on «مفاصله» supplies the image behind the fair judgement in 38. [Praise passage](https://shamela.ws/book/17724/939) · [Glosses](https://shamela.ws/book/17724/940)
- Al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, III, p. 52, gives Zuhayr’s genealogy, the family of poets, his residence at al-Hajir, the tradition of revising the *hawliyyat* and the date 13 before the Hijra (609 CE). [Biographical entry](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/2081)

## Metre and rhyme

Tawil with lam as *rawiy*. The final pronominal ha follows the lam as *wasl*, also in the two opening half-rhymes «باطِلُه / رواحِلُه». A dash is long and u short. The three-syllable foot may end in a short syllable through *qabd*.

| Recorded bayt | Sadr | Ajuz |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 «صحا القلب … وعُرّي أفراس الصبا» | `u-- / u--- / u-u / u-u-` | `u-u / u--- / u-u / u-u-` |
| 23 «وقلت تعلّم … وإلا تضيّعها» | `u-u / u--- / u-- / u-u-` | `u-- / u--- / u-u / u-u-` |

In the opening, «صَحَلْقَلْـ» makes the first فعولن in liaison. In 23.1 «وَقُلْتُ» gives its shorter فعولُ before the instruction to the young rider.
