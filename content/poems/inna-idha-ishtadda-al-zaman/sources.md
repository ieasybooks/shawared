# إنّا إذا اشتدّ الزمان

## Recording and edition

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed for al-Shankabutiyya, 14 May 2014. The voice introduces Ja‘far and his uncle between 20.35 and 30.76 s. The poem begins at 32.30 s and ends near 91.31 s. Keep original 31.80–91.78 s. Both complete-take transcripts give twelve consecutive bayts, with no reprise. The spoken prose supplies the occasion of the letter and is excluded from playback. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4V_aHIowZSE)

Sami al-Dahhan, ed., *Diwan Abi Firas al-Hamdani*, II, no. 280, pp. 341–342, under قافية الميم. The page images give the attribution, the twelve bayts and the metrical divisions. The apparatus records disagreement in the names attached to the letter in its manuscripts, and explains the editor’s adoption of «أبي محمد جعفر بن ورقاء» and «أبي أحمد عبد الله بن ورقاء». The introduction follows the same two names that al-Saeed gives. Ja‘far’s uncle is the other party and Ja‘far is the arbitrator. The scope of the underlying quarrel is less certain. Al-Tha‘alibi, *Yatimat al-dahr*, I, pp. 64–65, gives the four opening bayts expressly from a poem to Ja‘far ibn Warqa. Their pairing of courage with hospitality also occurs in Shakir al-Batluni’s *Nafh al-azhar*, p. 78. The first four circulated without the family appeal. Here all twelve are heard, so the English and sharh carry «إني وإن ... أصبو» across 6–7 and the message after «أبلغه» across 11–12. [Al-Tha‘alibi](https://shamela.ws/book/6743/41) · [Al-Batluni](https://shamela.ws/book/28175/76)

## Metre and readings

Truncated kamil, two متفاعلن feet per half, often contracted to مُتْفاعِلُن. In the first sadr the beats fall «إِنّا إِذَشْ / تَدْدَزْزَما», with final نُ in the ajuz, and 4.1 has «هاذا وَها / ذا دَأبُنا». Mim is the closed rhyme. Preserve the split words in 1 الزما/ن, 3 السيو/ف, 6 المزا/ر, 7 الخلا/ل, 8 الفرا/ق, 11 أقو/ل and 12 كره/ت. Reading a line aloud continuously naturally reunites them. The English places the complete lexical word in the corresponding phrase. In 2 the equipment is plural عُدَد, in 4 the passive يُودَى means that blood money is paid. In 6 أَمَم means near at hand. The last bayt in al-Dahhan reads «أَنّي رضيتُ وإن كَرِهْتَ أبا محمدٍ الحكم». The second-person ending belongs to the uncle hearing the message. The online corpus instead prints first-person كرهتُ. The signed recording description agrees with al-Dahhan on second person. An equal-window fit at app 55.90–56.84 s favours كَرِهتَ أَبا over كَرِهتُ أَبا by 4.61 log-score units (−13.91 against −18.52). The print and the addressee support that modest acoustic preference.

## Per-half verification

The narrow Cohere pass flags thirteen of twenty-four halves. The widened Audar results and both continuous passes were compared against the diwan.

| Half | Evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| 1.2 | Initial ن is the end of الزمان. The wider clip hears the tail of that word before «وناب خطب». Retain the printed caesura. |
| 3.1, 3.2 | Narrow «السيول» in the first clip and loss of ف in the second. The wider clips recover السيوف whole on both sides. The printed page names the swords explicitly. |
| 5.1 | Both clippers write ورقة where the full introduction names ابن ورقاء. The verse has the metrically shortened ورقا. |
| 6.1, 6.2 | The first recogniser finishes المزار in the sadr and drops its ر in the ajuz. Wider Audar brings part of the same word into 6.2 and writes أمام for أَمَم. Keep the attested adjective describing a nearby dwelling. |
| 7.2 | Wider «خلالي وأسطفي» carries the preceding الخلال into the clip. The page and continuous Cohere give the verb أصطفي and the second set of qualities. |
| 8.1, 8.2 | Both recognise full الفراق near the division. Wider Audar attaches عادية to the end of ألوم or brings syllables across the following cut. They form separate words in the line and the ق opens the printed ajuz. |
| 11.1, 11.2 | Narrow أقول and قول and widened أقول on both sides recover the complete verb. Keep the printed split. |
| 12.1, 12.2 | Narrow recognition completes كرهت early and supplies يا at the next onset. Wider Audar has «كرهت أبا محمد الحكم» with no vocative particle. أبو محمد is the person accepted as arbitrator. |
