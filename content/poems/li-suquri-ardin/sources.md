# لِصُقورِ أَرضٍ زَورَةُ العَلياءِ

## Recording and text

Khalid al-Sharafi's performance was published by Alqimmah Studio on 15 April 2025. The description names Ihab al-Shabatat as poet and links his contribution 118593 on al-Diwan. The local source runs 390.485 seconds. The edit keeps 2.90–387.95 seconds. All 35 bayts are recited in order and the last is recited a second time. A separate transcription of source seconds 358–388 recovers the end of 34 followed by two complete readings of 35. The apparent extra 35.2 in the intake transcript comes from its chunk boundary at 378 seconds. [Studio recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=18xIEhwwX1E) · [Author's al-Diwan text linked by the studio](https://www.aldiwan.net/poem118593.html)

On 1 December 2015 the poet posted «إلى روح ذلك الشاب» under his own name on Manabir Thaqafiyya. Its 34 bayts and his subsequent reply are preserved in `media/research/li-suquri-ardin/manabir.html`. The dedication names no individual. His later al-Diwan entry gives the complete 35-bayt text under the incipit. An independent reader recovered that page after the initial direct request met a challenge. The saved page text is `media/research/li-suquri-ardin/aldiwan-complete.txt`. The third witness is the text displayed during the recitation, checked against the three-second frames in `media/research/video/18xIEhwwX1E/` and the local source audio transcript. [Author's earlier post](https://www.mnaabr.com/vb/showthread.php?t=18754) · [Author's later al-Diwan text](https://www.aldiwan.net/poem118593.html)

The 35-bayt count follows the later authorial publication and the studio cards. Bayt 32 about exile in the company of the ignorant appears in both, between the cloud on the eastern shore and the closed gate. The early post omits it. The other 34 bayts follow the same order. The three-way word collation is in `media/work/shabatat/li-suquri-ardin-collation.txt`.

## Scansion and readings

The metre is kamil, with hamza as the rhyme consonant. In 1.1 the divisions are li-su-qu-ri-ar / din-zaw-ra-tul / al-ya-i, متفاعلن / مُتْفاعلن / مُتْفاعلْ. The opening is rhymed in both halves. In 19.1 ta-ta-sa-ba-qul / af-la-ku-haw / la-di-ya-ri-na gives full, contracted and full feet. The closing hamza follows the long ā throughout, including in the first-person rhymes «إنائي» and «أدوائي».

- **5.2 and 6.1.** The unusual verbs «هَيُؤت» and «فُئِدت» are visible at approximately 54 and 66 seconds and appear in the early post. The first describes good appearance, the second being injured in the heart. Taking the roots seriously preserves the contrast between the visible mark and the poet's insides. [Ibn Manzur, on هيؤ](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/122/8843/) · [Cairo Arabic Language Academy, فُئِدَ](https://www.arabicacademy.gov.eg/ar/محرك-البحث/فُئِدَ)
- **9.2.** The early text has the singular «كاحل». The later authorial entry and the studio at 96 s give the plural «كواحل», also recovered by both independent transcribers. The separate same-length candidate test gives −30.92 to the plural, −34.02 to the singular. The feet of the earth answer the height of the mountains.
- **16.2.** The forum post puts a kasra on the last consonant of «دينونةِ». The full later entry explicitly prints the accusative «دينونةَ», the direct object of «مراقباً». The variant test gives −38.67 to the accusative and −45.37 to the genitive. A separate Cohere clip explicitly supplies the accusative too.
- **18, 21 and 23.** The full later text and the studio give «خضرائها» with the genitive hamza. At 21 the reeds and turbid water are an interpretation of the drinking imagery in «حياضها» and «مراشف». The referent of «الكدراء» is unnamed. The studio marks «خُفّ» with a damma at 243 s. The hot ground and the travelling dew explain the image of footgear. [Cairo Arabic Language Academy, رشف](https://www.arabicacademy.gov.eg/ar/search_engine/roots/رشف)
- **22.1 and 24.2.** The intake recogniser proposed «في أنفاقها» against the author and the caption «في أنفاسنا». Comparing the complete sadr gives −50.35 for «أنفاسنا» and −60.23 for «أنفاقها». The breath also leads directly into the ribs in 22.2. At 24.2 the colloquial-looking spelling «الفأفأء» of the forum post is regularised to «الفأفاء» for the repeated f sound. [Ibn Manzur, فأفأ](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/122/6074/فأفأ)
- **27.1 and 28.2.** The early text marks «لِعِداتنا» with kasra, the plural of عِدة “promise”. Testing it against «لِعُداتنا» “for our enemies” gives −71.50 and −77.53. The cheek of the newborn wheat is an interpretation of «أصفر الوجناء». In classical use وجناء describes a sturdy or full-cheeked she-camel.
- **31–33.** The later authorial text and the studio at 327 s have «يَتيمةَ غُربةِ البُعداءِ» where the forum poem has «يتيمةَ قسوةَ البعداء». The same-length test favours «ضمّ» over «يضمّ» at 31.1 (−60.85 against −83.05) and «غربة» over «قسوة» at 31.2 (−49.64 against −62.82). The following bayt 32 belongs to the fuller version alone. At 33.2 the caption and both authorial publications have «بدماء» without a possessive yāʾ. Both separate clip transcribers give «بدماء». The unpossessed form scores −25.99 against −29.82 for «بدمائي». The reciter stretches the final kasra. The translation therefore leaves the blood unassigned.

The Arabic and English explanations are editorial readings of the two authorial versions and the studio's images. The childhood scenes and the unnamed grammatical referent in 17 remain open. The local grapes, henna hand and the body healing itself are traced from the actual wording. No commentary assigning them to a named person or event was located.


## Clip verification

The retained track runs 385.05 s after a gain of −8.8 dB to −20.0 LUFS. In app time the first voice is at 0.52 s and the last word ends at 381.48 s. All 70 distinct halves are timed, with both halves of 35 timed a second time. The independent Cohere pass found 15 differences in 72 clips, affecting 27 of 302 word tokens. Each flagged clip was also transcribed with Audar at wider boundaries. Seven complete bayts were transcribed again with both models. The raw results are in `media/work/18xIEhwwX1E/verify.log`, `audited-flags/asr/`, `flag-context-cohere/` and `flag-context-audar/` under the same work directory.
