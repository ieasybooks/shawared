## Printed witnesses

- Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, part I, Dar al-Awda, Beirut, 1988, pp. 68–72. «ذكرى المولد» is the printed heading and supplies the edition title. Page 68 has eleven bayts beneath the rule separating the previous poem. Page 69 begins «أخا الدنيا أرى دنياك أفعى» and has seventeen. Pages 70–72 have seventeen, fifteen and eleven respectively. The total is **71**. The excerpt stops at the snake’s skin. The next bayt gives the snakes their fangs, «وأن الرقط أيقظ هاجعات». The explicit celebration of the birth, «تجلّى مولد الهادي», comes at 55. The last bayt begins «وفي هذا الزمان مسيح علم», above the heading «مشروع ملنر». The heading states «وقال في ذكرى المولد النبوي سنة ثلاث وثلاثين وثلاثمائة وألف من الهجرة (١٩١٤م)». The introduction uses the stated Hijri year, 1333 AH, which overlaps 1914 and 1915 CE. Al-Abyari’s p. 257 distinguishes the heart of flesh from the heart as a speaker, glosses «وصفّق» as a beat one can hear, and likens the brief reunion to bubbles. ## Metre and rhyme

Wafir. In 1.1 the feet are مفاعيلن / مفاعلتن / فعولن, and in 1.2 مفاعلتن / مفاعلتن / فعولن. At 6.1 the second foot has عصب, giving مفاعلتن / مفاعيلن / فعولن. At 6.2 both first feet are full. The rawiy is ba in «ثابا / عتابا» and «العذابا». The final alif carries the prolonged vowel.
