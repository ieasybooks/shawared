## Attribution

- **Not in the old diwan.** محمد سعيد مولوي, ديوان عنترة، تحقيق ودراسة (al-Maktab al-Islami): scan archive.org `3562pdf` (printed page = leaf − 5). It gives al-A'lam al-Shantamari's recension with al-Batalyawsi's additions (pp. 185–331) and a supplement of pieces found in other books, «زيادات شعر عنترة (صلة الديوان)» (pp. 331–340). The poem is in none of them. Its first bayt is not in the rhyme index (pp. 364–367; the lam poems are on p. 366), and none of its abyat is in the edition's poems on the same meter and rhyme, read on the page images: no. 6 «طال الثواء على رسوم المنزل» (pp. 246–253), no. 7 «عجبت عبيلة من فتى متبذّل» (pp. 253–262) and the supplement's nos. 18–20 (pp. 336–338).
- **The printings it comes from.** Mawlawi, pp. 54–56: the first printed diwan, Iskandar Agha Abkarius's «منية النفس في أشعار عنترة عبس» (Beirut, 1864), «جمع كل ما وصلت إليه يده من شعر منسوب لعنترة، سواء ما كان منه في نسخ الأعلم أو ما كان في السيرة الشعبية» (p. 54); of it he writes «ومن الواضح أننا لا نستطيع أن نعتمد على هذا الديوان في استقاء شعر عنترة لجهالة روايته، ولأنه لا يعطي المصادر التي أخذ عنها، فضلاً عن أن الأشعار التي وردت في السيرة الشعبية يصعب قبولها جملة» (p. 55). Amin al-Khuri's Matba'at al-Adab in Beirut reprinted it many times, and later printings followed it, Amin Sa'id's commentary among them (pp. 55–56); the Dar Sadir diwan of 1958 holds «ما صح لعنترة وما حمل عليه، وما حفلت به السيرة الشعبية» (p. 56). The three printings used below are of this line.
- **In the Sira.** سيرة عنترة بن شداد (Cairo: Maktabat al-Jumhuriyya al-Arabiyya, n.d.), vol. 3 (part 17), pp. 289–291: scan archive.org `AAlexandrina-174539`, leaves 292–294. Antar meets al-Akhyal, the Banu Hariqa's champion, and «طعنه في صدره أطلع السنان يلمع من ظهره»; the tribe flees to the mountains, leaving herds and families; at evening Antar «ينشد ويقول» 23 lines on this meter and rhyme. Ten are the diwan's 1–6, 13, 17, 21 and 22, in another order and with other words in places («وإذا الذليل نهاك … من الرماح الذبل», «واحمل إذا حق اللقا», «فلقد نكبت», «وجهنم في العز أفخر منزل»); four draw on the authentic «طال الثواء» (Mawlawi no. 6, abyat 17–19 and 21: «…تخوّفني الحتوف كأنني», «فأجبتها إن المنية منهل», «أني امرؤ سأموت إن لم أقتل», «والخيل ساهمة/شاخصة الوجوه كأنما») and a fifth reworks its 20th («وكذا المنية لو تمثل شخصها…»); the other eight are in neither.
- **So:** the poem belongs to the Sira tradition, not to the early transmission. The intro says what the sources say: it is not in Mawlawi's edition, and it is in the printings he describes as drawing on the Sira.

## Editions

- **Starting text:** Tarad's voweled text, in the house style; where the reciter departs from it, the text follows him (below).
- **شرح ديوان عنترة للخطيب التبريزي**, ed. Majid Tarad (Dar al-Kitab al-Arabi, Beirut, 1992): scan archive.org `3926pdf`, poem 115, pp. 134–135 (leaves 133–134). 22 abyat, fully voweled, with the editor's short glosses and no commentary. His «تنبيهات» (leaf 18): the commented poems are al-A'lam's (27) and al-Batalyawsi's additions (13); the others are taken «من ديوان عنترة (تحقيق وشرح فوزي عطوي) أو من بعض الكتب التراثية», the latter with their source in a note. This one has none, so it is Fawzi Atwi's text.
- **ديوان عنتر** (Beirut: Matba'at al-Adab, 1893, 4th printing): scan archive.org `AAlexandrina-027929`, p. 70 (leaf 73). «وقال في إغارته على بني حريقة», 22 abyat, lightly voweled.
- **شرح ديوان عنترة بن شداد**, ed. Amin Sa'id (Cairo: al-Matba'a al-Arabiyya, n.d.): scan archive.org `AAlexandrina-142763`, pp. 114–115 (leaves 115–116). «وقال في إغارته على بني حريقة (من الكامل)», 22 abyat, voweled, with four footnotes.
- **Anthologies, all modern:** al-Hashimi, *Jawahir al-adab*, vol. 2, pp. 264–265 (shamela 21505/491–492, `hashimi.txt`), 13 abyat (1, 3–5, 8–14, 21–22), «وقال أيضاً في إغارته على بني حريقة»; Ahmad Qabash, *Majma' al-hikam wa-l-amthal*, vol. 4, p. 49 (shamela 12930/1549, `qabash.txt`), 1–7 and 21–22; *Mawsu'at al-qaba'il al-arabiyya*, vol. 4, pp. 790–791 (shamela 897/3503–3504, `qabail.txt`), all 22; al-Batluni, *Nafh al-azhar*, p. 66 (shamela 28175/64, `batluni.txt`), 11–12 alone. The old shamela's Antara diwan (book 4472, the Abu Dhabi encyclopedia's text, `oldshamela-diwan.txt`) has the 22 abyat as the YouTube description does.

`abyat_in_full` is 22, the count in all three printings.

- **4.1 and 16.1:** here the reciter departs from all three printings, as the description's text does. He restores the dropped alif in 2.1, 10.1 and 12.1 (the transcripts hear «ظالما», «العلا», «إذا») but says «بِهِ» (both transcribers hear «به»; bihī beats bihā by 4.3) and «كَأَنَّهُ» (both transcribers; the aligner by 2.0). Both readings make sense: «به» is the coward, «كأنه» the son likened to a hyena. Notes give the printings' «بها» and «كأنها».
- **7.1:** both transcribers hear «عزة»; in the first alignment «عِزِّهِ» was the weakest word (0.17), «عِزَّةٍ» aligns at 0.61.
- **15.1 «الحَريشَ»:** a coin toss for the aligner; both transcribers hear «الحريش».
- **15.1 «وَمالِكاً», open:** with candidates of equal length (mālikan, mālikin, mālikun) the aligner prefers -kin by 2.3; cohere writes «وَمَالِكٍ», audar «ومالك». The accusative is required (the objects of «قتلتُ» run from 14.1 to 15.2), all printings have it, and the word just before it, «الحَريشَ», is accusative in his mouth (+3.7 over «الحَريشِ»). The text keeps «وَمالِكاً»; where the description has the bare «وَمالِك» a slip in the case ending is likely, but it is not clear enough to put a wrong case into the text.
- **15.1 «وَابنَي»:** the aligner prefers «وَبَني» by 3.5, but it is the shorter candidate; audar hears «وأبنى», cohere «وَبَنَيْ»; all sources have «وابني», and both scan.
- **15.2:** a coin toss; Tarad's accusative, another object of «قتلتُ».
- **9.1:** both transcribers hear «النسبة»: the tanwin of «عبسٍ» running into «نسبتي». «النسبة» breaks the third foot; the text keeps «نِسبَتي».
- **4.2 «وَاقدِم»:** أَقدِم read with its hamza as wasl; with the hamza the first foot breaks. Sa'id prints «واقْدِمْ» and Tarad marks the alif as wasl, as he does in 5.1 «وَٱخْتَرْ»; Beirut leaves it unvoweled.
- **14.2 «اِبنَ»:** written with its alif (the printings write «بن» between the two names) because the house-style check reads a word-initial «بنَ» as unvoweled.
- **Meter decides:** 3.2 «مِنَ اِزدِحامِ»; 4.2 «اللِّقا», shortened from اللقاء (the clip transcript writes «اللقاء»); 20.1 «عِزُّكُم» (with «عِزُّكُمُ» the second foot breaks); 21.1 «لا تَسقِني» (Beirut and the old shamela text have «لا تسقيني», which breaks the first foot); 22 «كَجَهَنَّمٍ … وَجَهَنَّمٌ» with tanwin, a diptote given it for the meter, which needs it in both halves.
- **Scansion:** every half scans as kamil (`scan.py` with its feet set for kamil). Two use rare variants, both in every printing: 4.1 «فَاعصِ مَقا» −uu− (khazl: مُفتَعِلُن) and 20.1 «زُكُم وَذُل» u−u− (waqs: مُفاعِلُن).

## Commentary drawn on

- **Glosses:** Tarad's notes (القسطل, الجندل, السماكان, الأجزل, الشفار والأنصل, المحجّل, نكب, الأخيل as «المتكبّر», التقلقل); the *Mawsu'at al-qaba'il* notes (الذابل والمهند, العديد الأجزل, الشفار, المحجّل); Amin Sa'id's (الأخيل as a bird of ill omen, المهر, «ضبع ترعرع.. أي نما وشب»); al-A'lam's commentary in Mawlawi, p. 253, for الكريهة («إذا حملت نفسي على مكروه الحرب»); Ibn Qutayba, *al-Anwa'*, p. 62 (shamela 9093/109, `anwa.txt`) for السماك الأعزل والرامح; *Lisan al-Arab*, vol. 10, p. 137 (shamela 1687/4807) for الزبرقان «القمر». The rest are the common lexical senses.
- **Al-Akhyal and the Banu Hariqa:** the Sira, vol. 3, pp. 289–290 (above). Tarad and the *Mawsu'at* gloss الأخيل as «المتكبّر», Amin Sa'id as a bird; the note gives the Sira's man first, since the bayt names someone speared through the heart.
- **Allusions:** 2, Amr ibn Kulthum's «ألا لا يجهلن أحد علينا فنجهل فوق جهل الجاهلينا»; 6, Quran 4:78; 21–22, Amin Sa'id: «هذا البيت من الأبيات الحكمية البليغة», «هذا البيت من الحكميات».
- **The father who would not own him (9, 16):** Ibn Qutayba, *al-Shi'r wa-l-shu'ara'*, vol. 1, p. 243 (shamela 23785/236, `ibnqutayba.txt`).
- **The poet's life:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 5, p. 91 (shamela 12286/4233, `alam.txt`): name, mother, Abla, Dahis and al-Ghabra', his death and «أكثر ما فيه مصنوع»; the freedom story from Ibn Qutayba. `died` is left out of `poet.json`: al-A'lam gives «نحو ٢٢ ق هـ = نحو ٦٠٠ م», and the format (a whole hijri year shown as "d. … AH") can't hold an approximate date before the Hijra; both bios give it.
