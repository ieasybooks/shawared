# Abu al-Ghul and the defence of al-Waqba

## Recording and text

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed precedes the verse with a spoken historical account, ending «قال أبو الغول الطهوي في ذلك». The pause at 58.92–59.54 seconds separates that introduction from «فدت نفسي». Keep 59.10–110.66 on the original clock, with the final الهدون inside the cut. Seven bayts, in order, with no repetitions or omissions from the Hamasa piece. Both «فدت نفسي وما ملكت يميني» and «هم منعوا حمى الوقبى بضرب» scan in wafir, ending the half in فعولن. Nun is the rhyme consonant in ظنوني and الزبون. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G7oo5OlP2E4)

- Abu Tammam's *Hamasa*, opening of Bab al-hamasa, no. 3, with al-Marzuqi's *Sharh Diwan al-hamasa*, pp. 32–36. At 1 he reads the apparent statement as a wish of ransom. At 2 he traces الزبون to the kick of the milch camel. At 5 he offers several explanations of separate deaths joined by one encounter. At 6 he supplies الضرب as the subject of نكّب, and at 7 explains the metaphor of grazing on ease. - Abu al-Qasim al-Farisi, *Sharh Hamasat Abi Tammam*, II, pp. 83–84, gives all seven under Abu al-Ghul. He records «الطحون» for 2.2 and «أرض الهدون» beside «روض الهدون» for 7.2. The recording selects الزبون and روض. Abu Ali al-Qali, *Amali*, I, pp. 260–261, transmits the verses through a separate teaching tradition. At 1.2 «صدّقوا فيهم ظنوني» makes the riders the subject and the speaker's hopes the object. Both commentators record the alternative in which the hopes themselves prove true. - Al-Bakri, *Muʿjam ma istaʿjam*, IV, p. 1381, entry الوقبى, reproduces Abu Ubayda's account of the water passing from Bakr to Mazin and the ensuing battle with Shayban. He records both a still and a moving qaf in the place name. Al-Amidi, *al-Muʾtalif wa-l-mukhtalif*, pp. 212–213, distinguishes the Tuhawi, also called Abu al-Bilad, from the Nahshali poet of similar name. The ghoul encounter is his reported account of his own epithet. Neither the personal name of the Nahshali nor a guessed death year is entered in this profile. ## Readings

The word-window test at 1.2 gives −19.19 for صدّقوا and −20.91 for صدقت. A gap of 1.72 cannot settle the inflection on its own. Both complete transcripts give the plural verb, and al-Farisi's first reading takes the horsemen as the subject. At 2.2 الزبون scores −13.47 against −25.01 for الطحون. At 7.2 روض scores −7.73 against −15.89 for أرض. Al-Farisi records both alternatives in his commentary.

## Clip verification

The first pass flagged 2.2, 5.1 and 6.1. After the half boundaries were adjusted, the final pass also flagged 4.2, for **four of fourteen halves and eight differing words out of 64**.

| 6.1 | عنهمود راي | عنه مودر | Both fragment the transition between عنهمُ and درء. The full first transcript gives both words. Al-Marzuqi expressly explains الدرء and its role as the object of نكّب. |
