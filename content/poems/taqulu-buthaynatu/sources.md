- **الشَّعَرِ** goes against its score. The mutaqarib needs ša'ari here: with ša'ri the third foot would be − − ⏑. A fast «ša'ari» sounds close to ša'r.
- **Coin tosses (2.1, 2.2, 3.1)** were settled by sense and grammar. كَبِرَ means to grow old; أَتَنسَينَ is the feminine of نَسِيَ.
- **Dictionary forms.** Where the recitation departs from Dar Sadir (لَمَّتي, المَرزَبان, تُعصَري, تَكبُري), the word card gives the dictionary form in `head`.
