# Jarir before Abd al-Malik

## Recording and textual witnesses

Osama al-Nahari recites 22 consecutive verses, from the question to his heart through the distinction between the sick and the sound. The keep is 3.29–182.41 seconds of the 186.015-second source. The final sustained rhyme in «الصحاح» accounts for the isolated «وحيد» at the end of Cohere’s full transcript. The source cards, Audar’s full transcript and the lead’s closing-boundary review establish a single ending. The historical account has Abd al-Malik ask for «ألستم خير من ركب المطايا» a second time. The present voice passes from its second half straight to «وقوم قد سموت لهم». All 44 halves occur once. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_4L5Q5yE4iU)

Muhammad ibn Habib’s *Sharh Diwan Jarir*, ed. Nu'man Muhammad Amin Taha, I, pp. 87–90, prints all 22 in this order, immediately before poem 5. Its account on pp. 83–87 places Jarir among the Iraqi delegation, reports the caliph’s reluctance to hear al-Hajjaj’s poet and describes the eventual grant of camels. The attribution of that narrative is kept in the introduction. Ibn Habib’s glosses distinguish the salt ground from the rain cloud, the single white bull from the riding camel, and Abu Khubayb, Abd Allah ibn al-Zubayr, from the caliph Jarir addresses. [Printed Diwan](https://archive.org/details/3448pdf/page/n89/mode/2up) · [Electronic text](https://shamela.ws/book/133412/42) · [Account](https://shamela.ws/book/133412/38)

Abd al-Qadir al-Baghdadi, *Sharh abyat Mughni al-labib*, I, pp. 48–53, quotes our 1 and 8–17. He explains the self-address of verse 1, identifies Umm Hazra as Jarir’s wife, distinguishes the portions of cold water given to the children and explains the long flight feathers as the means of regaining the power to travel. The exchange and reward appear again in his account. At 8 both commentaries explain the reading about milk camels. [Sharh, excerpt](https://shamela.ws/book/150966/49) · [Glosses](https://shamela.ws/book/150966/53)

Dar Beirut’s 1986 *Diwan Jarir*, pp. 76–78, and al-Sawi’s *Sharh Diwan Jarir*, pp. 96–99, each print the same 22 verses in sequence. Dar Beirut groups the poem under its famous fifteenth verse. It explicitly gives «يقول العاذلات» and «الواردين ذوي امتناح» (the gloss at p. 77 defines «امتناح» as receiving a gift). Al-Sawi also gives «يقول» but has «الموردين ذوي لقاح» in his main text. Both and Taha’s printed p. 90 have «رأى الناس البصيرة». These scans distinguish the genuine recensional alternatives from missing or transposed letters in the electronic transcript. [Dar Beirut, pp. 76–78](https://archive.org/details/3447pdf/page/n75/mode/2up) · [Al-Sawi, pp. 96–99](https://archive.org/details/3913pdf/page/n111/mode/2up)

## Metre and division

Wafir, with ḥa (ح) as the rhyme consonant. The opening scans `ataṣḥū bal / fuʾāduka ghay / ru ṣāḥī` and `ʿashiyyata ham / ma ṣaḥbuka bir / rawāḥī`. At 12 the appeal and its object occupy successive halves, `aghithnī yā / fidāka abī / wa-ummī` and `bisaybin min / ka innaka dhūr / tiyāḥī`. The long ī audible to the transcribers at the rhyme does not add a possessive pronoun.

## Readings and half-verse verification

- **1.1, 2.1.** «صاح» is the contracted participle meaning clear-headed. The narrow clip supplies a final b as in «صاحب». The wider ends in «صاحي», and word scoring gives «صاح» a margin of 6.20 with stock padding and 7.44 in the short window. The studio and Ibn Habib give feminine «تقول العاذلات» at 2.1. Both full voice transcripts and the expanded clip give «يقول» (the narrow check also adds a suffix to «شيب»). The stock score prefers yāʾ by 2.60, the shorter window by 6.92. The singular verb precedes a broken plural, which allows this agreement. Both language layers carry the variant.
- **3.2, 4.1, 6.2.** The narrow transcripts give «ضعائن» then «وعائن», and the wider clips give «أو طعائن» and «وعائن». The same word on both cards, «ظعائن», carries the caravan from one verse into the next. The 0.92 word-score difference between ض and ظ is inconclusive. The editions, sense and repetition settle the noun. At 6.2 both narrow and widened clips lose the initial h of «هجان». Taha’s printed p. 88 and Dar Beirut p. 77 both give «هجان». Only the electronic Ibn Habib text has «هيجان». Its gloss explicitly defines «الهجان» as white. The card and word window support «هجان» (+4.85).
- **8.1, 8.2.** A narrow clip adds fāʾ to «تعزت». The wider begins «تعزت» and the short-window margin is 12.93. At 8.2 the narrow clip’s «الوالدين» would introduce parents into the sentence. The wider reads «رأيت الواردين ذوي امتناح» with the end of «قالت» carried over. The card and the relation between «امتناح» and Jarir’s «امتياحي» support this reading. It scores 13.23 above «الوالدين ذوي امتناح» and 16.63 above Ibn Habib’s and al-Baghdadi’s «الموردين ذوي لقاح», herders taking milch camels to water. Dar Beirut p. 77 supplies direct printed support for the recitation. The Arabic and English word cards explain both.
- **10.2, 15.2.** Both clip transcripts hear the t of «أذاة اللوم» in construct and miss the feminine ending in «وانتظري» to some degree. The addressee is Umm Hazra. At 15.2 the short clip adds m to «راح», the wider has «بطونا راح». «بطون راح» is a construct in which the palms of hands stand for generosity. The next verse begins with wāw, with no additional word between.
- **16.1, 17.1.** The narrow «قدس موت» divides «قد سموت» across its boundaries. The wider restores both words. At 17.1 both clips segment «حمى تهامة» as «حماتها متى». The studio names Tihama and Najd and Ibn Habib explains the contrast between the two territories. The connected phrase scores 10.52 above the recogniser’s division.
- At **13.1** al-Baghdadi and the recording give «فإني» (+5.75 over «وإني» in the short window). At **22.1** the three physical prints, both full transcripts and the card supply «الناس», which is absent only from the electronic Ibn Habib. The verb has an expressed subject and the metre its full measure. The word note marks the omission.

The eleven flagged halves account for the final differences in 44 independently transcribed halves. Narrow and widened transcripts, studio frames and the variant scores are preserved under the recording ID and poem slug in `media/work/` and `media/research/`.

At **20.1** both full transcripts give «فقد وجدوا», as in Taha and Dar Beirut. Al-Sawi reads «وقد وجدوا». His initial wāw leads by only 0.89 in the isolated word window (`variant-witnesses.txt`). The two transcripts and the two prints support the displayed fāʾ.

At **14.1** the studio card at 111 seconds explicitly writes «أَن رددت», as does Ibn Habib. The isolated particle scores 5.15 above «إِن», and the connected «سأشكر أن رددت» scores 12.04 above the conditional wording. Al-Baghdadi has «إن». The full transcripts’ bare ان cannot distinguish them. The translation gives the restoration of his feathers as the object of his thanks.

Ibn Habib glosses «هبرزيّ» at 20 as «الخالص». Ibn Manzur records both pure gold and personal excellence under هبرز. For «الناس» at 22, al-Jawhari gives «أناس» as the underlying form. The card follows that derivation. [Lisan al-Arab, V, p. 423](https://shamela.ws/book/1687/3007) · [Al-Sihah, III, p. 987](https://shamela.ws/book/23235/1951)
