# Al-Simma al-Qushayri · خَليلَيَّ عوجا مِنكُما اليَومَ أَو دَعا

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari recites 62 different bayts, once each. Keep 0.330–552.310 s on the 552.507 s source clock. The first voiced onset follows the opening silence at about 0.85 s. The cut preserves the drawn-out final «نتجمّعا» and its decay. The app excerpt is 551.980 s, with 124 half-lines. The continuous Cohere and Audar transcriptions put the verses in the order of al-Jabr 1–61, then 63. Printed 49, «رأى وهو في رأس الشرى», is present between the two verses on either side of it. Printed 62, «بكل بلاد أم بكل مظنة / أخو أمل منا يحاول مطمعا», is the single omission, so our 62 is printed 63. The video uses scenery during the passages in question. The voice and its independent transcriptions supply the performance readings. ## Sources and attribution

- Khalid Abd al-Ra'uf al-Jabr, *al-Simma ibn Abd Allah al-Qushayri, hayatuhu wa-shi'ruhu*, no. 29, printed pp. 106–113. The scan numbers for these pages coincide with their printed numbers. Al-Jabr's linguistic and geographical notes inform the reader layers, particularly the hearthstones at 3, the doe at 8–10, the beloved's broken speech at 14–15, the two ravens at 21 and the tethered animal at 48–52. His pp. 114–115 trace the varying lengths and attributions. [Opening and hearthstones, p. 106](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n106/mode/1up) · [The conversation, p. 108](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n108/mode/1up) · [Transmission, pp. 114–115](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n114/mode/2up)
- Usama ibn Munqidh, *al-Manazil wa-l-diyar*, pp. 42–43, quotes the opening turn towards al-Qubayba and the next two bayts under al-Simma, followed by a form of the tear verse. Hamad al-Jasir suspected that someone supplied the incipit to a defective text, on grounds of diction and the place-name. Al-Jabr answers the objection on p. 106. It remains an attributed, disputed incipit that the reciter actually delivers. The precise location of al-Qubayba is uncertain. [Ibn Munqidh, pp. 42–43](https://shamela.ws/book/5416/42) · [Al-Jabr, p. 106](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n106/mode/1up)
- Abu Ali al-Qali, *al-Amali*, 1/190–192 in the linked edition, gives eight bayts from Abu Hatim through al-Asma'i under al-Simma, then three from al-Riyashi. The latter group includes our 44–45 and a second version of 43. The first group begins «حننت إلى ريّا» and gives «بكت عيني اليسرى». Al-Qali's lexical discussion explains the name al-Bishr and the two parts of the neck that hurt from looking back. [Al-Asma'i's opening](https://shamela.ws/book/9160/190) · [The two transmissions and glosses](https://shamela.ws/book/9160/191)
- Al-Yazidi, *al-Amali*, pp. 148–150, records a family narrative about Rayya, the proposed marriage and the dispute over the bride-price. His uncle transmits the verses from Ibn al-Kaskari. The name in this version is «عبد الله بن الصمة». Its seventeen bayts rearrange the farewell and tether passages. The story supplies an early interpretation of the separation. It cannot date a particular meeting or identify every woman named in the performance. [Al-Yazidi, pp. 148–149](https://shamela.ws/book/8196/148) · [Closing verses](https://shamela.ws/book/8196/150)
- Al-Sarraj, *Masari' al-ushshaq*, 2/201–203, assigns a sequence of fifteen overlapping bayts to **Yazid ibn al-Tathriyya**, through Ibn al-Anbari and his father. It begins with the tethered animal, passes to the look back and the farewell to Najd, and ends with «وأذكر أيام الحمى ثم أنثني». The attribution belongs to that sequence. [Heading and beginning, 2/201](https://shamela.ws/book/26549/513) · [Continuation, 2/202](https://shamela.ws/book/26549/514)
- Al-Hamdani, *Sifat Jazirat al-Arab*, printed p. 182 in the linked text, quotes only our 49 when locating al-Shara and calls its author «القشيري». Al-Jabr acknowledges that several poets bear that nisba. He inserts the verse because its sight of Najd explains why the tethered animal strains. Al-Zirikli gives the profile and the approximate year of death in *al-A'lam*, 3/209. [Al-Hamdani](https://shamela.ws/book/12521/182) · [Al-Jabr's explanation, p. 112](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n112/mode/1up) · [Al-Zirikli](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/2548)

## Readings

At 5.1 the direct address «عينك اليسرى» agrees with al-Jabr. Other transmitters give «عيني» and some give the right eye. At 8.2 both running models and both local clips give «أدنى». The consonant-only fit for «أجنى» is ahead by just 0.43. Ibn Manzur expressly gives «دنا وأدنى ودنّى إذا قرب». With «وأينع» there is an intelligible pair of predicates about the arak, close within reach and bearing ripe fruit. Al-Jabr has «أجنى», ready for gathering. At 9.1 the models and the sense of shaking off dew support «تنفض». At 12.1 the recitation has active «يشفي» with the woman's utterance as subject, where the print has the passive «يُشفى». [Al-Jabr, pp. 107–108](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n107/mode/2up) · [Lisan al-Arab, 14/273](https://shamela.ws/book/1687/7397)

At 14.1 the performance has «ضبثة الحي بالحشا», the camp's hold on the speaker's heart. The next half describes his fear that the camp will divide. All four transcriptions support «الحي». The speech scores separate the two recorded consonants and the printed ending clearly. The p. 108 scan reads «ضبثة الحب بيننا», and the gloss explicitly calls it love's grip on the heart. Both have the same rare noun ضبثة. At 15.2 the scan reads singular «ترقرقتِ العينان», the verb preceding its dual subject. [Al-Jabr, p. 108](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n108/mode/1up)

At 21.1 «غرابي بيتها» puts the ravens at her tent. The printed «غرابي بَينها» makes them birds of parting. At 22.1 the recited question follows the long diwan. Al-Qali's group begins «حننت إلى ريّا». At 25.1 «قنين» has both continuous transcriptions and widened Audar, while the acoustic comparison leaves «قنيّ» open. Al-Jabr prints «قنيّ» and discusses the unstable place-name. At 25.2 «ليلى» is clear where the diwan names Rayya. At 31.1 all four recognisers hear an initial plain h in «هلّ», and the romanised CTC test gives the candidates identical scores. The written line in the diwan, al-Qali, al-Yazidi and al-Sarraj has «حلّ بالحمى». The farewell names the people who settled in the protected pasture. The word card records the apparently weakened consonant. [Al-Jabr, pp. 109–110](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n109/mode/2up) · [Al-Yazidi's farewell](https://shamela.ws/book/8196/149) · [Al-Sarraj's farewell](https://shamela.ws/book/26549/514)

At 44.2 al-Riyashi's «بالشراب» gives precisely the recorded answer to thirst. Al-Jabr prints «بالشباب». At 46–47 the exchange has «أدمعا» and «على الصخر الأصم تصدعا», beside the printed «مدمعا» and «على صم الصفا لتصدعا». The reference for the tethered creature at 48–52 does not specify a species in these bayts. The adjacent mother camel and her calf belong to a second comparison, beginning at 54. At 57.2 «إليه» in al-Yazidi supports the masculine pronoun heard here. The long diwan prints «إليها». At 61.2 the voice has «بتشتيتنا», with an extra syllable inside the word compared with printed «بشتيتنا». The final half has «أن نتجمعا» against printed «أن تجمعا». [Al-Qali, al-Riyashi's verses](https://shamela.ws/book/9160/191) · [Al-Jabr, pp. 111–112](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n111/mode/2up) · [Al-Jabr, p. 113](https://archive.org/details/diwan-al-simmah-qushayri/page/n113/mode/1up)
