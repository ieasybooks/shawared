# منى النفس في أسماء لو تستطيعها

Osama al-Nahari recites all 45 bayts of al-Sayrafi’s no. 516 in their printed order. The two continuous transcripts move from Asma and the poet’s greying cheek to the ride to the caliph and the feud among Taghlib. They end with the anxious mother (43–44), then «بقيت فكم أبقيت … حتى استمر ظليعها» (45). Every half occurs once.

## Editions and commentary

- Al-Buhturi, *Diwan*, ed. Hasan Kamil al-Sayrafi, no. 516, printed pp. 1296–1301. P. 1296 names the caliph al-Mutawakkil and the settlement among Banu Taghlib. The invocation on behalf of Rabi'a and the proposed date of 243 AH come from the editor’s note. P. 1297 identifies the white palaces and the camel gear. Pp. 1298–1299 explain taking turns at water or wine in 21, the appeal to kinship in 26–28 and the blood a clan cannot leave unavenged. P. 1300 identifies Fath ibn Khaqan and glosses «مجفوّ» as coarse and «صنيع» as polished. P. 1301 ends with «الظليع الذي يغمز في مشيته», the man whose limp can be seen in his gait. The edition sets 7.2 and 8.1 in brackets because one witness omitted them and joined their neighbours. Both halves occur in Cairo and in the recitation. [pp. 1296–1301](https://archive.org/details/20230507_20230507_1209/page/n1360/mode/2up) · [p. 1301](https://archive.org/details/20230507_20230507_1209/page/n1365/mode/1up)
- *Diwan al-Buhturi*, corrected and supervised by Abd al-Rahman al-Barquqi, Cairo, 1329 AH (1911 CE), II, pp. 316–318. The title page describes a pointed manuscript copied in Tabriz and collation with a manuscript in the Khedivial Library. P. 316 introduces the peace of Taghlib under the rhyme letter ʿayn. P. 318 has the same 45 bayts but places the benediction «بقيت فكم أبقيت» before «ومشفقة تخشى» and «ربطت بصلح القوم». The opening of a different poem below that last bayt fixes the extent. [Title page](https://archive.org/details/3302pdf1329/page/n0/mode/1up) · [II, pp. 316–318](https://archive.org/details/3302pdf1329/page/n551/mode/2up)
- Ibn Dawud, *al-Zahra*, p. 108, quotes our 1 and 6 together in the chapter on hopes and imaginings, with «تستطيعها» and «تعصيني هوى وأطيعها». Ibn al-Athir, *al-Mathal al-sa'ir*, II, pp. 200–201, quotes our 31, 32, 34 and 35. He has «تألفتهم» addressing the caliph. Both diwans and the performance give «تألفهم», continuing the account of Fath in the third person. Ibn al-Athir permits «شَرَدَت» and «شَرَّدَت» in 34 and prefers the intensive because of the depth of the quarrel. The two active readings tie in the acoustic test. Al-Sayrafi’s pointed lemma and the meaning support the intensive. [*Al-Zahra*, p. 108](https://shamela.ws/book/738/108) · [*Al-Mathal*, II, pp. 200–201](https://shamela.ws/book/10551/526)

## Wording and order

At 4.1 Cairo has «فإنما» where the recording and al-Sayrafi have «ولم يزل». At 14.2 Cairo prints «أن لن يرام» against «ألّا يرام». Cairo’s «التلاع» at 15.2 gives small watercourses or rises, while the performed «البلاد» gives fertile country. In 30.2 its «به» resumes a masculine referent. The recorded «بها» refers back to «جرثومة», the trunk or rootstock of Taghlib. Al-Sayrafi registers both alternatives. The continuous transcriptions agree on «البلاد» and «بها». [Cairo, II, pp. 317–318](https://archive.org/details/3302pdf1329/page/n552/mode/2up) · [al-Sayrafi, pp. 1298–1300](https://archive.org/details/20230507_20230507_1209/page/n1362/mode/2up)

At 37.2 the reading is «مجفوّها وصنيعها» in both editions. It includes rough blades and blades finished on the polishing stone. In 41.1 Cairo has «بأعين» and al-Sayrafi lists it in his apparatus. Both continuous transcripts have «بأوجه», and it leads the equal-length comparison by 9.29 points. At 42.2 Cairo reads «يسفه» against the performed «تسفّه». At 43.1 Cairo has «حماماً» for «الحِمام», death. At 44.2 its «فقرّ حشاها» has the verb in the masculine where al-Sayrafi and the performance have «فقرّت حشاها». The sequence 43, 44, 45 allows the community’s recovery to have the last word. Cairo’s 45, 43, 44 closes on the mother. [Cairo, II, p. 318](https://archive.org/details/3302pdf1329/page/n553/mode/1up) · [al-Sayrafi, pp. 1300–1301](https://archive.org/details/20230507_20230507_1209/page/n1364/mode/2up)

## Meter and rhyme

Tawil. At 1.1 «مُنَنْنَفْ / سِ فِي أَسْمَا / ءَ لَوْ تَسْ / تَطِيعُهَا» gives فعولن مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. At 22.1 «إِذَفْتَ / رَقُو عَنْ وَقْ / عَتِنْ جَ / مَعَتْهُمُو» gives فعولُ مفاعيلن فعولُ مفاعلن. The rawiy is ʿayn. The recurrent pronoun ـها follows it, as in «ولوعها», «دموعها» and «ظليعها».
