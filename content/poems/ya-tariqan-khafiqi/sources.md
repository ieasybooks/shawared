# يا طارِقاً خافِقي ما كُنتُ أَعرِفُهُ

## Text and recording

The author's al-Diwan account, @eehabodeh, prints twenty abyat. The studio independently sets both halves on screen and supplies short lexical notes. Khalid al-Sharafi reads all twenty once, in their published order. Alqimmah Studio posted the recording on 23 August 2025. The downloaded page is *media/research/ya-tariqan-khafiqi/author.txt*. Caption frames are stored under *media/research/video/eCy5Ncfn7GA/*. [Poet's text](https://www.aldiwan.net/poem118601.html) · [Studio recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eCy5Ncfn7GA)

The archived collation against the author page finds the same words in all twenty abyat. The author often leaves a space after the conjunction wa. At 18.1 one online kasra has slipped under the ta in مُرْتِحلاً. The edition has مُرتَحِلاً, the active participle of اِرتَحَلَ.

The edit keeps 3.05–215.85 s from a 218.411 s source. Leading and trailing silence are removed. The 212.80 s recording measures −20.0 LUFS after a −7.4 dB gain. The first and central abyat scan on basit, with مستفعلن فاعلن مستفعلن فَعِلُن and its usual variations. The fixed fa immediately precedes the attached ha throughout. Fa is the rawiy, with ha as wasl.

## Readings and commentary

- **6.1.** Both the author's text and the studio have مجالسُهُ following داراً. The full recording also has the masculine ending. It can refer back to the house as a place or dwelling. Al-Jawhari, quoted under دور by Ibn Manzur, explicitly records masculine agreement with دار understood as الموضع or المثوى. The word card explains the metonymy of the remembered gatherings. [Lisan al-Arab, entry دور](https://www.islamicbook.ws/adab/la/lsan-alarb-025.html)
- **7.2, 9.1 and 13.1.** The studio identifies سُوَيكِن as a diminutive of ساكن and glosses مُطرِف here as a later arrival or neighbour. Its حَبَب is morning dew, paired with حِبّ, a loved one. The imperative for the birds' call is from هدل. It is set with an initial alif of wasl. The studio's other glosses inform 4.2, 5.2, 6.2 and 11.1.
- **17 and 20.** The possessive «يوسفه» names a dearly missed person through the grief of Joseph's father. The concluding statement on what God asks of a soul recalls the Quran. The translations and longer explanations here are the editors' own. [Quran 12:84](https://quran.com/12/84) · [Quran 2:286](https://quran.com/2/286)

## Audio check

Cohere's isolated-clip verification flags eight of the forty halves, 13 words out of 217, plus one possible clip edge. The written witnesses, the whole-track transcript and independently transcribed wider clips were compared.

- **2.2 and 5.1.** The recogniser compresses «ذا لست» to فلست, where the wider Audar clip retains the initial ذ and the following «لست». It separates the end of «لَهَفي» into له في at 5.1. The full performance and the studio resolve both.
- **6.2 and 10.2.** حينان transcribes tanwin in the first «حيناً» as a letter. The two written instances are adverbs of time. At 10.2 the decoder makes two new words out of the boundary between «بمائهما» and «طيب». The reference to the two sources of water requires the dual pronoun.
- **14.1, 16.1–2 and 20.2.** The studio prints the plural سُوَيعاتٍ and the wider Audar clip hears it. The short clip gives سويعة. At 137.70–140.88 s the singular and plural score equally at −22.68, leaving the wider clip and both written witnesses to settle the form. Other differences are the written separation of «ولاكتحلت», الأحبات for the inflected الأحبةِ before في, and the misdivision الفتاة الله for الفتى تالله. The printed words and the full track support the edition.
- **3.2.** The isolated recognition prefixes «إن». The author, screen and full track start the alternative question at أَم, and the aligner places أَم before باكياً. The possible edge contributes no additional line.
