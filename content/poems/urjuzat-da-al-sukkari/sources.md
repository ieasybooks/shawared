# أُرجوزَةُ داءِ السُّكَّرِيِّ

## Recording, author and text

Alqimmah Studio released this recitation by Khalid al-Sharafi on 30 August 2024. Its credits name the author as «الدكتور حسام بن عبد الله المسعري الدوسري» and identify him as a consultant in paediatrics, paediatric endocrinology and diabetes. These are the biographical particulars used in the poet entry. The studio subsequently published his poems «أيؤسر القلب» (2025) and «سنام العمر» (2026). [Credited recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg) · [*Ayusar al-Qalb*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Dx5drB4zq0) · [*Sanam al-Umr*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4)

The recording has 70 bayts, from the invocation «بسم الرحيم رافع السماء» to the final blessing «على النبيّ سيّد الأنام». Each of the 140 halves occurs once. The edit keeps 4.20–472.85 seconds of the 475.285-second source, including the decay of the last rhyme. It trims only head and tail silence. Gain −8.9 dB, measured output −20.0 LUFS, duration 468.65 seconds.

The studio prints each full couplet on screen and supplies short Arabic glosses, including the English originals of some terms. The credited release is the primary written witness. A separate printed edition was not found. The performance transcript was checked against all 70 cards and their glosses. Frames can be reviewed at original-video times in `media/work/cnpSc1a5hfg/editor-frames/card*.jpg` and `gloss*.jpg`, with the uncropped originals in `media/research/video/cnpSc1a5hfg/`.

## Readings and form

- **8–18.** The studio spells the two teaching names «لادا» and «مودي». It splits Wolcott–Rallison across the rhyme of 16 and plays on the order to say the second surname or keep quiet. Its gloss spells out the syndrome in English. In 17 «المديد» names the Arabic metre that furnishes the comparison with rare conditions. The medical poem itself is in rajaz. The form «ظَمِي» at 18.2, “he thirsted”, lightens the final hamza of ظمئ. [LADA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=67s) · [MODY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=100s) · [Wolcott–Rallison](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=107s) · [Thirst](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=120s)
- **20–28.** «هجين» is the poet’s description of overlapping signs. The humours in «فِرى الأخلاط» at 23 are part of a portrait of a flustered doctor. The neck and armpits are the places at which he looks. Verse 27 has «بِالإِنسُلين», the studio’s compressed spelling for الإنسولين. In 28 «السِّقاما» denotes the sick people helped by the medicine. [The puzzled doctor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=152s) · [The insulin couplet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=180s)
- **31–37.** Toronto enters in 33. The poet specifies the vowel on its ra by comparison with «رُمتُ». The sum in 34 is ten plus ten plus two years into the previous century. The caption in 36 spells out *Leonard Thompson*. The verse itself calls him a boy. Verse 37 explicitly opens the normally silent dal of the boy’s first name to make it run in the metre. The Arabic reproduces that choice and the English retains his historical name. [Toronto and the date](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=219s) · [Thompson’s name](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=241s)
- **48–53, 56, 63.** The «حُنجور» on the banknote is a small vial. The captions at 5:34 and 5:41 explain «را» and «لو» through rapid and long-acting insulin. They are the poet’s mnemonic nicknames. At 56 the internet web, a spider and a snare turn on related images. At 63 the studio gives thyroid disease and protein in the urine as examples of two different follow-up concerns. [Vial and nicknames](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=326s) · [The web](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=373s) · [Follow-up gloss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg&t=419s)
- **Metre.** Paired rajaz (*muzdawaj*). Each half has three feet. With `–` for long and `u` for short, 1.1 gives `––u– | u–u– | u––`, and 35.1 `––u– | –uu– | u––`. The clipped final foot is audible in «السماء» and «المشافي». The rhyme belongs to each couplet locally, as in «السماء / الدواء»، «فضله / عدله» and «الكلاما / الأناما». There is no single rawiy for the whole poem.

## Sources for the medical and historical commentary

- **7–18.** American Diabetes Association Professional Practice Committee, *Standards of Care in Diabetes—2026*, section 2, pp. S27–S49, on classification, the autoimmune loss of beta cells at different ages, slowly progressive autoimmune diabetes in adults, monogenic diabetes and gestational diabetes. Section 15 gives the postpartum glucose test at 4–12 weeks and continued screening thereafter. For the syndrome in 16 see Cécile Julier and Marc Nicolino, “Wolcott-Rallison syndrome”, *Orphanet Journal of Rare Diseases* 5 (2010), article 29. [ADA classification](https://doi.org/10.2337/dc26-S002) · [ADA pregnancy](https://doi.org/10.2337/dc26-S015) · [Julier and Nicolino](https://doi.org/10.1186/1750-1172-5-29)
- **18–29, 58–64.** ADA section 14, pp. S297–S320, covers clinical overlap in young people, acanthosis nigricans as one indicator of insulin resistance, islet autoantibodies, urgent management of metabolic decompensation, nutrition, activity, complication screening and family participation. The explanations qualify the universal-sounding claims about childhood, visible symptoms and diet. A patient’s age or skin findings alone cannot settle the type. Insulin remains necessary in type 1. For the distinction between a pump, a tablet and an injectable medicine, see the US National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases. [ADA children and adolescents](https://doi.org/10.2337/dc26-S014) · [NIDDK, insulin and other treatments](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/insulin-medicines-treatments)
- **29–47.** Toronto General Hospital’s case records, reproduced by the University of Toronto’s Thomas Fisher Rare Book Library, document Leonard Thompson’s treatment. The studio caption and the Fisher exhibition give his age as fourteen. A later University of Toronto account, drawing on the hospital records, gives thirteen. The first injection on 11 January 1922 produced little benefit. The more highly purified extract on 23 January produced marked improvement. Thompson continued to take insulin and lived another thirteen years. Verse 45 compresses this into one apparent recovery. His mother’s embrace in 40 is a scene the poet imagines. The university’s Connaught history distinguishes the work with dogs from the bovine extract prepared for human use. [Hospital chart and exhibit](https://fisherdigitus.library.utoronto.ca/exhibits/show/insulin100/early-patients/leonard-thompson) · [University of Toronto on Thompson’s age](https://www.utoronto.ca/news/heritage-minute-showcases-life-saving-impact-u-t-s-insulin-discovery) · [The making of insulin](https://connaught.research.utoronto.ca/about/history/article3)
- **48–53.** The Bank of Canada Museum identifies the pictured vial as an early bottle dated 1923 and records the polymer $100 note’s release on 14 November 2011. The FDA history documents the use of animal pancreases and the introduction of biosynthetic human insulin. NIDDK distinguishes preparations by onset, peak and duration. These sources give the facts behind the poet’s waist-shaped vial and the family of insulin preparations. [Bank of Canada Museum](https://www.bankofcanadamuseum.ca/complete-bank-note-series/2011-2013-polymer-series/polymer-series-100-note/) · [FDA insulin history](https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/fda-history-exhibits/100-years-insulin) · [NIDDK, types of insulin](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/insulin-medicines-treatments)

## Verification

The first transcription flagged 20 of 140 clips, plus a possible word at one clip edge. Audar was run on each flag with wider margins. The whole take and the studio’s vocalised text were checked where the two short transcriptions disagreed.

- **3.2, 5.2.** Audar drops the spurious possessive in the first backend’s «غمامه». The studio has collective «غمام». In 5.2 both clip transcribers split «فذاع صيته» across the wrong boundary and give the rare «فاعا» a final ha. The complete recording preserves the opening words. The subtitle gives the sequence of verbs and the alif of rhyme.
- **9.2, 10.1, 13.2.** Audar recovers «ما نالا» where Cohere found a single «منالا», and «بعد ما لها قلى» where the short clip added a syllable. Neither short backend recognises the form «انكمى» reliably. The studio writes it at 1:08 and explains the concealed course of the disease.
- **15.2, 18.2.** The card and both Audar passes recover MODY where Cohere supplied «مدير». At 18.2 the extra margin restores «إن نالت الفتى», followed by the thirst and urination written on the card.
- **23.1, 24.1.** Cohere’s «الأخلاق» would turn the old bodily humours into morals. The card gives «يهذي» and «الأخلاط», and Audar recovers the latter. In 24 the address «لا أبا لك» is printed in three words. The joined sounds heard at the boundary carry the address between the verb and its subject «العجب».
- **29.2, 30.2.** Cohere prefaces 29.2 with an unrelated religious formula and returns no words for 30.2. The wider clips recover «لم يفدهم من سقمهم إلا الردى» and «أن ينجلي الغم» followed by the introduction to the story. These also occur in the whole take and on successive verse cards. In 30.2 the closed ending «أن ينجلي الغمّ» gives three rajaz feet. Against the more usual subjunctive «أن ينجليَ الغمّ» it scores +6.44 in the trimmed audio at 196.16–197.76 seconds. The note records the poetic licence.
- **33.2, 36.2.** «كما في رُمتُ» is a pronunciation instruction for Toronto. Audar gives the three words. Both backends struggle to divide the Arabic spelling of Leonard Thompson’s name. His Latin-script name is on screen, and 37 explains why the poet gives the dal of Leonard a vowel.
- **39.2, 45.1.** Audar restores the rhyme «كي يبعدا» where Cohere gave a final pronoun. It also separates «من الهزالِ مُهترئ», whose boundary falls in the middle of the short transcription’s «لمهتري».
- **47.1–2, 51.2.** In the first half Cohere supplied an unrelated speaker’s name, then split «كلّلهم» in the second. Audar recovers the congratulatory phrase «بسبقهم حقّ لهم» and the verb «كلّلهم». It also identifies «الكَرى», sleep, where the first pass gave «الكرم».
- **57.1, 62.1.** The forms found by the short transcribers obscure the clause crossing the verse break. The studio has «حدا الطبابةَ / علمٌ», with knowledge urging medicine onwards. In 62, «استقاما» takes «أمرُ العليل» as its delayed subject and the last alif sustains the rhyme. Audar recovers the form.
- **50.1 edge.** Cohere separates off an extra waw just before «والإنسلين». The verse card and whole performance have one opening conjunction. The next half proceeds directly to the child called «را».
