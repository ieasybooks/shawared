## Editions

- **Ibn Hisham, السيرة النبوية**, ed. al-Saqqa, al-Abyari and Shalabi: shamela 23833, vol. 2, pp. 501–515 (`media/research/banat-suad/sira.txt`). Ibn Ishaq's account of Ka'b's coming to Medina, the poem in 58 abyat, fully voweled, with the editors' glosses (drawn largely from al-Zurqani) and Ibn Hisham's variants. It gives «ما زلت أقتطع البيداء مدّرعا» in a note (p. 511 n. 3: manuscript أ has it after «لظل يرعد»); the reciter says it, which makes 59. Ibn Ishaq says Ka'b made the poem before setting out (p. 503); the recital in the mosque is Ibn Hisham's report from Ali ibn Zayd ibn Jud'an (p. 515), and Asim ibn Umar ibn Qatada's account of the Ansar's anger is on p. 514.
- **Abu Zayd al-Qurashi, جمهرة أشعار العرب**: shamela 1487, pp. 632–641 (`jamhara.txt`). 58 abyat in a somewhat different order; it lacks «ما زلت أقتطع» and bayt 30, has «يوماً تظل حداب الأرض ترفعها» in 30's place, and «أكرم بها خلةً» (for «يا ويحها خلةً»).
- **al-Sukkari, شرح ديوان كعب بن زهير** (Dar al-Kutub, Cairo): scan archive.org `hmmt00206`, leaves 32–56 (pp. 1–25). His text has most of the readings the reciter uses where the Sira differs (the table credits them), and the order يمشون → لا يفرحون (pp. 24–25). The Dar al-Kutub editor's footnotes note the Sira's «ما زلت أقتطع» (p. 21 n. 1) and the variant «منسوب» (n. 7).
- **ديوان كعب** on old shamela (book 4399): the Abu Dhabi encyclopedia's text, with gaps and corruptions (it lacks abyat 3, 19, 20 and 44, half of 41, and runs 15 into 52); not used for the text.

The text follows the Sira's order, with «ما زلت أقتطع» where the Sira's manuscript أ has it, and the readings the reciter says, most of them al-Sukkari's. `abyat_in_full` is 59: the Sira's 58 and the bayt it gives in a note.

## Commentary drawn on

- **Glosses** follow the Sira's editors (from al-Zurqani) and al-Sukkari's commentary (17.2 الميل, 21's kinship riddle, 47 ضراء, 50 ضامزة, 58's reason).
- **The story** of the poem's recital follows Ibn Ishaq in the Sira; that the Ansar took bayt 57 as a gibe is Asim ibn Umar ibn Qatada's report in the same place.
- **The mantle (burda):** neither Ibn Ishaq nor Ibn Hisham reports it; the intro gives it as al-Zirikli's statement in *al-A'lam*, which also supplies the poet's life (shamela 12286, vol. 5, p. 226).
- **The poet's nisba:** *al-A'lam* prints «المازني» in Ka'b's entry and «المزني» in his father Zuhayr's; the family is of Muzayna, so `full_name` has المزني.

- **sources.md:** al-Sukkari's text collated (most of the recited readings are his); three more departures from the Sira (36.1, 45.1, 47.2); notes credited to the right hand; page ranges.
- **Text:** 43.1 يُرعَدُ, the reading of al-Sukkari and the Jamhara, for a coin toss.
- **Intro and bio:** the order of events as Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Hisham tell them; the burda and the poem's afterlife in al-Zirikli's words; no claim that the poem is called al-Burda.
- **Arabic:** sharh 7, 17, 19, 20, 21 (the kinship riddle explained), 26, 27, 35, 38, 41, 57, 58; glosses of الميل, ضامزة, ضراء, يعاليل, عرقوب; new notes on أضحى, يركضن, يغدو, اللبان, ميل; two trivial notes dropped.
- **English:** quote marks of 37–38, sharh 4, 7, 19, 21, 35, 38, 55, 58, and three translations.
