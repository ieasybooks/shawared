## Editions and readings

- Shukri Faisal, *Abu al-Atahiya, ash‘aruhu wa-akhbaruhu* (Damascus University, 1965), poem **27**, printed p. **32**. All four abyat in the order recited. The apparatus records that bayt 1 is absent from two witnesses, gives «كما يعي» in one at 3.2 and «بما فَعَلَ» in two at 4.2. Al-Sharafi’s «بِما صَنَعَ» agrees with Faisal’s text. [Printed page, scan leaf 72](https://archive.org/details/0017pdf_202002/page/n72/mode/1up)
- Tha‘lab, *Majalis Tha‘lab*, ed. Abd al-Salam Harun, p. 53, gives five abyat beginning with our third. In place of our first it has «وَنُحتُ على الشباب بدمع عيني / ومنتحباً فما أغنى النحيب» and in the wish it has «بما فعل المشيب». It adds «تجلّاني وبيّض عارضي» at the end. None of these differences is heard on this recording. [Majalis](https://shamela.ws/book/605/53)
- Muhammad ibn Aydamur, *al-Durr al-farid*, V, p. 89, quotes the piece under Muslim ibn al-Walid, with «ألا ليت» and a different order. Abd al-Qadir al-Baghdadi explicitly gives it to Abu al-Atahiya, explains «الغضّ» and «النحيب», and discusses the impossible wish after «ليت». The attribution here follows Faisal, al-Baghdadi and the studio. [Al-Durr al-farid](https://shamela.ws/book/20762/2071) · [Sharh abyat Mughni al-labib, V, p. 163](https://shamela.ws/book/150966/1761)

The four abyat are counted against Faisal. The opening «بَكَيتُ عَلَشْ / شَبابِ بِدَمْ / عِ عَيْني» and the corresponding run in bayt 3 scan as wafir. The rhyme consonant is ب. The explanation of the dye in bayt 2 follows the juxtaposition of grey hair and the dyed head, without assigning a colour to the dye.
