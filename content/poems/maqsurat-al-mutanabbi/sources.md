## Title

The poem has no settled name. No book on shamela calls it «مقصورة المتنبي»; al-Badi'i (*al-Subh al-Munbi*, vol. 1, p. 144, shamela 21498/143) calls it «المقصورة التي أولها: ألا كل ماشية الخيزلى», and the uploads, the editions and the poetry sites name it by its opening. So the title is the first sadr. The id `maqsurat-al-mutanabbi`, set before the work began, keeps the descriptive name the reciter's upload and al-Badi'i use.

## Editions

- **al-Wahidi**, شرح ديوان المتنبي, ed. Friedrich Dieterici (Berlin, 1861): scan archive.org `carminacumcommen00muta`, leaves 196–201, printed pp. 699–704. Every bayt, voweled, with al-Wahidi's commentary. The heading: «وقال لمّا دخل الكوفة يصف طريقه من مصر اليها ويهجو كافورا في شهر ربيع الاول سنة ٣٥١». 36 abyat. Shamela 754, pp. 347–349 (`wahidi.txt`) is the same text, unvoweled and with typos («بخسمي», «والغبي», «وعادى») that the scan does not have.
- **al-Ukbari** (attributed), التبيان في شرح الديوان, ed. Mustafa al-Saqqa, Ibrahim al-Abyari and Abd al-Hafiz Shalabi (Dar al-Ma'rifa, Beirut): scan archive.org `20240104_20240104_1855`, leaves 61–69, printed vol. 1, pp. 36–44, poem 7 of the alif rhyme. Every bayt, voweled, numbered 1–36. Heading: «وقال يذكر خروجه من مصر وما لقي ويهجو الأسود». Its commentary, without the verse, is shamela 6908, vol. 1, pp. 36–44 (`ukbari.txt`).
- **al-Barquqi**, شرح ديوان المتنبي, a modern typeset reprint: scan archive.org `20201020_20201020_1638`, leaves 121–122 (text, printed pp. 122–123) and 150–156 (his notes 110–145, printed pp. 151–157). Every bayt, fully voweled. Heading: «وقال يذكر خروجه من مصر وما لقي في طريقه، ويهجو كافورًا». 36 abyat.
- **Ibn al-Iflili**, شرح معاني شعر المتنبي: shamela 37327, vol. 2, pp. 44–51 (`iflili.txt`). Every bayt with a prose paraphrase; his order is al-Wahidi's.
- **Starting text:** al-Barquqi's, put in the house style. The reciter follows it word for word.

`abyat_in_full` is 36, the count of all three editions.

| 6.1 | بِنَخلٍ | بِنَخلٍ | بِنَحلٍ (and in his note) |
| 13.1 | وَالصَّباحَ | وَالصَّباحُ | وَالصَّباحَ (and allows both) |
| 18.1 | وَبِتنا | وَبِتنا | وَثُبنا |
| 21.1 | وَما | وَما | وَلا |
| 21.2 | وَلا | وَما | وَلا |
| 22–23 | «ولا بُدّ للقلب» before «ومن يك قلبٌ» | the other way round | as al-Barquqi |

The rest is spelling: al-Wahidi writes some rhymes with alif where al-Barquqi has alif maqsura and the reverse (المشا, الأذا, غنا, القرا, الغضا, الصدا, العلى, العدى), and «الحيوة» for الحياة. All three print «فدا» in 1.2; the text writes «فِدى», the modern spelling of a word whose last root letter is ي, as the reciter's own description does. The rhymes are spelled as al-Barquqi spells them.

Ibn al-Iflili's text has «تُخبِّرنا» in 7.1 (al-Ma'arri's *Mu'jiz Ahmad* gives it as a variant, shamela 753/413), «وَمَا … وَلا» in 21, al-Wahidi's order in 22–23, and «أحمّ الرِّواق» for «أحمّ البلاد» in 15.2.

### Enjambed abyat

Five abyat run a word across the caesura: 4 (القِمار), 7 (النِّقاب), 9 (الدَّبور), 11 (الرِّداء), 17 (الرِّماح). All three editions and the reciter's own description split the word where the meter does («ضَرَبتُ بِها التّيهَ ضَربَ القِما / رِ إِمّا لِهَذا وَإِمّا لِذا»), and so does the text, so each half scans as a half. The notes on those words say where the rest of the word is.

- **1.2 «الهَيذَبى»:** the score favours «الهيدبى», but the aligner writes ذ as "th" and scores it badly, so the score says little here. The transcribers split: cohere hears «الهيذباء» in the full recording and «الهيذبان» in the clip, audar «الهيدا بارك». A spectrogram of the word (the audio is low-passed near 4 kHz, so frication doesn't show, but a stop's closure does) shows no closure or burst between «هَي» and «بى», where the reciter's د in «فِدى» (1.2) has a sharp burst and the ب of the same word a clear gap: the consonant is a continuant, ذ. The reciter's own description writes «الهَيْذَبَى». The text keeps al-Barquqi's ذ; al-Wahidi and al-Ukbari print د, and al-Ukbari says the word is said both ways. The note gives both.
- **6.1** is a coin toss by score; the transcript hears «بنخل», and Nakhl is the name al-Wahidi, al-Barquqi and Ibn al-Iflili give the water. The text keeps it.
- **17.2:** the description under the reciter's video prints «بَين مكارمنا», but he says «فوق», as every edition has it.
- **22–23:** he says «ولا بُدّ للقلب» before «ومن يك قلبٌ», al-Barquqi's and al-Ukbari's order (the transcript runs «… صم الصفا ومن يك قلب كقلبي»).

## Meter

Mutaqarib (المتقارب), each half four feet: فعولن or فعولُ three times, then فعَل in every ajuz (al-Ma'arri: «من المتقارب الثالث», *al-Lami' al-Azizi*, shamela 29591/28) and فعولن, فعولُ or فعَل in the sadr. Every half was scanned (the house scanner, with the feet set for mutaqarib). Three halves the scanner flagged scan correctly once its blind spots are allowed for: 3.1 «وَلَكِنَّهُنَّ» (lākinna, which it reads short), 4.2 «لِهَذا» (hādhā, the same) and 26.2 «مَهامِهُ مِن» (it lengthens the root ه of مَهامِه as if it were a pronoun).

The meter decides:
- 2.2 «بِيَ» (biya, as al-Wahidi and al-Ukbari vowel it): «بي» leaves the half a syllable short.
- 24.2 «قَدَرِ», as al-Wahidi and al-Barquqi vowel it: al-Ukbari's print has «قَدْرِ», which leaves the half a syllable short.
- 13.1 «صَوَرٌ»: the transcript's «صوار» is the stretched vowel; صُوار breaks the second foot.
- 7.2 «وادي» and 10.1 «رَوامي»: the ya is silent, as al-Wahidi says («سكّن الياء ضرورةً»).

  - **13.1:** the clip transcriber voweled «وَالصَّبَاحُ» and audar returned nothing; the aligner prefers «الصَّباحَ» by 4.0 (above), al-Barquqi's and al-Ukbari's vowel, and the text keeps it.
- **Edges:** 5.2 starts with «هو» and 15.2 with «ان», the last vowel and the tanwin of the half before; 25.1 ends «ليل نام», its rhyme «لَيلِنا» stretched. The timings are the aligner's.

## Commentary drawn on

- **al-Wahidi** throughout, for the meaning of each bayt and most glosses: الخيزلى and الهيذبى, النجاة, بجاوة (with Ibn Jinni's story from the poet), الخنوف, المشى, الميط, التيه as gambling, the camels' «choice» at al-Naqab (with «يشكو إليّ جملي طول السرى»), Ibn Jinni on Turban, هبّت from الهباب, روامي, Busayta, al-Jurawi (with the verse «ألا لا أرى ماء الجراوي شافيا»), صور and al-Jarmi's صوري, الشغور and «إذا وردت الشغور فقد أعرقت», الدئداء, الصوى, the dispute over 16 (Ibn Jinni, al-Qadi al-Jurjani, Ibn Furraja, whose reading al-Wahidi adopts), 17–20, 22–26, 27 (his 36); and the slaves who took a bull for a minaret and an ostrich for a palm at Busayta (pp. 698–699).
- **al-Ukbari:** التيه as the Wilderness of the Israelites between al-Qulzum and Ayla, which the poet crossed; the horses led beside the camels (5) and the pairing of white swords and brown spears; the second reading of the camels' choice (7); ها as a particle standing for its sentence (8); Hisma and the poet's praise of it (9); والصباح in the accusative or nominative (13); التوى and the «مقابلة حسنة واستعارة جيدة» of 23; الخويدم and the common use of خادم for a eunuch (25); «وثُبنا» (18).
- **al-Barquqi:** بجاوة as a land in Nubia; the second reading of 2.2 and of 6.2; the second reading of 8 (Turban far from Iraq, the camels' speed); وادي الغضى as a badal; Busayta between Kufa and Mecca; أعكش near Kufa; العواصم with Antioch as its capital; his reading of 20 (the vow to leave Egypt); «بيت القصيد» for the last bayt; الهيذبى as a horse's pace.
- **Ibn al-Iflili:** Turban «بعيد من أرض العراق» and al-Asma'i's reading of distant places made near by speed (8); الأضارع and الدنا as waters (14); الآلة as «الشدة والجرأة» (22).
- **The occasion:** al-Wahidi's heading (Kufa, Rabi' al-Awwal 351); his heading to «عيد بأية حال» for the flight from Egypt in 350 (shamela 754/343: «وقال يوم عرفة وقد خرج من مصر سنة خمسين وثلثمائة»). Al-Badi'i dates the arrival in Kufa to Rabi' al-Akhir 351; the intro gives al-Wahidi's date.
- **The intro's verdict:** Shawqi Dayf, *al-Fann wa-madhahibuhu fi al-shi'r al-'arabi*, p. 335 (shamela 8666/366, `dayf.txt`): «إذ يحشد فيها الألفاظ الغريبة حشدًا».
- **Wadi al-Qura:** Yaqut, *Mu'jam al-Buldan*, vol. 5, p. 345 (shamela 23735/2378): «واد بين المدينة والشام من أعمال المدينة كثير القرى».
