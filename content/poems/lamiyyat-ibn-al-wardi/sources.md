## Editions

- **ديوان ابن الوردي**, ed. Ahmad Fawzi al-Hayb (Dar al-Qalam, Damascus, 1986), pp. 435–439: scan archive.org `hm4154`, leaves 436–440. 76 abyat in brackets, headed «لامية شيخ الإسلام ابن الوردي رحمه الله». His note (p. 435 n. 7): manuscript م heads it «ومما ينسب إليه وقد اشتهر عند الخاصة والعامة ولكن لم يوجد في ديوانه», and the poem is an addition in ب and م, not in his base text. م is the copy of Ahmad ibn Mas'ud al-Nabulusi printed at the Jawa'ib press in 1300 AH (p. 439 n. 4). His apparatus gives the readings of ب and م; several of the reciter's readings are among them (below, "a manuscript").
- **ديوان ابن الوردي**, ed. Abd al-Hamid Hindawi (Dar al-Afaq al-Arabiyya, Cairo, 2006), pp. 277–281: scan archive.org `hm4153`, leaves 285–289. The same 76 abyat under م's heading («ومما ينسب إليه وقد اشتهر عند الخاصة والعامة ولكن لم يوجد في ديوانه»), with the Jawa'ib colophon. The text agrees with al-Hayb's except at 11.2, and its notes give some of the same variants («في رواية: أهنا», «يروى: "لين" بدلاً من "لدن"», «في نسخة: يصدر البيت بـ"مل" بدلاً من "غب"»). "The editions" below means these two.
- **al-Shirwani, نفحة اليمن فيما يزول بذكره الشجن**: shamela 35888, pp. 154–158 (`nafha.txt`). The popular text in 80 abyat, headed «لامية ابن الوردي رحمه الله». It and Nafh are the only sources here with bayt 65 («لا يضر الفضل إقلال»), and it alone has the three closing abyat of prayer (76–78); the reciter says all of them, and al-Shirwani has many of his readings where the editions differ.
- **Shakir Shuqayr al-Batluni, نفح الأزهار في منتخبات الأشعار**: shamela 28175, pp. 52–55 (`nafh.txt`). An abridged text of 44 abyat, headed «لابن الوردي», close to al-Shirwani's. It has bayt 65, and it is the only source here for several of the reciter's readings (below).

The reciter follows the popular text: al-Shirwani's 80 abyat less two («إن تبدّى تنكسف شمس الضحى» and «فاق إذ قسناه بالبدر سنا», which the editions also have, the second as «زاد إن قسناه»), with his own mixture of readings. `abyat_in_full` is 80, al-Shirwani's count; the editions have 76.

## Order

The reciter's order is al-Shirwani's, except:

- **7–9:** «واتّق الله», «واهجر الخمرة», «ليس من يقطع»; al-Shirwani has «واهجر» first, the editions «ليس» before «واهجر».
- **14–15:** «أين عاد» before «أين من سادوا», as in the editions; al-Shirwani has «أين من سادوا» first.
- **20–21:** «واهجر النوم» before «واحتفل للفقه»; both sources have «واحتفل» first.

- **29.1:** the aligner can't hear the long vowel of «جازتني», which the transcript writes; the meter takes «جَزَتني», the reading of م and al-Shirwani.
- **35.2 «أَذَل»:** the aligner prefers the editions' «أَزَل» by 5.2, but it compares ز with ذ written as "th", which it can't hear fairly, and both transcribers hear ذ («اذال», «أذى»). The text keeps «أَذَل», al-Shirwani's.
- **57.2 «وَمَثَل»:** all three transcripts hear «أو مثل», but that is how the tanwin of «لَوَعظاً» sounds when it merges into the و («waʿẓaw wa-mathal»); the aligner prefers «وَمَثَل», which both sources have.
- **9.2 «يَتَّقي»:** al-Shirwani writes «يتق», as it is said before the alif of wasl, but مَن is relative, so the verb is indicative; Nafh writes «يتقي».
- **69.1 «عَدِّ»**, the imperative of عَدّى (turn aside), as Hindawi prints it; «عُدَّ» was a slip in the first draft.
- **Meter decides:** 17.1 and 75.2 «مِنهُمُ» take the long mim; 31.1 «تُغنِ», short before «عَنهُ», and the word order the reciter uses needs it short; 39.1 the second «تُفِد» is cut short for the foot, as in ب and al-Shirwani; 43.2 «بَكرِ اِتَّصَل» drops the tanwin before the alif of wasl, since with it the last foot has a syllable too many, and both transcripts hear «بكر اتصل».

## Commentary drawn on

- **No classical commentary was at hand** (al-Zirikli names a manuscript one, al-Ghamri's «العرف الندي»), so the sharh rests on the text, the editions' notes and the lexicons.
- **al-Hayb's notes:** Nimrod and Canaan (13), Ad (14), the hadith «زر غبًّا تزدد حبًّا» (63; al-Sakhawi, *al-Maqasid al-Hasana*, p. 376), al-Ma'arri's line set beside 58, Thu'al and Imru' al-Qays (69), and the Quranic sources of 32 (43:32, 17:105).
- **The proverb in 14:** «من يسمع يخل», al-Maydani, *Majma' al-Amthal*, vol. 2, p. 300, no. 4012.
- **Quran and hadith:** 1.2 «قول فصل» (86:13), 11.2 (14:12), 47 (25:67); 21 «من يرد الله به خيرًا يفقّهه في الدين» (al-Bukhari 71); 48 «لا تسبّوا أصحابي» (al-Bukhari 3673); 56 «ما من أمير عشرةٍ إلا وهو يُؤتى به يوم القيامة مغلولًا حتى يفكّه العدل أو يوبقه الجور» (al-Suyuti, *Jam' al-Jawami'*, vol. 7, p. 768, no. 19251, from Abu Hurayra; shamela 127677). 44 «قيمة كلّ امرئ ما يحسنه» is among Ali's sayings in Ibn Hamdun, *al-Tadhkira al-Hamduniyya*, vol. 1, p. 247 (shamela 10646/237), whose editor cites *Nahj al-Balagha* and al-Jahiz's *al-Bayan*. 66–67 echo lines attributed to al-Shafi'i («سافر تجد عوضًا عمّن تفارقه», «إني رأيت وقوف الماء يفسده»); Ahmad Qabash, *Majma' al-Hikam wa-l-Amthal*, vol. 8, p. 73 (shamela 12930/3573), gives them to al-Buhturi, al-Shafi'i or Umara al-Yamani.
- **The title «وصيّة ابن الوردي»:** a manuscript in the Austrian National Library (Flügel 4571), in Muhammad Ayesh's catalogue (shamela 133381, p. 223); the catalogue lists a second copy on p. 419.
- **The poet's life:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 5, p. 67 (shamela 12286/4160, `alam.txt`), which also says the Lamiyya was not in his diwan and was added to its printed edition; his death in the plague of 749, soon after his maqama on it, from al-Malati, *Nayl al-Amal*, vol. 1, p. 178 (shamela 17097/170, `malati.txt`).

## Review

A second reader checked the text against the editions, al-Shirwani and Nafh, scanned every half, and checked the sharh, notes, intro and bio against the sources (2026-11-17). Fixed after the review:

- **Text:** 43.2 «بَكرِ اِتَّصَل» without the tanwin, for the meter; 9.2 «يَتَّقي» spelled as an indicative; the alif of wasl voweled in ten words (18.1 «اِسمَع» to 72.2 «اِنفَتَل»).
- **Credits:** al-Shirwani's «الغواني» (1.1), Hindawi's «سُبلَنا» (11.2), ب's «اجتراء» (31.2), Hindawi's heading and the wording of his notes; Nafh added as a source, with bayt 65 and eight of the reciter's readings; rows added for 10.2, 15.2, 25.1, 45.1 and 78.1, and 14–15 added to the order.
- **Sharh:** the hadith in 56 quoted from al-Suyuti; 15 (the dots of س and ش), 19 (the first counsel after the address), 38 (ردّ العجز على الصدر), 57 (the damma and kasra of the manqus) and 70 corrected; 54 and 60 hedged, since the poem may not be his; «بُنيّ» in 18 and the intro read as the address of counsel, not his own son; 2, 6, 14, 39, 48 and 58 deepened.
- **Notes:** 9.1, 1.2.2, 14.2, 40.2.6 and 63.1.3 corrected; 9.2.3 and 73.2.4 added; al-Shirwani's and Nafh's agreement given where the notes left it out; heads for the rhyme verbs.
- **English:** 5.1.1, 13.1.2, 14.1.2 and 26.1.2 romanized to the guide; 20, 26, 47, 49, 64 and 75 reworded; the exclamation marks dropped; the intro cut to five sentences.
- **Poet:** the full name as al-A'lam gives it.
