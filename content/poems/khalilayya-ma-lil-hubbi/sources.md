# خليلي ما للحب يزداد جدة

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari recites the ten bayts of Khalil Mardam’s poem 16, from the two friends to the promise that the caliph will restore the prisoner’s fortunes. His master retains 3.68–98.76 s of the original 99.115 s, giving 95.08 s. The opening is audible from about 4.21 s and the last vowel dies away at about 98.30 s. The continuous Cohere and Audar transcriptions follow all twenty halves once in this order. There is no intervening speech or omitted bayt. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FTRZsLHmJao)

The metre is tawil. The first sadr divides after the syllables of «خَليلَيْ | يَ ما لِلحُبْ | بِ يَزْدا | دُ جِدَّةً», giving فعولن مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. In «إذا سلمت نفس الحبيب تشابهت» both فعولن feet are shortened to فعولُ. The rhyme consonant is dal, audible before the attached ha in جديدها، عهودها، شديدها and يعيدها. The long vowel after ha carries the rhyme out.

## Text and commentary

- Ali ibn al-Jahm, *Diwan*, ed. Khalil Mardam Bek, no. 16, printed pp. 50–51. The note at the foot of p. 50 assigns the poem to his imprisonment. All four opening bayts appear there. P. 51 gives Layla’s answer, the interlacing necks, the anklet and the appeal to the caliph. In 4.2 Mardam corrects his exemplar’s «يحرش» to «يحرّج». His notes at 7–9 record «بالقلب يذكو», «خطوب» and «لمّا». These notes identify the sources of readings the later poet-reader might otherwise mistake for errors in transcription. [P. 50](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n49/mode/1up) · [P. 51](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n50/mode/1up)
- Ibn Aydamur al-Musta'simi, *al-Durr al-farid wa-bayt al-qasid*, ed. Kamil Salman al-Juburi, II, pp. 450–451, item 1440. The compiler selects 8 as his lemma, then supplies the preceding verses and the fetter verse. It is an independent anthology witness to 1–9. Its wording includes «والأشياء», «ألمّت بنا والليل», «وللحبس», «وفاضت دموعها» and «بخدّي». Its 7.2 places the fire in the heart, and 9.1 calls the shackles «قيودنا». The anthology does not supply the last address to the caliph. The edition’s sometimes corrupt digital lettering has not been treated as evidence of the reciter slipping. [II, p. 450](https://shamela.ws/book/20762/943) · [II, p. 451](https://shamela.ws/book/20762/944)
- Al-Tha'alibi, *al-Muntahal*, p. 266, quotes our 8–9 under «وقال آخر» with «خطوب» and an address in the masculine, «فلا تجزعن». Shawqi Dayf, *Tarikh al-adab al-arabi*, IV, p. 268, quotes the feminine address and the caliph’s prisoner in his discussion of Ali’s trials under al-Mutawakkil. The introductions follow Mardam’s assignment to prison. Layla’s direct speech is the poem’s narration of her visit. [Al-Tha'alibi](https://shamela.ws/book/21105/256) · [Shawqi Dayf](https://shamela.ws/book/11996/1740)

On the aligned audio, «والأيام» scores −19.53 against «والأشياء» −41.97. At 4.2 «يحرّج» scores −17.22 against «يحرّش» −27.64, and the free phone sequence on the original ends in j. For 6.1 «وجالت» leads «وفاضت» by 15.88. For 7.2 «بالشوق» leads «بالقلب» by 19.72. At 8 both full transcriptions have «صروف», ahead of «خطوب» by 17.19. At 9 «إمّا» leads «لمّا» by only 2.06, but both full passes and Mardam agree. «قيوده» scores −11.21, «قيودها» −14.29 and «قيودنا» −22.75. The switch to referring to himself as “the beloved” in 8 explains the third-person pronoun in 9. The final sharpened edge of 9.1 removes the m that the initial narrow pass had added after it.

At 2.1 the score favours the anthology’s «العهود» over «لعهود» by only 0.59. Both full passes and Mardam have the preposition that frames the question. At 3 the paired words «وجنح الليل» lead the anthology’s «بنا والليل» by 17.55, and «وللسجن» leads «وللحبس» by 18.82. At 4 the anthology’s electronic «إني» scores −20.00, well below both «أنّى» and the clipped «أنا». At 5 the full passes preserve «وشرّ», missing from the electronic anthology, and at 6 both begin «وأعلنت», ahead of the truncated «وأعلت» by 4.84. Both continue «على الخد» where the anthology makes the cheek the speaker’s own. These are places where the extent and wording can be recovered by comparing the whole sentence.
