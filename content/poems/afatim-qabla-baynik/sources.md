# أَفاطِمَ قَبلَ بَينِكِ مَتِّعيني

## Recording and extent

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed on موقع الشنكبوتية, uploaded 18 October 2019. The original is 374.387 s. The edit keeps 43.30–366.28 s, giving 322.98 s. It removes the introductory genealogy and the outro. The first voiced group of the poem begins at 43.82 s and the last ends at 365.72 s. The studio displays explanatory cards throughout. [Original recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EHid_p0UqAc)

There are 44 performed bayts, each once. In Ahmad Muhammad Shakir and Abd al-Salam Harun’s *al-Mufaddaliyyat*, no. 76, pp. 288–292, the poem has 45. The recording takes 1–12 and 14–45, omitting 13 «أرين محاسناً وكنن أخرى / من الأجياد والبشر المصون». Thus the first line after the omission is «ومن ذهب يلوح على تريب». The only editorial renumbering occurs there. [Opening](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/262) · [The omitted verse](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/263) · [Close](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/266)

## Witnesses and commentary

- Charles Lyall’s edition of al-Anbari’s recension, *The Mufaddaliyat*, no. LXXVI, English volume II, pp. 228–232, numbers the performed sequence through 44 and brackets the disputed line as 11a. The studio cites the Arabic commentary at pp. 574–588. Lyall describes the route of the litters, the grain used to build up the camel’s hump, the foregirth and saddle girth working in opposite directions, and the change from the camel’s gait to the movement of a ship. At 40 the tradition identifies the addressee as Amr ibn Hind. Al-Asma'i doubted a king would be addressed in the intimate terms that follow. The identification remains open in both introductions. [Lyall, English volume](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatanth00mufauoft/page/228/mode/2up)
- Hasan Kamil al-Sayrafi, *Diwan shi'r al-Muthaqqib al-Abdi*, no. V, pp. 124–215. The editorial discussion on pp. 124–129 names the recensions of al-Anbari, al-Marzuqi, al-Tibrizi and al-Yazidi. The index on p. 287 counts **47**. The recording takes diwan 1–5, 7–12, 15, 14 and 16–46 in that order. Recording 40, the address to Amr, is diwan 42 on p. 208. On pp. 178–179 the printer numbers two consecutive bayts ٢٨. The first, «تصكّ الجانبين», occupies position 27 and corresponds to recording 25. The note on p. 127 also documents the transfer of verses between poems with this metre and rhyme, including Suhaym ibn Wathil’s «أنا ابن جلا». [Critical diwan](https://archive.org/details/3397pdf_202001)
- Three diwan verses are absent from the recording. Diwan 6, «تبصّر هل ترى ظعناً عجالاً / بجنب الصحصحان إلى الوجين», is on p. 143. Al-Sayrafi explains that al-Anbari cites it from Abu Ubayda as another version of the preceding verse. Diwan 13, «أرين محاسناً وكنن أخرى / من الديباج والبشر المصون», is on p. 158. Shakir and Harun’s corresponding line has «من الأجياد». Diwan 47, «دعي ماذا علمت سأتّقيه / ولكن بالمغيّب نبّئيني», closes the text on p. 213. [Diwan 6](https://archive.org/details/3397pdf_202001/page/n187/mode/1up) · [Diwan 13](https://archive.org/details/3397pdf_202001/page/n202/mode/1up) · [Diwan 47](https://archive.org/details/3397pdf_202001/page/n257/mode/1up)
- Al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, III, p. 239, gives al-A'idh ibn Mihsan’s affiliation to Abd al-Qays in Bahrayn, the association with al-Hira, the explanation of his sobriquet and a death date around 35 before the Hijra (about 588 CE). That estimate is given as prose in the public profile. [Biographical entry](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/2635)

The women pierce the fabric to see out. Later the observer himself looks closely enough to recognise ropes, hooves and knee pads by their marks, before hearing a voice from the camel. The last two verses reverse the pursuit. The traveller seeks the good and the evil seeks him. The research folder contains the anthology pages, the diwan scan, Lyall’s pages and the 44 numbered uploader cards. The collation compares all 44 performed bayts with the 45 of Shakir–Harun and all 47 verse texts transcribed from al-Sayrafi’s printed pages.

## Metre and rhyme

Wafir. The rhyme consonant is nun. The first-person suffix in «يميني» or «ديني» sounds the same long release as the rhyme vowel after the nun of «الوضينِ». Each group below is one foot, with a dash for a long syllable and u for a short one.

| Recorded bayt | Sadr | Ajuz |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 «أفاطم قبل بينك … ومنعك ما سألت» | `u-uu- / u-uu- / u--` | `u-uu- / u-uu- / u--` |
| 22 «إذا قلقت أشدّ … أمام الزور» | `u-uu- / u-uu- / u--` | `u--- / u-uu- / u--` |

The opening «أَفاطِمَ قَبْـ» fills one مفاعلتن across a word boundary. In «أمامَ الزَّوْـ» the closed syllables give the contracted form with عصب.
