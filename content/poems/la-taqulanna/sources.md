# لا تَقولَنَّ إِذا ما لَم تُرِد

## Recording and extent

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed, published by Mawqi' al-Shankabutiya on 15 November 2019. Of 190.531 seconds, the master keeps 56.18–182.00 (125.82 s). The biographical introduction and announcement precede the keep. The voice begins with «لا تقولن» and ends at «إذ العرض سلم», before the closing material. There are 32 performed halves, each once. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nFpp4MEFX9w)

Cohere transcribed a website credit, «موسوعة النابلسي للعلوم الإسلامية», at the end of the full recording. The studio card, the other full-take transcription and both transcriptions of the twelve-second closing clip give «تلف المال إذ العرض سلم». Word alignment places «سلم» at 180.52–181.12 s on the source clock. The sound falls into the closing silence at 181.35 s.

## Witnesses and readings

- Al-Mufaddal al-Dabbi, *al-Mufaddaliyyat*, ed. Ahmad Muhammad Shakir and Abd al-Salam Harun, no. 77, pp. 293–295. The denominator of 18 is this edition’s numbered text. Recording 1–6 = print 1–6, recording 7–16 = print 8–17. The two absent verses begin «أنا بيتي من معد في الذرى» and «أجعل المال لعرضي جنة». Their first-person boasts would fit the themes, but neither is performed. [Opening](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/267) · [Middle](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/268) · [Close](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/269)
- Al-Anbari’s Arabic commentary, ed. Charles J. Lyall, *The Mufaddaliyat*, I, pp. 588–593, and Lyall’s introduction and notes, II, no. LXXVII, pp. 233–234. The Arabic scholia preserve an alternative opening at «حسن قول نعم». Lyall includes both, numbered 1 and 1b, and transmits all sixteen lines sung. He reports al-Marzuqi’s account that al-Mufaddal assigned the counsels to al-Hajhaj al-Abdi and the praise beginning «إنما جاد بشأس خالد» to al-Muthaqqib. Al-Asma'i transmitted the joined text under al-Muthaqqib. That attribution history explains the break in subject at displayed 12. [Arabic commentary](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatant01mufauoft/page/n346/mode/1up) · [Lyall’s notes](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatanth00mufauoft/page/n270/mode/1up)
- *Diwan shi'r al-Muthaqqib al-Abdi*, ed. Hasan Kamil al-Sayrafi, no. 6, pp. 216–233. The editor counts 24 partly reconstructed verses. Its first six begin with a night visit and a proverb. Its 7–11 praise Khalid, and the promise that opens the recording appears at its 12. Brackets distinguish supplemented lines. P. 227 reports both incipits, and p. 226 notes the praise section ends there in one transmission. The comparison files in `media/research/la-taqulanna/` retain the two arrangements separately. [Edited poem](https://archive.org/details/3397pdf_202001/page/n260/mode/1up) · [Apparatus on the incipit](https://archive.org/details/3397pdf_202001/page/n271/mode/1up)

Al-Sayrafi’s 7–11 have «إحدى العظم»، «الزول»، «باكر الجفنة»، «يجعل المال»، «بذل المال» and «عطب المال إذا». The anthology and studio have «إحدى الظلم»، «الشخص»، «مترع الجفنة»، «يجعل الهنء»، «بعض المال» and «تلف المال إذ». His 15 reads «بنجاح الوعد» beside the recorded «القول», and his 21 has «عنه أذناي» beside «أذني عنه». In 5.1 the old text opens the m of «واعلمَ» before «أنّ». On the card at 87–90 s the imperative and the following particle are separate. In connected recitation نقل accounts for the open m and suppression of the following glottal onset. The spelling preserves the particle for reading.

The jar in 6 has claims under protection as well as simple proximity. In 7 the food eaten in company is the flesh of the people absented from that company. In 12 Khalid of Lukayz has obtained the release of the speaker’s sister’s son, Sha’s al-Mumazzaq. In 14 his bowl and his meeting demonstrate how he feeds guests and restrains quarrels. At 16 the studio card expressly gives the nominative «طيّبُ النفس» as a statement of character and the accusative «طيّبَ النفس» as a circumstantial qualification. Shakir–Harun prints the latter. The edition sets the former.

In seven of the eight acoustic comparisons the gap exceeds nine points in favour of the anthology wording, including the order of «أذني عنه». At 16.1 the nominative leads the accusative by 4.29. The studio’s explicit distinction between the two cases helps resolve that smaller difference.

## Metre and rhyme

Ramal, with mim closed at the performed pause. In 1, «لا تقولنّ إذا ما لم ترد» gives **– u – – | u u – – | – u –** and «أن تتمّ الوعد في شيء نعم» **– u – – | – u – – | – u –**. In 8, «إنّ شرّ الناس من يكشر لي» and «حين يلقاني وإن غبت شتم» both give **– u – – | – u – – | u u –**. The short pair replacing the first long syllable of a foot is khabn. Inflected «ذمٌّ» in 4 and jussive «يُذَمَّ» in 5 retain the doubled consonant at the pause.

## Clip verification

The final verification on the master flagged 8 of 32 performed halves. The narrow transcriptions differed on 10 of 159 words (6.3%). The wider transcriptions are independent Audar decodes. The decisions compare these clips with the full-source transcriptions, the numbered studio cards and the literary witnesses.

| Half | Narrow clip | Wider clip | Editorial decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 4.2 | بنجاح القول ان الخلف ذنب | ما بنجاح القول إن الخلف ذم | The extra final b turns “blame” into “sin”. The wide clip returns ذم, printed in both traditions and echoed twice in the next verse. |
| 5.2 | ومتي لا يتقي الذنب يذنب | ومتى لا يتق ذم يذم أكرمه | The long clip restores the two related forms ذمّ and يُذمّ. The first is the thing to guard against and the second what happens to the man. |
| 8.2 | حين يلقاني وان غبت شتمت | في حين يلقاني وإن غبت شتم | The wider clip recovers شتم. The same man grins to the speaker’s face and insults him in his absence, hence the third person. |
| 9.2 | خذني عنه وما بي من صمم | وقد أتئذني عنه وما بي من صمم | At the join وَقِرَتْ / أذني the independent clip draws part of the first verb into the noun. Both anthology and card put the speaker’s singular ear here. The diwan’s «عنه أذناي» has a different word order. |
| 11.2 | عندي الخنا ابقي وان كان ظلم | عند الخنا أبقى وإن كان ظلم | The preposition عن ends 11.1, so its voiced tail plus ذي produces عندي or عند in the two clip outputs. The two cards show «والإعراض عن / ذي الخنا», a single construction across the break. |
| 12.2 | بعدما حاقت به احدي الظلم | بعدما حاقت به إحدى الظلم | بعد ما and بعدما are two written divisions of the same sounds. The cards and this text separate them. The wider clip also confirms الظلم. |
| 13.1 | من منا يا يتخاسين به | من منايا يتخاسين به | منايا is one plural, governed by من. The wide clip restores it without a spurious vocative يا. The Fates compete in the following half. |
| 14.1 | مترع الجفنه ربعي الندم | مترع الجفنة ربعي النداح | The rare rib'i expression goes with الندى, liberality figured as early rain. Both recognised alternatives alter its last sound. Al-Sayrafi preserves the same epithet and the card reads الندى. |
