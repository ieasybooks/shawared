# أُمَّةُ الأَرانِبِ وَالفيلُ

## Recording and extent

Alqimmah Studio published «حكايات أحمد شوقي» on 3 March 2023, crediting Osama Al-Waaedh with recitation and editing. The source lasts 966.844 seconds. Chapter 4 keeps **335.51–488.27 seconds of the original compilation** in one continuous cut. The source voice measurements place the first and last pitched sound of this chapter at approximately 336.04 and 486.96 seconds. The cut admits the entry and the decay of the final rhyme. [Compilation, chapter 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r6VpT0Lid0E&t=336s)

The assembly, all three proposals and the final refusal of first place are delivered complete, 24 couplets and 48 halves. The narrator’s final «يا معشر الأرانب» quotes the old rabbit’s own address at 5.1, and occupies a new position in the poem. Neither whole couplet is performed twice. The numbered changes of speaker on the studio cards are visual. The 483-second card places the quotation inside the third rabbit’s speech.

## Printed text and interpretation

- Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, part IV, «الحكايات», Dar al-Awda, Beirut, 1988. The references follow the pagination of part IV. [p. 142](https://archive.org/details/BBib-Alex-02915/page/n337/mode/1up) · [p. 143](https://archive.org/details/BBib-Alex-02915/page/n338/mode/1up)
- Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, «الحكايات», Hindawi Foundation for Education and Culture, Cairo, deposit 19938/2012, ISBN 978-977-719-166-1. The page images and the electronic chapter were compared. [p. 880](https://archive.org/details/alshawkeyat/page/n880/mode/1up) · [Electronic text](https://www.safahat.org/books/70706142/143/)

The first edition of *al-Shawqiyyat* (part I, Cairo, Matbaʿat al-Adab wa-l-Muʾayyad, 1898) provides an earlier text. The first printing places «فالاتحاد قوة الضعاف» in the call of the rabbit who convenes the assembly, before the selection of the three speakers. The third devises the pit but leaves first place to the rabbit who called the others together. The closing lines on p. 173 name that first voice by its cry, «يا معشر الأرانب». [p. 172](https://books.google.com/books?id=QIyl-Nj9R6UC&pg=RA1-PA172) · [p. 173](https://books.google.com/books?id=QIyl-Nj9R6UC&pg=RA1-PA173)

The Beirut printing puts the town meeting and the call to dig on p. 142, then finishes the deliberation and the offer of a throne on p. 143. Hindawi has all of it on p. 880. The two texts agree in wording. The notes distinguish the three proposed prices of survival, the homeland, two rabbits, and work shared by everyone. The crown is offered to the third speaker. His words give precedence to the rabbit who made the assembly possible. «أبو الأهوال» is the elephant here, a usage confirmed by Shawqi’s address to the elephant in the neighbouring fable «القرد والفيل». [Neighbouring fable, Hindawi p. 878](https://archive.org/details/alshawkeyat/page/n878/mode/1up)

The two printings transmit the same diwan tradition. The page reproductions and the word-by-word output from `scripts/collate.py` are in `media/research/ummat-al-aranib-wal-fil/`. The studio captions are performance evidence. The glosses and bilingual commentary are editorial close readings of the cited text.

## Metre and rhyme

Three rajaz feet in each half, in the paired-rhyme form. «يحكون أن | ن أمّة الـ | أرانب» scans *yaḥ-kū-na-an | na-um-ma-tal | a-rā-ni-bī*, **– – ⏑ – | ⏑ – ⏑ – | ⏑ – ⏑ –**. The answer *qad-a-kha-dhat | mi-nath-tha-rā | bi-jā-ni-bī* is **– ⏑ ⏑ – | ⏑ – ⏑ – | ⏑ – ⏑ –**. In 15, *fa-qī-la-lā | yā-ṣā-ḥi-bas | su-muw-wī* scans **⏑ – ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | ⏑ – –**. *Lā-yud-fa-ʿul | ʿa-duw-wu-bil | ʿa-duw-wī* answers **– – ⏑ – | ⏑ – ⏑ – | ⏑ – –**. Both halves contract their final foot. The pairs use different rawis, including b in الأرانب/بجانب, m in الكريم/الحريم and q in طريقا/تمزيقا.

## Readings against the performance

At 7 the spelling of رأيه and راية preserves a near-homophone across the break. At 14.1 the long transcriptions write «فلندعوه» while the 417-second card and both prints have «فلندعه». The governing lām requires the jussive of دعا, so its final wāw drops. The spelling with wāw leads by 2.88, a difference small enough to allow a lengthened vowel before the pronoun in the chant. At 23.2 the chapter transcription writes «إنما حليل المحل» across the joints of «إنّ محلّي للمحلّ». The same-word-count comparison is inconclusive, with a gap of 0.94. Both pages and the independent full Audar take have the emphatic lām before «المحلّ». He assigns himself second place in a ranking, which is the point of his refusal.

| 8.2 | 383.33–386.31 | «لا هرما ضعوا ولا حداثة» / «لا هرما ضعا ولا حداثا» | The 384-second card and both prints have «لا هرماً راعوا ولا حداثة». The candidate «ضعوا» leads by only 2.58.
