# Al-Akhtal's reply to Jarir

## What is heard and what is displayed

Osama al-Nahari recites the poem continuously. The source calls the video «كذبتك عينك … + الشرح». The second panel on the screen gives written glosses. For example, as he recites 34 it explains الجدّ and الحباب, and at 47 it explains the stranger at the water and the taunt «ابن المراغة». Neither gloss appears in the spoken sequence. Both complete transcriptions move straight from one bayt to the next. The last voice ends at «بلالا». The opening silence lasts to about 5.24 seconds and the closing silence begins near 415.42. The original-clock interval 4.73–416.03 retains 47 bayts in 94 consecutive halves. ## Witnesses and extent

- Al-Sukkari, *Shiʿr al-Akhtal*, transmitted from Muhammad ibn Habib, ed. Fakhr al-Din Qabawa, no. 10, pp. 84–92. Its numbered verses 1–47 match the entire performed sequence and determine the catalogue count. On p. 85 the promises follow the covenant without a gap, and the editor places a different additional verse about being called “my brother” in the footnote. Al-Sukkari explains al-Saffah withholding water on p. 86, the horse led in reserve on p. 87, the two branches of Wa'il on p. 90, Khazaz and the horses called “mountains” on p. 91, and the watering-place comparison on p. 92. - *Diwan al-Akhtal*, ed. and glossed by Mahdi Muhammad Nasir al-Din, Dar al-Kutub al-Ilmiyya, Beirut, pp. 245–251, under «اِنعق بضأنك يا جرير». The print has 48 bayts. Between our 7 and 8 it gives «إن الغواني إن رأينك طاوياً / برد الشباب طوين عنك وصالا». The voice goes directly from «عنْك مِذالا» to «وإذا وعدنك», so the additional bayt has no address here. Printed pp. 245–246 give the encounter and the winter guests, pp. 247–249 the horses, people and places, and pp. 250–251 the balance, the water and the donkeys. We use the printed explanations of these images in both languages. ## Performance readings

At 3.1 and 4.2 the recording has «لتروعنا جنية» and «الغواة», with the diwan. At 11.1 the voice gives «أم مخلد» where Nasir al-Din and Ibn Maymun have «أم محلم». Al-Sukkari also prints «أم محلم». Both full transcripts independently give the final dal. At about 93 seconds the verse panel itself gives «أم مخلد», while the adjacent glossary labels the woman «أم محلم». The paired note gives both names. At 15.2 the recording has «قتلا الملوك», at 22.2 «حالاً», at 31.2 «فرسانه», and at 43.1 «أن تسامي دارماً». Ibn Maymun reads «خلعا», «آلاً», «فتيانه» and «أن تكون كدارم» at those points. At 29.1 both complete decodes give «ما إن تركن», as in Nasir al-Din. Al-Sukkari’s recension opens the half with «وما تركن» and Ibn Maymun with «ولما تركن». The filmed text and the diwan show «وأزلن جدّ بني الحباب فزالا» at 34.2 and «قذف الغريبة ما يذقن بلالا» at 47.2. In both, the meaning in the right-hand panel belongs to the video presentation.

The following scores use equally long strings on the same aligned word or phrase, with 0.30 seconds of surrounding audio. The first figure is the reading adopted. At 3.1 لتروعنا جنية / لتردّعنّا خفية scores −34.91 / −71.91. The second candidate preserves the join in Ibn Maymun’s electronic text. At 4.2 الغواة / الرجال scores −25.54 / −39.72. At 9.2 خبالا / عذابا scores −9.79 / −18.67. At 11.1 مخلّد / محلّم scores −12.83 / −30.16. At 15.2 قتلا / خلعا scores −4.71 / −25.15. At 31.2 فرسانه / فتيانه scores −15.00 / −31.61. At 34.2 بني الحباب / ابن الحباب scores −44.20 / −52.26, and at 35.1 عرّينها / عرنينها scores −18.00 / −24.94. At 43.1 تسامي دارماً / تكون كدارم scores −9.88 / −43.21. At 47.2 قذف الغريبة / مرمى البعيدة scores −33.41 / −65.96.

At **22.2** the isolated word favours آلا (−3.72) over حالا (−6.38) by 2.66. Both complete decodes give an initial h sound, both printed diwans give حالاً, and this half passed the final clip check. The edition retains حالاً. The small acoustic difference is recorded because Ibn Maymun actually has آلاً.

## Verification of the released half boundaries

The final Cohere report compares 94 clips and marks 23, with 37 differences among 428 words. Each marked half was decoded a second time by Audar with 0.35 seconds added at each edge, within the master. The table quotes the part of each decoder's output that bears on the reading. Arabic spelling in the two middle columns is the recognisers' own.
