## Editions

- **ديوان البهاء زهير**, ed. محمد أبو الفضل إبراهيم and محمد طاهر الجبلاوي (Dar al-Ma'arif): scan archive.org `3303pdf_202001`, leaves 223–225, printed pp. 224–226. Headed by the editors «قهر الزمان», then «وقال يمدح الملك الناصر صلاح الدين يوسف بن الملك العزيز محمد بن الملك الظاهر غازي بن الملك الناصر صلاح الدين بن أيوب سنة ٦٤٢ هـ، «من ثاني الكامل قافية المتدارك»». 40 abyat, partly voweled, with glosses and variants from their sources (p. 12: the Dar al-Kutub manuscript ٥٥٧ أدب of 1286 AH, ا; the Alexandria manuscript of 996 AH, ل; the Cairo lithograph of 1277 AH, ح; Palmer's edition; a Beirut edition of 1964).
- **ديوان بهاء الدين زهير** (Dar Sadir, Beirut): scan archive.org `3439pdf_202001`, leaves 289–291, printed pp. 290–292. Titled «عرف الحبيب مكانه», with the same heading and date. 40 abyat, fully voweled, with short glosses and variants from two copies, أ and ب. The reciter follows this text where the two editions differ (13.1, 17.2, 33.1).
- **E. H. Palmer's edition of the Arabic text** (1876): scan archive.org `3440pdf12921876`, leaves 117–119, printed pp. 215–217. The same heading and date; 40 abyat, voweled.
- **The old shamela's diwan** (book 4508, the Abu Dhabi encyclopedia's text), pp. 432–435 (`old-shamela-4508.txt`): 40 abyat, partly voweled.
- **عبد الرحمن بن درهم, نزهة الأبصار بطرائف الأخبار والأشعار**: shamela 5375, p. 151 (`nuzhat.txt`). The whole poem, headed «وقال يمدح الملك الناصر صلاح الدين بن يوسف بن محمد الأيوبي سنة ٦٤٦», with many small differences.
- **ابن حجة الحموي, خزانة الأدب**: shamela 8675, vol. 1, p. 341 (`khizana.txt`), in the chapter on حسن التخلص (from p. 329): abyat 1, 2 and 12–16.

- **Starting text:** Dar Sadir's voweled text, put in the house style and checked against Dar al-Ma'arif's page images.

**Vowels and spelling:**
- 9.2 «وَلَوَ اَنَّني» and 32.1 «وَلَوَ اَنَّها»: the hamza is dropped and its fatha moved to the waw, as the old shamela writes «ولوَ انّها»; with the hamza the first foot is u – – u – and the half doesn't scan. Each has an Arabic note.
- 18.1 «يوسُفٌ» with tanwin, as all three editions print it: a called name takes damma without tanwin (يا يوسفُ), and tanwin there is a licence of verse. The meter allows either, since the sadr's last vowel can be lengthened; the aligner can't hear tanwin, so the text keeps the editions'. An Arabic note explains it.
- 27.1 «مُسبَلاً»: both editions put fatha on the ب; the lexicons also allow مُسبِل (أسبل المطر).
- 36.1 «يا مَن» (Dar Sadir, Palmer, old shamela, Nuzhat): Dar al-Ma'arif prints «ويا من», which breaks the meter (u – – u –); the transcript hears «يا من».
- 36.2 «يَتَأَوَّلا»: the draft had Dar al-Ma'arif's passive «يُتَأَوَّلا», the easier grammar, but the aligner hears the fatha (−5.7 for the passive) and Dar Sadir prints it; تأوّل is read as the mutawi' of أوّل (it admits interpretation). A note gives the passive.
- 38.2 «أَلّا» (Dar al-Ma'arif) for «أن لا» (Dar Sadir, Palmer): the same sound.
- 11.2 «وَسَطِ … وَسَطِ» as both editions vowel them; 22.2 «العُلا» with alif (Dar al-Ma'arif; root ع ل و) where Dar Sadir has «العُلى»; 19.1 «اِلتَفَتُّ» with the vowel of the alif of wasl.
- **Meter:** every half scans as kamil (mutafa'ilun or mustaf'ilun in each foot), with the pronoun's long vowel in «عَنهُ» (6.2, 23.1) and «مِنهُ» (17.2, 26.2).

## Commentary drawn on

- **Glosses** follow Dar al-Ma'arif's notes (الهيف; الغزالة, the sun; المسلسل, المسند and المرسل as hadith terms; ميّس, مريت, الأخلاف, حفّل; التعسّل, «الإسراع في المشي»; الخزامى and المندل; زياد, al-Nabigha, and جرول, al-Hutay'a; المفصّل) and Dar Sadir's (bayt 26: «أي لبس درعاً، وشبه حلقها بدوائر الماء في الغدير … أراد بالجدول السيف»; المفصّل).
- **Khizana's editor** glosses «رواه» as both «حكاه» and «من الريّ والسقاية» (14) and «أتنفّل» (16).
- **The lexicons:** al-Jawhari's *al-Sihah* (shamela 23235/3408) for أسبلت السماء (27); the rest are the standard senses (عسلان الذئب for التعسّل, العُذرة as both excuse and maidenhood, الغزالة as the sun at its rising).
- **The turn to praise (15–16):** Ibn Hijja and Ibn Ma'sum, above. The double sense of «صلاح الدين» is plain in the line; neither names it.
- **Bayt 26's second half** is word for word in a line of the Andalusian grammarian Abu Muhammad al-A'sha, quoted by al-Imad al-Isfahani, *Kharidat al-Qasr* (Maghrib section), vol. 2, p. 171 (shamela 12856/147, `kharida.txt`), and al-Qifti, *Inbah al-Ruwat*, vol. 1, p. 273 (shamela 10525/265, `inbah.txt`): «ملك إذا ادّرع الدِّلاص حسبته … لبس الغدير وهزّ منه جدولا».
- **Bayt 25** has no gloss in any source. The sharh reads the cloak as the riders spread over the ground.
- **The patron:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 8, pp. 249–250 (shamela 12286/7723–7724, `alam-nasir*.txt`): al-Nasir Yusuf ruled Aleppo from 634, took Damascus in 648 and was killed by Hulagu in 659.
- **The poet's life:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 3, p. 52 (shamela 12286/2082, `alam.txt`), and Ibn Khallikan, *Wafayat al-A'yan*, vol. 2, pp. 332–338 (shamela 1000/804–810, `wafayat.txt`), who knew him.
