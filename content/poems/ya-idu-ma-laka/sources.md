# يا عيدُ ما لَكَ مِن شَوقٍ وَإيراقِ

## Performance and extent

Osama al-Nahari recites on his own channel, uploaded 28 September 2023. The source is 282.355 s. The continuous cut from 6.00 to 281.05 s lasts 275.05 s. The source-voice windows place the first voiced group at 6.52 s and the last tail at 280.36 s. The opening title is outside the cut. Both source transcriptions and the uploader’s scrolling verse plate carry the poem from «يا عيد» to «بعض أخلاقي». There are 29 performed bayts and no repeats. [Original recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bOU1MV5Ny3g)

*Diwan Ta'abbata Sharran wa-akhbaruhu*, collected and edited by Ali Dhu al-Fiqar Shakir, no. 21, pp. 125–144, numbers 31 bayts. The performance takes diwan **1–4 and 7–31**. Diwan 5 «ممزوجة الود» and 6 «فالأول اللذ مضى» on p. 128 are absent. In the edition the supplementary love verses are marked with brackets. The default `abyat_in_full` follows this longer, numbered text. [Collected diwan](https://archive.org/details/3442pdf_202001)

## The textual traditions

- Al-Mufaddal al-Dabbi, *al-Mufaddaliyyat*, ed. Ahmad Muhammad Shakir and Abd al-Salam Harun, no. 1, pp. 27–31. This anthology has 26 bayts. Its first two are recording 1–2. It then goes straight to «إني إذا خلة», recording 6. The studio sings three of the five additional love verses found in the collected diwan, «طيف ابنة الحر», «تالله آمن أنثى», «تعطيك وعد أماني». They are visible together in successive scroll frames at 33–63 s of the original. [Opening](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/1) · [Close](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/5)
- Ibn Maymun, *Muntaha al-talab min ash'ar al-Arab*, pp. 289–290, also gives the 26-verse poem. Its «وكرّ طيف», «بضعيف الحبل», «مرجّع القول», «دملكه» and «أهل معزبة» establish a distinct wording. The film’s «ومرّ», «الوصل», «الصوت», «حدّأه» and «أهل معرفة» are all present in the scholarly tradition. [Text](https://shamela.ws/book/732/289)
- Al-Marzuqi’s commentary is reprinted in Shakir’s third appendix, heading p. 369, incipit on p. 371. The main edition gives Abu Amr al-Shaybani’s account of Ta'abbata, al-Shanfara and Amr ibn Barraq at the water of Bajila on p. 131. Al-Ma'arri quotes the additional «طيف ابنة الحر» verse in *Risalat al-ghufran* to ask about «التفرّاق» as a rare verbal noun. It thus has independent early testimony outside the standard 26. [Al-Ma'arri](https://shamela.ws/book/1589/103)
- Lyall’s *The Mufaddaliyat*, English volume II, no. I, pp. 1–5, discusses the horse and the bird in the runner’s boast. «ليس» can introduce an exception, as here, or intensify the claim that neither creature outstrips him. Lyall reads the shaggy companion and the shepherd’s nickname together and suggests changing the order of the final two bayts. The performance keeps the order printed in the Arabic anthology. The translations and comments were written anew. [Lyall’s edition](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatanth00mufauoft/page/1/mode/2up)
- Al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, II, p. 97, gives Thabit ibn Jabir’s name, home in Tihama, reputation for speed, and the report of his death in Hudhayl country. The date around 80 before the Hijra (about 540 CE) is an estimate. [Biographical entry](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/1215)

## Metre and rhyme

Basit with qaf as *rawiy*. The kasra at the rhyme is lengthened and can sound like a first-person suffix to a recogniser. Verse 29 actually ends in the possessive «أخلاقي». The opening makes the same cadence audible in both halves. A dash is a long syllable and u a short one.

| Recorded bayt | Sadr | Ajuz |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 «يا عيد ما لك … ومرّ طيف» | `--u- / uu- / --u- / --` | `u-u- / -u- / --u- / --` |
| 15 «عاري الظنابيب … مدلاج أدهم» | `--u- / -u- / --u- / uu-` | `--u- / uu- / --u- / --` |

At 1.1 «يا عِيْ دُ ما» fills مستفعلن. The contracted initial foot of 1.2 begins «وَمَرْرِ طَيْـ». The medial test uses the uncontracted and contracted forms of فاعلن in corresponding positions.

## Per-half verification

The verification pass summarised below processed all 58 halves and reported 17 differences and one edge flag. In the earlier pass 7.2 also differed. Widening that clip restored «خبت الرهط», which the final narrow clip also gives. The independent check used the widened Audar clips, both full-take transcripts, the corresponding visual scroll and matched word spans. The qaf cadence accounts for several spurious suffixes.
