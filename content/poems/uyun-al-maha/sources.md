## Editions

All three texts are in **Khalil Mardam Bek's edition** of the diwan (scan: archive.org `20201105_20201105_2037`). The poem is not in the diwan's manuscript; Mardam gives it three times:

- **First recension**, تكملة الديوان, poem 51, pp. 141–148 (leaves 140–147): 43 abyat Mardam gathered from طبقات الشعراء of Ibn al-Mu'tazz, الزهرة, محاضرات الراغب, مروج الذهب, the أمالي of al-Qali and of Ibn al-Shajari, سمط اللآلي, مرآة الزمان, عيون التواريخ and الكشكول, in an order of his own. His notes give each source's variants and the stories told about the poem.
- **Second recension**, from six manuscripts in Berlin, pp. 220–223 (leaves 219–222): 56 abyat, thirteen more than the first.
- **Third recension**, صلة التكملة, «القصيدة الرصافية: أكمل رواياتها», pp. 251–255 (leaves 250–254): 62 abyat from أبو الغنائم مسلم بن محمود الشيزري, جمهرة الإسلام ذات النثر والنظام (Leiden MS 480, copied 699 AH, via a photograph at Dar al-Kutub). One bayt («ولم يسأل الناس النبي محمد») Mardam supplies from the second recension.

The recitation follows the third recension's abyat and, except for abyat 27–29, its order (R3 runs 27, 29, 28; the text follows R2 and the web text). Its readings come from the first or second recension in sixteen places (below). `abyat_in_full` is 62, the third recension. Its abyat that the reciter does not say: «إذا ما أجال الرأي أدرك فكره», «ولا يجمع الأموال إلا لبذلها», «ولو قرنت بالبحر سبعة أبحر», «وإن ذكر المجد القديم فإنما», «لقد شكر الله الخليفة جعفراً», «ولن يقبل الإيمان إلا بحبكم», «أبو نضلة عمرو العلى وهو هاشم», «وساقي الحجيج شيبة الحمد بعده», «سقيتم وأطعمتم وما زال فضلكم», «وما ظهر الإسلام إلا وجاركم». His bayt 40 («إذا نحن شبهناه») is the first recension's form of the third's «أليس إذا ما قاس بالشمس وجهه».

## Ear check

- **Alignment:** `scripts/align.py` flagged 10 weak words, nearly all short; the weakest are «لا» in 17.1 (0.08), squeezed between «الأَشياءِ» and «أَنسَ», and «إِذا» in 52.1 (0.18). Their halves passed the ear check.
- **`scripts/verify.py`:** 25 of 104 clips differ from the text. Each difference is the transcriber's:
  - **ـة heard as ـات** nine times (الرصافات, الأهلات, الشبيبات, الغمامات, الأعنات, البشاشات, الخليفات, الصنيعات, القريبات): the reciter stretches the last short syllable of a shortened foot (فَعولُ), and the transcriber hears a long ā. These words can only be singular; the same habit explains the three "plurals" heard in his recitation of البحتري («سلام عليكم»).
  - a stretched final vowel heard as a pronoun or a syllable: «ناظرهم», «نكرم», «ذكرهم», «حجرهم», «الزهرية», «بالحرية», «مجرم», «نقرب»;
  - «لو انّ» run together as «لون» (11.2, 39.1);
  - words split or misheard: «خليلي» as «قليلا يا», «نعم» as «النعمة», «له» as «انه», «الأسر» as «الأمر»;
  - 15.1 and 44.1, where the transcriber made up words («والارض والارض», «انما يوم الارض…»); both halves align at 0.58–0.93 a word.

## Commentary drawn on

- **Mardam's notes:** the sources and their variants, Ibn Sharaf al-Qayrawani's praise of «الرصافية», the story from محاضرة الأبرار (which he calls «القصة الخيالية»), the three heirs of bayt 43, الحجون and الحِجر.
- **Ibn Rashiq**, العمدة 1:20, as Mardam quotes him, on abyat 29 and 32.
- **Shawqi Dayf**, تاريخ الأدب العربي (shamela 11996, vol. 4, p. 263), on bayt 36 and al-Mutawakkil's stand in the Mihna.
- The Quranic allusions of abyat 45 (4:59) and 46 (42:23).
- The poet's life follows al-Zirikli's *al-A'lam* (shamela 12286, vol. 4, pp. 269–270); the date of the three heirs (235 AH) is al-Tabari's.

## Review

A second reader checked the text against Mardam's three recensions and the sharh, notes, intro and bio against the sources (2026-11-17). Every half scans; al-Tabari's date for the heirs (Dhu al-Hijja 235) holds. Fixed after the review:

- **sources.md:** the second recension in full (56 abyat), which has the five readings first credited to the web text; four more readings taken from R1 and R2; the order of 27–29.
- **Intro:** Mardam's words on how the poem was found and quoted, the source of the Bedouin story named, «الرصافية» given as the books' name.
- **Arabic:** sharh 7, 8, 10, 12, 13, 19, 24, 27, 37, 40, 42, 43 and 50; notes on وَأَعلَمَني, جَلَّ, يُحَيَّونَ, ظَلوم, and the phrase notes of 22.2 and 35.1.
- **English:** sharh 1, 3, 12, 25, 28, 29 and 48 (Hashim's clan watered and fed the pilgrims; they did not keep the Kaaba), and five translations.
- **Bio:** Ibn Sharaf's words as he wrote them.
