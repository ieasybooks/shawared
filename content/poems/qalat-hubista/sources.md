# قالت حبست فقلت ليس بضائر

Osama al-Nahari performs all 28 bayts of Khalil Mardam Bek’s no. 14, once and in the printed order. The sun is third, immediately between the lion and the moon. The complete petition follows the prison similes, and «فبأي ذنب أصبحت أعراضنا» is last. The title takes the first sadr. In the sentence that runs across the opening division, «حبسي» is the delayed subject of «ليس بضائر».

## Editions and occasion

- Khalil Mardam Bek, ed., *Diwan Ali ibn al-Jahm*, no. 14, pp. 41–47. The rubric places Ali in prison addressing Ja'far al-Mutawakkil. The edited verses, his apparatus and the notes on obscure objects were read on the page images. P. 42 identifies الغيل and السّرار, pp. 43–44 explain the spear straightener, the returning turns of fortune and the visitors to the sick, and p. 45 explains يُحفد as being served. P. 46 names Ahmad ibn Abi Du'ad, and p. 47 carries the close and variants. [Facsimile, pp. 41–47](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n40/mode/2up)
- Ibrahim ibn Muhammad al-Bayhaqi, *al-Mahasin wa-l-masawi*, “Mahasin al-habs”, pp. 230–231. Its 22-bayt selection moves the fire forward, its sun is last, and it places the gatekeepers before the house of honour. It reads «يحمي غيله», «عما تحمد», «أتاك», «صبراً فإن اليوم يتبعه غد», «تزري فنعم», «الحبس», «ويحمد», «أبلغ», «بنو», «لغائب» and «الخصماء عندك مجلس». The following «قالت حبست فقلت خطب أنكد» belongs to a different response. [p. 230](https://shamela.ws/book/564/230) · [p. 231](https://shamela.ws/book/564/231)
- Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari, *Masalik al-absar*, XIV, pp. 465–466, gives a separate 17-bayt sequence. It supports «بضائر» and «يألف», brings «كم من عليل» close to the end, and gives «كرم … كرمت», «حساد نعمتك», «مجلس» and «بأي جرم … تقسمها». Its digital text shows «يعمد» for يغمد and «فرونه» in 17.1. These are recorded in the collation without silently correcting the witness. Ibn al-Mu'tazz, *Tabaqat al-shu'ara'*, p. 321, gives the speaker’s source through Ibn Abi Fanan, the prison occasion, and «ليس بضائري». [*Masalik*, p. 465](https://shamela.ws/book/30189/4953) · [p. 466](https://shamela.ws/book/30189/4954) · [*Tabaqat*, p. 321](https://shamela.ws/book/7867/305)

The rarer words in the running edition explain the actual sequence. «يُحفد» in 14 pictures service to a prisoner receiving visitors, then the imprisoned man no longer has to seek admission. In 15.2 «الأعبد» are themselves the enslaved attendants who can keep a petitioner waiting outside the ruler’s door. The English half therefore reads “no slave at court can shame you by barring the door.” At 16.1 the bare bāʾ in «أحمدَ بنَ أبي دؤاد» is correct between a son’s and father’s names. The tashkeel check asks about the silent word-initial bāʾ. The continuous linked reading is Aḥmada bna Abī Duʾād. In 18.1 «بني عم» is the accusative of specification after أنتم. In 25 the three derivatives of the same root enact an exchange of arguments. In 28.2 the edition’s «يشيد بها» means broadcasting a reproach. The performed forms agree with Mardam’s running text in each case.

## Meter and rhyme

Kamil, dal as rawiy, with a short u released at the rhyme. In 1.1 «قَالَتْ حُبِسْ / تَ فَقُلْتُ لَيْ / سَ بِضَائِرِنْ» gives متْفاعلن متفاعلن متفاعلن. In 14.1 «بَيْتُنْ يُجَدْ / دِدُ لِلْكَرِي / مِ كَرَامَتَنْ» has the same initial idmar followed by two full feet. Neither the shorter selections nor their position of the sun determines the recording’s sequence.
