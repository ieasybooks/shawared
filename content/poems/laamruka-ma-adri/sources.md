## Witnesses and occasion

- Nuri Hammudi al-Qaysi and Hatim Salih al-Damin, eds., *Diwan Ma'n ibn Aws al-Muzani* (Baghdad, Matba'at Dar al-Jahiz, 1977), no. 20, pp. 93–94, PDF leaves 92–93 counted from zero. All fifteen are numbered in the order recited. Lines 6 and 14 are bracketed and marked «أخلّ به الديوان». The editors supply them. Their note on 1 transmits from *Lisan al-Arab* a further bayt, «وكان لها جاران لا يخفرانها / أبو جعدة العادي وعرفاء جيأل», adding «ولم نجد مصدراً آخر يذكر هذا البيت». It is outside their numbered text and the recording. The full count here is fifteen. [Scan](https://archive.org/details/3604pdf_202001)
- Al-Baghdadi, *Khizanat al-adab*, 8, pp. 291–293, relates the story from the commentators on Abu Tammam: Ma'n divorced his friend's sister and married again. The friend swore to break off relations. He quotes 14 separately as evidence for the occasion and then quotes verses from the selection. His ordering puts our 5 before 4 and 13 before 9. His commentary explains «أبزاك» as overcoming a man or loading his back so heavily he cannot stand, and «فأعقل» as relieving him of blood-money, with tethering camels offered in payment as another interpretation. The shield and the right hand give the translation its two bodily images of withdrawing protection. [Al-Baghdadi, pp. 291–293](https://shamela.ws/book/12732/3829)
- Muhammad Ajmal al-Islahi, *Buhuth wa-maqalat fi al-lugha wa-l-adab wa-taqwim al-nusus*, pp. 278–279, in his discussion of al-Ghundajani's corrections to al-Namari, distinguishes a citation from the anthology text. Abu Tammam selects **thirteen** and opens «لعمرك ما أدري». Al-Ghundajani quotes «فلا تغضبن أن تستعار ظعينة» in his prose about the occasion. Al-Tabrizi adopts the explanation, and a later editor mistakes the quoted example for the opening. The recording's place for it, fourteenth, follows the Baghdad diwan. [Al-Islahi, p. 278](https://shamela.ws/book/18270/273) · [p. 279](https://shamela.ws/book/18270/274)
- Al-Tawhidi, *al-Sadaqa wa-l-sadiq*, pp. 249–250, prints twelve of the performed lines in this passage, giving «لم أخن» at 2, «ريبتي» at 5 and «فلم أدم» at 12. Al-Buhturi, *al-Hamasa*, no. 301, p. 155, selects our 2–5 and 8 under Ma'n's name. His versions include «إذا حال دهر», «من ذي قرابة» and an extensively different 5 ending «وما في نيتي لك معضل». The five are a selection in their own right. [Al-Tawhidi](https://shamela.ws/book/11485/222) · [Al-Buhturi](https://shamela.ws/book/148535/136)
- For the new profile, al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, 7, p. 273, gives «معن بن أوس بن نصر بن زياد المزني», his journeys to Syria and Basra, late blindness and death in Medina in 64 AH (683 CE). He quotes the opening with «تعدو». [Al-Zirikli](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/6749)

Under وجل, Ibn Manzur prints the opening and then «وكان لها جاران لا يخفرانها». The neighbours are a wolf and a hyena. He explains the saying that a flock is safe if the two animals meet and check one another. Ibn Hisham quotes the animal verse on its own in just that proverbial setting, with «العاوي» for «العادي». This corroborates the verse’s independent circulation. Its place in the lexicon explains how the Baghdad editors found it next to Ma'n’s opening. [Ibn Manzur, 11, p. 722](https://shamela.ws/book/1687/5899) · [Ibn Hisham, *Takhlis al-shawahid*, p. 266](https://shamela.ws/book/17916/226)

At 5 al-Baghdadi glosses «مساءتي وسخطي» as «إساءتك إليّ وسخطك عليّ». Ma'n is the person injured and the person at whom the anger is directed. The friend hopes to ease his own ailment by both acts. [*Khizanat al-adab*, 8, p. 293](https://shamela.ws/book/12732/3831)

The collatable transcriptions, `collation.txt` and word-level scores are under `media/research/laamruka-ma-adri`. The sixteen-line reconstruction that would result from incorporating the isolated lexicon citation would misrepresent this performance. In the sharh, the speaker initially offers his whole estate as a guarantee, then invites his friend to picture the hand he would cut off by renouncing him. The return to divorce at 14 follows that sequence of appeals.

## Metre and disputed readings

Tawil with lām as rawiy. At 1 the first half divides «لَعَمْرُ / كَ مَا أَدْرِي / وَإِنْنِي / لَأَوْجَلُو», فعولُ مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. In the second «عَلَى أَيْـ / ـيِنَا تَغْدُلْ / مَنِيْيَ / تُ أَوْوَلُو» the first foot is full and the third shortened. At 9 «إِذَا أَنْ / تَ لَمْ تُنْصِفْ / أَخَاكَ / وَجَدْتَهُو» falls into فعولن مفاعيلن فعولُ مفاعلن. Its second half «عَلَى طَ / رَفِلْهِجْرَا / نِ إِنْ كَا / نَ يَعْقِلُو» answers with فعولُ مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. The terminal vowel belongs to the rhyme.

## Verification

The narrow check flagged three of thirty halves. The independent widened readings are from `audited-flags/asr`.

| 2.2 | لا حل لنبذاك خصم أو نبابك منزل | The clip straddles «لم أحل». In continuous speech both backends recognise «إن أبزاك خصم», and al-Baghdadi explains the unusual verb. Keep the conditional and separate «نبا بك». |
| 3.2 | وأحبس مالي إن غرمت فأعقله | Like the narrow clip, widening writes a final ه. Both complete passes give «فأعقل», the intransitively used undertaking to pay the blood-money documented by al-Baghdadi and the diwan. The sustained u at the verse end explains the additional segment. |
| 9.2 | على طرف الهجران إن كان يعقل | Widening removes the narrow clip's extra final ه. All witnesses with this bayt give «يعقل», “has sense”, completing the conditional without an object pronoun. |
