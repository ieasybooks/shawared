Shawared is a library of Arabic poetry as it is recited: hear each poem in its reciter's voice, read its verses as they are recited, and understand what they mean.

• The verse being recited lights up as you listen. Move between verses, or repeat one to learn it by heart.
• Every verse comes with its explanation and the meanings of its words, and every poem with notes on it and its poet.
• Poems from before Islam to today: Imru' al-Qays, Antara, Jarir, al-Mutanabbi, Abu Tammam, al-Buhturi, Abu Firas al-Hamdani, Ibn Zurayq, Ahmad Shawqi and more.
• An English translation of every poem, beside the Arabic.
• Browse by poet, reciter and meter, or search by title, opening line or poet, with or without vowel marks.
• Fully vowelled texts that follow what the reciter says, checked against their sources.
• The recitation keeps playing while you browse the app or lock your screen.
• An Arabic or English interface, in day or night colors.

Shawared needs no account and has no ads or purchases. Loading poems and recordings needs an internet connection; the poems you've opened stay on your device.

Shawared is made by the EasyBooks initiative.
