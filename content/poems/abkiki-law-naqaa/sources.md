# أَبكيكِ لَو نَقَعَ الغَليلَ بُكائي

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari, 11 May 2024. The original runs 769.184 s. The master joins 1.42–7.25 and 9.45–763.25, giving 759.63 s. The first complete «أبكيك لو نقع الغليل بكائي» is followed by an abandoned «وأقول» and a fresh performance of that same first half. The keep removes the abandoned start. Both complete statements of the opening remain, pointing to 1.1 twice before 1.2. The tail after «في أحشائي» is out. The lead’s internal alignment divisions are at app times 261.26 s (23.2) and 502.82 s (45.1). These divide one continuous recitation. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0B4N_ASuE3Q)

Cohere and Audar both recover the opening sadr in two separate clips from the original, 0–7.7 s and 9.2–15.3 s. Audar drops the possessive yāʾ on «بكائي» in the first clip. These isolated clips confirm that the opening sadr is sung twice.

## Text, occasion and numbering

- *Diwan al-Sharif al-Radi*, Dar Sadir and Dar Bayrut, Beirut, 1961, I, pp. 26–30. The headnote identifies his mother as Fatima bint al-Nasir and dates her death to Dhu al-Hijja 385 AH. Its displayed heading «العمر روحة راكب» quotes verse 22. The earlier citations introduce the verses by the occasion. Here the title is the opening sadr. The print has 68 verses, the performance has 67 distinct verses and 135 performed halves. - *Diwan al-Sharif al-Radi*, Matba'at Nukhbat al-Akhbar, 1306 AH, pp. 10–12. The older setting also names Fatima and the date, and gives the complete close. It puts «قد كنت آمل أن أكون لك الفدا» before «وجرى الزمان» on p. 10. The recording follows the reverse order on Sadir p. 27. At 43 the older print has «فارج الغماء», at 46 «ويهاب», and near the close «معروفك الساري». The video and voice evidence favour «كاشف»، «ويخاف» and «السامي». P. 12 has «متحلباً»، «البيداء»، the following «الصهباء» verse, «لا تزل» and «ركض الغليل». - Al-Tha'alibi, *Yatimat al-dahr*, III, p. 171, explicitly introduces a selection from the lament for his mother. Among its thirteen verses are «في الصبر الجميل»، «تمسّكي»، «أتممتها» and «قد كنت أرجو». The recording has «بالصبر»، «تماسكي»، «تمّمتها» and «آمل». Al-Umari, *Masalik al-absar*, XXIII, p. 584, quotes sixteen selected verses including the beginning, the double loss at 34 and the last line. Neither selection sets the length. [Al-Tha'alibi](https://shamela.ws/book/6743/1173) · [Al-Umari](https://shamela.ws/book/30189/8055)

**The missed verse changes the meaning of the verse just before it.** At 56 the reciter says «وعليهم طبق من الصهباء», confirmed by both full-source transcriptions and the original on-screen line at 633 s. Both full prints read «وعليهم طبق من البيداء», a covering of desert earth over the dead. In the next printed bayt those lying on their cheeks look like people who have drunk their fill of red wine, «متوسدين على الخدود كأنما / كرعوا على ظمإ من الصهباء». The reciter anticipates its rhyme and then passes directly to «صور ضننت». A covering of wine over the buried people makes no sense of the first image. The text restores «البيداء», and the bilingual word card at 56.2 tells readers exactly what they hear and where it comes from. Displayed 1–56 correspond to printed 1–56. Displayed 57–67 correspond to printed 58–68.

## Other editorial decisions

In 9 Sadir explains «أغباب» as hollows or hidden parts of the ground. The studio interprets it more generally by the effects of night travel. Both lead into darkness serving as kohl for already irritated eyes. In 21 Sadir identifies the sides of the well over which the bucket-rope repeatedly moves. In 33 it glosses «لطّ الستر» as letting the curtain down. At 43 «ناصر للحق» also brings to mind the title of Fatima’s forebear al-Hasan ibn Ali al-Utrush. The gloss leaves that as a possible resonance of the ordinary phrase. [Lexical notes on pp. 26–28](https://archive.org/details/20191017_20191017_2122/page/n27/mode/1up) · [Family background](https://etheses.durham.ac.uk/id/eprint/5417/)

At 41 the card at 465 s, both prints and the sound pattern in the second whole transcription support «فزع اللديغ نبا عن الإغفاء». The first engine merges two words as «الذي غنى». At 54 the studio explicitly writes and explains «متحلّباً», a cloud’s water drawn like milk. It even names «متحلياً» as the other reading. Sadir p. 29 prints the latter and the 1306 print the former. At displayed 63 the card at 708–714 s, both full transcripts and the 1306 print support feminine «لا تزل» anticipating «رحمته». Sadir p. 30 has «لا يزل». The two editions agree on «أرضاه فعلك». At 67 the fetal movement in the mother's body is matched by the movement of grief in the son's. The older print and both independent early quotations give «ركض الغليل», grief kicking inside the son. Ibn Sinan, *Sirr al-fasaha*, p. 166, quotes the whole verse to object to the explicit bodily diction. His objection is also independent evidence for the precise words the reciter uses. [Studio reading at 54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0B4N_ASuE3Q&t=615s) · [Ibn Sinan](https://shamela.ws/book/11239/158)

At 12.1 the alignment model gives identical scores to «تماسكي» and «تمسّكي». It likewise fails to distinguish the two initial sibilants at 14.2. The studio and the idiom «تنفّس الصعداء» settle the latter. The possessive suggested at 6.2 is within 1.50 points of «بفداءِ» and the final kasra is stretched at the rhyme. At 23.1 both «أنضيت» and «أمضيت» fit the metre. The two prints and the main text of the studio card give «أنضيت», which extends the weary rider’s image to a life worn thin. The card glosses it with «أفنيت» and «أمضيت». The full Cohere transcription and the narrow Cohere and widened Audar clips all return «أمضيت». Full-source Audar gives «أنضيت». The isolated MMS comparison differs by only 1.16 points and the second test through «عيشك» by 0.21, both slightly favouring n. A separate cross-lingual phonetic decoder, given the audio without either candidate, locates [m] at 256.11 app seconds. On balance the edition prints the plausible performed «أمضيتِ», “you spent”, and the bilingual note preserves «أنضيتِ» and its travel image. The two alignments on their own leave the nasal unresolved. ## Metre and rhyme

Kamil with hamza as rawiy. The rhyme’s long alif precedes the hamza. At 1 «أبكيك لو نقع الغليل بكائي» gives **– – u – | u u – u – | u u – –**, and «وأقول لو ذهب المقال بدائي» gives **u u – u – | u u – u – | u u – –**. The rhyming first sadr shares the shortened ending of the ajuz. At 34 «رزآن يزدادان طول تجدد» gives **– – u – | – – u – | u u – u –**, and «أبد الزمان فناؤها وبقائي» gives **u u – u – | u u – u – | u u – –**. The caesura suspends the hearer between the announcement of two losses and their identification as her death and his survival.

With «أمضيتِ» at 23.1, the final verification flagged 15 of 135 performed halves. The narrow transcriptions differed on 19 of 625 words (3.0%). The table also preserves the earlier comparison at 23.1 against the printed «أنضيتِ». The wider transcriptions are independent Audar decodes. The decisions compare these clips with the full-source transcriptions, the numbered studio cards and the literary witnesses.
