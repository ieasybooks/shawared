# لِكُلِّ دَمعٍ جَرى مِن مُقلَةٍ سَبَبُ

## Recording and extent

Abd al-Rahman al-Humayn, Mawqi' al-Shankabutiya, 24 February 2016. The original runs 295.311 s. The master uses 20.98–281.60 (260.62 s), after the prose heading «قال وهو بسرنديب». It begins at the incipit and ends at «وكل دور إذا ما تم ينقلب». All forty numbered bayts occur once, giving eighty halves. The poet locates the speaking voice abroad at 27 before it identifies the political charge at 31. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kxGHk8VGx7Q)

## Witnesses and commentary

- *Diwan al-Barudi*, ed. Ali al-Jarim and Muhammad Shafiq Ma'ruf, with introduction by Jabir Asfur, Cairo, 1992, I, pp. 110–115. The heading says «وقال وهو بسرنديب». The whole forty-bayt poem was checked on the six page images, including the opening «لكل دمع» and «فإن يكن ساءني دهري» with its delayed answer at the close. The explanatory notes distinguish the shafts’ sinew (6), severance of a pledge (16), the actionable wrong (21), the silk, spear and tassels (23) and the substance of wealth (34). [First page](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-126053/page/n143/mode/1up) · [Closing page](https://archive.org/details/AAlexandrina-126053/page/n148/mode/1up)
- *Diwan al-Barudi*, Dar al-Awda, Beirut, 1998, pp. 72–75. This is a distinct setting of the same text and many of the same lexical observations. All forty occur here, with 1–10 on p. 72, 11–22 on p. 73, 23–34 on p. 74 and 35–40 on p. 75. It confirms «في الغيب»، «إذا هاج»، «في الحب»، «فجر»، «ذريعة تبتغيها النفس» and «من قبلي». The next poem begins on the last page. [P. 72](https://archive.org/details/20191210_20191210_1135/page/n71/mode/1up) · [P. 74](https://archive.org/details/20191210_20191210_1135/page/n73/mode/1up) · [P. 75](https://archive.org/details/20191210_20191210_1135/page/n74/mode/1up)
- Abdallah al-Tayyib, *al-Murshid ila fahm ash'ar al-Arab*, V, pp. 473–478. His opening quotation passes from 3 to 5, repeats 11 and gives «وقد هاج» in 12. He quotes 13–17 on p. 474 and returns to individual halves during the analysis. This is independent evidence for a substantial section. His ear for the echoes of al-Busiri’s *Burda* supplies the exact verbal link «دمعاً جرى من مقلة». The lightning can belong both to the old love prelude and to the poet’s actual landscape of exile. His reading of «سفح العقيق ... في سفحه» allows the second occurrence to mean either the slope or the pouring of tears. [P. 473](https://shamela.ws/book/150936/2846) · [P. 474](https://shamela.ws/book/150936/2847) · [P. 477](https://shamela.ws/book/150936/2850)

At 6 the arrows of time require neither feather nor the sinew that binds an ordinary arrow. At 12 the sustained picture is a bird in a trap. The interpretative notes distinguish the ancestral valley of a love poem from a literal itinerary from Sri Lanka. In 23 the woman moves in «سَرَق», silk, like a long spear dressed in iris tassels, «عَذَب». In 24 one full engine returns «بدر» where the other has «فجر» and the studio card at 174 s plainly shows dawn behind a veil of night. The moon itself follows one verse later. At 26 the card at 189 s as well as the two print settings gives «ذريعة تبتغيها النفس». At 38 the brothers’ bloodstained shirt in Qur’an 12.18 acts as lying evidence. The charge against the speaker thus acquires a much older apparent witness. [Verse 24 card](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kxGHk8VGx7Q&t=174s) · [Qur’an 12.18](https://quran.com/12/18)

At 30.2 the vocalisation is «الحَرَب». Ibn Manzur defines the noun with the middle consonant vocalised as «أن يُسلب الرجل مالَه», being stripped of one’s property. He also gives «اشتدّ غضبه» for the verb «حَرِبَ». The note in the Beirut edition on p. 74 takes that second sense. Verse 33 recalls in so many words what the hands of misfortune took from the speaker. The translation therefore gives his material loss here, with the other interpretation in the gloss. [*Lisan al-Arab*, entry حرب](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/122/1666/%D8%AD%D8%B1%D8%A8) · [Beirut note](https://archive.org/details/20191210_20191210_1135/page/n73/mode/1up)

At 5.1 and 9.1 the narrow and wide Arabic transcripts favour «بالغيب» and «بالحب». The full Audar transcript has «في الغيب» in 5. To control for the aligner’s preference for candidates with fewer written words, a second pass treated each connected pronunciation as a single unit. It still favours *bilghaybi* over *filghaybi* by 4.25 and *bilhubbi* over *filhubbi* by 5.36. For comparison it also prefers initial b by 4.09 in the well-attested «في سفحه» at 14.2, and barely distinguishes f and b in «في الحشا» at 2.2. An independent phonetic decoder operating without Arabic word prompts labels the two disputed onsets [ð] and [v], both fricatives. It returns that same [v] in «في سفحه» at 14.2, and [b] in «بليل» at 25.2. The captions and both prints give «في» and that reading stands. The preposition in 9.1 remains particularly uncertain. [Phonetic model](https://huggingface.co/facebook/wav2vec2-xlsr-53-espeak-cv-ft)

At 14.1 the isolated score favours «بي» by 4.62, the phonetic decoder returns [biː], and the narrow Arabic clip also writes «بي». The whole Audar take instead gives «فيه». Taking «بي» as “in me” would leave the following genitive «سفحِ العقيق» without a governor. Both diwan settings, al-Tayyib’s quotation and the card at 111 s leave «في» at the end of the half. The word card explains the junction. At 24.2 the isolated score favours «بدر» by 8.55, yet the independent decoder returns a clear initial [f] followed by [a]. The full and widened Audar transcripts also give «فجر». Together with the studio and diwan this supports dawn before the explicit full moon at 25. [Card at the caesura](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kxGHk8VGx7Q&t=111s) · [Dawn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kxGHk8VGx7Q&t=174s)

At 38.2 the card at 264 s and both prints read «في ثوب يوسف من قبلي دم كذب». The isolated word comparison favours «من قبلُ» by 3.54. Including the next word and comparing *minqablidamun* and *minqabludamun* gives −32.13 and −36.51. The independent decoder returns a front vowel before the onset of «دم». Its duration is hard to establish. With the possessive, the third foot scans *qab-lii-da-mun* (– – u –), while the shorter reading could occupy a licensed variant of the foot. These are complementary reasons for retaining “before me”. [Source card](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kxGHk8VGx7Q&t=264s)

## Metre and rhyme

Basit, with ba as rawiy and a sustained damma in the rhyme. In 1 «لكل دمع جرى من مقلة سبب» gives **u – u – | – u – | – – u – | u u –**, and «وكيف يملك دمع العين مكتئب» **u – u – | u u – | – – u – | u u –**. The first foot is shortened by khabn in both halves. In 20 «أليس في الحق أن يلقى النزيل بكم» gives **u – u – | – u – | – – u – | u u –** and «أمناً إذا خاف أن ينتابه العطب» **– – u – | – u – | – – u – | u u –**. The place where a half ends need not coincide with the end of its clause, as the preposition «في» at 14.1 makes especially clear.

## Clip verification

The final verification on the master flagged 53 of 80 performed halves. The narrow transcriptions differed on 89 of 447 words (19.9%). The wider transcriptions are independent Audar decodes. The decisions compare these clips with the full-source transcriptions, the numbered studio cards and the literary witnesses.

**38.1 edge.** The additional final بها flagged by the narrow pass is the obligatory complement of باء, already present once. The wide clip, the independent full text and the numbered studio verse contain one «باء بها».
