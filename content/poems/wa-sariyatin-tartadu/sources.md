# وَسارِيَةٍ تَرتادُ أَرضاً تَجودُها

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari, uploaded 6 May 2026. The original runs 459.778 s. The edit keeps 4.94–458.57 s, allowing the voice to enter on «وسارية» and the last vowel of «يعيدها» to decay. It removes the opening lead-in and the tail. The 96 performed halves give all 48 bayts of Khalil Mardam’s poem 18, each once, in the printed order. The complete cards run from a night cloud over Baghdad to the poet’s refusal to tell certain things again. One continuous ASR pass loses several lines around 25–28. The source cards, the other full pass and separate aligned halves account for that passage. [Original recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sW-7CfHVg8E)

## The diwan and the shorter witnesses

- *Diwan Ali ibn al-Jahm*, ed. Khalil Mardam, no. 18, printed pp. 56–64. The page distribution is 1 on p. 56, 2–9 on p. 57, 10–14 on p. 58, 15–20 on p. 59, 21–23 on p. 60, 24–31 on p. 61, 32–38 on p. 62, 39–45 on p. 63, and 46–48 on p. 64. Poem 19 begins below the last line on that page. Mardam’s opening note says he found five of these verses in *al-Sina'atayn* and scattered lines elsewhere, and had no other reference for the rest. His suggestion that the cloud recalls how swiftly the days of al-Mutawakkil went by is offered as an interpretation. The text itself makes the transition by comparing the cloud with the departing standards. [Beginning and editor’s note](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n55/mode/1up) · [The standards and the vizier](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n58/mode/1up) · [End and next poem](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n63/mode/1up)
- Ibn Tabataba, *Iyar al-shi'r*, p. 195, quotes our 1, 2, 9, 14 and 15. Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari, *Masalik al-absar fi mamalik al-amsar*, XIV, pp. 470–471, quotes the same five to exemplify a skilful transition. His cloud is an old woman led by a girl, and his watching eye sleeps “long”. Ibn Tabataba, Mardam’s main text and the recording put the young girl first and give «قليلاً». Both anthologists have the cloud outstripping the eye «سعياً» at 15.1. [Ibn Tabataba](https://shamela.ws/book/6911/191) · [Al-Umari, opening](https://shamela.ws/book/30189/4958) · [The change of subject](https://shamela.ws/book/30189/4959)
- Ibn al-Athir, *al-Kamil fi al-tarikh*, VI, p. 175, names Ali ibn al-Jahm and quotes 37 followed by 33 after recounting al-Mutawakkil’s death at Samarra in 247 AH (861 CE). He has «على وجه الزمان» at 33, where the diwan and reciter have «على طول الزمان». He confirms the political occasion and the striking «قتلنه». These two verses in a chronicle and the five in the rhetorical anthologies are partial witnesses. The complete count and order come from Mardam. [Ibn al-Athir](https://shamela.ws/book/21712/3121)
- Mardam, pp. 59–63, identifies the fleeing vizier Ubayd Allah ibn Yahya ibn Khaqan, distinguishes him from al-Fath ibn Khaqan who stayed with the caliph, and explains the Tahirid connection of the family of Mus'ab. He reads 35 against the division within the Abbasid family and explains 39 through mourners who mutilate themselves. The bilingual commentary follows the characters and accusations as the poet presents them. [The two men and the boat](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n58/mode/1up) · [Al-Fath](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n59/mode/1up) · [The Tahirids](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n60/mode/1up) · [The mutilated rhymes](https://archive.org/details/20201105_20201105_2037/page/n62/mode/1up)

## Metre

Tawil, with dal as the *rawiy* followed by pronominal ha and its long vowel. The opening also rhymes across the caesura, «تجودها / هجودها». The feet below show the linked pronunciation. The three-syllable فعولُ at the beginning of each half of 1 is the common shortened form of فعولن.

| Bayt | Sadr | Ajuz |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | wa-sā-ri ∣ ya-tin-tar-tā ∣ du-ʾar-ḍan ∣ ta-jū-du-hā | sha-ghal-tu ∣ bi-hā-ʿay-nan ∣ qa-lī-lan ∣ hu-jū-du-hā |
| 15 | fa-mar-rat ∣ ta-fū-tuṭ-ṭar ∣ fa-sab-qan ∣ ka-ʾan-na-mā | ju-nū-du ∣ ʿu-bay-dil-lā ∣ hi-wal-lat ∣ bu-nū-du-hā |
| 25 | wa-law-ḥa ∣ ḍa-rat-hu-ʿuṣ ∣ ba-tun-ṭā ∣ hi-riy-ya-tun | mu-kar-ra ∣ ma-tun-ʾā-bā ∣ ʾu-hā-wa ∣ ju-dū-du-hā |

At 15 the comparison particle occupies the final foot of the sadr. The cloud fills the first half and the army named after the caesura changes the subject of the poem.

## Per-half verification

Before adjustments to the cue endings, the narrow check of the 96 halves flagged fifteen, with no separate edge flag. Fourteen remained on the adjusted cuts. After «بها» was entered at 27.2, the alignment confidence there rose from 0.294 to 0.815 over the same interval. The final comparison agrees at 27.2 and retains thirteen machine differences.
