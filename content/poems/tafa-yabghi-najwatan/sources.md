# طاف يبغي نجوة

Abd al-Rahman al-Sa'eed, published 9 August 2014 by Mawqi' al-Shankabutiyya. Of the 102.911 s original, keep 25.92–88.24 s. The spoken author and occasion, including «وهو من صعاليك الجاهلية», end before that start. The retained performance begins with طاف and ends on قبلك, with a short natural decay. Twelve bayts, each half once, in the order given below. [Recording and reciter’s text](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5hlXa9dD5Ek)

## Editions and attribution

- Abu Tammam, *al-Hamasa*, ed. Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Rahim Usaylan (Imam Muhammad ibn Saud University, 1401 AH / 1981 CE), I, poem 312, pp. **447–448**. The video description cites I, 477. In this scan p. 477 begins no. 334, while no. 312 opens on p. 447 under «وقالت أم السليك ويقال إنها لأم تأبط شراً». P. 448 numbers the sequence through 12 and prints the recording's قِبَلَك. Its apparatus attributes بَدَلَك to other copies and al-Tabrizi, and records both alternative order and an additional question about the three snakes in two witnesses. The recording follows all twelve of Usaylan’s text. [Rubric and opening](https://archive.org/download/WAQ120493/01_120493.pdf#page=447) · [Final eleven verses and apparatus](https://archive.org/download/WAQ120493/01_120493.pdf#page=448)
- Al-Tabrizi, *Sharh Diwan al-Hamasa*, I, pp. 378–380. He identifies the woman as al-Sulaka, describes her son’s last raid and explicitly favours her attribution. The order of his twelve agrees with the recording. His last line means “in your place”. Bashir Yamut, *Sha'irat al-Arab fi al-jahiliyya wa-l-islam*, p. 63, reproduces these twelve under her name and also ends بدلك. [Al-Tabrizi, rubric](https://shamela.ws/book/6907/361) · [Al-Tabrizi, close](https://shamela.ws/book/6907/363) · [Yamut](https://shamela.ws/book/21508/52)
- Al-Marzuqi, *Sharh Diwan al-Hamasa*, pp. 645–648, introduces an unnamed woman. He distinguishes safety from the raised ground on which safety can be found, and comments on the unanswerable question «أي شيء قتلك». Al-Farisi, *Sharh Hamasa Abi Tammam*, II, no. 49, pp. 417–418, gives eight verses in another order. His heading names the mother of Ta'abbata Sharran, his sister, al-Sulayk’s mother and the mother of al-Shanfara. The biographical entry for al-Sulayk names his mother and gives his descent through Umayr, of Sa'd of Tamim. [Al-Marzuqi](https://shamela.ws/book/26536/624) · [Al-Farisi](https://shamela.ws/book/16435/407) · [Al-Zirikli, al-A'lam III, 115](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/2270)

## Metre and rhyme

The opening *ṭāfa yabghī / najwatan | min halākin / fahalak* scans فاعلاتن فاعلن | فاعلاتن فَعِلُن. At 7, *kullu shayʾin / qātilun | ḥīna talqā / ajalak* gives the same feet. Al-Zamakhshari cites this very opening while describing the four-foot madid, then gives al-Zajjaj’s analysis as مجزوء الرمل with حذف in the final foot of each half. The catalogue follows al-Zajjaj. Al-Farisi and Yamut use madid, and Usaylan records both analyses at p. 447. The rawiy is the closed ك, recurring in both verb forms and words with a second-person pronoun. [Al-Qistas fi ilm al-arud, pp. 77–78](https://shamela.ws/book/658/65)

## Recording decisions

Equal-word MMS scores use the aligned half and 0.30 s of context. At 3.1 تُعَد “be visited in sickness” scores −16.60 against تَعُد −28.04 and agrees with Usaylan. At 11.2 صبرَه is the direct object of ملك, whose subject is the heart, as al-Tabrizi explains. It scores −25.88 against the description’s صبرُه −28.05. At 12.2 قِبَلَك scores −8.45, بَدَلَك −20.76 and قَبلَك −13.05. Both full transcripts give قبلك, and Usaylan confirms the sense and vowels. The final word card records the other recension. The comparison files and scores are in `media/research/tafa-yabghi-najwatan`.
