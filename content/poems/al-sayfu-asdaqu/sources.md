## Title

The poem is known by two names: «بائية أبي تمام» (the video's description; al-Maqqari, *Nafh al-Tib*, ed. Ihsan Abbas, vol. 3, p. 592, shamela 1002/2001: «معارضاً بائية أبي تمام: السيف أصدق أنباء من الكتب»; the editors of al-Maqrizi's *al-Muqaffa* and al-Sam'ani's *al-Ansab*) and «قصيدة فتح عمورية» (Ibn Ma'sum, *Anwar al-Rabi'*, p. 512; Ihsan Abbas, *Tarikh al-Naqd al-Adabi*, p. 150). The title takes the first, on the pattern of لامية ابن الوردي and تائية الإلبيري. The id keeps the first words.

## Editions

- **al-Tibrizi**, شرح ديوان أبي تمام, ed. Muhammad Abduh Azzam (Dar al-Ma'arif), vol. 1, poem 3, pp. 40–74: archive.org `3901pdf`, leaves 84–117, read from the page images (`tibrizi-notes.md`). Every bayt, voweled, with al-Tibrizi's commentary, the comments of al-Suli [ص] and Abu al-Ala' al-Ma'arri [ع] that he gathers, and Azzam's apparatus of manuscript readings (م، ل، س، ظ، د، ب) and of Ibn al-Mustawfi and al-Marzuqi. Heading: «وقال يمدح المعتصم بالله أبا إسحق محمد بن هارون الرشيد (ويذكر حريق عمورية وفتحها)»; meter «الأول من البسيط».
- **al-Suli**, شرح الصولي لديوان أبي تمام, ed. Khalaf Rashid Nu'man (Baghdad), vol. 1, poem 3, pp. 189–207: archive.org `diwan-abo-tamam-shareh-alsouly`, read from the page images (`suli-notes.md`). Every bayt, voweled, with al-Suli's commentary and the editor's apparatus.
- **The old shamela diwan** (old.shamela 4387, the Abu Dhabi encyclopedia's text; one source, not an edition), pp. 17–24 (`diwan-old.txt`). Used as a check: it has «جلودهم» (59.2) and «ناظري» (33.2) with the reciter.

`abyat_in_full` is 71, as in both editions.

## Commentary drawn on

- **The sharh** follows al-Tibrizi throughout, with al-Suli's own commentary where his edition gives it (20, 22, 26, 28, 31, 38, 43, 46, 49, 51, 59, 61), the comments of Abu al-Ala' that al-Tibrizi reports (25, 48, 49, 52, 57, 59, 60, 71), al-Marzuqi's readings of 30, 49 and 55 and al-Kharzanji's of 45 and 61 (from Ibn al-Mustawfi and MS ظ in the editors' notes), and Ibn al-Mustawfi's own remarks on 11, 17 and 69.
- **The badi'** the commentators name is credited to them: tibaq in 2, 14, 26 (with his remark that true tibaq sets night against day), 47 and 61, jinas in 47, 63 and 66, tasdir in 66 and mumathala in 43 are al-Suli's; «تجنيس القلب» in 2 is the critics' term that al-Tibrizi reports. The sharh also points out figures they don't name, where they are plain in the words: tibaq or contrast in 1, 18, 28, 29, 31, 32, 34, 35, 45, 55, 62 and 68; jinas in 1, 12, 20, 23, 44, 52, 54, 64, 65 and 71; the end of 53 returning to its first word.
- **The astrologers and the figs and grapes:** al-Tibrizi's heading (p. 40) and al-Suli's note on 59 (p. 203), from Abu Malik Awn ibn Muhammad al-Kindi, from his father, who was at Amorium; al-Suli also gives it in *Akhbar Abi Tammam*. Ibn al-Mu'tazz's censure of 59 («من خسيس الكلام») is reported by Ibn al-Mustawfi in Nu'man's note (p. 204).
- **Zibatra and the woman's cry:** al-Suli on 46 (p. 199); al-Tibrizi and al-Kharzanji's version on pp. 61–62; Yaqut for the place.
- **The poet's life:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 2, p. 165 (shamela 12286/1418, `alam.txt`).
