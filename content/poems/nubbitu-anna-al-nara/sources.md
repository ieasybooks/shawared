# نُبِّئتُ أَنَّ النّارَ بَعدَكَ أوقِدَت

## Recording and extent

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed, Mawqi' al-Shankabutiya, 21 August 2009. The original runs 121.104 s. The master keeps 22.38–112.20 s (89.82 s), after «قال مهلهل بن ربيعة يرثي أخاه كليباً» and through the dying sound of «لا يسلس». The studio numbers ten consecutive bayts. Each is performed once. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dYekfSw4L_c)

## Witnesses and commentary

- Tha'lab, *Majalis Tha'lab*, ed. Abd al-Salam Muhammad Harun, I, p. 111. All ten are present, from «أودى الخيار من المعاشر كلها» to «في الحرب يوم عنانها لا يسلس». The second half of its opening is the reciter’s. Immediately after the garment verse Tha'lab connects the hooded cloaks with Christian women. The last verse supplies the point of Kulayb’s old name for his brother. [Ten-bayt text](https://shamela.ws/book/605/111)
- Abd al-Karim al-Nahshali, *al-Mumti' fi san'at al-shi'r*, p. 117. Its nine run 1–3, 5–10 of this recording. It has «أنبئت أن النار»، «وتقاولوا في أمر كل عظيمة»، «لو كنت حاضرهم بها» and «الصريخ». Its parallel scholion explains the burnus and the nickname zir. The electronically transmitted «بعثرة» and «ششت» were also compared with the more intelligible «بعبرة» and «شبّ» in Tha'lab. [Text and scholion](https://shamela.ws/book/14786/104)
- Al-Bakri, *Simt al-la'ali*, I, p. 299, gives the opening «ذهب الخيار من المعاشر كلهم», cites readings transmitted from Ibn al-Sikkit for verse 2 and also quotes the fire incipit. The fire has two old explanations. Kulayb’s hospitality sufficed for a whole encampment, so that no other cooking fire was needed. Alternatively his killing reignited the war. Al-Zamakhshari, *al-Mustaqsa fi amthal al-Arab*, I, p. 247, supports the recording’s exact «نبئت أن النار», with «وتقاولوا» in the next verse. [Al-Bakri](https://shamela.ws/book/1586/297) · [Al-Zamakhshari](https://shamela.ws/book/6895/253)

The two whole-source transcripts, studio cards and the two longer literary witnesses establish the sequence. At 2 «تنازعوا في كل أمر عظيمة» agrees with Tha'lab. At 3 Tha'lab writes «إبني ربيعة». The studio at 42–45 s gives «ابنَي ربيعة» and glosses them explicitly as Kulayb and al-Muhalhil. The surrounding invocation can bring the brothers together even though one is dead. Al-Bakri and al-Nahshali allow a collective address, «أبني ربيعة». The same card explains «يحبس» as holding injury back from the weak. Its preceding «يردّ» makes provision flow the other way, towards the destitute man. At 4 the card explicitly explains «استعال» as acquiring dependants, and reads interrogative «أنّى المجلس» (51–54 s). At 6 the chant resumes the woman as subject of «وتنفَّسُ», shortened from «تتنفّس». At 7 the horses «تعبِس», grimace, as the card’s «تتجهّم» makes clear. At 8 Jassas is left under the weight of the spear in the poet’s account. No historical claim of his death in this encounter is required by «ينوء».

For the profile, al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, IV, pp. 219–220, supplies the name Adi ibn Rabi'a, his branch of Taghlib, his brother’s nickname for him and the approximate date, 525 CE. The Cairo printing of *Qissat al-Zir Salim* names Abu Layla al-Muhalhil in its title. The episodes he gained in popular storytelling are discussed in *Oriens*, 2022, no. 6. [Entry, opening](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/3625) · [Continuation](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/3626) · [Columbia’s copy of the epic](https://hdl.handle.net/2333.1/8gthtf0m) · [Study in Oriens](https://vostokoriens.ru/articles/the-memory-of-the-crusades-in-the-arabic-folk-epics-images-and/)

The acoustic gap for dual «ابنَي» against collective «أبني» is 2.65, and for the two possible vowels of «تعبس» it is 2.78. The numbered cards identify the brothers and explain the horses’ expression. At 6.2 the wider gap of 9.99 favours the verb with a single initial t over the verbal noun.

## Metre and rhyme

Kamil, with sin as rawiy. In 1 «نبئت أن النار بعدك أوقدت» scans **– – u – | – – u – | u u – u –**, and «واستب بعدك يا كليب المجلس» **– – u – | u u – u – | – – u –**. In 5 both «وإذا تشاء رأيت وجهاً ناعماً» and «وذراع باكية عليها برنس» give **u u – u – | u u – u – | – – u –**. Iḍmar contracts the initial pair of shorts into a long. The case of «وجهًا» and of the coordinated «ذراعَ» belongs to the same verb across the caesura.

## Clip verification

The final verification on the master flagged 3 of 20 performed halves. The narrow transcriptions differed on 3 of 99 words (3.0%). The wider transcriptions are independent Audar decodes. The decisions compare these clips with the full-source transcriptions, the numbered studio cards and the literary witnesses.

| Half | Narrow clip | Wider clip | Editorial decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | نبيت ان النار بعدك انقذت | نبئت أن النار بعدك أنقدت | The narrow أنقذت and wide أنقدت are substitutions for أوقدت. Both uncut transcripts recover أوقدت. The studio, al-Nahshali and al-Zamakhshari all light the fire here. |
| 4.2 | لما استعال وقال ان المجلس | لما استعال وقال أن المجلس | Both clips lose the long second syllable of interrogative أنّى. The card and Tha'lab’s transmission support «أنّى المجلس». This prompted the correction of the earlier أين in the text supplied to the verifier. |
| 10.1 | الست زيرا حين شب وقودها | أن لست زيرا حين شب وقودها في | The wide clip restores أن لست زيراً, completing what the buried bones would have known in 9.2. The same clause occurs in both long witnesses. |

**2.2 edge.** The narrow clip inserts قل before «لو كنت شاهدهم». Both full-source transcripts proceed from عظيمة to لو and neither the numbered studio verse nor the witnesses insert an imperative. The beginning of the retained half is لو.
