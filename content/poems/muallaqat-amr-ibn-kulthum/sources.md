## Title and attribution

- **Title:** the books call the poem by this name: al-Zawzani heads it «معلقة عمرو بن كلثوم» (shamela 11253/232, and the Dar al-Ma'rifa print, p. 173), as does the Jamhara (shamela 1487/240, p. 272); al-Zirikli calls it «معلقته» (*al-A'lam*, vol. 5, p. 84) and the Aghani edition's heading on vol. 11, p. 36 is «تعظيم تغلب قصيدته المعلقة». Ibn al-Anbari heads it «قصيدة عمرو بن كلثوم» (p. 367). In the title «بنِ» is written without its alif, as between two names; the verse text writes «اِبنِ» with the voweled alif the house style requires (below).
- **Attribution:** every source read gives the poem to Amr ibn Kulthum: Ibn al-Anbari (p. 369, with the full lineage), al-Tibrizi (p. 217), al-Zawzani, the Jamhara, al-Aghani (vol. 11, p. 36: «ففي ذلك يقول عمرو بن كلثوم: ألا هبّي بصحنك فاصبحينا»), Ibn Qutayba (*al-Shi'r wa-l-Shu'ara'*, vol. 1, p. 230: «وعمرو بن كلثوم هو القائل: ألا هبّى بصحنك فاصبحينا», shamela 23785/223) and al-Zirikli. Two local doubts, both in the sharh: al-Tibrizi (p. 219) says some give abyat 6–7 to Amr, the son of Jadhima al-Abrash's sister (the Jamhara print quotes him in its note 24, p. 275); and Ibn al-Anbari (p. 423) reports al-Farra' citing al-Mufaddal that bayt 92 «ليس هو من هذه القصيدة», and (p. 424) Abu Ja'far calling bayt 96 «منحول». Bayt 116, which only the Jamhara gives to Amr, is Farwa ibn Musayk al-Muradi's in Ibn Hisham's *Sira* (ed. al-Saqqa, vol. 2, p. 582, shamela 23833/1319: the first of nine abyat on the day of al-Radm between Murad and Hamdan) and in al-Baghdadi's *Khizanat al-Adab* (vol. 4, p. 115, shamela 12732/1517: «وهذا البيت من أبيات لفروة بن مسيك المراديّ رواها أهل السّير»); al-Durra (p. 445) explains it with Farwa's next two abyat without remarking on it. The sharh of 116 says so.

## Editions

The poem's length and order differ greatly between recensions. Four were collated, every half (concordance at the end):

- **Ibn al-Anbari**, *Sharh al-Qasa'id al-Sab' al-Tiwal al-Jahiliyyat*, ed. Abd al-Salam Harun (Dar al-Ma'arif, Dhakha'ir al-Arab 35, 5th ed.): scan archive.org `3855pdf_202001`, leaves 387–445 = printed pp. 369–427, every verse line read from the page images; commentary text from shamela 124335/363–422 (whose page numbers follow the print). 94 abyat: 91, ending with «ألا لا يجهلن أحد علينا», which he says (p. 427) is «آخر القصيدة في رواية أكثر الناس», then three that «روى بعض الرواة» (our 113, 114, 117). Our 35 appears twice in his text (25 «شرقيَّ سلمى» and 81 «شرقيَّ نجدٍ»).
- **al-Tibrizi**, *Sharh al-Qasa'id al-Ashr* (Idarat al-Tiba'a al-Muniriyya, 2nd ed., 1352 AH): scan archive.org `20200312_20200312_0442`, leaves 216–248 = pp. 217–249, verse lines read from the page images; commentary from shamela 37488/216–249 (same print). 96 abyat; he ends with 118, 117 and 57.
- **al-Zawzani**, *Sharh al-Mu'allaqat al-Sab'*, introduced by Abd al-Rahman al-Mustawi (Dar al-Ma'rifa, Beirut, 2nd ed., 1425/2004): scan archive.org `elhilalymohamad_gmail_20180215_0617`, leaves 172–194 = pp. 173–195, every verse line read from the page images. Commentary and the page numbers in the concordance from shamela 11253/232–255 (Dar Ihya' al-Turath al-Arabi, 1423/2002, pp. 215–235), which numbers the abyat the same way; that file has typos the scan does not (12.2 «اليقينا», 53.2 «فتصبحَ», 54.2 «متلبّينا», 61.2 «مُقتوينا», 77.2 «الأيسرَين», 83.2 «وأسيافٍ», 85.2 «رأيتُ», 98.1 «مثلَ»). 103 abyat. **The recording's text is al-Zawzani's:** all 103 of his abyat, in his order (except that it puts his 38 before his 37, as Ibn al-Anbari and al-Tibrizi do: our 41–42), with 15 more inserted from other recensions (5, 9, 10, 19, 60, 82, 96, 102, 105, 108, 109, 113, 114, 115, 116).
- **The Jamhara of Abu Zayd al-Qurashi**, ed. Ali Muhammad al-Bajawi (Nahdat Misr): shamela 1487/240–269, pp. 272–304, 113 abyat. Checked on the page images of another print (archive.org `20231218_20231218_0842`, leaves 271–299 = pp. 272–300, 121 numbered abyat with an apparatus of the Amiriyya print «ع» and three manuscripts «م ب جـ»; its editor's name is not on the pages read), for 5, 9–10, 19, 21–22, 82, 92, 107, 108 and 115–116.
- **Other witnesses:** *Sharh al-Mu'allaqat al-Tis'*, attributed to Abu Amr al-Shaybani, ed. Abd al-Majid Hammu (al-A'lami, 2001), shamela 21574/288–331, pp. 305–348, whose editor adds in brackets the abyat missing from his manuscript, among them our 60, and who prints our 41.2 exactly as recited; Muhammad Ali Taha al-Durra, *Fath al-Kabir al-Muta'al*, shamela 151017/320–324 (his list of 118 abyat, vol. 1, pp. 325–329) and his notes on the added abyat (pp. 340, 344–345, 355, 426–427, 443–445).
- **`abyat_in_full` is 120:** the distinct abyat of the four collated recensions. The recording has 118 of them; it leaves out two that only the Jamhara has: «أفي ليلٍ يعاتبني أبوها … وإخوتها وهم لي ظالمونا» (Bajawi p. 276) and «ونعدو حيث لا يُعدى علينا … ونضرب بالمواسي من يلينا» (p. 304). The other abyat of the four that are not recited are variants of recited ones (marked * in the concordance). No single edition has all 118, so no single edition's count could serve.

Fixed:

- **Text:** no word or vowel changed. The on-screen text confirms the departures at 32.2 and 92.1 as the reciter's own and settles ten coin tosses as the text has them; where it differs (54.1, 65.1, 89.2, 103.1, 117.2) the audio or the evidence is split or against it, and the table records both. 113.1 «وَمَن أَمسى» is not a combination no edition has: Harun's manuscript م has it (p. 427, n. 7), and so does al-Hashimi's *Jawahir al-Adab*; the notes in both languages now say so. The 55.1/65.1/97.1 scores were biased by the voweled alif of «اِبنِ» and were done again without it: the kasra still wins.
- **Attribution:** 116 is Farwa ibn Musayk al-Muradi's in Ibn Hisham's *Sira* and al-Baghdadi's *Khizana*; the sharh in both languages and the attribution paragraph say so.
- **Intro and poet:** the Arabic intro, the Arabic bio and the Arabic sharh of 61 had the king wanting his mother Hind to serve Layla («أن تخدم أمُّه هندٌ … ليلى»), the reverse of al-Aghani and of the English; corrected in all three.
