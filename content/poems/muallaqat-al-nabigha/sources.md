# معلقة النابغة الذبياني

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari, «معلقة النابغة .. يا دار مية بالعلياء». The 438.358 s recording has silence at 0–3.517 and 437.120–438.358 s. The cut 3.02–437.62 s preserves the first attack and the full cadence on «النكد». There are 49 distinct verses and 98 performed halves in sequence. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gWDFXnt6BS4)

## Numbered diwan and classical commentaries

- Muhammad Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim, ed., *Diwan al-Nabigha al-Dhubyani*, Dar al-Ma'arif, poem 1, **pp. 14–28**. Verse 1 opens p. 14 and verse 49 closes the poem on p. 28. The singer performs **all 49, in exactly this order**. This is the basis for the full-length field. The headnote relates the plea to what was reported to al-Nu'man about al-Mutajarrida. The introduction attributes that occasion to the diwan, and the verses themselves name the slander without repeating it. The commentary establishes the tent bank and furnishings (pp. 15–16), the wild bull's two hounds and the horn (pp. 18–20), the gift and what follows it (p. 22), and the arithmetic of the doves (pp. 23–25). - Abu Ja'far al-Nahhas, *Sharh al-qasa'id al-tis' al-mashhurat*, ed. Ahmad Khattab, Baghdad, 1973, vol. 2, **pp. 733–767**. His first numbered bayt (p. 733) has «سالف الأبد». He glosses الأواري as the ties or stakes securing the horses (p. 736) and the earth of the نؤي as unusually hard to dig (p. 737). At 4 he prints the passive «رُدّت», records the active and discusses the syntax. His **50th** bayt on p. 766 has «قد تاه في البلد» as lemma and explicitly records «فإن صاحبها مشارك النكد» and «ها إن ذي عذرة» as alternatives. - Al-A'lam al-Shantamari, *Ash'ar al-shu'ara' al-sitta al-jahiliyyin*, section on al-Nabigha, poem 1, **Shamela text divisions 32–33**. The electronic edition numbers its text independently of the printed pages. Counting its fifty lines from «يا دار مية», the performance gives **1–10, 15–20, 11–14, 21–40, 42–50**. The singer describes the storm and the attack before the hound gnaws the horn. He leaves out this edition's 41, «إذن فعاقبني ربي معاقبة». Division 33 has both «فانقها» in the gifts and «شراع» in the dove count, and supplies the slander verse «إلا مقالة أقوام شقيت بها» in its main text. The work circulated under Abu Amr al-Shaybani’s name, but its quotations include later authorities. Its printed p. 95 reports «ما قلت من سيئ مما أتيت به» beside the other reading and supplies the following «إلا مقالة أقوام شقيت بها» in square brackets. Al-Zirikli's *al-A'lam*, **3:54–55**, supplies the name, estimated chronology, the court affiliations, and the attributed account of the red tent at Ukaz in the poet's biography. ## Order and readings

| Recorded verses | Counterparts in al-Tibrizi’s 50-verse text |
| --- | --- |

At 2 the singer gives «أصيلاناً أسائلها» for the lemma «أصيلاً كي أسائلها», at 6 «أمست خلاء وأمسى» for «أضحت … وأضحى», at 9 «يوم الجليل» for «بذي الجليل», at 11 «أسرت» for «سرت», and at 14 «وكان ضمران» for «فهاب ضمران». Al-Tibrizi records the last opening on al-Asma'i's authority. At 15 the veterinary surgeon's «طعن» replaces the lemma's repeated «شكّ». At 24 «فانفعه» is the diwan's reading. |
| 27–31 | 32, 33, 36, 34, 35. The gifts precede the counting of the doves. The singer has «المئة المعكاء» against «المائة الأبكار» and «الراكضات ذيول الريط» against «الساحبات ذيول المرط». The diwan treats «فارهة» as a choice she-camel or fine gift, with further gifts following. |
| 32–36 | 27, 29, 28, 30, 31. The description of the eye following the doves comes before the woman's wish. The flock is 66, half as many again is 33, and her own dove makes 100. |
| 37–39 | 37–39 with changes inside the verses. «الذي مسّحت كعبته» replaces «الذي قد زرته حججاً». Recorded 39 opens «ما قلت من سيّئ مما أتيت به», Tibrizi 39 «ما إن أتيت بشيء أنت تكرهه». |
| 40–41 | The diwan's «إلا مقالة أقوام شقيت بها» is recited in full. Tibrizi 40 «إذا فعاقبني ربي معاقبة» and 41 «هذا لأبرأ من قول قذفت به» are absent as verses in this recording, although the latter also describes the injury to the liver. «أنبئت أن أبا قابوس أوعدني» then comes forward from Tibrizi 48. |
| 42–49 | 42–47, then 49–50. «إذا هبّ الرياح له / ترمي غواربه» replaces «إذا جاشت غواربه / ترمي أواذيّه» at 44. At 45 «مترع» and «ركام» are both attested alternatives to «مزبد» and «حطام». Al-Tibrizi prints the recited 48 «فإن تسمع به حسناً / فلم أعرّض … بالصفد» as an alternative. He also records «مشارك النكد» for his concluding «قد تاه في البلد». |

Al-Nahhas numbers his fifty principal verses in the same order as al-Tibrizi in the table. The numbering conceals some differences of transmission. At his **32** (recorded **27**), he says al-Mufaddal recited «أعطى لفارهة» and that al-Asma'i and al-Athram did not transmit it here. At his **36** (recorded **29**), «والأدم قد خيّست», he again sets the verse apart from al-Asma'i's account. His **40** is omitted. At his **41** he quotes the alternative sadr «إلا مقالة أقوام شقيت بهم», with the singer's «بها» in the apparatus. The singer completes it with the diwan's «كانت مقالتهم قرعاً على الكبد» and then moves «أنبئت أن أبا قابوس» forward from al-Nahhas **48**. At his **42**, al-Nahhas explicitly gives the recorded accusative «فداءً لك» as a verbal noun. [p. 756](https://archive.org/details/sharih_nahhas/sharih_nahhas_2/page/n312/mode/1up) · [pp. 758–759](https://archive.org/details/sharih_nahhas/sharih_nahhas_2/page/n314/mode/1up) · [pp. 760–761](https://archive.org/details/sharih_nahhas/sharih_nahhas_2/page/n317/mode/1up) · [p. 765](https://archive.org/details/sharih_nahhas/sharih_nahhas_2/page/n321/mode/1up)

The meter is basit, with dāl as the rhyme consonant. In 1a «يا دارَ مَيْ | يَتَ بِلْ | عَلْياءِ فَسْ | سَنَدِي» gives مستفعلن فَعِلُن مستفعلن فَعِلُن. In 32a «اُحْكُمْ كَحُكْ | مِ فَتا | تِلْحَيِّ إِذْ | نَظَرَتْ» the same pattern carries a direct instruction to the king. The rhyme releases the final kasra in the chant.

## Readings checked against the voice

Score differences below compare the same number of words within a cut from the edited recording. A larger lead favours the named reading. Near ties give little guidance on case, doubling or a vowel lengthened in song.

- **1.2–6.1.** «الأبد» leads «الأمد» by 3.86, and «أصيلاناً» leads «أصيلالاً» by 2.28. Al-Tibrizi reports both diminutives. At 3.1 «ما أُبيّنها» is his lemma and the singer's spelling on the card. The score favours «ما أَبينها» by only 1.67. The accusative «والنؤيَ» continues «الأواريَّ» and leads the nominative by 0.47. At 4.1 the active «رَدّت» leads «رُدّت» by 3.24. Its understood subject is the servant named in 4.2. Both classical commentators discuss the readings. «ورفّعته» in 5.2 ties «ورفعته» and follows al-Tibrizi's vocalised lemma. Both full transcripts give «أمست» at 6.1, which leads «أضحت» by 2.86. ## Final timing check

The final highlight for 49.2 ends at 434.05 s on the app master. The 58 half-lines whose end times changed were transcribed again. The final per-half check flags 10 halves.
