This frame starts in Arabic, which has two explanations per verse. Switch it to English to see the translations.

واحرَّ قلباه

  1. Press play. The playing half-verse turns full ink, and the list keeps it centred.
  2. Scroll the verses yourself. Following pauses. Five seconds after you stop, it glides back to the playing verse. The line under the phone shows the countdown.
  3. Tap a verse. Its explanation opens over the verses while the recitation carries on. The verse is shaded so you know which one you're reading.
  4. Let it play on. The explanation stays on your verse and offers “البيت الحالي / Now playing” to catch up.
  5. Tap a verse number. Playback jumps to that verse and everything follows again. On a mouse, hovering a number shows ▶.
  6. Tap شَوارِدِها or any word with a ◆. Its meaning opens without stopping the audio.
  7. Switch the explanation. Arabic has two here, مختصر and مفصّل. English has one, so it shows no switch.
  8. Switch to English. Each line gets its translation underneath. Verses 19 and 23 use a one-sentence translation for the whole bayt. The “Translation” switch hides them.

واحرَّ قلباه

واحرَّ قلباه ·

  • Halves side by side.With room to spare, sadr and 'ajuz face each other as in a printed diwan, and each translation sits under its own half.
  • The explanation stays open.The side panel follows the playing verse. Click a verse to hold the panel on it; “Now playing” or a verse number releases it.
  • Word meanings in two places.Every ◆ word of the verse is listed under its explanation, and clicking one in the verse opens its card at the top.
  • Previous, next and the progress bar count as playing something.Like a verse number, they release the panel and resume following.
