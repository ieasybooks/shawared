# قَد أَكثَرَ النّاسُ في المَنسوخِ مِن عَدَدٍ

## Recording and witnesses

Osama Al-Waaedh recites all ten bayts, once and in order. Alqimmah Studio posted the recording on 17 September 2021 under «الناسخ والمنسوخ في القرآن الكريم», credited itself with recording and al-Waaedh with editing. Its verse cards and the typed description occasionally differ. The source is 105.408 seconds long. Opening and closing silence run to 6.08 and from 102.41 seconds. The app retains 5.58–102.96 seconds (97.38 seconds), with −1.0 dB gain, −20.1 LUFS integrated and −5.0 dBTP peak. The last syllable of حضروا remains voiced to about 96.28 seconds on the app clock. The final highlight extends to 96.32 so the held vowel stays with its verse and the reverberation can decay. [Recording and verse cards](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YHe5baPfjOE)

- **Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti, الإتقان في علوم القرآن**, ed. Muhammad Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim, vol. 3, pp. 72–77, especially pp. 76–77. The poet introduces his own ten verses with «وقد نظمتها في أبيات فقلت», which supplies the attribution. The app names the poem by its opening. His prose starts with twenty-one disputed or accepted cases, removes permission to enter and distribution of an estate, then includes the change in prayer direction on the authority of Ibn Abbas, bringing his preferred count to twenty. The last bayt reports the two other claims. [Discussion](https://shamela.ws/book/11728/836) · [Tally](https://shamela.ws/book/11728/840) · [Poem](https://shamela.ws/book/11728/841)
- **Al-Suyuti, معترك الأقران في إعجاز القرآن**, Dar al-Kutub al-Ilmiyya, 1408 AH / 1988, vol. 1, p. 91. This second version preserves the same ten bayts and divides نجواه across the halves of 9. Al-Suyuti prints his legal discussion alongside the verse in both books. [Poem](https://shamela.ws/book/1417/90)
- **Muhammad al-Amin al-Shinqiti, بيان الناسخ والمنسوخ من آي الذكر الحكيم**, ed. Ali ibn Muhammad al-Imran, pp. 175–179. He prints the ten verses together and then keys their cues to Quranic passages. **Muhammad ibn Ahmad Ibn Aqila al-Makki, الزيادة والإحسان في علوم القرآن**, vol. 5, pp. 298–302, supplies a third scholar’s quotation and a detailed explanation. These two commentaries and al-Suyuti’s own prose underlie both sharh layers. The repository collator compared the edition against the two authorial texts and al-Shinqiti, ten verses each. - **Al-Zirikli, الأعلام**, vol. 3, p. 301, gives the full name, dates (849–911 AH / 1445–1505 CE), childhood in Cairo, withdrawal to the Rawda and writings used in the biography. The biographical introduction to عقود الجمان في علم المعاني والبيان, p. 20, specifies a Cairo birth at the opening of Rajab 849 AH. The metre is basit with rāʾ as rhyme consonant. The first half of 1 scans مستفعلن فاعلن مستفعلن فَعِلُن (– – ⏑ – / – ⏑ – / – – ⏑ – / ⏑ ⏑ –). Bayt 5.2 gives مُتَفْعِلُن فَعِلُن مستفعلن فَعِلُن (⏑ – ⏑ – / ⏑ ⏑ – / – – ⏑ – / ⏑ ⏑ –). In the ninth bayt نجـ belongs to the first half and ـواه to the second, exactly as displayed and as printed in معترك الأقران. The recording carries the word through the break.

## Text and commentary

At 3.2 both independent full-recording transcriptions yield «لأهله». Ibn Aqila and the uploader’s typed verse explicitly give the same form. The display, الإتقان, معترك الأقران and al-Shinqiti give «لأهليه». A separate 13-second window across the verse also transcribes «لأهله». The isolated half with 0.4 seconds of extra context transcribes «أهليه», while scoring the two alternatives on the same interval favours «لِأَهلِهِ» by 4.05. A separate phone pass places the vowel after the l at approximately 25.76–25.98 and the following pronoun vowel at 26.04–26.28 on the app clock. Neither is exceptionally lengthened here. The edition gives the short form and records the other in its word card. Both forms fit basit, the short one with a short pronoun vowel in «لِأَهلِهِ عِندَ» (second foot فَعِلُن), the long one with فاعلن. The recited «يوصي» retains its long final vowel under poetic licence after أن. At the rhyme, مُحتَضِر (a dying person) scores 1.92 above the passive and agrees with the display and الإتقان.

At 4.1 the reciter joins «مَعْ رَفَثٍ». The typed text of الإتقان gives «مِن رفث». The other three witnesses and the studio support مع, which scores 2.81 above مِن. At 5.1 «فيما صحّ مِن أثر» agrees with الإتقان and the display. The studio description and al-Shinqiti have «في أثر», while معترك also has «فما» for «فيما». Both continuous transcriptions recognise «فيما». The electronic word score favours مِن over في by 8.49, and an independent phone pass catches the final n at 42.02 seconds. At 6.1 the difference between مَعْ and مَعَ is only 0.04 in the score. The shortened مَعْ suits the metre and is explicitly marked in the author’s verse.

Bayt 7 names six cases. Al-Shinqiti, pp. 177–178, identifies alliance and inheritance (4:33), confinement (4:15), declining to judge a case (5:42), witnesses to a travelling man’s will (5:106), constancy in combat (8:65–66) and the general summons to go out (9:41, 91, 122). The verse cards, معترك and al-Shinqiti carry «وترك أولي / كفرٍ وإشهادُهم». The electronic الإتقان has «وترك أولى / كفروا شهادتهم». The two whole-recording transcripts support كفر وإشهادهم, and the equal-word-count trial favours those words by 33.60. At 8.2 the similar-sounding مُحتَظَر (forbidden) has the sense required by the two marriage restrictions. The word recognisers alternate between ظ and ض here and the score margin is only 0.67. A dying person, مُحتَضِر, has already appeared as the subject at 3.2. [Al-Shinqiti on 7–8](https://shamela.ws/book/20770/4)

At 9.1 «دفع مهر لمن جاءت» evokes migration and repayment on the dissolution of a marriage. Quran 60:10 deals with repaying what the former husband of a woman coming over had spent. Al-Suyuti’s prose and al-Shinqiti also cite 60:11, which compensates a husband whose wife left in the other direction. The commentary therefore identifies the settlement passage as 60:10–11. At 9.2 the performance and both authorial texts give «كذاك» (12.17 above «كذلك», al-Shinqiti’s form). At 10.1 the displayed and typed «مَلَكَتْ» alludes to «مَلَكَتْ أيمانُكُم» (24:58). The electronic الإتقان gives «مَلَكْتَ». The word score differs by only 0.45, but the widened pass transcribes «مَن ملكت» and the phone sequence places a vowel between k and the final t. The edition follows the third-person form. [Al-Suyuti on the two dower directions and the tally](https://shamela.ws/book/11728/840) · [Al-Shinqiti on the final bayts](https://shamela.ws/book/20770/6)

## Independent half-verse check

The isolated pass transcribed all twenty halves. Eight differed in the first cut and one had a possible extra word at the edge. On the final cut seven differ. The longer closing vowel lets the recogniser recover the full last half, including singular «القسمة». A second recogniser also transcribed all halves with the windows widened by about 0.4 seconds on each side. Both whole-track transcripts, the cards and the verse witnesses were consulted at the flagged points.
