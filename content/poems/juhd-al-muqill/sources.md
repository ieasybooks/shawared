# جهد المقل

## Recitation and witnesses

- Hamza Muadh, Alqimmah Studio, 13 December 2024. The description credits عبد الله السالم بن محمدو بن حنبل الحسني الشنقيطي and supplies a copy identified as collated against the manuscript. All 50 couplets in that copy are recited in sequence. The intake transcript includes both the line about the rising sun and the line on the smile. Audio keep 3.70–366.15 s, cutting only the silence before and after. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zfTscaClVZs)
- Studio’s manuscript-checked text, pp. 1–3, with 1–15 on the first page, 16–33 on the second and 34–50 on the third. The studio does not identify the manuscript or name the collator. The Arabic follows the booklet's page images in conjunction with the sound. [Approved text](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qEZamHXzyN89C9xPyDqpXquJVS5aczVA/view)
- A second three-page copy circulated by الموسوعة الشنقيطية, ending with a copyist’s statement naming الشيخ أحمد بن مود الجكني, ends at number 48 but prints number 25 twice. It contains 49 actual couplets, including the smile verse, and lacks the studio text’s 19. A magnified comparison gives شذى at 23.2 in both copies. Both also give منهما at 26.1 and اللذ at 29.1. The studio’s 38.2 reads مع المشاش وهو عبل العضد against عبل الذراعين معا والعضد, 39.1 رقيقة against دقيقة, and 42.2 وعقب against وعكن. At 34.2 the booklet and recitation give فكان where the other text has وكان. The booklet and the continuous audio support the readings presented here. Images, PDFs and the comparison are in `media/research/juhd-al-muqill/`. - The poem itself names its metre in 7.1. In 1.1 حَمْدَنْ لِمَنْ / شَرْرَفَ رُو / حَلْحَقْقِي gives the rhythmic lengths – – ⏑ – / – ⏑ ⏑ – / – – – (the doubled consonant of شرّف is counted in both syllables). In 23.1 the whole line moves – ⏑ ⏑ – / – – ⏑ – / ⏑ – ⏑ –. Each coupling supplies a new rhyme. The word بأعالي is divided between 40.1 and 40.2 in both witnesses. At 44–45 it is the construction ينحط من صبب that crosses from one couplet to the next.

## Commentary and life

- Al-Tirmidhi’s *al-Shama'il al-Muhammadiyya*, chapters on Muhammad’s appearance, the seal of prophethood, hair, grey hairs, speech and laughter. The long description attributed to Hind ibn Abi Hala supplies much of the rare vocabulary, including the palm, hairline on the torso and arches of the feet. Its chain is weak, and it is one among several reports assembled in the poem. The final prayer and the rising-sun comparison are given as the poet’s wording. [Opening chapter](https://sunnah.com/shamail/1), [the report and grading](https://rasoolallah.com/hadith/shamail/1), and [Arabic text of the description](https://www.kalim.info/shamail_muhammadiyah/1/)
- Taqi al-Din ibn Abd al-Hakim al-Awadi, *Qurrat al-muqal bi-sharh nazm juhd al-muqill*, 1st ed., Markaz al-Imam Malik, 1443 AH (2021 CE). This verse-by-verse commentary gives the moon or first crescent for الجيلم (p. 31), comments on the rising sun in our 19 (p. 43), discusses the two interpretations of أشكل (pp. 48–49), and explains why the light at 26 might seem to come either from the teeth or from inside the mouth (p. 52). For 38.2, p. 64 prints the reading mentioning both forearms, says the author used it in his own commentary, and gives the recording’s مع المشاش وهو عبل العضد as a variant. It documents both رقيقة and دقيقة there and on p. 65. [Published commentary, first edition](https://www.scribd.com/document/1030835749/)
- For the alternative explanations of أشكل in 24, compare the report from Jabir ibn Samura and subsequent glosses in the same chapter. *Mu'jam matn al-lugha*, root ج ل م, gives the moon for الجيلم at 15.2. [Lexicon entry](https://books.islam-db.com/book/معجم_متن_اللغه/560)
- The local biography by Abd Allah Ould Ihmayd names the poet’s aunt Maymun, his scholarly studies and his own commentary on the poem. Both that account and the Mauritanian manuscript catalogue give his death in 1353 AH. The biographical-dictionary entry reproduced by Bawabat al-Shu'ara gives 1934 CE. The local account and the dictionary differ by two years on his birth, so neither a birth year nor an estimated age is used. [Local biography](https://oughl.wordpress.com/2012/10/11/hanbel/), [Mauritanian manuscript catalogue](https://makrim.org/manuscrits.cfm?PN=974), and [biographical entry and bibliography](https://poetsgate.com/poet.php?pt=4242)

## Audio verification

- The final 362.45-second track contains 100 successive halves. The verifier marked 12 clips and 15 of 432 words. All 12 were compared with wider Audar clips, the continuous recording transcript and the two sets of page images. Score pairs below are for the same clip in the order given.
- At 4.1 Audar restores نعوته, which the short clip wrote with ط. The forced scores for نعوته and نعوطه tie at −47.4. At 11.2 the short clip split ربعة قد after a different syllable. Audar puts the two words in their printed places. At 13.2 it separates يعلوه الورى again but shortens the final حاشاه. The printed interjection fits the emphatic claim, and حاشاه and حشى scored −43.4 and −43.6.
- The short clip at 19.1 merged بل لو. The full recording and both wider passes recover both words, with the syllable لو aligned at 137.88–138.10 (confidence 0.81). At 25.1 the wide clip has أفلج, the term for the small spaces between teeth, which scored −33.2 against −47.3 for أثلج. At 25.2 the two machines grouped the consonants as عنك البرد. The continuous syntax calls for the missing object after يفتر عن followed by its comparison كالبرد. The printed division scores −34.7, the other −35.1. Al-Awadi also supplies “teeth” at this point in his commentary.
- At 29.1 the shortened relative اللذ appears in both printed texts and the complete transcript. The short clip spells it الذ, and the wide clip expands it to الذي. The printed form scored −56.2 against −59.8 for الذي. At 29.2 the sustained final vowel of الحجله was transcribed as الحجلا, which scored −34.2 against −39.8 for الحجله. The two texts and the tradition naming زر الحجلة identify the word as the noun حجلة. At 32.2 the short backend dropped the final object pronoun of يضفره (−54.0 with it, −48.5 without). Both wider Audar passes recovered the pronoun, which refers to the hair.
- At 40.2 the word أعالي crosses the division of the halves. The last syllable لي is aligned at 288.42–288.52, immediately before الصدر. At 45.1 the wider clip restores نظره (−35.4 against −44.8 for نظرهم). At 45.2 the wider audit gives غض بصره, as do the continuous transcript and both texts. بصره and the short backend’s بصاري scored −59.4 and −60.7.
- Wide clips were also made for the substantial differences between witnesses. At 34.2 فكان scored −42.7 against −48.2 for وكان. At 39.1 رقيقه scored −31.0 against −37.4 for دقيقه. At 42.2 وعقب scored −30.2 against −41.8 for وعكن. The second backend independently gives all three studio readings. At 38.2 it gives all five words of مع المشاش وهو عبل العضد. The four-word alternate would have a length advantage in a direct score. In the recorded half, المشاش and وهو align separately with confidences 0.61 and 0.56. These comparisons preserve the studio wording at each of the four places.
