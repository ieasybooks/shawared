## Editions

- **Shukri Faisal**, أبو العتاهية: أشعاره وأخباره (Damascus University, 1965), poem 439, pp. 421–424: all 44 abyat, «من مجزوء الرمل», fully voweled, with variants from his manuscripts (ت) and (ل). The poem isn't in his manuscript (ظ); (ل) lacks abyat 1–2 and orders 41–44 as 43, 44, 42, 41; 18–21 are in the margin of (ت), and (ت) lacks 31–40. His note on 43–44 sends the reader to al-Aghani (vol. 4, p. 11, Dar al-Kutub): «سمعت الأصمعي يستحسن قول أبي العتاهية: أنت ما استغنيت…» Scan: archive.org `0017pdf_202002`, leaves 461–464. **The text is Faisal's**, put in the house style, but for 35.1 (below).
- **عبد العزيز السلمان, مجموعة القصائد الزهديات**: shamela 38155, vol. 2, pp. 349–351 (`salman.txt`). A modern anthology, fully voweled: Faisal's 44 abyat and readings, but «النّا / سُ» in 24 ((ل)'s reading), «فَهُوَ» in 31.2 (which breaks the meter), a garbled «يَُرَأَ» in 32.2 and «مَنِ النّاسِ» in 37.2.
- **Ibn Abi al-Dunya**, الإشراف في منازل الأشراف, no. 314, pp. 252–253: shamela 12994/315 (`ibn-abi-al-dunya.txt`). Faisal's 31–40, 43, 44 and 42 (not 41), with two abyat Faisal lacks («نحن في دهر على المعدم لا يجدي أبوه», «وعلى الوالد لا يفضل إن عال بنوه»), headed «أنشدني أعرابيّ من بني تميم من بني حنظلة». It names no poet.
- **Ibn Durayd's Amali** (تعليق من أمالي ابن دريد), pp. 168–169: shamela 26237/96–97 (`ibn-durayd-amali.txt`). The same passage (31–40, 43–44, 42), from al-Asma'i: «أنشدنا أعرابيّ من بني تميم ثم من بني حنظلة».
- **The last two abyat as Abu al-Atahiya's:** al-Jahiz, *al-Bukhala*, p. 239 (shamela 10501/231, `jahiz-bukhala.txt`); Ibn Qutayba, *Uyun al-Akhbar*, vol. 3, pp. 95–96 (shamela 23790/957–958, `uyun-957.txt`, `uyun-958.txt`); al-Abbasi, *Ma'ahid al-Tansis*, vol. 2, pp. 297–298 (shamela 6951/675–676, `maahid.txt`): «وكان الأصمعي يستحسن قوله». Ibn Qutayba quotes 42–44, 41 and 34 again, after a line not in Faisal («إنّ للمعروف أهلًا»), as «وقال آخر وقد أحسن» (vol. 3, pp. 216–217, `uyun-1078.txt`, `uyun-1079.txt`).
- **Safi al-Din al-Halabi**, أنس المسجون, no. 445, p. 184: shamela 120146/177 (`uns-al-masjun.txt`). Eight abyat as Abu al-Atahiya's, with «ربّ محبوب» (3.1), «لو رأى الترك» (34.1) and «بل همو لو طمعوا» (35.1); its editor points to Faisal's p. 421.

`abyat_in_full` is 44, Faisal's count.

**Words across the halves.** The meter is majzu' al-ramal, and 22 of the 40 abyat run a word across the break (mudawwar). The halves are cut where the meter cuts them, as Faisal prints them («لا يَبـ / ـقى») and as «إِنَّ السَّلامَةَ» does (8.1 «فَوَ»): so 4.1, 8.1 and 15.1 end in the article «ال». Where Faisal's spacing doesn't show the place, the meter decides: 22.1 «مِنَ الأَم / والِ» and 33.1 «وَإِن أَم / لَقَ» (he prints «وإنْ / أملقَ»). The Arabic notes give the whole word on each fragment that ends a sadr; 2.1 says why.

- **1.1, the verb:** the meter can't settle it, since يُسلِمُ and يَسلَمُ scan alike (yus-li-mul, yas-la-mul), and the scores are a coin toss on vowels the aligner barely hears. The grammar does: «المَرءَ» is an object (the upload vowels it so), which يَسلَمُ, an intransitive verb, can't take. Both editions print يُسلِمُ, and 18.2 «أَسلَموهُ» takes the verb up again.
- **25.1:** the clip transcript, the second transcriber and both transcribers on a wider clip all hear «وعن الموتى», but the aligner can't tell the onsets apart (ظ, ط, ض and و all within 2; scored on the first word alone, وَعَنِ comes last but one), and «وَعَنِ المَوتى إِلى ما قَدَّموهُ وَجَدوهُ» has no sense. The transcribers favour a common word over a rare one, and a reciter who says ظ as a soft ḍ can sound like «و» to them; the text keeps the editions' ظَعَنَ.
- **35.1:** the reciter says إِن, Ibn Abi al-Dunya's reading: both transcribers hear «وهم إن طمعوا», and the aligner gives the word 0.45 against 0.08 for لَو. The word note gives Faisal's لَو; the sense is the same.

## Meter

Majzu' al-ramal, فاعلاتن فاعلاتن with خبن (فَعِلاتن) allowed in either foot; rawiy ه (the pronoun, after the ridf و). Every half was scanned (`_scratch/tools/ramal.py`). The pronoun ـهُ/ـهِ is lengthened where the meter needs it, as poets do: 5.2 «عَلَيهِ», the first verb of each half in 7, 9, 11, 16, 17 and 18, 10.1 «عَجِّلوهُ», 13.1 «أَخرِجوهُ» (the tool flags this half because it reads the final alif of the fragment «أَعوا» as a plural alif; with «أَعوا» long and «أَخرِجوهُ» lengthened it is -u-- -u--), 20.2 «فيهِ», 25.2 «قَدَّموهُ», 29.2 and 30.2 «إِلَيهِ», 32.2 «مِنهُ»; and the mim of 35.1 «وَهُمُ».

## Commentary drawn on

- No classical commentary on the poem is known; the sharh is a plain reading. Faisal has no glosses on it.
- **Glosses:** *Lisan al-Arab* (shamela 1687) for حرّف («عدل»), أسحقه («أبعده»), أملق («افتقر»), أوقر, الحنوط; the other words in their common classical senses.
- **Allusions:** Quran 74:38 (15), 73:20 (25), 67:11 (17.1 note), 17:31 (33.1 note), 2:111 «قل هاتوا برهانكم» (14.2 note); the hadiths «لقّنوا موتاكم لا إله إلا الله» (7.2 note; Muslim, shamela 1727/2054), Abu Salama's eyes, «إنّ الروح إذا قُبض تبعه البصر» (9; Muslim, 1727/2059), and «أسرعوا بالجنازة» (10; al-Bukhari and Muslim).
- **Uyun al-Akhbar** (vol. 3, p. 95, `uyun-957.txt`): the saying «ولا الأخ إلّا عند الحاجة إليه» (31) and Jarir's «فأنت أخي ما لم تكن لي حاجة» (39), which Ibn Qutayba quotes just before the closing pair.
- **Other readings in the sharh:** (ل)'s «النّاسُ» (24); Ibn Abi al-Dunya's 32 and 33; Ibn Qutayba's «إنما يعرف الفضل» (37).
- **The intro's last sentence:** al-Jahiz, Ibn Qutayba, al-Abbasi and Ibn Abi al-Dunya as above.

## Review

A second reader checked the text against Faisal's page images (leaves 461–464) and the shamela texts, scanned every half, re-scored the doubtful words on the word alone, and checked the sharh, notes and intro against the sources (2026-11-17). They found no error in the text, its harakat or its meter; 1.1 يُسلِمُ, 24.2 «سَ», 35.1 «إِن» and 37.1 «يُعرَفُ» are confirmed on the word alone, and 25.1 and 32.2 stay as the editions have them (above). Fixed after the review:

- **Meaning:** 8 (he is dying, not yet dead: «حرّفوه» is said to the dying, as the note says) and 14 («هاتوا» is “bring him”, not “come”, as in «قل هاتوا برهانكم» and (ت)'s «قالوا هاتوا قرّبوه») corrected in both languages; 20 given the reading that fits «فيما كان فيه» (they had no part in what he went through), the other as «وقيل»; the Arabic sharh of 35 paraphrased «لو» where the text has «إِن».
- **Sharh:** 9 (the closing of the eyes, from the hadith of Abu Salama in Muslim) and 10 (the hadith «أسرعوا بالجنازة» quoted, and the turn of the bayt) deepened in both languages; 11, 18, 29, 31 and 39 deepened; 39 gives Jarir's «فأنت أخي ما لم تكن لي حاجة», which precedes the pair in *Uyun al-Akhbar*; 32 quotes Ibn Abi al-Dunya exactly; 34's rhetorical «فكيف» removed.
- **Notes:** added 2.1.1-2, 14.1.2-3, 14.2.2 (English), 18.1.1, 21.1.1, 26.1.2, 27.1.1-3, 28.2.2, 29.1.4 and 34.2.1; 7.2.2 gives the hadith «لقّنوا موتاكم»; 20.1.3 and 26.1.4-5 no longer repeat the sharh; 5.2.3's head is أَقرَبون.
- **English:** 1 quotes the verb as 18 translates it; 20.1 retranslated.
- **Intro:** the ending now names the closing abyat on the fair-weather friend, which the recording ends on.
- **sources.md:** al-Salman's other departures (24, 37.2) and Ibn Abi al-Dunya's order (no 41) recorded; 13.1 added to the halves that lengthen the pronoun; the word-alone scores added.
