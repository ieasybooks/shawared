## The two published lengths

Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, I, *al-Siyasa wa-l-tarikh wa-l-ijtima'*, Cairo, Matba'at Misr, printed pp. 224–228, «العلم والتعليم وواجب المعلم». Yusuf Ilyan Sarkis records the original 1926 Matbaʿat Misr issue, with Muhammad Husayn Haykal’s introduction and 376 printed pages, in *Jamiʿ al-tasanif al-haditha*, I, p. 78, no. 680. The cover of *al-Musawwar*, no. 80, announced the volume’s appearance on 23 April 1926. Columbia University’s bound copy and the Wikimedia facsimile give the same printed text, lineation and pagination here. Their title leaves carry the same press numbers and neither spells out the year of impression. Columbia catalogues the bound volumes as 1936. The Wikimedia description gives 1370 AH. The five pages contain respectively **10, 15, 16, 15 and 12** verses, **68** in all, the basis for `abyat_in_full`. These are full two-hemistich bayts. Small typographical dividers after Cairo 17, 21, 36 and 49 group the argument. The single lam rhyme and the metre continue across all five divisions. [Cairo scan, p. 224 (PDF leaf 226)](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikisource/ar/1/1a/الشوقيات1.pdf#page=226) · [Columbia copy, p. 224](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/books/columbia_aco003812/250) · [p. 228](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/books/columbia_aco003812/254) · [Columbia title leaf](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/books/columbia_aco003812/5) · [Wikimedia description](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/ملف:الشوقيات1.pdf) · [Sarkis, p. 78, no. 680](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/13828/79?lang=ar) · [*Al-Musawwar*, no. 80, dated cover](https://www.trycollect.com/images/items/595/lg-58b7f51ba3b72cf60ed178e7161a7e30.jpeg)

The Beirut Dar al-Awda printing, I, pp. 180–184, and Hindawi’s chapter 36 both give the **66** recited verses. Recited 1–23 = Cairo 1–23, 24–64 = Cairo 25–65, 65–66 = Cairo 67–68. Cairo 24 begins «وَنِيَتْ خُطَى التعليم بعدَ محمّدٍ», with the slow walk after Isma'il in the ajuz. Cairo 66 begins «أدّوا إلى العرش التحيّة واجعلوا», with praise of the Creator in its ajuz. Cairo p. 226 prints 34.2 «من أن تُكافأ بالثناء جميلا», whereas the Beirut p. 182 has «كفاءً» in the corresponding line. The recitation agrees with Cairo and Hindawi at that word. The closing line of gratitude to the throne is absent from both shorter versions. [Dar al-Awda, pp. 180–184](https://archive.org/details/BBib-Alex-02914/page/n183/mode/2up) · [Hindawi, ch. 36 with notes](https://www.safahat.org/books/70706142/36/)

The Cairo volume prints two corrections to these pages in its errata. For p. 226, line 4, it supplies the complete «إذا» in recorded 28.1. For p. 228, line 1, it restores the missing lam of «للبرلمان» in recorded 56.2. The reciter uses both corrected forms. [Printed errata, p. 374](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikisource/ar/1/1a/الشوقيات1.pdf#page=376) · [Columbia, p. 226](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/books/columbia_aco003812/252) · [p. 228](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/books/columbia_aco003812/254)

The Egyptian Ministry of Education’s *al-Shawqiyyat lil-madaris*, title leaf 1352 AH / **1933**, pp. 3–7, calls it «العلم والتعليم». It retains the two additional Cairo verses, but on p. 5 passes straight from «الجهل لا تحيا عليه جماعة» to «ربّوا على الإنصاف», omitting the five lines corresponding to recorded 31–35. The 1927 date on the Cornell catalogue record does not date this title leaf. For the introduction, Cairo p. 224 names the teachers’ college club, while p. 227 n. 2 identifies Saturday 15 March 1924 as the opening of the first Parliament. The school edition puts the recital shortly before the opening. Its sectional summary and the Cairo notes underpin the commentary on Socrates’ cup, Dunlop and elephantiasis (recorded 24), the invisible constitutional soldier (55), and the look and squint (40). Shawqi presents the death of Socrates and the crucifixion in his own argument. Neither account serves here as an independent historical source for those deaths. [Cornell school edition, title leaf](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/books/cornell_aco000189/9) · [pp. 3–7](https://sites.dlib.nyu.edu/viewer/books/cornell_aco000189/11)

## Performance and scansion

The spoken introduction «وقال في العلم والتعليم وواجب المعلم» finishes before the verse. Keep **6.42–483.75 s** on the original clock. All 132 complete halves occur once. Within 33.1 he starts «عرفت مواضع…» at 239.83 s and breaks off at the start of the next word about 241.14 s. He begins again at about 242.26 s and completes «جدبهم فتتابعت». The first Cohere transcript writes the two beginnings, Audar smooths them together. The local phone trace gives /…t m…ḍ…j…/ on the first attempt and the consonants /d b/ of «جدبهم» on the second. The restart belongs to this one half. At 53.2 the word aligner places «وضعوا» at 388.26–388.80 s with 0.76 confidence, followed by «على» with 0.67. The full utterance continues to the wreath. No complete half is missing.

Kamil, three feet in each half. The opening «قُمْ لِلمُعَلْ / لِمِ وَفْفِهِتْ / تَبْجِيلَا» gives – – ∪ – | ∪ ∪ – ∪ – | – – –. The opening sadr takes the rhyme and curtailed ending of its ajuz (tasri'). At 33.2 «كَالعَيْنِ فَيْ / ضَنْ وَالْغَمَا / مِ مَسِيلَا» gives – – ∪ – | – – ∪ – | ∪ ∪ – –. The rhyme is lam followed by the prolonged a of the cadence.

In 5.2 the genitive «الحديدِ» after «صدئَ» has a 1.46-point advantage over the nominative and has the printed syntax. In 7.1 the two gemination candidates score identically. The edition supports the undoubled «فَجَرتَ». At 24.1 the recogniser scores its substitution «خبيبة» 4.93 higher, but widened Audar recovers «خفيفة» and the contrast with the swollen foot depends on its lightness. At 33.1 «جَدبهم» scores 11.23 above «جِدّهم». At 62.1 the recited «بالكرامة» scores 5.37 above «بالكرم». At 63.1 word-level tests of «في» against a whole prefixed noun give unequal phonetic spans. The printed text and continuous context support the preposition.
