# سائل بنا في قومنا

Abd al-Rahman al-Sa'eed, published 6 July 2014 by Mawqi' al-Shankabutiyya. The original is 103.480 s. The spoken account of the Fijar war and the last «قالت عاتكة بنت عبد المطلب» precede the first sung سائل. Keep 54.45–89.16 s, including the decay of ضباعه. All six bayts follow in printed order, each half once. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9S5qWDJu1l0)

## Editions and attribution

- Abu Tammam, *al-Hamasa*, ed. Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Rahim Usaylan (Imam Muhammad ibn Saud University, 1401 AH / 1981 CE), I, no. 262, p. 381. A single page gives the rubric «وقالت عاتكة بنت عبد المطلب», six numbered bayts and the split الناظريـ / ـن. Its مُلتَمِعٌ، شُعاعُه، غادَرنَه and تَنهَشُه agree with the performance. [Scanned edition, PDF p. 381](https://archive.org/download/WAQ120493/01_120493.pdf#page=381)
- Al-Marzuqi, *Sharh Diwan al-Hamasa*, ed. Gharid al-Shaykh (Beirut, 2003), pp. 526–528, explains سماعه as a warning that a report of this battle is enough to experience its horror. At 4.2 شعاعُه is the postponed subject of يُعشي, with an understood object for لمحوا. At 6.1 the feminine subject of غادرنه is the horses. His text has تنهسه and he expressly records تنهشه. Al-Tabrizi likewise gives the horses, the mail and the leader’s helmet. The Shamela page before I, 310 is labelled 209, a break in this electronic pagination. [Al-Marzuqi, opening](https://shamela.ws/book/26536/505) · [Al-Marzuqi, close](https://shamela.ws/book/26536/507) · [Al-Tabrizi, middle and close](https://shamela.ws/book/6907/293)
- Abu al-Qasim Zayd ibn Ali al-Farisi, *Sharh Kitab al-Hamasa*, ed. Muhammad Uthman Ali, II, p. 356, gives the six, including ملتمع قناعه and تنهشه. Ibn Tayfur, *Balaghat al-nisa'*, p. 191, heads them «وقالت عاتكة بنت المطلب ويقال صفية». Its وكفاك، شناعه at 4.2 and غادرته have independent interest. The attribution in the catalogue follows the Hamasa rubric, with Safiyya named in the introduction. Al-Zirikli records Atika’s name, the uncertainty over her conversion and the report concerning Badr. [Al-Farisi](https://shamela.ws/book/16435/347) · [Ibn Tayfur](https://shamela.ws/book/12848/189) · [Al-A'lam III, 242](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/2645)

## Metre and rhyme

The opening scans *sāʾil binā / fī qawminā | wal-yakfi min / sharrin samāʿuh*. Each of the first three feet is the syncopated form of متفاعلن, with the extra closing syllable of ترفيل in the fourth. At 3, *fīhis-sanaw / waru wal-qanā | wal-kabshu mul / tamiʿun qināʿuh* restores the two initial short syllables in feet two and four. These identify مجزوء الكامل المرفّل. Al-Farisi’s electronic heading reads مرفل الوافر. The recurring radical ع is the rawiy, preceded by alif of ridf and followed by the pronoun هاء الوصل.

## Recording decisions

The two full transcripts, the original recording, the editions and the widened Audar clips are collated locally in `media/research/sail-bina` and `media/work/9S5qWDJu1l0`. Equal-word alternatives were scored with the repository MMS model on the aligned half with 0.30 s context. At 1.2 وليكف scored −20.85 against يكفي −24.86. At 3.2 nominative ملتمعٌ scored −32.04 against al-Marzuqi’s circumstantial ملتمعاً −38.04. At 4.2 شعاعه scored −47.82 against the Ibn Tayfur text’s شناعه −53.84. At 6.1 غادرنه and غادرناه tied at −15.71, but both continuous transcriptions and the two Hamasa readings preserve the feminine plural. At 6.2 تنهشه scored −40.10 against تنهسه −44.41. The print and the two transcripts support shīn.
