## Editions

- **ديوان بدوي الجبل**, with an introduction by Akram Zu'aytir, Dar al-Awda, Beirut, first edition 1978 (the scan's description: «دار العودة ـ بيروت» and «الطبعة الأولى ١٩٧٨»): archive.org `20190731_20190731_1945`, pp. 542–546, leaves 541–545 (each printed page is the leaf number plus one), read from the page images (crops in `media/research/ghanni-ya-bulbulu/_scratch/crops/`). The poem stands in the section «البواكير» (title page p. 431; the contents list «دموع ودموع» at p. 542), headed «دموع ودموع» and dated «(١٩٢٥)» under its last line. Partly voweled. Bayt 1 alone opens it; then four stanzas of 9, 7, 8 and 9 abyat, each followed by the refrain (bayt 1 and «لست تدري…») between rows of asterisks: 35 distinct abyat, 42 as printed. The same PDF is uploaded twice more (`20251008_20251008_0331`, `20251114_20251114_2223`, same file size). *Tatimmat al-A'lam* (below) lists this edition: «ديوان بدوي الجبل - بيروت: دار العودة، ١٣٩٨ هـ، ٥٥١ ص». This is the text the poem follows and `abyat_in_full` counts (35, each refrain bayt once).
- **poetsgate.com** (بوابة الشعراء), poem 15670, «دموع و دموع», added 1 March 2007 (`web-poetsgate.txt`): unvoweled apart from shadda, the whole poem with the refrains, but the third stanza's fifth and sixth abyat run together into one bayt («وقضوا الأحجار والثمه ثرى / طاهرا واعقر على القبر المطيّة»). - **Rabih Farruji**, *al-Tajriba al-shi'riyya fi shi'r Badawi al-Jabal* (doctoral thesis, Université Ferhat Abbas, Setif, 2011–2012): archive.org `lis01738`, pp. 126, 212 and 215–216 (leaves 129, 215, 218–219), read from the page images. He quotes abyat 1, 2, 3, 11, 12, 13 and 16 from the diwan, pp. 542–544, and has two slips of his own (16.1 «زحم» for «زخم», 1.2 «عنّي» for «منّي» in the second refrain). - **Not found:** the first diwan, «ديوان بدوي الجبل – الشفق», printed at Matba'at al-Irfan in Sidon in 1925 (Nabil Sulayman, below, p. 12; he says the 1978 diwan's «البواكير» holds most of it, p. 20), and the later complete editions. archive.org (`archive.py find` بدوي الجبل, ديوان بدوي, badawi al-jabal; `grep` on the other Badawi al-Jabal items) and shamela (search on five halves of the poem) have no other text of it. ## Title and attribution

- **Title:** the diwan's heading «دموع ودموع», which Farruji (pp. 212, 215), Sulayman (p. 20) and poetsgate also use; as with the Shawqiyyat's heading «نكبة دمشق» for `nakbat-dimashq`. The upload calls it «غن يا بلبل»; its first sadr would be «غَنِّ يا بُلبُلُ فَوقَ الدَّوحِ غَنِّ». The English title translates the heading.
- **Attribution:** the poet's own diwan, published in his lifetime (Sulayman, p. 19, notes it appeared three years before his death); Sulayman lists «دموع ودموع: ١٩٢٥» among the dated poems of the first diwan (p. 20); Farruji discusses it as Badawi al-Jabal's (p. 215). No source gives it to anyone else. In the poem the speaker is nineteen («في التسع بعد العاشرة», 31.2), and the diwan dates it 1925, which agrees with the birth year 1905 of *Tatimmat al-A'lam* and *al-Mawsu'a al-Arabiyya*.

## The meter

Ramal, three feet a half (فاعلاتن فاعلاتن فاعلن). `scan.py` passes every half; the seven it marks are the second stanza's ajuz endings, where the rhyme «ـاتْ» ends in a consonant (it shows that consonant as X). Each stanza has its own rhyme and its own last foot (darb), as strophic poems do:

- bayt 1, the first stanza and the refrain, on «ـنّي»: darb فاعلاتن (or فعلاتن), and the opening sadr ends the same way by tasri' («غَنِّ», -u- -);
- the second stanza, on «ـاتْ»: darb فاعلانْ (maqsur), -u- with an extra consonant;
- the third, on «ـيَّهْ»: darb فاعلاتن or فعلاتن;
- the fourth, on «ـِرَهْ»: darb فاعلن (mahdhuf).

The 'arud is فاعلن or فعلن throughout except in bayt 1. `rawiy` is left out of poem.json: the format takes one letter, and the stanzas rhyme on ن, ت, ي and ر. Meter decides 6.1 «أَتَهَيَّب», 18.1 «وَهِيَ» (wa-hi-ya; «وَهيَ» would leave the first foot – – – –), 20.1 «طَيِّبَةٌ», 25.1 «لَيلايَ», 27.1 «يَشهَدُهُ» (the jussive «يَشهَدهُ» would break the third foot), 28.1 «وُدّي» (not «وُدِّ») and 30.1 «نَفسِيَ».

## Commentary and facts drawn on

- **No commentary on this poem was found.** The sharh is the drafter's reading of the diwan's text, with the grammar stated where the reading depends on it: 5–6, «إنّما الدنيا … غارةٌ», read as one sentence across the two abyat with «وفي أمثالها عِبَرُ الدنيا وأصداءُ التمنّي» as an aside (this explains the nominative «غارةٌ» the diwan prints; the likelier reading, said so in the sharh); 7, «غاب حاميه» an aside between the two objects of «رُبَّ», set between dashes in the diwan; 10, «ها» in «أستثرها» for «الدنيا», «هِمَماً» a tamyiz, «لا ورى … ولا ظلّل» a prayer against himself; 16, «لا» negating a subject whose predicate is understood; 18, «هِيَ» a pronoun the following sentence explains; 23, «أنتِ» for a non-human plural; 27, «حتّى النجومُ» with «حتّى» of the limit; 30, «ها» in «غمرتها» for the battle; 32, the answer to «فإذا متُّ» of 31. ## The poet

- **Name, dates, life:** Muhammad Khayr Ramadan Yusuf, *Tatimmat al-A'lam*, vol. 2, p. 164 (shamela 133395/483, `tatimma.txt`): «محمد سليمان الأحمد (١٣٢٣ - ١٤٠١ هـ- ١٩٠٥ - ١٩٨١ م) الشاعر المعروف ببدوي الجبل»; the name given by Yusuf al-Isa of «ألف باء» because «إن الناس يقرؤون للشعراء المعروفين، وهذا التوقيع المستعار يحملهم على قراءة- الشعر للشعر»; the ministries of 1954–1956; leaving Syria in 1956, Beirut to 1958, Istanbul and other countries, back in 1963; «وفي آخر حياته رقد سنة كاملة في المستشفى العسكري بدمشق عاجزا عن الحركة»; the Dar al-Awda diwan. It gives his birthplace as «ريغا» near Latakia and his death as «يوم الأربعاء ١٨ آب (أغسطس)»; neither is used. The same text is in his *Takmilat Mu'jam al-Mu'allifin*, p. 492 (shamela 36031/1456–1457).
- *al-Mawsu'a al-Arabiyya* (Damascus), vol. 4 (2001), p. 772, «بدوي الجبل», by Ihsan al-Nass (arab-ency.com.sy/details/4243, `arabency.txt`): 1905–1981; born in «قرية «ديفة» من أعمال منطقة الحفة في محافظة اللاذقية»; his father «من علماء اللغة المتمرسين بها ومن الفقهاء المتعمقين»; imprisoned after the French took Damascus (Hama, a year in Beirut, then Latakia) and later eight months in the citadel of Kassab; «وانتخب سنة 1937 نائباً في المجلس النيابي ثم تكرر انتخابه مرات»; fled to Iraq in 1939; minister of health in 1954 in Sabri al-Asali's cabinet, then of «الدعاية والأنباء»; «فارق الوطن سنة 1956» (Beirut until 1961, then Istanbul, Tunis and Vienna, at last Geneva); back in 1962; in 1968 «تعرضه للاختطاف والضرب وكاد يفقد حياته»; Yusuf al-Isa signing his poems «بدوي الجبل».
