# أَأَنكَرتَ داراً أَم تَناكَرتَ دَيّارا

## Recording and text

Khalid al-Sharafi recites Ihab al-Shabatat's 35 bayts in order. Alqimmah Studio credits both men on the opening card and in the description, and released the performance on 30 January 2024. The source runs 333.803 seconds. The edit keeps 2.85–330.45 seconds, from just before the voice to the end of the final cadence. Each of the 70 halves occurs once. [Studio recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oaB_Lsbvx6Y)

Al-Shabatat's entry on al-Diwan, under his account @eehabodeh, prints all 35 bayts. The initial direct request returned HTTP 403. An independent reader subsequently recovered the complete page, saved in `media/research/a-ankarta-daran/aldiwan-complete.txt`. The second witness is the text displayed as the studio recording proceeds, checked in three-second frames in `media/research/video/oaB_Lsbvx6Y/`. The local WebM transcript supplies the recording order. The complete authorial text was collated by word in `media/work/shabatat/a-ankarta-daran-collation.txt`. These digital publications have no printed page numbering. [Author's al-Diwan entry 118682](https://www.aldiwan.net/poem118682.html) · [Author's profile](https://www.aldiwan.net/user/eehabodeh)

The witnesses agree in verse count and words. The edition joins free-standing conjunctions and prepositions to their words, writes «بإذن» at 13.1 and restores the hamzat al-wasl in «اكتئاباً» at 22.2. The single mīm in «أُسميه» at 17.2 and the unusual «تَناجَدتُمُ» at 18.2 follow the studio and the author's text. The title is their incipit, including the doubled yāʾ in «دَيّار», an inhabitant.

## Scansion and interpretation

The metre is tawil with rāʾ as the rhyme consonant and a lengthened final a. The matched halves of bayt 1 run فعولن مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعيلن. In 1.2 the divisions are fi-las-ti / nu-lam-tun-kir / mi-nal-haq / qi-miʿ-sha-ra. The middle bayt 19.1 has ta-sa-wa / la-kum-fiʿ-lun / bi-waz-ni / ma-qa-li-kum, with قبض in the last two feet. The first-person poet, second-person listeners and quoted political speaker are kept distinct in the translations.

- **1, 4–8.** The Academy's dictionary supports the singular «دَيّار». A keffiyeh lets its wearer speak without words. The speaker in 5 boasts that normalisation will “work wonders”. Ibn Manzur records «أسحار» as a plural of both سِحر (magic) and سَحَر (the last hours of night). The verb «سنصنع» favours a feat of magic, as does al-Shabatat’s «أسحار بابل» in bayt 22 of «ألا يا زهور الدار». The alternative promise of new dawns is retained in both explanations. [Lisan al-Arab, سحر](https://sunna.alifta.gov.sa/BookToc/ViewServicePage?bookId=79&mainId=775625) · [Lisan al-Arab, second copy](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/122/3733/سحر) · [Al-Shabatat, «ألا يا زهور الدار», 22.1](https://www.aldiwan.net/poem118680.html) At about 72 s the studio identifies the white colonisers and the original inhabitants of America. The pronoun in «هنودهم» belongs to the possessive view of Indigenous people that the poem evokes. Both clip transcribers recover «هنودهم», and the acoustic comparison with «جنودهم» gives −70.08 and −78.92 respectively. The interpretation of the lām in 7.2 as that of the definite divine name «القهّار» is editorial. The studio prints «باللام» and supplies no further explanation of that letter. [Cairo Arabic Language Academy, ديّار](https://www.arabicacademy.gov.eg/ar/محرك-البحث/الديار) · [Quran 13.16](https://quran.com/13/16)
- **12–13.** The studio glosses «آلاء» as abundant blessings and «مسيح» both from the verb مسح and in the expression for a beautiful face. The simultaneous reference to Jesus, his restoring life by God's leave and the grammatical reversal «أحيا / أمات» are kept together. [Quran 38.41–44](https://quran.com/38/41-44) · [Quran 3.49](https://quran.com/3/49) · [Quranic Arabic Corpus, root of آلاء](https://corpus.quran.com/wordmorphology.jsp?location=%2855%3A13%3A2%29)
- **17–20.** «نثرها» means what the arms scatter and invites the contrast of prose with verse. «وزن» in 19 is both the measure of a deed and poetic metre. «كُل» and «صُم» in 20.1 are the imperatives “eat” and “fast”. Both witnesses join «وكل» to what follows. The translation follows the sense required by its pair «صم».
- **21–25.** The verse card at about 195 s reads «سُودَ تُرابِكم», the dark of the soil. The full-half score is −118.70 for «سود» and −142.46 for the intake recogniser’s «أسود». An independent Cohere clip explicitly vowels «سُودَ تُرابِكم». At 25 «حُرّ دم» gives nobility to the blood. The two expressions also keep distinct the red on the earth and the figurative blackening of the speaker's heart.
- **27, 30–31 and 33.** Studio glosses identify «حشا الترب» as the interior of the earth, «نثا» as announcing, «هفيف» as the breeze's sound, «معطار» as fragrant and «نجيس» as intensive. The complete al-Diwan entry and the studio agree on «وإن كال أظفارا» at 31.2. The imagined exchange is explicit. He would give his rhymes to bring the dead back. «أظفار» can denote nails or claws. Ibn Manzur also records a dark, nail-shaped aromatic used in incense under this plural, a possible link with the scent in bayt 30. Neither primary witness identifies what the measure holds, so the English retains the word azfar and explains both senses. [Lisan al-Arab, ظفر](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/122/5232/ظفر)
- **35.** The last verse crosses good and evil with wicked people and good people. The English takes good and evil as what the world has shown to the two groups. No gloss in either primary witness fixes the syntax.

The Arabic and English explanations are editorial readings of the poem, informed by those glosses. Neither the studio nor the author's page names a particular battle or a named individual as its occasion.

## Clip verification

The 327.60 s track measures −20.1 LUFS after a −7.2 dB gain. The first word starts at 0.48 s, and the last ends at 325.40 s. The independent half-by-half pass flagged 13 of 70 clips and 32 of 377 word tokens. There were no edge insertions. Wider Audar half-clips and full-bayt runs with both Cohere and Audar cover all flags and weak word alignments. Their transcripts and confidence values are in `media/work/oaB_Lsbvx6Y/clip-review.txt`, `flag-context-cohere/` and `flag-context-audar/` under the same work directory.
