- **Who sings:** nobody listened, so this rests on two measures. The aligned halves were run through a pitch tracker (librosa's pyin, 90–600 Hz): in abyat 3–9 and in the closing refrain it finds one clear voice (mean voicing probability 0.02–0.11, median pitch 200–345 Hz), and in the first three refrains and the first echo it finds none (0.010–0.014, the pitch falling to the bass at 92–97 Hz), as with several voices singing together. With the description's credit of a chorus, the first three refrains and the echo are the chorus's, abyat 3–9 al-Nufais's, and the closing refrain his (perhaps with the chorus behind him).

## Editions

- **al-Wahidi**, شرح ديوان المتنبي: shamela 754, pp. 170–171 (shamela's own numbering; `wahidi.txt`). All 9 abyat with commentary, under the heading «وقال وقد كبست انطاكية وقتل المهر الحجر» (so shamela prints it), after the rajaz poem on the horse. His text has «حَقيرٍ» in 2.1; for 4.1 he gives Ibn Jinni's «قربن» and reports «قُرين» and «قَرين».
- **al-Ukbari**, التبيان في شرح الديوان, ed. Mustafa al-Saqqa, Ibrahim al-Abyari and Abd al-Hafiz Shalabi (al-Halabi, 1936–38), vol. 4, pp. 119–120, poem 248: scan archive.org `20240104_20240104_1855`, leaves 1323–1324 (page images read; `ukbari.txt`), with the commentary also on shamela 6908. Fully voweled. Heading: «وقال: وقد كبست أنطاكية، فقتل مهره الذي وصفه والحِجر أمّه، وهي من الوافر والقافية من المتواتر». His text has «صَغيرٍ» (2.1), «مِثلُ» (7.2) and «القَريحَةِ» (9.2).
- **al-Barquqi**, شرح ديوان المتنبي (Hindawi reprint, 2014), p. 1253, with his notes 686–694 on pp. 1439–1440: scan archive.org `20201020_20201020_1638`, leaves 1252 and 1438–1439 (page images read; `barquqi.txt`). Fully voweled. Heading: «وقال، وقد كبست أنطاكية فقتل مهره الطخرور والحِجرُ أمه». His text has «صَغيرٍ» and «مِثلَ», and his notes give the three readings of 4.1 as Ibn Jinni's «قربن», «قُرين» (from القِرى) and «قَرين» (active).
- **al-Ma'arri**, اللامع العزيزي, ed. Muhammad Sa'id al-Mawlawi (2008), p. 1281 (shamela 29591/1273; `lami.txt`): abyat 1–4 and 9 with notes; meter «من الوافر الأول»; «حَقيرٍ». He passes on the report («يقال») that the poet lost a mare and a colt at Antioch in the days of his poem in ش for Abu al-Asha'ir.
- **معجز أحمد**, attributed to al-Ma'arri: shamela 753, pp. 192–193 (shamela's own numbering; `mujiz.txt`). All 9, heading «وكبست أنطاكية، فقتل المهر والحجرة فقال يندب مهره وفرسه»; «حَقيرٍ».
- **Anthologies:** al-Sahib ibn Abbad, الأمثال السائرة من شعر المتنبي, ed. Al Yasin (1965), pp. 34–35 (shamela 37460; `sahib.txt`), gives abyat 2 and 6–9 among the poet's proverbial lines, with «حَقيرٍ», «فَخرٌ» (6.1) and «الفُهومِ» (9.2); al-Raghib al-Isfahani, محاضرات الأدباء, vol. 1, p. 524 (shamela 9078/520), gives abyat 1–2 with «صَغيرٍ» under «تحمّل المكاره في نيل المكارم».
- **Starting text:** set from al-Barquqi and al-Ukbari, put in the house style.

## Commentary drawn on

- **Glosses and meaning** follow al-Wahidi throughout (غامرت, القَرَب and the reversal in 4, the swords' tears in 3, the reading of 7, Abu Tammam's answer in 8, القريحة), with al-Ukbari (مروم, الشجو, الصفائح, الصياقل, الكلوم, «تغني من الغَناء لا من الغِنى», القريحة as the first water of a well) and al-Barquqi (مخلصات «خالصات من الخبث», الآفة «العاهة» and its pronoun for the saying, «لا مثلَ» with the predicate left out, the readings of 4.1).
- **8:** al-Wahidi takes it as a saying on its own and traces it to Abu Tammam; the second reading, that the coward finds fault with courage because he does not know its worth, is the first of the two in معجز أحمد.
- **3:** شجوها in the accusative as a verbal noun (معجز أحمد).
- **The occasion** comes from the headings of al-Wahidi, al-Ukbari and al-Barquqi; the link to Abu al-Asha'ir from al-Lami' al-Azizi; Abu al-Asha'ir as the Hamdanid governor of Antioch from al-Barquqi's introduction, p. 36 (leaf 35).
