# لَو رَأَونا وَالهَوى ثالِثُنا

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Waaedh, 25 February 2022. The 53.333 s source begins with outdoor background sound. The first sustained voice is at about 1.9 s. The edit keeps 1.30–52.90 s, including the decay of «ملعبا», and runs 51.600 s. Four bayts, eight half-lines, each once. This is the passage from «روّعوه فتولّى مغضباً» beginning at the conditional «لو رأونا». The seaside belongs to the setting of the performance. The gazelle’s «سفح» in Shawqi’s text is a hillside. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ipkW5yDwuqM)

## Printed text and boundaries

- Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, Dar al-Awda, Beirut, 1988, part II, «باب النسيب», pp. 116–117. A rule separates the preceding poem from «وقال: روّعوه». Eight bayts are on p. 116 and eleven on p. 117. The next separate composition is expressly introduced as an adaptation from a Turkish poet. The recording has printed **5–8 of 19**. Verses 1–4 identify the meddlers and 9–19 continue from «لك ما أحببت من حبّته» to «ومُنحت الخلد ذكراً ونبأ». The printed footnote to 8 explains «أهل به» with «عمر». ## Readings, metre and check

At 2.2 both printed pages separate «بأن لا» and show a shadda on the nun. The first continuous transcript and the half-line verifier have «بألا», the independent Audar transcript «بأن لا». The spelling «بِأَلّا» represents the familiar contraction of أنْ and لا before the subjunctive يقرب. The middle foot in *nadh-ku-ruṣ-ṣub ∣ ḥa-bi-ʾal-lā ∣ yaq-ru-bā* is فَعِلاتن. Pronouncing the additional short vowel of a fully articulated *anna lā* would give that foot an extra syllable. The transcripts alone cannot distinguish a light nun from assimilation into lam. Both prints put the beloved’s «حفظ الحسنَ» beside the speaker’s first-person «صنتُ الأدبا» at 3.2. The unexpressed male subject and the object are kept distinct in both commentaries. The dual in «بردينا» supplies two cloaks.

Ramal, with ba as *rawiy* and an alif after it. The opening sadr scans *law raʾaw nā | wal ha wā thā | li thu nā* (فاعلاتن، فاعلاتن، فَعِلُن). The third sadr gives *milʾu bur day | nā ʿa fā fun | wa ha wā* (فاعلاتن، فاعلاتن، فَعِلُن). The corresponding ajuz scan *wad-du-jā-yur | khī-ʿa-lay-nal | ḥu-ju-bā* and *ḥa-fi-ẓal-ḥus | na-wa-ṣun-tul | ʾa-da-bā*. In the latter the first two feet are خبن forms of فاعلاتن. The opening clause continues across the caesura. In the last bayt «قَلْبِيَ السَّفْحُ» supplies the possessed noun, then its predicate. The yāʾ opens in liaison before the article.

The narrow verifier flagged **4.2** alone among eight halves. Before the final cue adjustment it gave «قلبي السفح واحلام العبث». On the adjusted cut it gave «قلبي السفح واحلام العباء». The widened Audar snippet ends «وآحنه ملعبا», and its continuous pass ends «وأحنا ملعبا». The first full take also has «وأحنى ملعبا». A separate equal-word comparison over the two aligned final words favours «وأحنى ملعبا» over the original narrow reading «وأحلام العبث» by 30.63 log-score units and the adjusted clip’s «وأحلام العباء» by 23.41. Both printed witnesses give the comparative followed by the accusative «ملعبا». The preceding half supplies the gazelle to which the heart offers shelter. The narrow output would also lose the bāʾ rhyme. No additional half was flagged.
