# على مثلها من أربع وملاعب

Osama al-Nahari performs all 45 bayts of the ode to Abu Dulaf al-Qasim ibn Isa al-Ijli. Al-Tabrizi and al-Suli both number it 15. The recitation moves from the abandoned camps (1–8) through the travellers and their camels (9–13), reaches Abu Dulaf by name at 14, and closes on the poet’s wish to be a giver himself. The two continuous transcriptions and both running commentaries establish the same order. There are 90 performed halves.

## Editions and interpretation

- Al-Tabrizi, *Sharh Diwan Abi Tammam*, ed. Muhammad Abduh Azzam, I, no. 15, pp. 198–215. Pp. 198–199 set the poem in tawil, explain the companion’s inexperience of separation and record the different openings of 4. P. 203 explains generosity’s childhood amulets in 15. Pp. 205–209 give the tribal names and the story of Hajib’s bow pledged to the Persian king. Pp. 210–212 take Abu Dulaf’s counsel at Arshaq back to the campaign against Babak and distinguish the edge of a sword from an edge of character. Pp. 213–215 explain the poet’s absence from court, the basins filled with praise, and the rain continually renewed by human minds. [I, pp. 198–215](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n242/mode/2up) · [I, pp. 210–211](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n254/mode/2up)
- Al-Suli, *Sharh al-Suli li-Diwan Abi Tammam*, ed. Khalaf Rashid Nu'man, I, no. 15, pp. 276–288. P. 276 interprets «قرحان» by someone who has escaped a disease. P. 279 connects the traveller’s diminutives «جذيل» and «عذيق» with the traditional boast of a man on whom others rely. Pp. 280–281 explain why the homes themselves want to ride out to meet the visitor. P. 282 identifies al-Hisn as Tha'laba ibn Ukaba and sets «عواص» opposite «قواض» across the caesura. P. 284 explains «أهابي» as a plural formed on an already plural word for dust. Pp. 285–288 distinguish the ways one absent from court may nevertheless be present there and preserve a different wording of the poet’s final wish. [I, pp. 276–288](https://archive.org/details/diwan-abo-tamam-shareh-alsouly/page/n276/mode/2up) · [I, pp. 282–285](https://archive.org/details/diwan-abo-tamam-shareh-alsouly/page/n282/mode/2up)
- Al-Amidi, *al-Muwazana*, I, p. 451, quotes 1 and records an objection to tears being both guarded and flowing. His answer distinguishes the time when they were guarded from the present moment when grief releases them. Ibn al-Athir, *al-Mathal al-sa'ir*, I, p. 264, quotes 26 with «صدّعوا» and explains the two occurrences of «صدور», the spearheads and the fronts of the battalions. Ibn Manzur, *Lisan al-Arab*, s.v. سحك, gives the root and the darkness of night behind «اسحنكك» in 32. [*Al-Muwazana*, I, p. 451](https://shamela.ws/book/26183/449) · [*Al-Mathal*, I, p. 264](https://shamela.ws/book/10551/266) · [*Lisan*, سحك](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/122/3739/سحك)

## Readings and sequence

At 2.2 the performed «بين الحشا» is al-Suli’s lemma. Al-Tabrizi prints «تحت الحشا» and reports «بين» in the apparatus. At 3.1 both prints give «فإنني», which supplies the last foot and appears in continuous Cohere. At 4.1 al-Tabrizi prints «وما صار في ذا اليوم», al-Suli «فما صار في ذا اليوم». Al-Tabrizi quotes the performed «وما زال يوم الدار عذلك كله» immediately under the lemma with «عذرك» in the second half. The reciter combines that opening with the lemma’s «جهلك». Both transcriptions support the latter, which also leads the test against «حلمك» and «عذرك». In 6.2, 7.1 and 8.2 the performance gives «السوارب», «البلى», «هواي». These are al-Tabrizi’s readings. Al-Suli’s lemmas have «السواكب», «الردى», «هواك», and his apparatus also records the readings used here. [Al-Tabrizi, pp. 198–201](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n242/mode/2up) · [al-Suli, pp. 276–278](https://archive.org/details/diwan-abo-tamam-shareh-alsouly/page/n276/mode/2up)

The person in 20 is an «آيب» who returns, the reading of al-Tabrizi. Al-Suli prints «آمل», someone who hopes. In 25.2 the continuously chunked Cohere transcript gives only «قواضب» after «بأسياف» and the Audar chunk stops at «بأسياف». The isolated 4.56 s Cohere clip returns «تصول بأسياف قواض قواضب». On the trimmed app clock «قواضٍ» is aligned to 222.88–223.44 s (word confidence 0.863), followed by «قواضب» at 223.70–225.10 s (0.750). The next half starts at 225.16 s. An unconstrained phone pass gives two successive sequences, q-a-w-a-d-i-n and q-a-w-a-d-i-b-i. Both commentaries print «قواض قواضب». The first adjective gives the swords power to decide, the second the power to cut. Each has its own card. In 26.1 al-Suli prints «صدموا», “they struck”. The comparison with «صدّعوا» is close. Both continuous transcripts give «صدعوا», the reading in al-Tabrizi and in Ibn al-Athir’s quotation. [Al-Tabrizi, pp. 205–207](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n249/mode/2up) · [al-Suli, p. 282](https://archive.org/details/diwan-abo-tamam-shareh-alsouly/page/n282/mode/1up)

At 35.1 al-Tabrizi’s lemma is «نضوت له رأيين سيفاً ومنصلاً». Al-Suli prints «نصلت لهم سيفين رأياً ومنصلاً» and gives «نضوت لهم رأيين» among the variants. In the recitation counsel is named first, then the two blades. Both continuous passes give this order and the equal-length test favours it by 39.51 points. At 36.2 the reciter has «رقاق المضارب» with al-Tabrizi. Al-Suli prints «رقاب» and records «رقاق» in his apparatus. At 38.1 al-Tabrizi discusses both the deed being remembered and the caliph himself remembering. Al-Suli prints «تنس تذكر». The two continuous passes hear an initial yāʾ in the answer. The active «يَذكُر» leads the passive third person by 4.99 points and fits the reference to the caliph’s heart in 37. An independent free-phone pass gives j-a-ð-k-o-r, with the vowels of the active. Both witnesses print jussive «تنس». Both also print «تمهّل» at 40.2. [Al-Tabrizi, pp. 211–214](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n255/mode/2up) · [al-Suli, pp. 284–285](https://archive.org/details/diwan-abo-tamam-shareh-alsouly/page/n284/mode/2up)

At 43.1 al-Suli reads «انثنت», “turned away”, and cites «انجلت», “cleared”, in the apparatus. Al-Tabrizi and both continuous passes give «انجلت». At 45.1 al-Suli prints «عاجلاً أن تردني» and records «أن ترد ركائبي». The latter is al-Tabrizi’s lemma and the audible sequence identified by both recognisers. His camels figure in the journey home just as they did in the approach to the patron. [Al-Tabrizi, pp. 214–215](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n258/mode/2up) · [al-Suli, pp. 287–288](https://archive.org/details/diwan-abo-tamam-shareh-alsouly/page/n287/mode/2up)

## Meter and rhyme

Tawil with bāʾ as rawiy, followed in performance by the lengthened kasra. At 1.1 «عَلَى مِثْ / لِهَا مِنْ أَرْ / بُعِنْ وَ / مَلَاعِبِي» gives فعولن مفاعيلن فعولُ مفاعلن. At 25.1 «يَمُدُّو / نَ مِنْ أَيْدِنْ / عَوَاصِنْ / عَوَاصِمِنْ» gives فعولن مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن.
