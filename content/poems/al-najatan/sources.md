Chapter 8 keeps **751.68–831.29 seconds of the original compilation** in one continuous cut. The source voice measurements place the first and last pitched sound of this chapter at approximately 752.26 and 829.68 seconds. The cut admits the entry and the decay of the final rhyme. The 816–825-second cards show both halves and the sweep over them a second time. The independent source-end transcript gives the last half of the first pass followed by both halves of the second. [p. 888](https://archive.org/details/alshawkeyat/page/n888/mode/1up) · [Electronic text](https://www.safahat.org/books/70706142/143/)

The first edition of *al-Shawqiyyat* (part I, Cairo, Matbaʿat al-Adab wa-l-Muʾayyad, 1898) provides an earlier text. On p. 182 the fat ewe boasts with «بأنها ذات الثمن». On p. 183 her sister turns it back on her in the call «يا ذات الثمن». The article is visible in both places in this printing. The performance makes the first occurrence indefinite and keeps the second definite. [p. 182](https://books.google.com/books?id=QIyl-Nj9R6UC&pg=RA1-PA182) · [p. 183](https://books.google.com/books?id=QIyl-Nj9R6UC&pg=RA1-PA183)

Each diwan gives the complete «النعجتان» on a single page. The consonant that distinguishes the two nouns in 5 is pointed in different places in the two printings. Beirut gives «الإذلال / الإدلال» and Hindawi «الإدلال / الإذلال». Humiliation is what the lean ewe endures and presumption is what her sister brings to bear on her. Their exchange in 9–10 turns the market value the fat sister prized into the explanation for the knife. The translation carries the lean ewe’s reply through the final maxim. Because the narrator introduces no further speaker, the close can also pass as his own comment on her patience.

The two printings transmit the same diwan tradition. The page reproductions and the word-by-word output from `scripts/collate.py` are in `media/research/al-najatan/`. The studio captions are performance evidence. The glosses and bilingual commentary are editorial close readings of the cited text.

## Metre and rhyme

Rhyming pairs in full rajaz. In bayt 1, *kā-na-li-baʿ | ḍin-nā-si-naʿ | ja-tā-nī* gives **– ⏑ ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | ⏑ – –**. *Wa-kā-na-tā | fil-ghay-ṭi-tar | ʿa-yā-nī* gives **⏑ – ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | ⏑ – –**. In 6, *ḥat-tā-a-tal | jaz-zā-ru-dhā | ta-yaw-mī* gives **– – ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | ⏑ – –**. *Wa-qal-la-ban | naʿ-ja-ta-dū | nal-qaw-mī* gives **⏑ – ⏑ – | – ⏑ ⏑ – | – – –**. The contracted final foot occurs in both forms. The pairings change from the dual in نعجتان/ترعيان to other sounds, including يوم/القوم and الإذلال/الإدلال. There is no single rawiy.

## Readings against the performance

At 3.2 each printed page gives «ذات الثُّمَن», with the article and a ḍamma over the thāʾ. In the lean ewe’s answer at 10.2 both give the familiar «الثَّمَن». The free phoneme trace at 770.75–771.19 seconds runs directly from the u of «ذاتُ» into θ, an open vowel and m. The 768-second studio card, both full takes and the 762–788-second window give «ذات ثمن». The indefinite reading leads by about 11.9 against definite «الثَّمَن» and by 16.71 against the actual printed pointing «الثُّمَن». The indefinite noun is idiomatic for an animal worth money, and in 10 the sister pointedly calls her «يا ذات الثمن». At 5 the 780-second card and the contextual Cohere window support the Beirut order. Audar’s long take writes «الإذلال» in both slots, losing the internal one-letter echo. The comparison is tied to within 0.12 in 5.1 and favours «الإدلال» by 8.00 in 5.2. The free phoneme trace has /ð/ in the first noun at 780.23 seconds and /d/ in the second at 783.37. That contrast independently supports «الإذلال / الإدلال». The text and cards preserve the distinction that makes the two halves answer each other.
