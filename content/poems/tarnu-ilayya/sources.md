## Identification and extent

Hamza al-Malik Tumbul, *Diwan al-tabi'a*, part I, in *al-Adab al-sudani wa-ma yajibu an yakuna alayhi wa-Diwan al-tabi'a*, with an introduction by Muhammad Ibrahim al-Shush (Sudanese National Council for Arts and Letters, Beirut, 1972). The diwan title leaf dates its first edition to **1931** and this second edition to **1972**. «في جوف الليل» is the heading on printed p. 190. «مولاي قد نامت عيون» is its incipit. The two pages contain **15 + 16 = 31** verses. The recording takes only original 3 and 4. Print 1–2 establish who is awake, the eyes of the stars, and 5–31 turn from those eyes to human strife, sleep and suffering. The fourth printed verse is sung twice in full: app order 1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 2.2, 2.1, 2.2. Keep the whole **0–32.725 s**. The final /n/ is still present at 32.60 s. ## Recording against print

- **1.1, original 3.** The book and concordance have «ترنو إلينا وهي سا / هية عن الدنيا الخؤون». Both full passes hear a singular first person. Cohere spells it «إلي» and Audar «إليا». With the same 0.32–3.15 s comparison window, «إليَّ» scores −13.42 and «إلينا» −17.75. The difference is 4.33, below the five-point threshold. The two transcripts and the distinct address to the watching speaker tip the decision towards **«إليَّ»**. Both candidates are grammatical.
- **1.1–1.2.** Both continuous passes write «سالية». The phone evidence shows /l/ at about 6.00 s. «سالية» scores −18.69 against −24.67 for printed «ساهية». The former is someone whose mind or heart has turned away, the latter someone inattentive. The app therefore divides «سا / لِيَةٌ» at the printed metrical break and translates the joined word. «عن» and «على» score within 0.61. The print, Cohere, and the construction with «سالية عن» support «عن». «الخؤون» agrees with the print and the long u and final nun. The vowel and orthographic variants here score within two points.
- **2.1–2.2, original 4, both occurrences.** The print asks which of two things distracted the stars: divine majesty or the passing of centuries. The halves divide «جَلا / لُ». Full Cohere repeatedly joins «أم مرّ» into «أمر», while full Audar recognises the alternative in its first pass through the verse. Constrained scoring of the shorter sequence «أمر القرون» against the two words strongly favours the contraction, an unequal-word-count comparison. The second occurrence gives a distinct /m/ followed by the onset of «مَرّ» around 30.5–30.9 s, and the edition supplies two grammatical subjects for «أذهل». The translation preserves the break inside the divine title.

Majzu' al-kamil, two feet per half with recurrent idmar. In the original opening «مَوْلايَ قَدْ / نامَتْ عُيُونْ» gives – – ∪ – twice. In original 18 «مَنْ ذَا يَرَى / هَذَا الْجَلَا» gives the same two feet. In the recited 2.1 «أَتُرَاهُ أَذْ / هَلَهَا جَلَا» gives ∪ ∪ – ∪ – twice. The printed break runs through the nouns and adjectives, while the voice carries them across it. Nun is the rawiy. The ending is generally heard in pause.

## Earlier verification pass

**1.2, 5.98–11.54 s.** On the earlier text «هِيَةٌ عَنِ الدُّنيا الخَؤونِ», isolated Cohere returned «مليه عن الدنيا الخون». The initial log and `verification-final/verify-before.log` both flag that opening fragment. The final transcript actually spells «وَالْيَهُونَ عَنِ الدُّنْيَا الْخَوْنِ». Its first word reduces to the same consonants as «لِيَةٌ» under the verifier’s removal of weak letters and final nun. The final comparison therefore clears it. The positive evidence for joining «سا / لِيَةٌ» is the /l/ at 6.00 s, the two continuous passes and the comparison with printed «ساهية» above.
