# مَنهَجُ الحَقِّ

## Recording and text

Osama al-Waaedh, «منظومة منهج الحق», Alqimmah Studio, 1 August 2025. The studio credits Husayn al-Sada for language review. The reciter reads all 65 bayts in order, once each. The source video lasts 525.131 seconds. The opening and closing silences extend to 3.646 and from 522.511 seconds. The app retains 3.10–523.10 seconds (520.00 seconds), with −7.0 dB gain, −20.0 LUFS integrated and −6.5 dBTP peak. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_UtOvqzfi8M)

- **Abd al-Razzaq ibn Abd al-Muhsin al-Badr, شرح منهج الحق**, introduction dated Medina, Sha'ban 1432 AH, pp. 3–4, continuous numbered verse on pp. 5–9, followed by his exposition. The editor describes two manuscript copies. An unnamed pupil copied the complete manuscript in 1345 AH. The other copy lacks 23, 27, 43 and 50–62 and changes the order in places. The title comes from the opening. Al-Badr also notes that the poet already used fourteen verses on remembrance in his commentary on another poem, completed in 1333 AH. The 1345 manuscript date therefore cannot date composition. [Edition](https://d1.islamhouse.com/data/ar/ih_books/single5/ar_Explain_the_right_approach.pdf)
- **Sulayman ibn Muhammad al-Wabisi, التؤدة في التعليق على منهج الحق**, first ed., 1444 AH / 2022, complete verse pp. 3–7, then bayt-by-bayt discussion. Its printed pages and al-Badr's were consulted together. They retain the distinctive repeated «وكلّ جميع الخلق» at 6 and 10, «وقهره» at 11, and «ومعه» at 57. At 38, 41 and 50 the shortened forms الريا، الرجا، الشقا retain the poem's long final vowel without a further hamza. At 48 the rhythm compresses طُرُق to طُرْق. Both printed editions give «في كلّ حالة» at 49.1. [Edition](https://saaid.org/book/22/18974.pdf)
- The independent spoken sequence and the studio's verse cards were checked against the two books. The vocalised Wikisource transcription was run through the repository's bayt collator. It has 65 bayts in the same order but drops «فما» before خاب at 63.2, present in both prints and the performance. The aligned recording gives «فما» a separate word interval. Since the rival text omits an entire word, a forced score comparing different numbers of words would favour the shorter candidate. [Electronic comparator](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/منهج_الحق)

## Attribution and reading

Al-Badr and al-Wabisi identify the poet as Abd al-Rahman ibn Nasir al-Sa'di. The poem is distinct from his shorter منظومة السير إلى الله والدار الآخرة. Its long measure can be heard at the opening and at the central passage on the signs of creation. In 1.1 the sounds divide as فَيا سا | ئِلَن عَن مَن | هَجِل حَقْ | قِ يَبتَغي, فعولن مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. The consonant on which every rhyme turns is dal. Al-Badr and al-Wabisi supply the doctrinal and ethical context for the Arabic and English explanations.

The signs in the earth and the self at 33–34 recall Qur'an 51:20–21. Taking what comes readily from people's character at 46 recalls 7:199. Qur'an 17:44 and 2:152 illuminate the praise of created things and God's remembrance of those who remember him. For 16 see al-Bukhari 1145 and Muslim 758. For 52–58 see Muslim 2676 on the men and women who remember God and go ahead, Abu Dawud 1522 on the prayer taught to Mu'adh, al-Tirmidhi 3375 on keeping the tongue moist and 3462 on planting in Paradise, Muslim 2675 on God's remembrance, and Muslim 2835 on the inhabitants of Paradise breathing praise. The two sets of sharh and the translation are written for this reading. [Bukhari 1145](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1145) · [Muslim 2676](https://sunnah.com/muslim:2676) · [Abu Dawud 1522](https://sunnah.com/abudawud:1522) · [Tirmidhi 3375](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3375) · [Tirmidhi 3462](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:3462)

For the poet, al-Zirikli's **الأعلام**, vol. 3, p. 340, gives the full name, his work in Unayza, the library founded in 1358, and the paired death years 1376 AH / 1956. The catalogue's paired years follow that entry. His family dates his death to 23 Jumada II 1376 AH, which the biographical study by Markaz Tafsir maps to **24 January 1957**. [Al-A'lam](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/2932) · [Family biography](https://ketabonline.com/ar/books/98403/read?index=2935818&page=10&part=1) · [Markaz Tafsir study](https://api.tafsiroqs.com/storage/attachments/pdfs/2022-11/31941-1669546387-63834193d385e3.85227222-alrhmn-bn-nasr-a.pdf)

## Independent half-verse check

The recogniser transcribed all 130 aligned halves again in isolation. Seven carry word differences. The intake transcript runs through the whole recording and the displayed verses and the two editions provide separate checks.

| Half | Difference and decision |
| --- | --- |
| 28.1 | The short transcript adds a final ه to حَوَت. The two books and the displayed حوت carry the subject forward to ممالكه in the second half. |
| 33.1 | The isolated clip hallucinates the introduction of a different speaker, «لفضيلة الدكتور محمد راتب النابلسي». All six expected words align at 253.66–258.14 of the app audio, and the continuous pass plainly transcribes the words of the bayt. |
| 44.2, 46.2 | It supplies an object pronoun after the same closing verb, ك in the first place and ه in the second. Both descriptions end ويرشد in the books and cards. The same verb with the same rhyme is heard again at 64.2. |
| 55.1 | «بالّا» is the connected pronunciation of the written بأن لا. The prints and the displayed verse separate the two particles. |
| 56.1 | It makes «أرسل» out of غرسٌ. The two printed explanations explicitly identify remembrance as the planting and جنات عدن as its location, and the studio displays غرس. |
| 58.1 | It gives بجنته for بجنّةٍ, treating the pronounced final t of the indefinite noun as a possessive. The printed verse agrees with the continuous pass. |
| 21.2, 48.2 | The isolated clips insert يا and أوه at their edges. The continuous pass joins عندنا معاشر and تقدّموا إلى without an added word. At 21 the last word in the first half ends at 162.76 and معاشر begins at 163.26. At 48 the last syllable of تقدّموا ends at 378.28 and إلى begins at 378.30. The transcript has misidentified a held vowel or a breath in the tight cut. |

A separate wider Audar pass transcribed both sides of 21 and 48 without the two edge words and recovered the expected Qur'anic wording at 33. Both recognisers still had difficulty with the consonants of غرس at 56, though the next half's gardens of Eden were clear. The word is printed and glossed by both commentators.
