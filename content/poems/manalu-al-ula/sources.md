- **12.1, 42.2:** the reciter departs from the edited text for readings the manuscripts have. At 12.1 both transcribers and the full transcript hear «أصبح الحي»; the aligner puts «صَبَّحَ» ahead by 0.7, a coin toss (and «صبّح» has a letter fewer), and prefers «الحَيُّ» to «الحَيَّ» by 1.0, which the grammar wants (أصبح is intransitive: when the camp wakes). At 42.2 both transcribers and the full transcript hear «وهيب», and the aligner agrees (+7.7 over «وَهِنبِ»); al-Khatib's ك and ح have «وُهَيب», which he calls an error (n. 4, p. 115), and his vowel «وُ» is kept (+0.7 over «وَهَيبِ»). Notes give the editions' readings. With «وُهَيبِ» the list runs on without «و», as the meter requires (with «وَوُهَيب» the ajuz would have a syllable too many).
- **5.1, 19.1, 30.1, 39.1, 46.1:** the transcribers hear «بالغ», «مانعت», «حاشدوا», «خاضعت», «عادمت»: each is the third syllable of a first foot shortened to فعولُ, which this reciter stretches throughout. The editions' short vowels stay (at 5.1 the aligner also prefers «بَلَغَ» by 10.3).

## Commentary drawn on

- **The occasion** (date, the taking of al-Ahsa, «أول شعره بعد قصيدتين», the amir's offer and the poet's answer) is the heading in al-Janbi, pp. 236–241; the anecdote of the answer is only in the Berlin, Russian and Tehran manuscripts (his n. 318). His notes on the amir and his reign, pp. 237–239 (nn. 313–314), and on the Syrian campaign, p. 258 (n. 339: «مؤرخو العراق قد أهملوا ذكرها»).
- **Glosses** follow the old commentary as al-Janbi prints it: العلا, المرهفات, القواضب, سمر العوالي, العتاق, الشوازب, النقع, المصاعب, الهام, الهول, المقدام, طروق الأغاني, الحرّة and قلّة الفكر في العواقب (5), المدى, جمّ الندى, السرى, جوب الأرض, العيس, جوز, السباسب, النجيع, الكتائب, العوالي, المَلْك, شنّ الغارة, الأروع, الجُلّى, بعيد المطالب (11), الصبوح, رقاق المضارب, الحيّ, حماها (13), جزت الأرض, أركان الأرض and المناكب (14, both readings), الكاعب, السلاهب and the meaning of 18, العسّال and الأبيض and القاضب, the meaning of 20, جوز الطريق and the story of 21, the meaning of 22, شعواء, الفيافي, اللبث and الفواق, السواغب, الترائب and ضاحكات, القلاص, الشلّ, البيد, عبدلي, التوالي, الرغائب, هيهات, المناقب, الضرغام, الثواقب, الأغلب, الغيهب, the meaning of 40–41, عاد and جرهم and هنب (42), أمير المؤمنين (43), ناعب (44).
- **The battles and people:** Banu Malik of Tayyi' and their end in 607 (15), Ghuzayya and al-Muntafiq, Dahmash ibn Sanad ibn Ajwad and Nasir ibn Madhkur (21, 26–27), the amirs of the Syrian Arabs, their march and their plea of kinship (29–30, 37), all from the commentary (al-Janbi, pp. 245–262). Ghaziyya's place in Tayyi' and the vowels of its name: al-Janbi n. 328 (Ibn al-Kalbi, Ibn al-Athir, al-Qalqashandi). Al-Muntafiq as a clan of Uqayl: al-Janbi n. 328, discussing a report of Ibn al-Jawzi. Abd Allah ibn Ali for «عبدلي»: the commentary; al-Janbi n. 340 takes him to be Abd Allah ibn Ali al-Shaykh, grandfather of the founder of the same name, in whom the poet's and the amir's lines meet.
- **Other readings mentioned in the sharh and notes:** 12 «صبّح الحيّ» and 42 «وهنب» (the editions' text); 14, the two senses of «المناكب» (the commentary); 45 «فقرّ» (al-Janbi n. 345, the Umm al-Qura copy).
- **The poet's life:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 5, p. 24 (shamela 12286/4030–4031, `alam.txt`); «بن ضبار» in the full name from al-Janbi's heading of the diwan; the place of counsel to the Uyunid amirs in his verse from the preface to al-Khatib's edition, p. 3.

## Review

The drafter checked the draft against the scans first, without a second reader. A second reader then read every half against al-Janbi's pages (rendered as images), al-Khatib's leaves and the lithograph, scanned every half as tawil, scored the doubtful vowels on the app audio with a control for vowel bias, and checked the sharh, notes, intro and bio against the commentary, al-Janbi's notes, al-Khatib's preface and *al-A'lam* (2026-11-17). Fixed after the review:

- **Text:** 4.2 «طُروقَ … عِناقَ», al-Khatib's accusative, which the reciter says (+10.7), for al-Janbi's nominative; 19.1 «غَزِيَّةُ», al-Janbi's vowels and al-Qalqashandi's, which the reciter says (+9.1), for «غُزَيَّةُ», a departure no edition has.
- **Notes:** 31.2 «عَبدَلِيٌّ» credited as al-Janbi n. 340 has it: Abd Allah ibn Ali al-Shaykh, the founder's grandfather, in whom the poet's and the amir's lines meet, not the founder; 4.2 gives al-Janbi's nominative; 24.1 gives al-Khatib's «فَواقاً»; 18.1 reworded so it no longer repeats the sharh; the name of the tribe in 19.1 and its English notes.
- **Sharh:** 42 names «وهيب بن أفصى», as the text has it, for «بني أفصى»; 18 «weapon», as the commentary says, for «armour»; 32 (Arabic), 12 and 24 (English) no longer repeat their notes; 25 the women left to him, not to his horses; the English sharh of 39 and 47 deepened.
- **English:** Ghaziyya for Ghuzayya throughout (intro, translation, sharh, notes).
- **sources.md:** 4.2 credited (al-Khatib's accusative against al-Janbi's nominative, not both editions alike); 19.1 and 24.1 added to the readings; the 18.1 qabd recorded.
