# معلقة عبيد بن الأبرص

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari, «أقفر من أهله ملحوب». The 289.259 s original has initial silence through 3.107 s and final silence from 283.258 s. Keep 2.61–283.76 s (281.15 s) around 45 distinct verses and their 90 successive halves. The video prints a running text with glosses, in places expressly correcting its own singer. The contiguous sound transcript and the half-by-half checks determine the performed sequence. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=laxPHpIqjbY)

## Textual witnesses

- Ashraf Ahmad Adra, *Diwan Ubayd ibn al-Abras*, Dar al-Kitab al-Arabi, 1994, poem 1, **pp. 19–26**. The printed verses are explicitly numbered to 50, followed on p. 26 by the next poem. This supplies the catalogue count. The recording gives **1–5, 7–18, 20–24, 27–43, 46, 45, 44, 47, 48, 50**. Entirely omitted as independent verses are **6** «إما قتيلاً وإما هالكاً», **19** «لا يعظ الناس…», **25** «بالله يدرك كل خير», **26** «والله ليس له شريك», and **49** «فعاودته فرفعته». The second half of 6 recurs in the grey-hair verse, print 28 / recorded 24. Adra 9–10 and recorded 8–9 put the pouring and sounding of water in opposite order. His p. 21 notes that witnesses often exchange the two ajuz. At 24 the singer has «علتني ذرأة» where Adra 28 has «علتني كبرة». The last pursuit changes order as well as wording. The catalogue's basit family here means **مخلّع البسيط**. Its lengths vary. The opening «أَقْفَرَ مِنْ | أَهْلِهِي | مَلْحُوبُو» yields long-short-short-long, long-short-long, long-long-long. A regular comparison is 35a «كَأَنَّها | لِقْوَةٌ | طَلُوبُو», متفعلن فاعلن فعولن. In 17 the classical midline falls within «بالـ / ضعف», a verse with *tadwir*. The translation and Arabic gloss give the one word its full meaning.

Osama al-Nahari publishes this and the Mu‘allaqa of al-Nabigha under his own name. These two performances and the on-screen explanations in the Ubayd recording supply the examples in his biographical note. ## Other readings checked against the voice

The comparisons use the same number of words on the aligned recording. The numerical differences report the model's preference. Where two spellings score alike, the witnesses and the complete utterance decide the reading.

- **1.2–4.1.** The single and doubled ṭ in «القطبيات» score alike. The text follows al-Tibrizi's single ṭ. «ثُعالِبات» leads his alternative «ثُعَيلِبات» by 8.89. At 4.1 both full readings give «أن بدلت أهلها». The passive «أَن بُدِّلت» leads «إِن بُدِلت» by 3.83 and the active «أَن بَدَّلت» by 1.90. The people's place is taken by wild animals. [Al-Tibrizi, p. 324](https://shamela.ws/book/37488/323) · [Lyall, Arabic p. 5 and apparatus](https://archive.org/details/diwansofabidibna21abiduoft/page/n351/mode/1up)
- **14.2–20.2.** «سَلَب» (spoil) leads «سالب» by 6.89 and completes the wordplay with «مسلوب». At 16.1 «رِحم» leads «رَحِم» by 8.68. At 17.1 the imperative «أفلِح» leads the past «أفلَح» by 2.95 and al-Tibrizi's variant «أفلج» by 5.03. Both full transcriptions preserve «إنّني» in 20.2, where the shorter «إنّي» is only 1.56 behind. [Al-Tibrizi, pp. 326–328](https://shamela.ws/book/37488/325)
