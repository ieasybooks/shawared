# الغراء

## Recording and named text

- Osama Al-Waaedh, Alqimmah Studio, 11 August 2026. The opening couplet is sung twice, followed by all the rest of the poem in order. The rendered audio keeps 0.42–367.00 s of the source and lasts 366.58 s. The only cuts are the head and tail silence. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T7ViZ9l9EIo)
- Fawwaz ibn Hayif ibn Jurays al-Dawsari, *Al-Gharra, nazm maghazi wa-saraya khayr al-anbiya'*. The author signs the introduction on printed p. 3, 27 Safar 1447 AH, 21 August 2025. The complete text is pp. 4–8, numbered 1–58. The event identifications in the margins helped distinguish people bearing the same name, particularly the two men called Sa'd. The text was read from the images in `media/research/al-gharra/`. No second edited text of the contemporary poem was identified. [Author’s copy linked by the studio](https://drive.google.com/file/d/17neBLiVBMW9edvbPLK6HWrdNQGDgBKub/view)
- Paired rajaz has a new rhyme in each couplet. The opening اَلْحَمْدُ لِلَّاهِ الَّذِي تَجَلَّى gives – – ⏑ – / – – ⏑ – / ⏑ – –. In 31.1 في رابعٍ ذاتُ الرقاعِ أُرّخت the feet are – – ⏑ – / – – ⏑ – / ⏑ – ⏑ –. سيف البحر and آي الحشر end on consonant groups in pause. The house checker’s warnings on their final two bare letters reflect those pauses.

## Historical sources

- Ibn Ishaq, transmitted in Ibn Hisham’s *al-Sira al-nabawiyya*, the sections for the named expeditions from Waddan to Tabuk and the closing inventory of expeditions and detachments. Al-Waqidi’s *Kitab al-maghazi* and Ibn Sa'd’s *al-Tabaqat al-kubra*, volume 2, provide independent comparanda for the places, dates and identifications. The chronology as sung includes disputed dates, especially Dhat al-Riqa' at 31 and Banu al-Mustaliq at 38. In 40 only Ibn Awf’s destination is Dumat al-Jandal. [Ibn Hisham](https://islamwww.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/siratibnhsham.pdf), [al-Waqidi](https://shamela.org/gen/a1adcfa94c2a91227dca606e13321d7e_gen.pdf), and [Ibn Sa'd on Ibn Awf](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/17772/68?lang=ar)
- The stories concerning Asma bint Marwan and Abu Afak in 20 circulate with weak transmission. The commentary records their status. In 25 the poet turns a story about a particular community into a demeaning generalisation. The half, translation and explanatory card distinguish his polemic from anything that can be inferred about a people. See the accounts of the two deaths in al-Waqidi and Ibn Sa'd and the juxtaposition in Ibn al-Jawzi’s *al-Muntazam*. [Ibn al-Jawzi and references to the older accounts](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4631/135?lang=ar)
- The commentary follows Qur’an 2.217 for Nakhla, 8.9 and 8.41 for Badr, 3.152 for Uhud, 59.2 for the departure of Banu al-Nadir, 4.102 for prayer in danger, 33.9 for the siege, 24.11 onward for the accusation against Aisha, 48.1 for Hudaybiya and 9.25–26 for Hunayn. The report of the great fish is Jabir’s account of Abu Ubayda’s expedition, and Sa'id al-Maqburi transmits Abu Hurayra’s report about Thumama ibn Uthal. [Sahih al-Bukhari, the expedition to the seashore](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4360) and [Thumama’s account](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:4372)

## Verification by half and decisions

All 118 sung halves, including both occurrences of the opening, were clipped and transcribed with Cohere. The first pass flagged 21 halves (32 of 528 words). After the recorded singular راية was restored at 5.2 and 7.1, a fresh transcription cleared both halves. The final pass flags 19 halves (30 of 528 words), plus a fragment at the end of 17.1. Wide clips of the disputed readings were also transcribed with Audar. Scores below are total log probabilities from the same MMS candidate-scoring calculation as `scripts/variants.py`; a difference near zero has little evidential value. The local score sheet, wide clips and second transcripts are under `media/work/didactic/al-gharra*`.
