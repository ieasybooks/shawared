- **Rabih Farruji**, *al-Tajriba al-shi'riyya fi shi'r Badawi al-Jabal* (doctoral thesis, Université Ferhat Abbas, Setif, 2011–2012): archive.org `lis01738`, pp. 126, 212 and 215–216 (leaves 129, 215, 218–219), read from the page images. He quotes abyat 1, 2, 3, 11, 12, 13 and 16 from the diwan, pp. 542–544, and has two slips of his own (16.1 «زحم» for «زخم», 1.2 «عنّي» for «منّي» in the second refrain). Sulayman (p. 19) reports that Hashim Uthman charges Zu'aytir with changing titles, adding, dropping and altering abyat and changing words between the first diwan and the 1978 one, and answers that the poet revised his own poems, «وهو أمر معروف فيما بين النشر في دورية والنشر في ديوان», the diwan having appeared three years before his death. The differences between al-Irfan and the diwan in this poem are of that kind; whose they are, no source says.

## Title and attribution

- **Title:** «دموع ودموع», the poet's own heading from its first printing (al-Irfan, 1923, and its index) to the diwan, and the name Farruji (pp. 212, 215), Sulayman (p. 20) and poetsgate use; as with the Shawqiyyat's heading «نكبة دمشق» for `nakbat-dimashq`. The upload calls it «غن يا بلبل» and qafiyah lists it by its first sadr, «غَنِّ يا بُلبُلُ فَوقَ الدَّوحِ غَنِّ», which would be the title if the heading were not the name the books use. The English title translates the heading.
- **Attribution:** al-Irfan prints it in December 1923 signed «بدوي الجبل»; the poet's own diwan, published in his lifetime (Sulayman, p. 19, notes it appeared three years before his death), prints it; Sulayman lists «دموع ودموع: ١٩٢٥» among the dated poems of the first diwan (p. 20); Farruji discusses it as Badawi al-Jabal's (p. 215). No source gives it to anyone else.
- **Date:** the diwan's «(١٩٢٥)», which Sulayman repeats, is later than the poem's printing in al-Irfan's issue of December 1923 (Jumada al-Ula 1342); it may be the year of the first diwan, «ديوان بدوي الجبل – الشفق», which al-Irfan's own press in Sidon printed in 1925 (Sulayman, p. 12), or of a revision; no source says. In the poem the speaker is nineteen («في التسع بعد العاشرة», 31.2), and the same issue of al-Irfan calls the poet «فتى في التاسعة عشرة من سنيه» (p. 262): both put his birth about 1904, not the 1905 of *Tatimmat al-A'lam* and *al-Mawsu'a al-Arabiyya* (see The poet).

- **2.1 «مِثلَ».** The diwan prints «مِثْلُ», an adjective of «سِحرٌ». The reciter says «مِثلَ» by 7.9 on the half and 6.2 on the two words, and the vowel after the lam (8.50–8.60 s) has F1 450–500 Hz and F2 about 1500 Hz: his fatha in «مِثلَ حُزني» (2.2, 12.76–12.84 s) has F1 500–610 Hz and F2 1270–1410 Hz, and a damma would have F2 near 800–1000 Hz. The text follows him; «مِثلَ» reads as a hal, the meaning unchanged. The note gives the diwan's reading.
- **17.1 «عَقِلَت».** The diwan prints the passive «عُقِلَت», "were hobbled", which the sense needs (its subject is «عِتاقُ السّابِقات»). ## The meter

Ramal, three feet a half (فاعلاتن فاعلاتن فاعلن). `scan.py` passes every half; the seven it marks are the second stanza's ajuz endings, where the rhyme «ـاتْ» ends in a consonant (it shows that consonant as X). Each stanza has its own rhyme and its own last foot (darb), as strophic poems do:

- bayt 1, the first stanza and the refrain, on «ـنّي»: darb فاعلاتن (or فعلاتن), and the opening sadr ends the same way by tasri' («غَنِّ», -u- -);

## The poet

- **Name, dates, life:** Muhammad Khayr Ramadan Yusuf, *Tatimmat al-A'lam*, vol. 2, p. 164 (shamela 133395/483, `tatimma.txt`): «محمد سليمان الأحمد (١٣٢٣ - ١٤٠١ هـ- ١٩٠٥ - ١٩٨١ م) الشاعر المعروف ببدوي الجبل»; the name given by Yusuf al-Isa of «ألف باء» because «إن الناس يقرؤون للشعراء المعروفين، وهذا التوقيع المستعار يحملهم على قراءة- الشعر للشعر»; the ministries of 1954–1956; leaving Syria in 1956, Beirut to 1958, Istanbul and other countries, back in 1963; «وفي آخر حياته رقد سنة كاملة في المستشفى العسكري بدمشق عاجزا عن الحركة»; the Dar al-Awda diwan. It gives his birthplace as «ريغا» near Latakia and his death as «يوم الأربعاء ١٨ آب (أغسطس)»; neither is used. The same text is in his *Takmilat Mu'jam al-Mu'allifin*, p. 492 (shamela 36031/1456–1457).
- *al-Mawsu'a al-Arabiyya* (Damascus), vol. 4 (2001), p. 772, «بدوي الجبل», by Ihsan al-Nass (arab-ency.com.sy/details/4243, `arabency.txt`): 1905–1981; born in «قرية «ديفة» من أعمال منطقة الحفة في محافظة اللاذقية»; his father «من علماء اللغة المتمرسين بها ومن الفقهاء المتعمقين»; imprisoned after the French took Damascus (Hama, a year in Beirut, then Latakia) and later eight months in the citadel of Kassab; «وانتخب سنة 1937 نائباً في المجلس النيابي ثم تكرر انتخابه مرات»; fled to Iraq in 1939; minister of health in 1954 in Sabri al-Asali's cabinet, then of «الدعاية والأنباء»; «فارق الوطن سنة 1956» (Beirut until 1961, then Istanbul, Tunis and Vienna, at last Geneva); back in 1962; in 1968 «تعرضه للاختطاف والضرب وكاد يفقد حياته»; Yusuf al-Isa signing his poems «بدوي الجبل».
