# Amr ibn al-Ahtam’s winter guest

## Recording and extent

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed’s source runs 231.201 seconds. The spoken attribution occupies 24.065–28.801, and the opening verse begins at 29.964. The keep is 29.46–216.43, just beyond the last sung word at 215.916. This removes the introduction and the closing tail. The numbered studio cards and both full transcripts give 23 verses in succession, each once. Verse 8 ends with the lightning, «وبروق», whose verb «تألق» opens verse 9. The audible caesuras and the change of card preserve both poetic lines even though the sentence crosses the rhyme. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dqACkwZjn5s)

Al-Mufaddal al-Dabbi, *al-Mufaddaliyyat*, eds. Ahmad Muhammad Shakir and Abd al-Salam Harun, no. 23, pp. 125–127, gives the complete performed sequence. Ibn al-Anbari’s commentary in Charles Lyall’s Arabic edition, *The Mufaḍḍalīyāt*, I, no. XXIII, pp. 245–254, explains the scene verse by verse, including the guest who calls for a dog to answer him, the rain beneath the cloud, and the animals sheltering behind the pale dam. Lyall’s English volume, II, no. XXIII, pp. 83–86, identifies the maternal lines at the close. It also preserves two interpretations of the fine, smooth covering in 19, a bed-cover and the skin on fresh milk. The app translates the bedding, prompted by the cold east wind and the immediately preceding blanket. The commentary records the other reading. The half-by-half translations are original. [Shakir and Harun, print](https://archive.org/details/20240818_20240818_1710/page/n124/mode/2up) · [Electronic text](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/99) · [Lyall’s Arabic volume, p. 245](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatant01mufauoft/page/245/mode/2up) · [Lyall, English volume](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatanth00mufauoft/page/n121/mode/2up)

Ibn Hamdun’s *al-Tadhkira al-Hamduniyya*, II, no. 690, pp. 264–265, gives 21. It omits our 3 and 9, moves 6 ahead of 5, and places 10 after 20. Its merciful welcome, «فهذا مبيت صالح وصديق», offers a good lodging in place of the present morning drink. Al-Tabrizi’s *Sharh Diwan al-Hamasa*, II, pp. 300–301, quotes a smaller selection concerned with generosity. *Al-Hamasa al-basriyya*, II, p. 237, preserves «وضاحكته من قبل عرفاني اسمه», laughing with the stranger before asking his name. The full Mufaddaliyyat and this recitation go directly from the welcome to the camels. Al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, V, p. 78, gives the name Amr ibn Sinan, the explanation of his father’s epithet, his public speaking and death in 57 AH / 677 CE. ## Metre and variant readings

Tawil, with qaf as the rhyme consonant. The opening divides `alā ṭa / raqat asmā / ʾu wahya / ṭarūqū` and `wabānat / ʿalā annal / khayāla / yashūqū`. At the moment of welcome in 11 the feet run `faqultu / lahū ahlan / wasahlan / wamarḥaban` and `fahādhā / ṣabūḥun rā / hinun wa / ṣadīqū`. The morning drink comes after the greeting, in the second half of the line.

The studio cards, two full transcripts, lexical sense and printed texts were checked against the word windows. In the repository scorer with one-second emission context, «وبانت» at **1.2** leads «وباتت» by 5.85, «محزون» at **2.1**, visible on Shakir and Harun p. 125 and Lyall p. 246, leads the electronic transcription’s «مخزون» by 7.12, and «وهى» at **2.2** leads «وهت» by 5.73. The night visitor leaves, and the grief belongs to the man whose heart trembles like a broken wing. At **7.2** «نجم» leads «ساري» by 11.93. At **9.2** «داني السحاب» leads the Basran «جم السجال» by 44.13. At **11.2** «صبوح راهن» leads «مبيت صالح» by 44.69. At **15.1** «فأوفدا» leads «فأوقدا», lighting a fire, by 10.42. The translation follows the explanation that the two butchers climbed up over the large animal. The verb can also describe their haste. At **18.2** «شواء» leads «عشاء» by 21.91 and «زاهق» leads «راهن» by 9.90. At **20.2** «وللخير» leads al-Tabrizi’s «وللحق» by 9.53 and the Basran «وللحمد» by 11.89. The final **23.1** «مكارم» leads Ibn Hamdun’s «أكارم» by 5.33. Exact candidate strings and timings are in the local `variant-windows.txt`.

Lyall, II, p. 83, conjectures a gap between the phantom’s departure in verse 3 and the appeal to Umm Haytham in 4. Both Arabic editions and the numbered performance pass directly between them. The recorded sequence supplies no intervening material. [Lyall’s introduction to XXIII](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatanth00mufauoft/page/n121/mode/2up)

At **5.1** the numbered studio card at 63 and 66 seconds writes the two nūns of «فإنني». Both full transcriptions preserve them, as do Shakir and Harun on p. 126. Lyall’s Arabic p. 246 reads «فإني». The longer form leads by only 0.71 in the word window (`variant-witnesses.txt`). Both forms introduce the speaker’s reason for asking Umm Haytham to agree with him.

## Half-verse verification

- **1.1 and 5.2.** The narrow «تروق» loses the emphatic initial of «طروق». Widened Audar restores the noun, echoing «طرقت». At 5.2 the short «الحسف» becomes «الحسب» in the wider clip. The previous half’s «فإنني» gives the wider clip its initial fragment «مني». The guest’s host is defending family standing.
- **9.2 and 12.2.** Both windows divide «هيدب داني» at the wrong place. The wide transcript recovers «دفوق» and the two full halves form one picture of hanging rain. At 12.2 the split «مقاحي دكوم» moves the last d of «مقاحيد» across the word boundary. The expanded «وقعت» also picks up the previous «فاتقت». The two adjectives describe the herd’s high humps. The full texts and the studio agree.
- **13.1 and 20.1.** At 13.1 the expanded clip still splits «النتاج كأنها» as «نتاجك أنها» and gives «بأدمى» for «بأدماء». The productive pale she-camel of the previous sentence is the referent of feminine «كأنها». At 20.1 both clips hear «بالقراءة». The noun «القِرى» is hospitality to a guest, the subject of the preceding twelve lines. The studio and the editions preserve it.
- Before the lead revised boundaries, **6.2** added articles and a possessive to «نوائب ... وحقوق», **13.2** split «العشار فنيق» and **22.2** added a suffix to «عروق». Each cleared in the final clips. The two edge reports at **17.2** («أو») and **23.2** («والله») occur only on isolated cut edges. The continuous studio verses begin «أخ بإخاء» and «يفاع وبعض الوالدين». Neither edge adds a performed word or a verse.

The final check counted six flagged clips in 46. These decisions use the recorded source’s card sequence, transcriptions and alignment. The local research directory preserves the collation and word-window scores.
