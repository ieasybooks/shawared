# يا ظبية البان ترعى في خمائله

## Recording and text

Osama al-Nahari recites the eighteen bayts once each, in the order of both complete diwans. The retained interval is 0.90–178.46 s of the original. The time outside it comprises the opening and closing production material. The two full Arabic acoustic transcripts, the measured timeline, both printed texts and the on-screen verse cards were compared. The five discrepancies from the 36 individual half-line transcriptions are accounted for below. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SBGsgpvk-4k)

- *Diwan al-Sharif al-Radi*, Beirut, Dar Sadir and Dar Bayrut, 1380 AH (1961 CE), II, pp. **107–108**, under «ما أمَرَّك وما أحلاك». Page 107 gives the date as Muharram 395 AH and calls this one of the Hijaziyyat. It prints 1–10. Page 108 prints 11–18 and the beginning of the following poem. The scans show «مدمعي الباكي», «الجزع», «فاكِ» and «أفلتِّ أشراكي». [Printed p. 107](https://archive.org/details/20191017_20191017_2122/page/n793/mode/1up) · [p. 108](https://archive.org/details/20191017_20191017_2122/page/n794/mode/1up)
- *Diwan al-Sharif al-Radi*, edited by Yusuf Shukri Farhat, Beirut, Dar al-Jil, II, pp. **99–100**, headed «ظبية البان». Page 99 gives the date, meter, first fifteen bayts and five lexical or geographical notes. Page 100 gives the last three and glosses وخدت as travel at speed. The heading differs between these editions, so the reader's title follows the recited first half. [Printed p. 99](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/14321/99) · [p. 100](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/14321/100)
- Al-Jirawi, *al-Hamasa al-Maghribiyya*, ed. Muhammad Ridwan al-Daya, II, no. **626**, pp. **1031–1032**, attributes ten bayts to al-Sharif al-Radi and identifies the basit. They correspond to our 1–5, 7, 9, 10, 14 and 18, in that order. Its final place name is vocalised الغُمَيْم. The divided typography in «عُدَدي» does not create a word or a different hemistich. [Printed p. 1031](https://shamela.ws/book/6902/973) · [p. 1032](https://shamela.ws/book/6902/974)
- Ibn Aydamur, *al-Durr al-farid wa-bayt al-qasid*, VI, p. **436**, no. **8827**, quotes 11–12. After «سقى منى» he has «بحيث يلقى غريم الدين ماطله / ويجتمع المشكو والشاكي». The voice gives the two diwans' «إذ يلتقي كل ذي دين وماطله / منّا ويجتمع المشكو والشاكي». Ibn Dihya separately records transmission of the poem beginning with this incipit through the poet's brother al-Murtada. No change of authorship is implied by that chain. [Ibn Aydamur](https://shamela.ws/book/20762/2893) · [Ibn Dihya, *al-Mutrib*, p. 42](https://shamela.ws/book/93/42)

The working collation compares 18 bayts against each complete edition, 10 against the Jirawi selection and 2 against Ibn Aydamur. The independent transcriptions and collation output are in `media/research/ya-zabyata-al-ban/`. The words at 12.2 represent a complaint against a person, hence المَشكُوُّ. At 13 «غريم» takes its force from the preceding debt. At 2 and 16 the water first imagined as tears returns as a sip that touched her teeth.

## Meter, translation and biography

The meter is **al-basit**, with kaf as rawiy. In 1.1 «يا ظبيةَ البانِ ترعى في خمائله» scans *yā ẓab ya tal | bā ni tar | ʿā fī kha mā | ʾi li hī*, **– – ⏑ – | – ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ –**. In 2.1 «الماء عندك مبذول لشاربه» gives *al mā ʾu ʿin | da ki mab | dhū lul li shā | ri bi hī*, **– – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ –**. The final pronoun in both examples lengthens to *hī*. The opening ajuz gives *li yah na kil | yaw ma ʾan | nal qal ba mar | ʿā kī*, **⏑ – ⏑ – | – ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | – –**. At 9.1 *ʾan tin na ʿī | mu li qal | bī wal ʿa dhā | bu la hū* gives **– – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ –**. The matching 9.2, *fa mā ʾa mar | ra ki fī | qal bī wa ʾaḥ | lā kī*, gives **⏑ – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | – –**.

The paired English translation and the Arabic and English explanations were written for this edition. Farhat's glosses locate the named encounters and explain the herd's browsing movement. The two complete editions preserve the creditor and debtor at 12, the purpose of speaking to a mouth at 10, and the lover's reversal from quarry to would-be hunter at 18. The poet's name, dates, Baghdad offices and literary works follow al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, VI, p. **99**. [Biographical entry](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/5253)
