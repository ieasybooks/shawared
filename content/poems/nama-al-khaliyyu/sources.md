# نامَ الخَلِيُّ وَما أُحِسُّ رُقادي

## Recording and sequence

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed says «قال الأسود بن يعفر النهشلي». The source lasts 286.047 s. The edited interval, 24.16–271.02 s (246.86 s), starts in the silence after the attribution. The first verse starts at about 24.66 s. The last «بفساد» decays into silence at 270.336 s. The 35 bayts each have one performance of each half. In both al-Qaysi and Shakir–Harun the correspondence is **1–13, 15–36**. The voice goes directly from the Euphrates running down from the mountains to «فإذا النعيم وكل ما يلهى به». Neither the full secondary transcript nor the numbered cards insert a bayt there. The repeated «البيض» and «ينطقن» in the middle each introduce distinct verses. The metre is *kamil*, the rawiy dal. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rwGCdziPYQw)

## Printed text and commentary

- Nuri Hammudi al-Qaysi, *Diwan al-Aswad ibn Ya'fur*, Silsilat kutub al-turath 15, no. 13, pp. 25–31. The 36 numbered items set the full count. On p. 28 he brackets «أين الذين بنوا فطال بناؤهم / وتمتعوا بالأهل والأولاد» and credits the addition to *Muntaha al-talab*. This is the sole omission. On p. 31 he also brackets the final «فإذا وذلك لا مهاه لذكره / والدهر يعقب صالحاً بفساد». The recording includes this last line. On p. 26 al-Qaysi gives both proposed senses of «ذي الأعواد», a dead man on his bier and the chief carried on a litter. On pp. 29–31 his notes identify the wine merchants, the Persian coins, the plants and the hunting vocabulary. [Scan, beginning of no. 13](https://archive.org/details/diwan-aswad/page/n25/mode/2up)
- Al-Mufaddal al-Dabbi, *al-Mufaddaliyyat*, ed. Ahmad Muhammad Shakir and Abd al-Salam Harun, no. 44, pp. 215–220. This text includes both of the bracketed bayts. The electronic transcription has «هل» in 9.1, «تشمي» in 24.1, «حاون» in 27.2 and «لامهاه» in 35.1 (all numbers hereafter are the app's). The printed Shakir–Harun pages themselves give «أهل الخورنق» on p. 217, «تمشي» on p. 218 and «حاولن» on p. 219. Al-Qaysi and the recording agree. The last line on p. 220 begins «فإذا وذلك» and separates «لا» from «مهاه» in the gloss. The app sets the two words separately. The separation of the negative particle is also independently witnessed by Abu Ubayda. [P. 215](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/189) · [p. 217](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/191) · [p. 219](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/193) · [p. 220](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/194)
- Al-Akhfash al-Asghar, *al-Ikhtiyarayn*, no. 94, pp. 558–570, has 33 bayts. It shares the omission after the Euphrates and also lacks the app's 17 («فتخيروا الأرض الفضاء») and 35. The four drinking-party lines represented by app 24–27 occur in the order **25, 27, 24, 26** in this witness. Pages 559–563 explain the ravine, the bier, old and new property, the Iraqi/Arabian localities and the marriage dispute in the house of Zayd. Pages 564–566 distinguish the coin interpretations and explain the cupbearer's earrings, mulberry-red fingers and the eggs implied by the ostrich's nest. Pages 567–570 distinguish the horse's gears, a bull that trusts its speed, and the camel whose spring grazing fills the folds where a tick might rest. [P. 559](https://shamela.ws/book/136/539) · [p. 563](https://shamela.ws/book/136/543) · [p. 565](https://shamela.ws/book/136/545) · [p. 568](https://shamela.ws/book/136/548) · [p. 570](https://shamela.ws/book/136/550)
- Abu Ubayda, *Majaz al-Qur'an*, I, p. 37, quotes the whole final bayt with «فإذا وذلك» and glosses «لا مهاه» as «لا طعم ولا فضل». C. J. Lyall, *The Mufaddaliyat*, II, no. XLIV, pp. 161–166, gives the shorter text and discusses this closing line on p. 166. The absence of this line from al-Akhfash does not cancel its independent attestation. The bilingual commentary follows the sense of pleasure or savour lost in recollection. [Abu Ubayda, I, p. 37](https://shamela.ws/book/23630/85)
- Al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, I, p. 330, gives the lineage Nahshal/Darim/Tamim, his home in Iraq, his association with al-Nu'man, his sight loss in old age and an approximate death around 22 before the Hijra / 600 CE. The speaker's own «ما نيل من بصري» links the recollection to that loss. [I, p. 330](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/919)

## Readings in the recitation

App 4 has «فيها … بين العراق» against al-Akhfash's «منها … بين العذيب». In 7 he says «لن يرضيا» where al-Akhfash has «لن يقبلا». App 10 gives «لدار أبيهم», which al-Akhfash records as an alternative to «لبرد مقيلها». The spelling «كعبُ بنُ مامة» loses the alif of *ibn* between two personal names. In 11–12 the recited words are «مكان ديارهم» and «بأنعم عيشة». Al-Akhfash has «محل ديارهم» and «بأفضل عيشة».

At 19 the singer's «أصحاب الصبابة … ولان قيادي» contrasts with «أصحاب البطالة … وذل قيادي» on pp. 563–564 of al-Akhfash. Al-Qaysi's main text at 20.1 has «ولقد أروح». His apparatus on p. 29 already gives «فلقد» from the *Mufaddaliyyat*, as recited here. It is «على التجار» in the recording, «إلى التجار» in al-Akhfash. The recited «وللشباب لذاذة» in 21 is «وللشباب بشاشة» in al-Akhfash.

In 24 and 26 he says «والبيض تمشي» and «وهن نواعم». Al-Akhfash gives «والحور تمشي» and «وهن موانع». In 28–30 the recording has «متناذر»، «الصفراء» and «مغامر», against «متحفر»، «الصفار» and «مرامر» in al-Akhfash. At 31.1 the recorded «بمشمر عتد جهيز شده» agrees with the *Mufaddaliyyat*. Al-Qaysi prints «جهير» on p. 31. Al-Akhfash has «بمقلص عتد شديد أسره» and also records «جهير شده». At 32 al-Akhfash gives «فيصيدنا العير … بشأوه» and cites «يشوي لنا الوحد …» among the alternatives. The voice and the diwan give «يشوي لنا الوحد المدل بحضره», the horse making roast meat of the solitary quarry by catching it. At 33.1 the recording has «بجسرة» where al-Akhfash has «بحرة».

Al-Qaysi, p. 27, expressly allows both a kasra and a damma on the qaf of the Euphrates place name in 13.1. The card at source 111 s vowels it «أَنْقُرَة». The recording has a rounded vowel after the qaf closure, with its second formant around 1.0–1.1 kHz. The reading with u leads the one with i by 6.92 in the complete half. At 9.2 the card's «الشُّرُفات» leads «الشُّرَفات» by 3.33, and at 15.2 «أُسوة» leads «إِسوة» by 2.94. Shakir–Harun's note on p. 219 defines «جواد» in the horse passage as «الكثير العدو». Its speed makes it a fetter for quarry and for racing rivals. [Al-Qaysi, p. 27](https://archive.org/details/diwan-aswad/page/n27/mode/2up) · [Shakir–Harun, p. 219](https://shamela.ws/book/6904/193)

In the rhymed opening both halves scan مُتْفاعلن متفاعلن متفاعِلْ. The sadr of 18 scans مُتْفاعلن مُتْفاعلن متفاعلن. Its ajuz has مُتْفاعلن متفاعلن مُتْفاعِلْ. Both shapes keep the three feet of *kamil*. [Lyall, II, p. 161](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatanth00mufauoft/page/n198/mode/2up) · [p. 166](https://archive.org/details/mufaddaliyatanth00mufauoft/page/n203/mode/2up)
