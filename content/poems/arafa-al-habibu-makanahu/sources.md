## Editions

- **ديوان البهاء زهير**, ed. محمد أبو الفضل إبراهيم and محمد طاهر الجبلاوي (Dar al-Ma'arif): scan archive.org `3303pdf_202001`, leaves 223–225, printed pp. 224–226. Headed by the editors «قهر الزمان», then «وقال يمدح الملك الناصر صلاح الدين يوسف بن الملك العزيز محمد بن الملك الظاهر غازي بن الملك الناصر صلاح الدين بن أيوب سنة ٦٤٢ هـ، «من ثاني الكامل قافية المتدارك»». 40 abyat, partly voweled, with glosses and variants from their sources (p. 12: the Dar al-Kutub manuscript ٥٥٧ أدب of 1286 AH, ا; the Alexandria manuscript of 996 AH, ل; the Cairo lithograph of 1277 AH, ح; Palmer's edition; a Beirut edition of 1964).
- **ديوان بهاء الدين زهير** (Dar Sadir, Beirut): scan archive.org `3439pdf_202001`, leaves 289–291, printed pp. 290–292. Titled «عرف الحبيب مكانه», with the same heading and date. 40 abyat, fully voweled, with short glosses and variants from two copies, أ and ب. The reciter follows this text where the two editions differ (5.1, 8.2, 13.1, 17.2, 33.1, 36.1, 36.2).
- **E. H. Palmer's edition of the Arabic text** (1876): scan archive.org `3440pdf12921876`, leaves 117–119, printed pp. 215–217. The same heading and date; 40 abyat, voweled.
- **The old shamela's diwan** (book 4508, the Abu Dhabi encyclopedia's text), pp. 432–435 (`old-shamela-4508.txt`): 40 abyat, partly voweled.
- **عبد الرحمن بن درهم, نزهة الأبصار بطرائف الأخبار والأشعار**: shamela 5375, p. 151 (`nuzhat.txt`). The whole poem, headed «وقال يمدح الملك الناصر صلاح الدين بن يوسف بن محمد الأيوبي سنة ٦٤٦», with many small differences.
- **ابن حجة الحموي, خزانة الأدب**: shamela 8675, vol. 1, p. 341 (`khizana.txt`), in the chapter on حسن التخلص (from p. 329): abyat 1, 2 and 12–16.
- **ابن معصوم, أنوار الربيع في أنواع البديع**: shamela 5446, p. 243 (`anwar.txt`), in the chapter on حسن التخلص (from p. 229): abyat 1, 2 and 14–16.
- **Starting text:** Dar Sadir's voweled text, put in the house style and checked against Dar al-Ma'arif's page images. The text departs from it once, for the reciter: 19.1 «حَولي» (Palmer «حَولِي») where Dar Sadir and Dar al-Ma'arif have «حَولِيَ».

`abyat_in_full` is 40, the count in both editions and in Palmer.

**Vowels and spelling:**
- 9.2 «وَلَوَ اَنَّني» and 32.1 «وَلَوَ اَنَّها»: the hamza is dropped and its fatha moved to the waw, as the old shamela writes «ولوَ انّها»; with the hamza the first foot is u – – u – and the half doesn't scan. Each has an Arabic note.
- 18.1 «يوسُفٌ» with tanwin, as Dar al-Ma'arif, Dar Sadir and the old shamela print it and the reciter says it (above); Palmer prints «يا يُوسُفَا», the alif of a cry for help. A called name takes damma without tanwin (يا يوسفُ), and tanwin there is a licence of verse; the meter allows any of the three, since the sadr's last syllable can be lengthened. An Arabic note explains it and gives Palmer's reading.
- 27.1 «مُسبَلاً»: both editions put fatha on the ب, and the scores agree. The passive is «let down», as a curtain is (أسبل إزاره: أرخاه), rain hanging from cloud to earth, which Abu Zayd calls السَّبَل (al-Sihah); the active مُسبِل would be «pouring» (أسبل المطر: هطل). The note glosses the passive.
- 36.1 «يا مَن» (Dar Sadir, Palmer, old shamela, Nuzhat): Dar al-Ma'arif prints «ويا من», which breaks the meter (u – – u –); the transcript hears «يا من».
- 36.2 «يَتَأَوَّلا»: the draft had Dar al-Ma'arif's passive «يُتَأَوَّلا», the easier grammar, but the aligner hears the fatha (−5.7 and −6.5 for the passive, same length), the first transcriber writes «يَتَأَوَّلَ», and Dar Sadir and the old shamela print it; تأوّل is read as the mutawi' of أوّل (it admits interpretation). A note gives the passive.
- 38.2 «أَلّا» (Dar al-Ma'arif) for «أن لا» (Dar Sadir, Palmer): the same sound. «أَقومَ» in the subjunctive, as both editions print it and the reciter says it (above), though after a verb of knowing (عالِماً) the grammarians usually read «أَن» as lightened from «أَنَّ», with «أَقومُ».
- 11.2 «وَسَطِ … وَسَطِ» as both editions vowel them; 22.2 «العُلا» with alif (Dar al-Ma'arif; root ع ل و) where Dar Sadir has «العُلى»; 19.1 «اِلتَفَتُّ» with the vowel of the alif of wasl.
- **Meter:** every half scans as kamil (mutafa'ilun or mustaf'ilun in each foot), with the pronoun's long vowel in «عَنهُ» (6.2, 23.1) and «مِنهُ» (17.2, 26.2).

## Commentary drawn on

- **Glosses** follow Dar al-Ma'arif's notes (الهيف; الغزالة, the sun; المسلسل, المسند and المرسل as hadith terms; ميّس, مريت, الأخلاف, حفّل; التعسّل, «الإسراع في المشي»; الخزامى and المندل; زياد, al-Nabigha, and جرول, al-Hutay'a; المفصّل) and Dar Sadir's (bayt 26: «أي لبس درعاً، وشبه حلقها بدوائر الماء في الغدير … أراد بالجدول السيف»; المفصّل).
- **Khizana's editor** glosses «رواه» as both «حكاه» and «من الريّ والسقاية» (14) and «أتنفّل» (16).
- **The lexicons:** al-Jawhari's *al-Sihah* (shamela 23235/3408) for أسبلت السماء (27); the rest are the standard senses (عسلان الذئب for التعسّل, العُذرة as both excuse and maidenhood, الغزالة as the sun at its rising).
- **The turn to praise (15–16):** Ibn Hijja and Ibn Ma'sum, above. The double sense of «صلاح الدين» is plain in the line; neither names it.
- **Bayt 26's second half** is word for word in a line of the Andalusian grammarian Abu Muhammad al-A'sha, quoted by al-Imad al-Isfahani, *Kharidat al-Qasr* (Maghrib section), vol. 2, p. 171 (shamela 12856/147, `kharida.txt`), and al-Qifti, *Inbah al-Ruwat*, vol. 1, p. 273 (shamela 10525/265, `inbah.txt`): «ملك إذا ادّرع الدِّلاص حسبته … لبس الغدير وهزّ منه جدولا».
- **Bayt 25** has no gloss in any source. The sharh reads the cloak as the riders spread over the ground, and gives the thin dust of their horses as a second reading.
- **The patron:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 8, pp. 249–250 (shamela 12286/7723–7724, `alam-nasir*.txt`): al-Nasir Yusuf ruled Aleppo from 634, took Damascus in 648 and was killed by Hulagu in 659.
- **The poet's life:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 3, p. 52 (shamela 12286/2082, `alam.txt`), and Ibn Khallikan, *Wafayat al-A'yan*, vol. 2, pp. 332–338 (shamela 1000/804–810, `wafayat.txt`), who knew him.

## Review

The drafter checked the draft first, as no second reader could be started then. A second reader then checked every half against the page images of Dar al-Ma'arif, Dar Sadir and Palmer, scanned every half as kamil, re-scored the doubtful readings on the half alone with calibration tests, ran the second transcriber on nine clips, and checked the sharh, notes, intro and bio against the sources (2026-11-17). Fixed after the review:

- **Text:** 19.1 «حَولي» for «حَولِيَ», as the reciter says it and Palmer prints it (−14.8 for the editions' reading, more than twice the bias of a dropped vowel).
- **Audio:** the timeline holds 38.2 until the voice stops at 379.8 s instead of 377.4 s, so the stretched last «ولا» is lit.
- **sources.md:** 5.1 «مِنهُ زارَ» credited to Dar Sadir, Palmer and the old shamela (Dar al-Ma'arif has «زارَ مِنهُ»); 18.1 no longer says all three editions print «يوسُفٌ» (Palmer has «يوسُفا»); the reciter's tanwin in 18.1, his «أَقومَ» in 38.2 and Nuzhat's «مُتَقَوِّلا» in 5.2 scored and recorded; Dar al-Ma'arif's ل in 17.2 recorded; the list of places where he follows Dar Sadir completed (5.1, 8.2, 36.1, 36.2).
- **Notes:** 27.1 «مُسبَلاً» glossed as the passive it is (let down, like a curtain) rather than the active «pouring»; 18.1 gives Palmer's «يا يوسُفا» and the reciter's tanwin; heads that repeated the word dropped (11.2, 13.1, 31.2 in both languages); 23.2 «فَعَلامَ» given the head «عَلامَ».
- **Sharh:** 25 adds the second reading of the cloak (the dust of their horses) in both languages; the English of 27 now says first what the bayt means.
- **English:** 4 retranslated so the imagined things, not the poet, stand plain in his thoughts (أحسب with two objects).
- **Intro:** the bride is no longer said to close the poem; four abyat of praise and thanks follow her.
