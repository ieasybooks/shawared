# لَمَعَ البَرقُ اليَماني

Osama Al-Waaedh recites the poem. The upload is dated 2022-02-19. Source length 78.135 s. The poetry-only file keeps 3.37–77.81 s on the original clock and runs 74.44 s. Gain -2.1 dB, measured output -20 LUFS, peak -3.2 dBTP. [Source recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1o-IVi3FPuo)

## Text and attribution

Husam al-Din Isa ibn Sanjar al-Hajiri, *Bulbul al-gharam, diwan al-Hajiri*, ed. Khalid al-Jabr and Atif Kan'an (Amman, University of Petra, 2003), pp. 258–259. The short pieces have no individual title here. «بلبل الغرام» is the name of the collection. Its **19** numbered verses supply `abyat_in_full`. The recording gives print 1–8 and 10–17, in order, once each. The omitted verses begin «يا مَغاني الشِّعب» (9), «كلّما قلتُ تقضّى» (18) and «حكمت فينا» (19). The editors mark 9 and 18 as peculiar to witness م. [Petra title leaf](https://archive.org/details/a1113n/page/n2/mode/1up) · [pp. 258–259 and apparatus](https://archive.org/details/a1113n/page/n258/mode/2up)

Two independently transmitted selections name the poet. Ibn Duqmaq, *Nuzhat al-anam fi tarikh al-Islam*, p. 64, gives thirteen verses, including the fortress, transposes the last two, and in our 15 puts captivity first and fear last. Baha' al-Din al-Amili, *al-Kashkul*, II, pp. 266–267, gives ten, ending at our 12. It has «أيام التداني», «فَرماني», «فأراني» and «وزمان العنفوان». Their omissions and order preclude using either excerpt to count the full poem. The Petra footnotes document the shorter readings and spellings. [Ibn Duqmaq, p. 64](https://shamela.ws/book/16363/58) · [Al-Kashkul, II, p. 266](https://shamela.ws/book/1571/612) · [p. 267](https://shamela.ws/book/1571/613)

The opening flash, protected pasture, two landmarks, bowstring and loose rein are successive images in the verse. The fortress identification comes from the editors at p. 259 n. 3. Ibn Khallikan, the poet’s friend, describes his imprisonment at Khiftidkan and subsequent transfer to Erbil. The specific enemy or event behind the personified separation remains unnamed. The biography uses Ibn Khallikan, *Wafayat al-a'yan*, III, pp. 501–505, and al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, V, p. 103. Both explain his poetic connection with Hajir. [Ibn Khallikan, pp. 501–505](https://shamela.ws/book/1000/1512) · [The imprisonment, p. 503](https://shamela.ws/book/1000/1514) · [Al-Zirikli](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/4268)

## Sound, readings and metre

The first /l/ begins at original 3.92 s. The last /n/ and /i/ are heard at 76.56 and 76.64 s and the sustained sound decays until about 77.4 s. **Keep 3.37–77.81 s** leaves the opening room tone and the decay intact. There is no internal cut or reprise.

At 2.1 both continuous transcripts and the Petra main text have «دهري وزماني». The indefinite wording in the two anthologies scores 10.15 points higher, but the /i/ after each noun, the lack of a distinct ending before the half-break, and both full-context recognitions favour the possessives. In 3.2 «أوقات» beats «أيام» by 13.06, in 5.2 «وَرماني» beats «فَرماني» by 11.10, and in 7.2 «وأراني» beats «فأراني» by 6.62. At 6.2 the **main** text on p. 258 reads «مِن تَجَنّيهِ سَقاني». The enlarged type has two dots below the letter before the pronoun. The recorded sound runs /n n i h i/ at 29.68–30.19 s. «تجنّيه» scores 10.55 above the conjecture «تجنّبه». The lexical card accordingly explains unjust treatment. At 16.2 the print points the fortress with initial ḥu. The performance has ḥa (ḥa −13.25, ḥu −21.71, kha −20.10). Ibn Khallikan gives a fuller place-name with initial kh. [Print and notes](https://archive.org/details/a1113n/page/n259/mode/1up) · [Ibn Khallikan on the name](https://shamela.ws/book/1000/1516)

Majzu' al-ramal, two feet in each half, with khabn. In 1.1 «لَمَعَ البَرْ / قُ اليَماني» gives ∪ ∪ – – | – ∪ – –. In 11.1 «حَيْثُ مَجْرَلْ / لَهْوِ رَحْبُنْ» gives – ∪ – – twice. The rawiy is nun, often with an audible final i. The printed divisions run through «ترجع»، «الشمل»، «البين»، «البشاشات» and the article of «الدّمع». At our 15, print 16, Petra expands the doubled dal as «الدْ / دَمْعِ». The reader writes «ال / دَّمعِ», preserving ordinary spelling on joining the fragments. In connected speech the article supplies the first of the two d sounds (original 69.64 and 69.72 s). The cut and the 32 addresses remain the same.

## Earlier verification passes

The first isolated Cohere pass (`verification-initial/9fc6d399…log`) reported 12 lexical differences. Two of those addresses cleared on the subsequent clips. Times here are original seconds on the 3.37–77.81 s cut.

- **1.2, 6.13–8.13 s.** The early clip gave «فشجعني ما شجعني», “it encouraged me”, twice inserting an ʿayn. The release clip and both continuous recognitions give «فشجاني ما شجاني». The repetition of شجا, to sadden or stir longing, is also there in Petra 1. The flash reawakens his grief.
- **5.1, 22.15–24.43 s.** The early clip completed the last fragment as «البيت». Release Cohere stops at «البيـ». Petra 5 divides «البَيـ / ـنُ» across the columns. Full Cohere gives «البينا», and the widened 5.2 gives «بين مصيبا ورماني». It is parting that nocks the arrow. The free phone trace gives /j/ at 24.41 s and /n/ at 24.61 s. The nun belongs to the start of 5.2.

On the same early pass 4.1 ended in «الشمع» and 13.2 changed «الغيد» to «الغيل». The release readings of both halves and their widened clips are in the table. The older «الد / دمع» at 15.1–15.2 reflects the printed spelling of the joined d sounds discussed above. [Petra, printed pp. 258–259](https://archive.org/details/a1113n/page/n258/mode/2up)
