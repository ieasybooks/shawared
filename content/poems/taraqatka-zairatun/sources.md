# Marwan ibn Abi Hafsa addresses al-Mahdi

## Performance and extent

Osama al-Nahari recites the 38 surviving bayts in the order of Husayn Atwan's no. 61. The source is silent until about 4.36 seconds. The final word مثَالَها ends near 337.47. The selected interval is 3.84–338.15 on the original clock. There is one continuous passage, with no repeated half. Its opening and «كلتا يديك جعلت فضل نوالها» follow kamil, three feet to each half with the usual contraction of متفاعلن. The rhyme consonant is lam. The refrain-like ها comes after it. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kL5naMqQl4I)

## Text, glosses and occasion

- Husayn Atwan, ed., *Shiʿr Marwan ibn Abi Hafsa*, third edition, Dar al-Maʿarif, no. 61, pp. 96–99. The four pages give respectively 1–7, 8–17, 18–29 and 30–38, then no. 62 begins on p. 100. Footnotes identify the riders' nodding heads, the ostrich and her chicks, the Jayhan on the frontier, the advance squadrons, the horses' narrowed flanks and the sandal pattern at the close. [P. 96](https://archive.org/details/Shmrwan/page/n95/mode/1up) · [P. 97](https://archive.org/details/Shmrwan/page/n96/mode/1up) · [P. 98](https://archive.org/details/Shmrwan/page/n97/mode/1up) · [P. 99](https://archive.org/details/Shmrwan/page/n98/mode/1up)
- Al-Sharif al-Murtada, *Amali*, ed. Muhammad Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim, I, pp. 540–541 and 569–570, quotes and comments on two stretches of the poem. At 2.1 he gives «مالت بقلبك», with «ملكت» in a marginal witness. Atwan prints the recited «قادت فؤادك» and cites al-Murtada's reading below his text. He explains «المراعشة» as the nodding of a sleepy rider. In the campaign passage the commentary distinguishes the place where the back of the saddle rests from the bit in the mouth, gives «نحائزها» as the horses' dispositions, and cites earlier comparisons of long sword-straps and a tall warrior. The note on the last bayt explains how cutting sandals on the same pattern becomes an image of following an example. [Opening](https://shamela.ws/book/20937/571) · [The riders](https://shamela.ws/book/20937/572) · [The frontier](https://shamela.ws/book/20937/600) · [The horses and last verses](https://shamela.ws/book/20937/601)
- Al-Tanukhi, *al-Faraj baʿd al-shidda*, ed. Abbud al-Shalji, I, pp. 377–379, gives the account of the poet's rebuff over his praise of Ma'n, his return the following year, and the award of a hundred thousand dirhams. In that account Marwan gives the length as **a hundred bayts**. The catalogue count of 38 describes Atwan's extant, numbered text. The anecdote supplies neither the words nor the positions of the additional verses. Ibn Abd Rabbih, *al-Iqd al-farid*, I, pp. 262–263, gives another court report with excerpts and places the al-Anfal line before the question about the stars. [Al-Tanukhi, I, pp. 377–379](https://shamela.ws/book/12828/302) · [The reported count](https://shamela.ws/book/12828/304) · [Ibn Abd Rabbih](https://shamela.ws/book/23789/265)
- The appeal to the relatives' prior claim at 34 draws on Qur'an 8:75. It is Marwan who extends it to the Abbasid succession. The biography takes his genealogy, his upbringing in al-Yamama, his patrons and his death in Baghdad in 182 AH (798 CE) from al-Zirikli, *al-Aʿlam*, VII, p. 208. ## Readings

At 1.2 the print and the recording have «بالحياء», in contrast to al-Tanukhi's «بالجمال». At 2.1 the recording gives «قادت فؤادك», the reading Atwan prints. At 22.2 both the release from captivity and the letters on Atwan's p. 98 support «أغلالها». At 31.2 the two full transcribers give r where Atwan prints z in «نحائزها». Both Atwan's gloss «الطباع» and al-Murtada's «طباعها» identify the rare word. At 33.1 the audience is addressed in the second person plural, «عن ربكم», as in Atwan and Ibn Abd Rabbih. At 34.2 the full reading is «بتراثهم فأردتم إبطالها». The speaker's opponents are still the grammatical subject of «أردتم».

The word comparisons use equally long readings on the same alignment window with 0.30 seconds on either side. At 1.2 «بالحياء» scores −20.40 against −40.39 for «بالجمال». At 2.1 «قادت فؤادك» scores −20.61 against −65.57 for «مالت بقلبك». At 22.2 «أغلالها» and «أظلالها» nearly tie at −17.23 and −17.61. Full Audar gives «أغلالها» and the video displays it alongside the gloss «الأغلال: القيود». At 33.1 «ربكم» scores −17.85 against −22.26 for «ربه». At 34.2 «فأردتم» scores −14.85 against −28.48 for «فرجوتم». The two less certain consonants in 31.2 and 36.1 have paired word notes in the reader.
