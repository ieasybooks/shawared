# الأَسَدُ وَوَزيرُهُ الحِمارُ

## Recording and extent

Alqimmah Studio published «حكايات أحمد شوقي» on 3 March 2023, crediting Osama Al-Waaedh with recitation and editing. The source lasts 966.844 seconds. Chapter 1 keeps **9.06–80.55 seconds of the original compilation** in one continuous cut. The source voice measurements place the first and last pitched sound of this chapter at approximately 9.60 and 78.99 seconds. The cut admits the entry and the decay of the final rhyme. [Compilation, chapter 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r6VpT0Lid0E&t=9s)

All 16 printed couplets occur in order, once each, from «الليث ملك القفار» to «من رأيكم في الحمار». The 9-second chapter title is on screen. The first utterance is the first half of the opening bayt. The 75-second card already shows the monkey’s «رأي الرعية فيكم». The 32-entry plan ends before the separate birth announcement in chapter 2.

## Printed text and interpretation

- Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, part IV, «الحكايات», Dar al-Awda, Beirut, 1988. The references follow the pagination of part IV. [p. 147](https://archive.org/details/BBib-Alex-02915/page/n342/mode/1up)
- Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, «الحكايات», Hindawi Foundation for Education and Culture, Cairo, deposit 19938/2012, ISBN 978-977-719-166-1. The page images and the electronic chapter were compared. [p. 882](https://archive.org/details/alshawkeyat/page/n882/mode/1up) · [Electronic text](https://www.safahat.org/books/70706142/143/)

The first edition of *al-Shawqiyyat* (part I, Cairo, Matbaʿat al-Adab wa-l-Muʾayyad, 1898) provides an earlier text. The Cairo opening reads «لليث ملك القفار»: the wild places are the lion’s kingdom. Shakib Arslan preserves that construction. The two later diwans set «الليث ملك القفار», the lion as king, which agrees with the recorded opening. Cairo closes with the collective «رأي الرعية فيكم» and the echo «من رأيكم في الحمار». [p. 175](https://books.google.com/books?id=QIyl-Nj9R6UC&pg=RA1-PA175) · [p. 176](https://books.google.com/books?id=QIyl-Nj9R6UC&pg=RA1-PA176)

Shakib Arslan quotes all 16 couplets immediately after the bat fable in *Shawqi: Sadaqat Arba'in Sana*, «أماثيل من شعر شوقي», §8. His quotation opens «للّيث ملك القفار», a possessive construction, where both diwans and the two full-take transcriptions begin «الليث». The collective crowd starts by addressing the king and then asks, in the third person, what he saw in the donkey. The monkey’s exchange of «رأي الرعية فيكم» and «رأيكم في الحمار» answers that question without requiring a speech from the appointee. [Arslan, §8](https://www.safahat.org/books/96809026/24/)

The two printings transmit the same diwan tradition. The page reproductions and the word-by-word output from `scripts/collate.py` are in `media/research/al-asad-wa-waziruhu-al-himar/`. The studio captions are performance evidence. The glosses and bilingual commentary are editorial close readings of the cited text.

## Metre and rhyme

Mujtath, two feet to a half, مستفعلن فاعلاتن. At the opening, *al-lay-thu-mal | kul-qi-fā-rī* gives **– – ⏑ – | – ⏑ – –**, with the contracted مَلْك. Its answer *wa-mā-ta-ḍum | muṣ-ṣa-ḥā-rī* gives **⏑ – ⏑ – | – ⏑ – –**. In bayt 9, *lam-yash-ʿu-ril | lay-thu-il-lā* again gives **– – ⏑ – | – ⏑ – –**, and *wa-mul-ku-hū | fī-da-mā-rī* gives **⏑ – ⏑ – | – ⏑ – –** with the connecting vowel of the pronoun. The shortened first foot is khabn. The returning consonant in the second halves is rāʾ, with long ā before it and a sustained kasra or written yāʾ after it.

## Readings against the performance

The opening comparison favours the diwan’s «الليث» over Arslan’s prefixed lām. At 15.1 the two spellings tested romanise identically. Both diwans, Arslan, the 75-second studio card and the chapter-length transcript give «عالي الجاه». The same adjective returns in «عالي الأنظار» a breath later. At 16.1 the aligner also collapses the three candidates to the same phonemes. The open-vowel interval after the y sound in «الرعية» is about 0.32 seconds in a free phoneme trace, comparable to the 0.30-second vowel before the linked t in the singular «بعظمة» at 11.2. The three written witnesses and the studio card agree on the collective noun. Neither a feminine plural nor a reference to grazing females is required by the performance.
