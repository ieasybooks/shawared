# ذَريني لِلغِنى أَسعى فَإِنّي

## Recording and sequence

Abd al-Rahman al-Saeed opens with «قال عروة بن الورد العبسي». The downloaded source lasts 67.872 s. The edit takes 22.72–54.20 s (31.48 s), from the silence after the name through the decay of «غفور». All five bayts of the diwan are recited in their printed order, both halves once. The full-file Cohere transcript prints a second «غفور» at the end. The last vocal phrase runs straight into the terminal silence at 53.730 s. The independent Audar transcript ends with a single «رب غفور». The repeated word in the sample transcript does not constitute a second performance of 5.2. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=crFBfuA-ksA)

## Text and commentary

- Ya'qub ibn al-Sikkit, *Diwan Urwa ibn al-Ward*, ed. Abd al-Mu'in al-Maluhi, pp. 91–92. The five bayts are headed «الناس شرهم الفقير». The gloss on p. 91 takes «الخِير» as generosity and honour. On p. 92 «جم» means numerous and «رب» means a master or possessor. The editor describes the honour shown to wealth and the injustice done to the poor. The text is in *wafir*, with ra as the rawiy. [Printed pp. 91–92](https://archive.org/details/Pdf018/page/n109/mode/2up)
- Al-Jahiz, *al-Bukhala'*, pp. 239–240, and Ibn Qutayba, *Uyun al-akhbar*, I, p. 348, both give all five under Urwa's name among other verses on wealth and poverty. Ibn Qutayba labels the metre *wafir*. Al-Jahiz has «وتلقى ذا الغنى» and «ولكن الغنى رب غفور». Ibn Qutayba has «وتلفي ذا الغنى» and «ولكن للغنى رب غفور». The different constructions in the close change who does the forgiving. [Al-Jahiz, p. 239](https://shamela.ws/book/10501/231) · [p. 240](https://shamela.ws/book/10501/232) · [Ibn Qutayba, I, p. 348](https://shamela.ws/book/23790/341)
- Al-Zamakhshari's *Rabi' al-abrar*, V, p. 88, gives four lines as anonymous verse. Its second sadr reads «وأهونهم وأحقرهم عليهم». This witnesses the two superlatives heard together in the recording. The reciter's «لديهم» gives the onlookers' own estimate of the man. [V, p. 88](https://shamela.ws/book/10668/1920)
- For the biography, al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, IV, p. 227, gives the Abs/Ghatafan genealogy, his care of unsuccessful raiders, Abd al-Malik's comparison with Hatim and an approximate death around 30 before the Hijra / 594 CE. [IV, p. 227](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/3646)

## Half verification

The independent per-half transcription of the edited audio agreed in all 10 halves (40 words). The aligner's weakest word was the short «ذا» in 4.1 (0.046). Both full transcriptions and the separate half clip preserve «ذا الغنى», and the next word begins inside the same half. No extra half or cut is needed. The distinctions in case endings and vowel quality above come from the print witnesses, vocalised cards and candidate comparisons. The verifier normalises harakat.

Ibn Abi al-Dunya, *Islah al-mal*, report 479, p. 127, names Urwa for the four bayts narrated by Ibn al-A'rabi. The transmitter then adds the fifth from another informant, with «قليل عيبه والعيب جم». Al-Maluhi, al-Jahiz and Ibn Qutayba transmit the fifth with «ذنبه والذنب», as recorded. In the opening sadr both مفاعلتن feet undergo *asb* (مفاعيلن مفاعيلن فعولن). At 3.1 the feet are مفاعيلن مفاعلتن فعولن, and at 3.2 مفاعلتن مفاعلتن فعولن. [Islah al-mal, p. 127](https://shamela.ws/book/13187/465)
