## Recording

The sound never drops below −45 dB between the first word and the last, so there was no pause inside the poem to trim.

He leaves out 26–28 («وله بمكّة إذ أميّة أهلها», «أغنت قرابته وكان لزومه», «وسموت عن أخلاقهم فتركتهم»), 33–34 («وشكوت غرمًا فادحًا فحملته», and «فأعد فدىً لك», which Jamal adds from Muntaha al-Talab) and the closing 45 («وأرى المدينة حين صرت أميرها»).

The bayt numbers below are the recording's: 1–25 are Jamal's 1–25, 26–29 his 29–32, and 30–39 his 35–44.

## Editions

- **عادل سليمان جمال, شعر الأحوص الأنصاري** (2nd ed., Maktabat al-Khanji, Cairo, 1411/1990), poem 114, pp. 207–214: scan archive.org `diwan-al-ahwas`, leaves 206–213, read from the page images (`jamal.txt`). 45 abyat, fully voweled, with notes on every variant and on the hard words. His base text («الأصل») is al-Aghani 21:108–112; he adds 34 and 38 from Muntaha al-Talab and 3–4 from Tarikh al-Islam and Khizanat al-Adab, and his note to the poem quotes the Aghani's account of it in full (pp. 207–208). **The text follows Jamal word for word and vowel for vowel except at three places:** 3.2, where the reciter departs from every source; 39.2, where he follows al-Buhturi; and 13.2, where Jamal prints «لا يَنْهَلُ» (p. 211, with no note), which repeats the rhyme word of 12.2 and makes no sense here, while every other source has «لا يَذهَلُ» and the reciter says it (below).
- **إبراهيم السامرائي, شعر الأحوص بن محمد الأنصاري** (Matba'at al-Nu'man, Najaf, 1388/1969), poem 134, pp. 152–160: scan archive.org `ar107diwan25`, leaves 151–159, read from the page images (`samarrai.txt`; the scan has no OCR). Headed «جاء في الأغاني ٢١/١١٠–١١٢ ومصادر أخرى قوله»; the same 45 abyat in the same order, barely voweled. His differences from Jamal are scored below; his 10.1 «أول رقبتي» and 13.2 «مذيت» are misprints for «لولا» and «منيت».
- **ابن ميمون, منتهى الطلب من أشعار العرب**: shamela 732, pp. 292–293 (`muntaha.txt`). 42 abyat in a different order (6 comes second), voweled, headed «وقال الأحوص يمدح عمر بن عبد العزيز».
- **عبد القادر البغدادي, خزانة الأدب**, vol. 2, pp. 48–55: shamela 12732/517–524 (`khizana.txt`, `khizana2.txt`). Bayt 2 as Sibawayh's shahid for «قسمًا»; abyat 1–6, 9–10, 26, 28–29 and 39 (and Jamal's 28 and 45); notes on 1, 3, 5, 6, 8, 10 and «مذق»; the anecdote of al-Mansur (from al-Mada'ini) and the story of the poem from al-Husri's *Jam' al-Jawahir*.
- **البغدادي, شرح أبيات مغني اللبيب**, vol. 6, pp. 246–249: shamela 150966/2194–2197 (`mughni.txt`). Abyat 1–2, 5–6, 8–14, with a full commentary on 8 (Ibn Hisham's text has «فؤادها»; al-Baghdadi corrects it to «فؤاده» and refers it to the kashih) and glosses on 11–14. He counts the poem at 42 abyat.
- **البحتري, الحماسة**: shamela 148535, p. 229 (abyat 30–31) and p. 299 (bayt 39) (`buhturi.txt`).
- **صدر الدين البصري, الحماسة البصرية**, vol. 2, p. 215: shamela 6901/498 (`basriyya.txt`). Abyat 1, 6, 2, 5, 11 and 12.
- **أسامة بن منقذ, المنازل والديار**, p. 93: shamela 5416/93 (`manazil.txt`). The story, with abyat 1–2, 6, 9–14 and 39.
- **ابن عساكر, تاريخ دمشق**, vol. 66, pp. 39–40: shamela 71/30204–30205 (`ibn-asakir.txt`). The story from the same isnad as the Aghani, with abyat 1–2, then 29, 27, 28 and 39 introduced «ثم قال يعرّض بأبي بكر بن عبد العزيز». The editor's notes give the Aghani's readings (21/98–100 in the edition he used), among them «حاجة» in 29.1.
- **Abu Tammam's Hamasa** has none of the poem; al-Marzuqi's commentary on it (shamela 26536/928, p. 951, `marzuqi.txt`) quotes bayt 1 as a parallel to «أصدّ بأيدي العيس عن قصد أهلها، وقلبي إليها بالمودّة قاصد».

`abyat_in_full` is 45, the count of both editions (al-Baghdadi's copy had 42).

- **3.2:** every source has «ما كانَ غَيرُكَ وَالأَمانَةِ يَنزِلُ», an oath by one's faith (al-Baghdadi: «والواو في قوله والأمانة واو القسم»). The reciter clearly says «فيهِ يَوماً»: the full transcript hears «فيه يوما», the aligner gives the two words 0.75 and 0.83, and the editions' words lose by 44. The line scans either way. The text follows the reciter, and the note on «فيهِ يَوماً» gives the editions' reading.
- **39.2:** the reciter says al-Buhturi's «مَذِقُ اللِّسانِ», which al-Manazil and Ibn Asakir also have; the note gives «مَذِقُ الحَديثِ», the reading of both editions.
- **12.2 «حَزَناً»:** «حُزناً» scores 8.7 better, but it has a vowel fewer, which the scorer always favours. ## Commentary drawn on

- **Glosses** follow Jamal's notes on these pages (تعزّل, تقاعس, الأقبل, اللذّ, الثغام والمحول, الثواء, نبوة, الفجر, السجال, الممحل, الحدث, الرونق, البغاث والأجدل, السورة, حوى, سابق, المتمهّل, الفعال, حُصِّلوا, رجع متعدّيًا, غرائب, التنخّل, هوى والقلص والذمّل, بيشة, الخادر, المتبسّل) and al-Baghdadi's (أتعزّل, موكّل, قسمًا, المتعلّل, الكاشح, الرقبة, اللذّ, نجذل, يُعلّ ويُنهل, منيت, أودى, أخلق, المعول, مذق). The rest (العضب, الصيقل, الأرومة, البشاشة and the like) are the dictionaries' common senses. Checked against *Lisan al-Arab*: الأقبل («الذي أقبلت حدقتاه على أنفه»), العَمَم («أمرٌ عَمَمٌ: تامٌّ عامٌّ»), الذميل, التمهّل («والمَهَل والتمهّل: التقدّم»).
- **Bayt 8:** «فؤاده» is the kashih's, as al-Baghdadi argues in *Sharh Abyat al-Mughni* (he corrects «فؤادها»); 7 is read the same way, since the beloved is addressed in the second person throughout 2–10.
