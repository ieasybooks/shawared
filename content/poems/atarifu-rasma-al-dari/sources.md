# Adi ibn Zayd · مُجَمهَرَةُ عَدِيِّ بنِ زَيدٍ

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari recites all 49 numbered bayts of al-Mu'aybid's poem 23, in order, once each. The cut is 3.400–423.920 s on the original 424.484 s source. This keeps roughly half a second before the first voice and the decay of «ومسعد» at the end. The app excerpt lasts 420.520 s and has 98 half-lines. The full transcriptions and the studio cards place «وللبخلة الأولى» between «وللخلق إذلال» and «وأبدت لي الأيام». The widely circulated 48-bayt text lacks this verse. The numbered scan, the complete source sound and the lexical quotation under Adi supply independent checks. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wpEU4wH6bgM) · [Printed bayts 40–41, pp. 107–108](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n107/mode/2up) · [Al-'Ayn, 4/272](https://shamela.ws/book/1682/1380)

## Editions, transmission and commentary

- Muhammad Jabbar al-Mu'aybid, *Diwan Adi ibn Zayd al-Ibadi*, no. 23, printed pp. 102–109. The numbering includes the material the editor brackets and identifies by source in the footnotes. Those verses are heard here. His glosses on «ما تقدّم من ردى» (12), reversal from hardship to good fortune (18), the turn in a friendship (31), «لهد» (41), the lesson recorded from time (42), the mourner's cloth (44) and the last «مسعد» (49) guide the two original reader layers. The last word has also been understood as someone who joins a mourner. The reader's rendering follows the sense of the rejoicing listener given on p. 109. [Opening, p. 102](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n102/mode/1up) · [pp. 104–105](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n104/mode/2up) · [pp. 108–109](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n108/mode/2up)
- Abu Zayd al-Qurashi, *Jamharat ash'ar al-Arab*, ed. Ali Muhammad al-Bijawi, Nahdat Misr, printed pp. 390–398. Al-Bijawi numbers 45 bayts here. His closing notice gives 44, with 42 reported for the Amiri printing in note 70. The searchable text stops at the equivalent of printed 42 and also combines parts of printed 16 and 17. The printed poem places counsel about the wavering friend before the warning about jokes. It gives «قبل التجلّد» at 1 and «لذّة الفتى» at 6. Its mourning passage at 41–42 precedes three further bayts. Ibn Sallam mentions the incipit among Adi's admired verses. The speaker stands at Umm Ma'bad's former home and speaks of his shackles at our 10. [Al-Bijawi, opening, p. 390](https://archive.org/details/20231218_20231218_0842/page/n389/mode/1up) · [Printed 16–17, p. 393](https://archive.org/details/20231218_20231218_0842/page/n392/mode/1up) · [Closing verses and apparatus, p. 398](https://archive.org/details/20231218_20231218_0842/page/n397/mode/1up) · [Searchable ending](https://shamela.ws/book/1487/356) · [Ibn Sallam, 1/141–142](https://shamela.ws/book/6738/139)
- At 2.2 Ibn Faris defines التصريد as giving less drink than would quench thirst. The negation makes this a generous draught. Hisham ibn Ahmad al-Waqshi's *al-Ta'liq ala al-Muwatta'* quotes 5.2 with «طُوًى» and records «ثُنًى», both expressing repetition. Al-Mu'aybid's text and the recited consonants support «ثنى». His note at 31 reports Ibn Qutayba's explanation, turning one's speech away from a friend who is beginning to withdraw. At 41 the early *Kitab al-'Ayn* explicitly quotes Adi to illustrate «البخلة». The recorded last verb is «يُلهَد», put down or roughly handled. Al-Mu'aybid reports «يزهد» from other lexical witnesses, including al-Qurashi. [Ibn Faris, 3/349](https://shamela.ws/book/21710/1417) · [Commentary, 2/332](https://shamela.ws/book/18194/821) · [Diwan, p. 106](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n106/mode/1up) · [Al-'Ayn, 4/272](https://shamela.ws/book/1682/1380)

## Bayt 34 in Adi and Tarafa

Al-Mu'aybid brackets «وظلم ذوي القربى أشد مضاضة / على المرء من وقع الحسام المهند» as Adi 34 on p. 107. He names al-Tha'alibi's *al-Tamthil wa-l-muhadara* and Ibn Tabataba's *Iyar al-shi'r* as his sources for the addition. Al-Tha'alibi groups it under «عدي بن زيد» with «عن المرء لا تسأل» and the adjacent counsel. Ibn Tabataba gives it in his extended quotation of Adi's ode. Al-Bijawi's edition of the *Jamhara* also prints it as the **last bayt of Adi's poem**, no. 45, with «على النفس». His note 69 identifies the witnesses as أ and ع. The searchable text leaves out all three closing additions, including this one. [Diwan, p. 107 and note 34](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n107/mode/1up) · [Adi in the Jamhara, p. 398 and note 69](https://archive.org/details/20231218_20231218_0842/page/n397/mode/1up) · [Al-Tha'alibi, heading p. 52](https://shamela.ws/book/5683/50) · [Verse p. 53](https://shamela.ws/book/5683/51) · [Ibn Tabataba, pp. 105–107](https://shamela.ws/book/6911/101)

Under **Tarafa**, al-Bijawi prints the same verse as no. 86 on p. 333 with «على المرء», the phrase al-Nahari recites. Note 215 cites Ibn al-Anbari reporting Abu Ja'far's judgement that the bayt belongs to Adi. Al-Tabrizi likewise quotes and glosses it in Tarafa's ode and reports «قيل أن هذا البيت لعدي بن زيد العبادي وليس من هذه القصيدة». These witnesses establish transmission under both names. Priority remains uncertain. The recording and studio text place it at Adi 34. Al-Mu'aybid's apparatus reports related disputes for 32, «عن المرء لا تسأل», and for the counsel at 36. [Tarafa in the Jamhara, p. 333 and note 215](https://archive.org/details/20231218_20231218_0842/page/n332/mode/1up) · [Al-Tabrizi, printed p. 93](https://archive.org/details/3857pdf_202001/page/n92/mode/1up) · [Second printing, bayt 78 on p. 137](https://archive.org/details/9assaid10/page/n137/mode/1up) · [Al-Mu'aybid, pp. 106–107](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n106/mode/2up)

## Other readings

The printed and recorded «ذلّة» at 7.1 has only a small acoustic advantage over al-Qurashi's «لذّة». The ethics of ignorance as a source of shame support the former. At 10.1 «يزع» is a check or deterrent. At 15.1 the man who puts himself right in private has «يصلح», and at 17.2 his years stand «دون مولدي». At 19 the performed «الخنا» includes foul speech. At 22.2 he expects a friend to keep his good name «حفظ مشهد». Ibn Tabataba prints «تتزيّد» at 23 and expressly supplies «تتزنّد» as an alternative. Al-Nahari follows the second reading. His performance gives «ما» at 30.1 and «المرء» at 34.2. [Diwan, pp. 103–104](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n103/mode/2up) · [Ibn Tabataba, p. 106](https://shamela.ws/book/6911/102)

At 38.2 «فامدد» calls for generosity towards a complaining friend. The *Jamhara* has «فافتد». At 42.2 the diwan prints «فأرّخت» and records «وأوجب … المال» below it. At 43.1 «لذّات الفتى» includes a young man's pleasures. At 44 the rare «مِجلد» is explained as the cloth a wailing woman holds. At 46.1 the middle consonant of «زاجر» sounds z-like in both short transcriptions and running Audar. The continuous Cohere transcript gives «زاجر». The diwan, the *Jamhara* and the studio card have the word for a deterrent, and «زازر» supplies no such word. The paired note records the apparent substitution. At 48.1 the recitation distinctly has «تقوم قيامتي», followed in the next half by the people who would mourn him. [Diwan, pp. 107–109](https://archive.org/details/diwan-adi-ibn-zayd/page/n108/mode/1up) · [Jamhara, pp. 397–398](https://shamela.ws/book/1487/355)

## Metre and rhyme

Tawil, rhyming on د with kasra sustained at the line ending, as in معبدِ، التجلّدِ، المهندِ. Slashes divide the feet. The first foot is shortened to فعولُ in both halves of 1. The fourth foot of each half below is مفاعلن.
