# قَد قُلتُ لِلنَّفسِ الشَّعاعِ أَضُمُّها

Osama al-Nahari recites the poem. The upload is dated 2024-12-01. Source length 227.904 s. The poetry-only file keeps 1.83–227.904 s on the original clock and runs 226.074 s. Gain +6.2 dB, measured output -20.1 LUFS, peak -1.4 dBTP. [Source recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eOn1sGRd3-o)

## Editions, occasion and order

Al-Sharif al-Radi, *Diwan al-Sharif al-Radi*, Beirut, Dar Sadir / Dar Bayrut, I, **pp. 220–221**, headed «يا ضيعة الأمل», a phrase from verse 21. The Bombay Matba'at Nukhbat al-Akhbar impression (1306 AH), **pp. 100–101**, heads it with the occasion. Both state that he set out for Wasit to meet his father on his return from Fars in **395 AH**. The incipit titles this performance. The Beirut pages contain 11 and 14 verses, the Bombay pages 13 and 12. All **25** occur in the same order in the recording, once each. The men whose help disappointed him are unnamed. Their identity and the particular journey evoked by the sea image in 22 cannot be established from the rubric. [Beirut, p. 220](https://archive.org/details/20191017_20191017_2122/page/n219/mode/1up) · [p. 221](https://archive.org/details/20191017_20191017_2122/page/n220/mode/1up) · [Bombay, pp. 100–101](https://archive.org/details/3349pdf1306/page/n102/mode/2up) · [Umar Farrukh on the printings](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/13903/63?lang=ar)

Muhammad ibn Aydamir, *al-Durr al-farid wa-bayt al-qasid*, II, pp. 65–66, no. 166, quotes verse 20 as its lemma, then quotes much of the poem to illuminate «يختتي» and dates the father’s return. Its transmitted excerpt omits 7 and 22. Its explicit gloss is “feels shame or draws back”. The Beirut notes explain الشعاع as divided purposes, سُمَع as reputation, الخلاج as thoughts that contend in the heart, and الجلّة as fully grown or aged animals. The paired half-translations preserve the doubled «ملمّة» (5), the rhyme of death and a wish (10), and the rhymes sent off like riderless horses (18). [Ibn Aydamir, p. 65](https://shamela.ws/book/20762/558) · [p. 66](https://shamela.ws/book/20762/559)

## Disputed words and metre

At 6.1 the two printings give the short verb «تَخِذَ». The full context and word score (−16.38 against −24.93 for «واتّخذتكم») support it. At 12.2 the two printings and the quotation in *al-Durr* have **«ويقرح»**. In the paired image, green wood hardens and the young horse grows into a *qarih*, a stage identified by its teeth. Both full recognisers and both half-clip recognitions give «ويطرح». The model scores the latter 4.82 higher and the phone trace has a stop at 107.64 s which does not reliably distinguish the two Arabic consonants. The recording probably substitutes «يطرح», “casts off”. No object is expressed and this verb does not by itself name the stage of an animal’s growth. We retain the attested equine verb, whose action completes the parallel. Both language cards identify what the reciter appears to say. [Beirut, p. 221](https://archive.org/details/20191017_20191017_2122/page/n220/mode/1up) · [Lisan al-Arab, قرح](https://ar.wikisource.org/wiki/لسان_العرب/قبأ_-)

The noun in 13.2 is مَطِيَّة, a riding mount. Its possessed form is «مَطِيَّتي». The two diwan pages preserve the consonants. *Lisan al-ʿArab*, XV, p. 285, supplies the kasra of the dictionary form. The independent phone pass is indistinct inside the word. [Ibn Manzur, مطا](https://shamela.ws/book/1687/7896)

At 22.1 both editions print **«وسُرى السفائن»**, “and the ships’ night passage”, extending the preceding cry over wasted hopes. Full and clipped Cohere give the syntactically possible «وترى السفائنَ», “and you see the ships”. Continuous Audar gives «وسر السفائن», widened Audar «وسرى السفائن». At original 192.80 s the phone model marks an affricated/sibilant sound /ts/ between /wa/ and /a/. It does not isolate a definite plain t. The paired score favours «وترى» by 4.51, below five. We retain the common printed reading and identify the uncertainty on the word card. The adjacent *Durr* excerpt omits this verse. At 20.2 «يرعوي» and «يروي» differ by 1.48. Widened Audar gives «لا يرعوي» and the two editions support the sense “desists”. The last half has «بليّتي», with /bali…ti/ extending past 227 s. ## Earlier verification passes

Both initial logs and `verification-final/verify-before.log` record the following differences. The final isolated clips preserve the opening words at both addresses.

- **13.1, 110.35–114.75 s.** Both printings begin verse 13 with «يأس ثنى». Initial Cohere wrote «ياس تنا سنن المطالب عنكم», and full Audar ran the sounds together as «يأسن تناسنا». Full Cohere and the final half give «يأس ثنى سنن المطالب». Despair *turned* his petitions away from these men, then *bent* his mount’s neck homeward in 13.2. The two transitive verbs, ثنى and لوى, share their subject. The release clip ends 0.66 s later than the earlier one. The free phone trace gives a dental fricative /θ/ at 111.06 s where early Cohere wrote ت.
