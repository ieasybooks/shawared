## Editions

- **ديوان حسان بن ثابت**, Dar al-Kutub al-Ilmiyya (Beirut, 2nd ed., 1994), «بوركت يا قبر الرسول», pp. 60–64: 46 abyat with glosses. Scan: archive.org `20201105_20201105_0909`, leaves 59–63. This is the text the reciter follows, and the one in the Abu Dhabi encyclopedia (old shamela 4478, `diwan-old-shamela.txt`).
- **Ibn Hisham, السيرة النبوية**, ed. al-Saqqa, al-Abyari and Shalabi: shamela 23833, vol. 2, pp. 666–669 (`sira.txt`). The same 46 abyat, fully voweled, headed «وقال حسان بن ثابت يبكي رسول الله ﷺ، فيما حدثنا ابن هشام، عن أبي زيد الأنصاري», the first of the three elegies by Hassan that Ibn Hisham gives there; with the editors' glosses and the readings of their manuscript أ. Several readings differ (below).
- **Later books of the Prophet's life** that repeat it: al-Suhayli, *al-Rawd al-Unuf*, vol. 7, p. 564 (shamela 6864/3415); Ibn Kathir, *al-Bidaya wa-l-Nihaya* (ed. al-Turki), vol. 8, p. 175; al-Kala'i, *al-Iktifa*, vol. 2, pp. 72–73.

`abyat_in_full` is 46, as in both.

## Ear check

- **Alignment:** one weak word in 46 abyat (27.1 «إِذ», 0.33).
- **`scripts/verify.py`:** 37 of 94 clips differ from the text. One was a real reading, 24.1 (above). The rest are the transcriber's:
  - **ـة heard as ـات**, again and again (2.1 حرماتهم, 18 رزيات, 24 نعمات, الطريقات, 29.2 لغيبات, 32.1 الجمرات, 36.2 القيامات, 45.2 جنات): the same reciter's stretch of the short syllable that ends a shortened foot, seen in his «عيون المها» and «سلام عليكم». The reverse happens too: 4.1 «حجرات» as «حجره», 31.1 «فالموحشات» as «فالموحشه»; the meter needs the plurals.
  - **Words run together or split:** «فبينا هم» as «فبيناهم», «وما لك» as «وملاكي», «كنف» as «كان في», «مع المصطفى» as «ما علمصطفى», 11.2 «على طلل القبر» as «تعالي قال للقبر», 18.1 «رزية هالك» as «رزياتها لك», 23.1 «وإن ناب أمر» as «وانا بامر», 30.1 «قفارا سوى» as «قفرنس», 40.1 «وأمنع ذروات» as «وامني عذروات».
  - **A stretched final vowel heard as a pronoun:** 10.1 «عشيرهم», 26.1 «جناحهم», 43.1 «بكفهم».
  - **Misheard words:** 4.2 «ويوقد» as «ويقدر», 5.1 «تطمس» as «تقمس», 7.1 «ظللت» as «وللت», 8.2 «لها محصيا نفسي» as «ان الله محصي النفس», 12.1 «قبر» as «قدر», 15.2 «عشية» as «حاشيات», 37.2 «نائلا» as «الا», 42.1 «فاستتم» as «فاستمي», 44.2 «عازب» as «عاذب»; and spelling only: 2.1 «تمح», 5.2 «فالايه», 14.1 «التربه», 22.1 «الزله», 38.2 «ظنا».
  - **33.1**, where the clip drops «يا»; the full-recording transcript has «يا عيني», and the meter needs it.

## Commentary drawn on

- **Glosses** follow the Sira's editors and the Beirut edition's notes (الصفيح المنضّد, أسعد, يغور وينجد, لا يثني جناحه, الجمرة, يُتلد).
- **Quranic echoes:** bayt 16 and 28:35, 17 and 44:29, 25 and 9:128, 26 and 26:215, 42 and 93:6.
- **بلاد الحُرم (29):** the Sira's gloss makes it Mecca; abyat 30–31 are in Medina (the grave, al-Balat, al-Gharqad) and 32 in Mecca, so the notes read it as both sanctuaries and give the gloss as the other reading.
- **al-Balat:** al-Fayruzabadi, *al-Maghanim al-Mutaba fi Ma'alim Taba*, vol. 2, p. 684 (shamela 1172/682): paved ground in Medina between the mosque and the market.
- The poet's life follows al-Zirikli's *al-A'lam* (shamela 12286, vol. 2, pp. 175–176).

## Review

A second reader checked the text against both editions, the sharh and notes against the commentaries, and the intro and bio against the sources (2026-11-17). Fixed after the review:

- **Text:** 24.1 «وَسطَهُم», by ear (gap 16.1), for the editions' «بَينَهُم»; 32.2 «وَعَرصاتٌ» for the meter; and 38.2 «يُتلَدُ», the Sira's, by ear (gap 5.2).
- **Place in 29–32:** بلاد الحُرم read as Mecca and Medina.
- **Sharh:** rewritten in both languages at the depth of the first poem: the Quranic echoes, the wordplay in 28, 42 and 45, the image in 44, and no sharh repeating its note.
- **Notes:** 15.1 حِلماً, 24.1 وَسطَهُم, 32.1 ثَمَّ, 38.2 يُتلَدُ, 46.1 المُصطَفى and the editions' readings in 3.1, 5.1 and 26.1 added; 8.1 آلاء, 14.2 أسعد, 29.1, 32.1 and 43.2 corrected; heads that repeated the word dropped. The English notes went from 8 to 27.
- **English:** 6.2, 14.2, 15.1, 21.2, 28.2, 30 and 35.2 retranslated.
- **Intro:** a verb in the first sentence, Ibn Hisham's placing of the poem, the death and the address to the eye, and "in Medina" for where Hassan defended the Prophet.
- **sources.md:** the later books named, every reading where the editions differ, and each `verify.py` flag explained.
