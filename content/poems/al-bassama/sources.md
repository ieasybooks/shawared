## Editions

- **عبد الواحد المراكشي, المعجب في تلخيص أخبار المغرب**: shamela 8559, printed pp. 62–68 (`muajjab.txt`). The whole poem, 75 abyat, with the editor's glosses. This is the text the reciter follows, in its order and in nearly every word.
- **ابن بدرون, شرح قصيدة ابن عبدون المعروفة بالبسامة** (Cairo, 1340 AH): archive.org `AAlexandrina-137835`, read as page images. The commentary (pp. 5–297) quotes the abyat in its own order, each before its story, as far as bayt 42 here (al-Muqtadir), with glosses from Ibn al-Athir's abridgement in the footnotes; it then quotes the elegy (45–68) without comment (pp. 297–298). The whole poem is printed again on pp. 299–302: 69 lines, ending with the two lines of blessing the reciter says. It lacks abyat 19, 22, 34, 36, 55, 70 and 71 and has «ثلاثة ما رأى العصران مثلهم». Dozy's Leiden edition of the same commentary (1846, archive.org `3943pdf`) was checked at p. 5 and for the Habbaba line, which it also lacks.
- **ابن بسام, الذخيرة**, ed. Ihsan Abbas: shamela 1035, vol. 4, pp. 721–724 (`dhakhira.txt`). 58 lines, one of them supplied by the editor; his notes compare Ibn Badrun's text («شرح البسامة») and al-Fawat.
- **ابن دحية, المطرب من أشعار أهل المغرب**: shamela 93, pp. 27–33 (`mutrib.txt`). The whole poem, 76 abyat, voweled; close to al-Mu'jib in the elegy (45–71).
- **ابن شاكر الكتبي, فوات الوفيات**: shamela 1003, vol. 2, pp. 388–391 (`fawat.txt`); **لسان الدين بن الخطيب, الإحاطة**: shamela 11682, vol. 4, pp. 32–37 (`ihata.txt`). Consulted for single readings.

`abyat_in_full` is 77: al-Mu'jib's 75 and the two lines of blessing from the end of Ibn Badrun's Cairo text. The abyat of al-Mu'jib the reciter leaves out: «وفي ابن هند وفي ابن المصطفى حسن» and «فبعضنا قائل ما اغتاله أحد» (after 28), «وأعملت في لطيم الجن حيلتها» (after 32), «ثلاثة ما رأى السعدان مثلهم» (after 53).

- **37.1** is a coin toss by score. Both transcripts (the full one and the clip) hear «نائية», and it is the reading of al-Mu'jib, whose text the reciter follows, so the text keeps it; the note gives نابية and its sense.
- **9.2 «عَن»** is between 2 and 5; both transcripts hear «عن» («ذكراك عن خبري», «ذكرك عن خابرها»).
- **33.1 and 62.2**, where the reciter departs from every edition: every transcript hears «قضية» at 33.1 (cohere and audar on a wide clip, and the full transcript) and «الخاضر» at 62.2 (both backends), and the scores agree. The word notes give the editions' readings. At 62.2 al-Mu'jib's «الحضر» (the city of al-Hadr, a fallen kingdom like Ad's) would suit the poem well; the reciter's al-Khidr is read in the sharh as the immortal of legend.
- **Other readings the transcript settles without a score** (the editions' variants are of a different length): 16.1 «وَلَم تَرُدَّ» (al-Dhakhira, al-Fawat: «وما أعادت»), 49.1 «وَطَوَّقَت» (al-Mutrib: «وطُرِّزت»), 64–66.1 «مَن لي وَلا مَن بِهِم» (al-Mutrib: «من لي ومن لهم»), 17.1 «وَإِخوَتَهُم عَبساً» (al-Dhakhira, al-Fawat: «وجيرتهم لخما»), 26.2 «إِلّا» (al-Fawat: «غير»), 29.2 «يَبُؤ» (al-Dhakhira: «يبر»), 39.2 «بِريقِ الصّارِمِ الذَّكَرِ» (al-Mutrib: «بكأس الصاب والصبر»), 44.2 «لَم تَنفِر مِنَ الذُّعُرِ» (Ibn Badrun and the others: «من بيض ومن سمر»), 45.1 «ما بَرِحَت» (al-Mu'jib: «لا نُزِلت»), 46.2 «غابِرِ» (Ibn Badrun: «مقبل»; al-Fawat: «سالف»), 50–51 in al-Mu'jib's order and words, 52.1 «وَيبَ … البَأسِ» (Ibn Badrun: «ويح … الجود»), 54.1 «اِرتَقى», 60.1 «مَضَوا» (Ibn Badrun: «نأوا»), 66.2 «يَدعو» (Ibn Badrun: «يفضي»), 68.1 «أَمَلٌ» (Ibn Badrun, al-Fawat: «طمع»).
- **Meter and harakat:**
  - 16.2 «حُجُرِ» for the name Hujr, and 30.2 «زُفَرِ», take the rhyme's kasra;
  - 21.1 «رُستُمٍ», 36.1 «حَبابَةٌ» and 36.2 «وَأَحمَرٌ» need their tanwin for the meter (al-Mutrib writes the last two so);
  - 25.1 «عُثمانٍ» scans with or without tanwin; the text follows al-Mutrib;
  - 49.2 «الذِّكَرِ» (plural of ذِكرة) is the only form the meter allows;
  - 46.1 «لِيَومِكُمُ», 62.1 «فَاستَهوَتهُمُ», 44.1 «لَهُمُ» and 49.1 «بيضَهُمُ» take the long mim of the plural (kumū, humū);

## Ear check

- **Alignment:** the timeline comes from the earlier draft's alignment of the whole app audio. 15 weak words, all particles (مِن, ما, إِن, أَو, اِبنِ); the weakest is 54.1 «ما» (0.10), which runs into «ارتقى» («ثلاثة مرتقى»). Three words were changed after it (9.2 «عَن», 33.1 «قاضِيَةً», 62.2 «الخَضِرِ»), which move no half's start or end, so it was not run again.
- **72–73** are said twice at the end (648.7–666.3 s and 666.5–685.0 s); the full transcript and the clips of both rounds hear them.
- **`scripts/verify.py`:** 34 of 150 clips differ from the text, each the transcriber's:

  - **words run together or split:** 2.2 «بيننا بالليث», 8.2 «الجنيمن», 11.2 «لبنينان», 16.1 «الظل لصحاتهم», 31.2 «المختارف», 63.1 «ويل امه» (the same word), 68.1 «يرجوع سواله», 44.1 «لعلي همهم»;

## Commentary drawn on

- **Names and stories** follow Ibn Badrun (Cairo pages): Dara and Alexander 16–27; the Sasanids and the Greeks 35–60; Tasm and Jadis 66; Yemen and Mudar 86–106; Saba' 106–112; Kulayb and Muhalhil 112–121 («بين سمع الأرض والبصر», 121); Imru' al-Qays and Hujr 123; Dhubyan, Abs and al-Haba'a 126–131; Adi ibn Zayd and al-Nu'man 132–136; Khubayb 136; Ja'far and Hamza 140; Yazdajird 142–144; Rustam, Dhu al-Hajib (Khurrazad) and Sa'd 144–149; Uthman and al-Zubayr 149–154; Ammar and the ضيح 158; Ibn Muljam and Ali 159; Amr and Kharija 169; Abu Anas and Zufar 185–186; Ibn Ziyad and Muhalhil's «بؤ بشسع نعل كليب» 186–189; Mus'ab, al-Mukhtar and Kufa as «الشاهقة» 189–195; Ibn al-Zubayr 195; Abd al-Malik, «قاضبة» and al-Latim 199–206; al-Walid ibn Yazid 206–211; al-Saffah and Marwan 211–223; Fakhkh and his objection to it 223–225; the Barmakids and the inversion in bayt 39, 226; al-Amin and the oaths in the Kaaba 246–249; al-Mutawakkil and al-Muntasir 260–263, 287; the titles of 42–43 and those who bore them, 263–297; the Abbasids and their Turks (bayt 44) 289; al-Musta'in and al-Mu'tazz 292.
- **Glosses** from Ibn al-Athir's notes in the Cairo footnotes (غرب, عضب, أُثُر, ناقض المرر, رائح ومبتكر, قارعة, الغيل والظلّام للجزر, نابية, ريق الصارم, لعا, زبّاء as a calamity) and from al-Mu'jib's editor (الغير, الضيح, الغابر, الشجا, ويب, لم يربع, لم يحر, السدر, الشرائع, الشقاشق).
- **Lines not in Ibn Badrun:** 19 and 22 from the general histories (Ibn Badrun's own story of Yazdajird's death at Merv, under bayt 20, serves 19); 34 (Zayd ibn Ali) from al-Mu'jib's editor; 36 (Habbaba) from Ibn al-Jawzi, *al-Muntazam*, vol. 7, p. 111 (shamela 12406/2338, `muntazam.txt`). Nobody consulted explains «وأحمر قطّرته نفحة القطر»; the sharh takes the red thing as the seed itself, and says so.
- **The proverb «كلّ أزبّ نفور»** (44.2): al-Maydani, *Majma' al-Amthal*, vol. 2, p. 133, no. 2996 (shamela 12929/3080).
- **The Aftasids and the occasion:** al-Mu'jib, printed pp. 61–62 (al-Muzaffar's anthology, al-Mutawakkil's verse and prose, his death with al-Fadl and al-Abbas «في غرة سنة ٤٨٥»).
- **The verdict quoted in the intro:** al-Mu'jib, p. 62.
- **The poet's life:** al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 4, p. 149 (shamela 12286/3414, `alam.txt`), which gives his death as 529 AH and notes 520 and 527 in other sources.
