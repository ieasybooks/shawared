## Text, attribution and commentary

- Abu Ali al-Qali, *al-Amali*, I, pp. 280–281, from his explicit reading of this poem to Abu Bakr ibn Durayd under al-Muqanna' al-Kindi's name. This is the base for the count and order. The alternate pagination at I, p. 284 supplies كيف at 2.1, عمي at 7.2 and بنحس at 11.1 where one electronic text drops a word or mispoints letters. Al-Qali follows the poem with Ibn Durayd's observation on the transitivity of كسب, relevant to the relatives **earning** praise in 1.2. [P. 280](https://shamela.ws/book/9160/280) · [P. 281](https://shamela.ws/book/9160/281) · [Parallel edition, p. 284](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/1772/284)
- Abu Ubayd al-Bakri, *Simt al-la'ali*, I, pp. 615–617, comments on al-Qali's passage, quotes تداينت in the opening, and prints وما in 3.2. Al-Bakri records Ibn al-Sikkit's attribution to Hatim of a differently ordered version with a different opening. Although verses 2–3 stand in the extant *Amali* and are heard here, al-Bakri says that neither al-Qali nor Ibn al-Sikkit transmitted the two verses about poverty and prosperity in the accounts he discusses. His explanation of the horse at the door as a prized animal its owner keeps constantly in view informs verse 6. The studio card gives another interpretation, a horse that lets visitors recognise a generous household. [P. 615](https://shamela.ws/book/1586/613) · [P. 616](https://shamela.ws/book/1586/614) · [P. 617](https://shamela.ws/book/1586/615)
- Sadr al-Din al-Basri, *al-Hamasa al-Basriyya*, II, pp. 30–31, no. 78, transmits fifteen verses under al-Muqanna's name. Its sequence in our numbering is 1, 4, 3, 5–14, followed by two verses of praise beginning على أن قومي and بفضل وأحلام. It supplies ثاوياً at 14.1 and the past-tense variants at 9. Neither the two added verses nor an extra bayt between 10 and 11 occurs in this recording. The verse-by-verse Arabic and English explanations follow al-Qali's order and read the generosity comparisons in the light of al-Bakri's discussion. [P. 30](https://shamela.ws/book/6901/313) · [P. 31](https://shamela.ws/book/6901/314)
- Al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, VI, pp. 319–320, gives Muhammad ibn Umayra's genealogy, Wadi Daw'an as his birthplace, variant forms of his father's name, and competing explanations of his epithet. He notes attributions of some shared verses to Hatim and Muhriz ibn Sharik, and al-Suli's preference for al-Muqanna'. The two biographies retain his approximate date, around 70 AH (690 CE), in prose. [P. 319](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/5907) · [P. 320](https://shamela.ws/book/12286/5908)

The opening sadr scans فعولُ مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. At 8.1, أراهم إلى نصري بطاءً وإن همُ gives فعولن مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. The metre is tawil and the rhyme consonant is dal, before the sustained final alif.

## Flag review

The initial verification flagged four of 28 clipped halves. At 4.2 Cohere invents a repetition of المؤمنون. Audar recovers the full ثغور حقوق ما أطاقوا لها سدا after a stray lead-in, and both whole-recording transcripts contain it. At 11.1 the wider context and scores above settle the pronoun. At 14.1 Audar resolves Cohere's دا مثاوياً into دام ثاوياً. At 14.2 Audar retains the separate لي غيرها, though it misrecognises شيمة. The complete line is on the last card and in al-Qali and al-Basri. These are the original PCM and aligned-clip checks, using acoustic transcriptions and variant scores. Direct playback to the editor was unavailable.

Al-Qali’s note immediately after the poem discusses whether the verb meaning to earn something for someone else takes the form «أكسب» or «كسب». The card at 1.2.4 gives his acceptance of both forms.
