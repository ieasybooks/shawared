Kashida: every half the same width

The poem «تَقولُ بُثَينَةُ لَمّا رَأَت»: every half is set to the width of the longest (9.23 em), the sadr at the start, the ajuz at the end. Kashida go only where Raqim's Naskh rules allow; a space is normally 0.29 em and a tatweel 0.185 em.
