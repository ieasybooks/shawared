# ما في وُقوفِكَ ساعَةً مِن باسِ

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari on his own channel, 25 February 2025. The 285.071 s source keeps 5.70–279.20 s (273.50 s), from the first address to the companion through «وبنيت فوق أساس». The opening title and closing tail fall outside it. The performance has all 34 bayts in order, with no repeated half. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WPBePNOgBQc)

## Editions and collation

- Al-Khatib al-Tibrizi, *Sharh Diwan Abi Tammam*, ed. Muhammad Abduh Azzam, II, no. 81, pp. 242–252. The heading names Ahmad ibn al-Mu'tasim. We compared the verse text and the readings below the rule on all eleven pages. The 34-bayt text is the one counted here. The opening uses «باس» with a long vowel in place of the hamza in «بأس». Al-Tibrizi explains how this fits the rhyme, which is also heard in «الكاس» and «الراس». [Beginning](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n730/mode/1up) · [Middle](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n735/mode/1up) · [End](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n740/mode/1up)
- Al-A'lam al-Shantamari, *Sharh Diwan Abi Tammam*, ed. Ibrahim Nadin, II, no. 62, pp. 75–79. Its 35 verses were compared directly with Azzam’s 34. At 1 it has «هل في وقوفك» and a second-person «تقضي». In 2, «تجود ... فيه» stands against the recited «تعين ... منه». His 6 gives «بادرة الهوى / خطأً» beside «بادرة النوى / ولعاً». At 7 he has «برملة ميعاس», at 9 «حمي الفراق», at 12 «بها بنو العباس» and at 16 «المشتري», the man who buys praise with his wealth, as its gloss on p. 77 explains. He brings the shared milk and wine (his 18) before the growing branch and the soil (his 19–20). The recording puts them in the reverse order, carrying forward the soil’s effect on the shoot. [Opening](https://archive.org/details/3902pdf_202001/page/n642/mode/1up) · [Reordered passage](https://archive.org/details/3902pdf_202001/page/n644/mode/1up) · [Close](https://archive.org/details/3902pdf_202001/page/n646/mode/1up)
- On p. 79 of al-Shantamari, «عدت الهموم على عدوي بالذي» introduces a different close. After it stands an additional bayt, «أمل من الآمال أحكم فتله / فكأنه مرس من الأمراس», which compares firmly established hope to a twisted rope. His «عدل الرجاء على الحناء» follows. Al-Nahari’s card actually mentions a variant in 31, but he performs «غلب السرور على همومي» and «عدل المشيب على الشباب» and proceeds to the marks of striving and age without the rope verse. Both families end with the foundation. The numbered witness transcriptions and collation are kept under `media/research/ma-fi-wuqufika/`.

Other variants in Azzam’s apparatus matter to the images. In 5 the studio has «الترائب», the upper chest bright with ornaments, where «الشمائل» would give the woman smiling qualities. In 7 the flash of teeth lights «نَور الأقاحي في ثرى ميعاس», and the soft soil returns in the cultivated family imagery. At 8 «بصدرك» and «كثرة الوسواس» carry the noise from jewellery into the lover’s breast. The apparatus also records «بقلبك» and «شدة». At 19 the studio uses feminine «منبتها ... عليها» against al-Shantamari’s «طينته ... عليه بقاس». At 20 it clearly prints «بينهما», with the two participants meeting in the simile of milk-kinship. At 27 the studio’s «أُنتجت» makes the sparks give birth to a fire. Al-Shantamari’s «أنجمت» makes one appear like a star.

For the four exemplars in 23 and the objection behind 24–25, compare al-Tibrizi II, p. 250, al-Shantamari, p. 78, and Ibn Khallikan, *Wafayat al-a'yan*, II, p. 15. The men are Amr ibn Ma'dikarib, Hatim al-Ta'i, al-Ahnaf ibn Qays and Iyas ibn Mu'awiya. Al-Kindi is said to have objected to comparing the prince to his inferiors. Ibn Khallikan distinguishes al-Suli’s reported exchange from an embellished story about a caliph, an appointment and the poet’s death after forty days, which he explicitly rejects. The explanation of the niche and lamp refers to Qur’an 24.35. The lexical notes distinguish the sword’s watered pattern, the garment, the saddlecloth, and the different sorts of light used in these comparisons. [Al-Tibrizi](https://archive.org/details/3901pdf/page/n738/mode/1up) · [Ibn Khallikan](https://shamela.ws/book/1000/487) · [Qur’an 24.35](https://quran.com/24/35)

At 20.1 the acoustic scores for «بينهما» and «بينها» are virtually identical. Al-Shantamari also reads the dual, although he applies it to Ahmad and the praise he longs for. The numbered studio card supplies the dual’s missing consonant more securely than the recognisers here. At 7.2 the acoustic model compares two words with a single word. The card and the editions’ two locative phrases give independent evidence for that decision.

## Metre and rhyme

Kamil, with sin as rawiy. In 1 the sadr «ما في وقوفك ساعة من باس» scans **– – u – | u u – u – | – – –** and «نقضي ذمام الأربع الأدراس» **– – u – | – – u – | – – –**. The opening sadr takes the curtailed ending of its rhyming companion. In 17 «والحمد برد جمال اختالت به» gives **– – u – | u u – u – | – – u –**. Its ajuz «غرر الفعال وليس برد لباس» gives **u u – u – | u u – u – | u u – –**. The audible long i after the rhyme consonant is the release of the kasra.

## Clip verification

The final verification on the master flagged 13 of 68 performed halves. The narrow transcriptions differed on 20 of 329 words (6.1%). The wider transcriptions are independent Audar decodes. The decisions compare these clips with the full-source transcriptions, the numbered studio cards and the literary witnesses.

| Half | Narrow clip | Wider clip | Editorial decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5.2 | ارهاف خوط البانه المياسر | فاطرها فخوط البانة المياس | The wide clip gives المياس, the branch swaying. The narrow r has no support in the printed simile or in the studio text. |
| 7.2 | نور الاقاحي في ثري الميعاسي | نور الأقاح في ثرى ميعاسي | The studio writes الأقاحي في ثرى ميعاس and al-Tibrizi gives that phrase. The wider engine shortens the flower name but loses the spurious article of the narrow clip. The extra written y at the rhyme represents its release. |
| 9.2 | قد خلط الساق به والحاسب | قد خلط الساقي به والحاسي | The wider output restores الساقي and الحاسي, the server and drinker. The shared cup leaves both bewildered. حاسب in the narrow clip introduces an unrelated third profession. |
| 13.1 | القوم ظل الله اسكن دينهم | القوم ذل الله أسكن دينه في قبره | The wider clip recovers دينه. In both print and studio the singular possessor is God. The following فيهم locates the faith among the people just named. |
| 15.1 | هدات علي تاميل احمد همه | أداءت على تأمين أحمد همتي | The wide clip restores همتي, with the speaker’s first-person ending. His reliance on Ahmad is carried on by «تقليدي ... وقياسي». |
| 16.2 | بالحمد والحاليب والكاس | الحمد والحالي به والكاسي | The wider clip separates الحالي به والكاسي. The initial ل belongs to «المسترى للحمد», which crosses the caesura. The studio and al-Tibrizi both show للحمد. |
| 19.1 | لا تهجر الانواء من بتها ولا | لا تهجر الأنواء من بيتها ولا قد | The engines divide منبتها at its initial syllable. The studio has the single noun for the shoot’s growing place. Al-Shantamari’s alternative طينته would also be one noun. |
| 20.1 | وكان بينهما رضاع السديم | وكأن بينهما رضى عثدي من طرده | A fair copy of the milk-kinship simile needs «رضاع الثدي من / فرط التصافي». The wide clip retains the sounds of الثدي but divides them badly. «من» governs the opening noun of the next half. |
| 21.2 | نشر الخزامي في اخضرار الاسير | نشر الخزامى في اخضرار الآسي | The studio identifies الآس as myrtle and labels it separately beside lavender. The sustained final kasra helps produce الأسير or الآسي in the clips. |
| 22.1 | ابليت هذا المجد ابعد غايتي | أبليت هذا المجد أبعد غاية | The wide clip restores أبعد غاية, an object measuring the limit of honour. The narrow engine turns the inflected noun into “my end”. |
| 23.1 | اقدام عم | وأيضا إقدام عمر في سماحة حاتم | The wide clip recovers both names and سماحة. Al-Tibrizi, al-Shantamari, Ibn Khallikan and the studio all pair Amr’s courage with Hatim’s generosity. |
| 29.1 | امددته في العدم والعدم الجوار | أمدته في العدم والعدم الجوى | The wider clip recovers الجوى, the inward malady. «الطبيب الآسي» in 29.2 supplies its healer. الجوار at the end of the short clip has no such counterpart. |
| 31.1 | غلب السرور علي همومي | غلب السرور على همومي بالذي | The wider clip restores بالذي. Its relative clause is completed across the caesura by «أظهرت من برّي». The card mentions the variant about distress visiting the poet’s foe, then prints the performed wording in the body. |
