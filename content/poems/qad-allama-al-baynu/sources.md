# قد علّم البين منا البين أجفانا

Al-Waaedh captions the upload «وهكذا كنت في أهلي وفي وطني», the opening of bayt 11. He starts at «قد علّم البين منا البين أجفانا», follows 1–14 in order and repeats **both halves of 14**. The original-clock transcripts, the 30 entries in the performance plan and the sequence in both commentaries agree. Between «تهدي البوارق» and the upload title he also says «إذا قدمت على الأهوال» and «أبدو فيسجد». There is no internal jump. Nothing after «ولو حملت إليّ الدهر ملآنا» is performed.

## Editions and commentary

- Al-Wahidi, *Sharh Diwan al-Mutanabbi*, pp. 137–138. The rubric immediately before the opening names Abu Sahl Sa'id ibn Abd Allah ibn al-Hasan al-Antaki al-Himsi. Al-Wahidi explains the glimpsed wrist as a way of delaying the departure, the folds of musk at 6 and the heart that deserts its owner at 9. His next lemma after 14 starts «لا يجذبن ركابي نحوه أحد». [p. 137](https://shamela.ws/book/754/137) · [p. 138](https://shamela.ws/book/754/138)
- *Mu'jiz Ahmad*, transmitted under Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri’s name, pp. 155–156. The two passages agree on the extent and the start of 15. The line «إذا أبصروني طالعاً من ثنية» inside the discussion of 10 is an illustration by Jamil. It is distinguished from the running text by «ومثله لجميل». The explanations use this commentary for «إهواناً» and the recipient’s wish to earn praise through his own generosity. [p. 155](https://shamela.ws/book/753/155) · [p. 156](https://shamela.ws/book/753/156)
- Abu al-Ala al-Ma'arri, *al-Lami' al-Azizi*, pp. 1373–1376, comments selectively on this opening. It confirms both iterations of «البين», explains «حشياناً» from shortness of breath and prints «الدهر ملآنا». The note to Ibn Fadl Allah al-Umari’s *Masalik al-absar*, XV, p. 93, expressly counts **41** in the full poem and gives its incipit. The recording leaves out 15–41. [*Al-Lami'*, p. 1373](https://shamela.ws/book/29591/1365) · [p. 1376](https://shamela.ws/book/29591/1368) · [*Masalik*, XV, p. 93 n. 2](https://shamela.ws/book/11790/5319)

The two extracted series in the research folder contain the fourteen recorded bayts and the first unperformed bayt as an end check. The digital Wahidi has «البيت» for the second «البين» in 1.1, although the explanation depends on parting between the eyelids. It also has «إلى الجهر» at 14.2, while the explanation itself says «إليّ الدهر». At 10.1 the digital *Mu'jiz* transposes letters to «فسيجد» while its explanation says «يسجد لي». At 4.2 al-Wahidi gives the alternative «خشياناً», fearful in an unfamiliar journey. Both the acoustic comparison and the independent commentaries support the breathless «حشياناً».

## Meter and rhyme

Basit with nun as rawiy and the released alif after it. In the first sadr the four feet break at «قَدْ عَلْلَمَلْ / بَيْنُ مِنْ / نَلْبَيْنَ أَجْ / فَانَا», giving مستفعلن فاعلن مستفعلن فعْلن with the opening rhyme. At 8.1 «تُهْدِلْبَوَا / رِقُ أَخْ / لَافَلْمِيَا / هِ لَكُمْ» gives مستفعلن فعِلن مستفعلن فعِلن.
