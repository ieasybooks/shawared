# يوم الفراق لقد خلقت طويلا

## Recording and occasion

Osama al-Nahari recites the complete panegyric for Nuh ibn Amr al-Saksaki. The two diwan headings identify Nuh, and al-Suli adds «من كندة». The name of his father returns at 28. In «نوح بن عمرو» at 20.1, «بن» has the conventional spelling between two personal names. The checker flags the unvowelled initial b. All thirty bayts are heard once in order. The source interval 2.08–308.90 s preserves voice from about 2.60 to 308.42 s, with one continuous passage between the opening address to the day of separation and the final wish about a friend. The twice-spoken «تراها» in 17 belongs to one half. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z7bLRuPwHTk)

## Diwans and commentary

- Al-Khatib al-Tibrizi, *Diwan Abi Tammam bi-sharh al-Khatib al-Tibrizi*, ed. Muhammad Abduh Azzam, Dar al-Ma'arif, III, no. 123, pp. 66–71. Page 66 prints 1–6. Its apparatus gives «جعلت», «صبراً», «يجد», «نفَسي», «تلذّذاً» and «أيسر». Page 68 prefers accusative «الرزقَ» at the head of a sentence with a prohibitive verb and resumptive pronoun. The note records a nominative reading. At 15 the commentary identifies the ostrich in «ابن البيضة» and distinguishes the crossing from its usual effect on a timid bird. On pp. 69–70 the commentary transmitted from Abu al-Ala' considers seeing with the eye and seeing with the mind in 17. It records several quite different verbs in 18, then two identifications of Ubayd. The note on 23, p. 71, distinguishes a tribe claiming one father from a company that may come from several lines. - Abu Bakr al-Suli, *Sharh al-Suli li-Diwan Abi Tammam*, ed. Khalaf Rashid Nu'man, Iraqi Ministry of Culture and Arts, 1978, II, no. 127, pp. 290–296. The printed heading and first nine lines are on p. 290. Al-Suli’s main text has «لم يجد» in 2 and «أهل البخل» in 25. At 18 he identifies Ubayd with al-Ra'i al-Numayri, a poet especially skilled at describing camels, and treats Shadqam and Jadil as two breeding males whose qualities such a rider would forget. An alternative preserved in the other commentary makes Ubayd a camel doctor. At 26 al-Suli prints «حبوت», “you bestowed”, and Nu'man records «حويت», “you acquired”. The page ends the poem at 30 before a new numbered heading on the next page. [Printed pp. 290–296](https://archive.org/download/diwan-abo-tamam-shareh-alsouly/Diwan%20Abo%20Tamam%20Shareh%20Alsouly%202.pdf#page=292)
- Al-Amidi, *al-Muwazana bayna shi'r Abi Tammam wa-l-Buhturi*, II, pp. 14–15, quotes the first bayt. He compares it with al-Buhturi’s claim that the day of farewell is short. In the latter lover’s circumstances, the coming departure offers a rare chance to see someone otherwise kept out of sight. Abu Tammam’s day lasts long enough to cost him his composure and his reason. The final words of 30 adapt the regret for a chosen friend in Qur'an 25.28. Abu Tammam makes it something Nuh’s friend could never say. [Al-Amidi](https://shamela.ws/book/26183/574)

The poet mounts an argument for contentment immediately before addressing his riding camel. The change of subject is abrupt enough for the commentators to remark on it. The image of a messenger at 14 helps explain what the journey has become. Livelihood needs no one to fetch it, yet this rider and his camel have somewhere to go. In the praise section the loss Nuh keeps inflicting is on his own wealth, and generosity is his agent with authority to dispose of it.

## Metre and variant readings

Kamil with lam as rawiy and a long a after it. The halves of 1 scan – – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – – and – – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – ⏑ – | – – –. At 16.1 «بنت الفضاء متى تخد بك لا تدع» scans – – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – ⏑ –, and at 16.2 «في الصدر منك على الفلاة غليلا» gives – – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – ⏑ – | ⏑ ⏑ – –.

In 1 the recording favours «خلقت» over «جعلت» by 20.41 and «جلداً» over «صبراً» by 20.73. In 3.2 «نَفسي» leads «روحي» by 18.24 and the apparatus’s «نَفَسي» by 6.62. In 4.1 «تلدّداً» leads «تلذّذاً» by 20.79. The lover is at a loss and cannot find consolation. In 6.1 «أسهل» leads «أيسر» by 22.84, in 8.2 «أمسى» leads «أضحى» by 14.22, and in 9.2 «مع الهوى» leads «صبر الهوى» by 32.96. At 10.1 the contest between «بالزمان» and «للزمان» is close, 0.98 for b. The wide context in Audar, the text of both editions and the idiom «أخذ فلاناً بكذا» support the b. At 12.2 «الأماني» leads the truncated «الأمان» by 4.27. Wishing is the poor food on which an ambition starves.

The case of «الرزقَ» in 14.1 is an editorial choice supported by al-Tibrizi’s explanation. The nominative leads the accusative by 1.14, too little to decide a short vowel. At 15.2 «يوحش» leads «توحش» by 7.63. The crossing itself need cause no alarm. At 16.2 «الفلاة» leads «الفضاء» by 10.71. At 18.2 the comparison barely distinguishes the vowels of «لأُنسيَ» from «لأنسى» (0.45), but «لأُنسيَ» is visible on the reciter’s card and gives the rider as the person made to forget. The three transmitted alternatives «لزنّى», «لعنّف» and «لرثّى» fall behind it by 8.84, 12.52 and 15.83. The commentary takes the last as an expression of pity for the two sires.

In the closing run, «أرض» leads al-Suli’s «أهل» by 12.82, «حبوت» leads the variant «حويت» by 15.21, «يتّرك» leads «يترك» by 9.03 and «في الناس» leads «في الأرض» by 28.25. Both continuous transcripts and the isolated Cohere clip prefix a conjunction at 29.1. Both editions and the reciter’s card at 291 s give «اشدد», which leads «واشدد» by 10.49.

## The 60 recorded halves

The initial narrow pass flagged fourteen halves. Twelve remain in the final pass. The separate edge flag at 4.2 occurs in both. The wider Audar readings, the complete recording transcripts and the video cards supplement the acoustic comparisons.

After the held syllables were included, the final clips recover «كم وقعة» at 24.1 and «من اللّهى» at 26.1. At 20.1 Cohere still attaches an article to Nuh’s name after «لا تدعونّ». Both commentators name Nuh ibn Amr in their headings, and the written and continuous readings agree with them. Twelve of the 60 final clips have a machine difference.
