## Text and occasion

- Muhammad Abu al-Fadl Ibrahim, ed., *Diwan Imri' al-Qays*, Dar al-Ma'arif, pp. 65–66. Verse 34, «بكى صاحبي», closes p. 65. Verse 35, «فقلت له», opens p. 66. The first note identifies Amr ibn Qami'a al-Yashkuri, accompanying the poet to Caesar and longing for home at the frontier. The second explains that reaching Caesar would help them reclaim the kingdom and that death could intervene after they had exhausted their efforts. The introductions, translations and word cards follow that explanation. [p. 65](https://archive.org/details/20200722_20200722_1101/page/n64/mode/1up) · [p. 66](https://archive.org/details/20200722_20200722_1101/page/n65/mode/1up)
- Abd al-Rahman al-Mustawi, ed., *Diwan Imri' al-Qays*, Dar al-Ma'rifa, second edition, 2004, **pp. 93–98**. The two verses occur together on p. 96 between «ولو شاء كان الغزو» and «وإني زعيم». Counting the verse lines from the incipit to the end gives 60. The recording takes 34–35 and omits 1–33 and 36–60. Both editions give «وأيقن أنّا لاحقان بقيصرا» and «نحاول ملكاً أو نموت فنعذرا». [Opening](https://shamela.ws/book/27112/82) · [p. 96](https://shamela.ws/book/27112/85) · [Ending](https://shamela.ws/book/27112/87)

The long vowel the singer sustains in «لا تبكِ عينك» can give the recogniser «تبكي». The subject is «عينُك» and the verb after prohibitive لا is jussive, «تبكِ», in both witnesses. «أنّا» joins the two travellers, «مُلكاً» means sovereignty and «فنُعذرا» is passive. These details shape the two voices in the paired translation.

## Metre and rhyme

Tawil, with rāʾ as the rhyme consonant and a released final alif. In the first sadr the four feet divide «بَكى صا | حِبي لَمّا | رَأَدْدَرْ | بَ دونَهُو» as فعولن مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. The final ajuz divides «نُحاوِ | لُ مُلْكَنْ أَوْ | نَموتَ | فَنُعْذَرا», with the shortened فعولُ in the first and third positions.
