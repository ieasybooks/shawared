# أمن المنون وريبها تتوجع

## Recording and order of the takes

Osama al-Nahari recites the elegy for Abu Dhu'ayb's sons. The original lasts 780.469 s. The continuous retained range is 4.12–778.82 s. The two internal alignment breaks at edited 258.12 s (23.1 next) and 506.09 s (46.1 next) fall in original-clock silences 262.027–262.460 and 510.020–510.399 respectively. No poetry or pause inside the performance was removed at those breaks. [Original recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TJV7KAKYFPM)

The opening runs through the father's answer, the attempts to save his sons and the contrast between public restraint and private grief. After «إني بأهل مودّتي لمفجّع» the reciter gives «والدهر لا يبقى على حدثانه» twice. The first completes 19 with «في رأس شاهقة أعزّ ممنّع» and the second completes 20 with «جون السراة له جدائد أربع». Later occurrences at 41 and 55 introduce the bull and the armoured man. «ولكل جنب مصرع» likewise belongs at the end of both 6 and 50. The 138 ordered takes preserve all four occurrences of the refrain and both occurrences of the shorter formula. The poem ends with the two men, neither of whom survives the other's wounds.

## Scholarly witnesses and explanation

- *Diwan al-Hudhaliyyin*, Cairo, Dar al-Kutub al-Misriyya, 1:1–21, the first poem of Abu Dhu'ayb. This is the 69-bayt base for the full count. It preserves all the recorded bayts in order, including the ibex line before the wild ass. The introductory reports give five sons dying of plague in one year and also transmit a story about poisoned milk. The poem itself does not count or name the sons or specify a cause of death. The printed annotations repeatedly cite Ibn al-Anbari's commentary on the *Mufaddaliyyat*. Its notes identify two traditions about Abu Rabi'a (1:4), identify Yunabi' as a Hudhali valley and the lands around Dhu al-Arja' as the ground around a hill (1:6), and distinguish the red-striped mantles of Banu Yazid from those of Banu Tazid in another transmission (1:10). ## Metre

Kāmil with ʿayn as the rhyme consonant. Bayt 1 scans `uu–u– | uu–u– | uu–u–` and `––u– | uu–u– | ––u–`. At 34 («ونميمة من قانص متلبّب») the patterns are `uu–u– | ––u– | uu–u–` and `––u– | ––u– | uu–u–`. Here u is short and – is long. The substitution of a long first syllable for two shorts is iḍmār.

| 1.1 | فمن المنون وريبها تتوجع | أمن المنون وريبها تتوجع | The first word is the question أَمِنَ, followed by the two causes of grief. Widening restores it. The first printed bayt has the same interrogation. |
| 1.2 | والدهر ليس بمعتب من يجزعوه | والدهر ليس بمعتبا من يجزع | Final rhyme prolongation accounts for the apparent plural or attached h. The relative clause has the singular verb يجزع, and the widened clip ends there. |
| 2.2 | منذ ابتذلت ومثل ما لك ينفع | منذ ابتذلت ومثل مالك ينفعون | Both transcriptions can be pronounced alike at this juncture. مالك means “your wealth”, the resource Umayma thinks will help. Separating ما لك obscures her question. |
| 3.1 | اما لجنبك لا يلايم مضجعا | أو أما لجنبك لا يلائم مضجعا | The continuous questioning construction أم ما لجنبك is present in all three witnesses. Two neighbouring m sounds draw together. No printed or acoustic evidence supports a separate opening أو. |
| 4.1 | فاجبتها انما لجسمي انه | — | The Egyptian text and al-Sukkari separate أن ما. Al-Sukkari reports that al-Akhfash takes ما as an extra particle here. The joined ASR spelling also occurs in the anthology's form أمّا. The following «أنه» belongs to this half. |
| 4.2 | اودي بني من البلاد وودعوا | أودى بني من البلاد وودعوه | Both continuous transcripts, the first isolated pass and the widened clip converge on the waw of وودّعوا. Al-Sukkari independently prints it in no. 4. The plural refers to the sons. The earlier text was corrected from the Egyptian and anthology فودّعوا. |
| 5.1 | او دابني واعقبوني غصه | أودبني وأعقبوني غصة | The subject follows the verb in أودى بَنِيَّ. Both recognisers blur the boundary, while the ending غصّة and the deliberate repetition of the preceding half identify it. |
| 9.1 | موسوعه النابلسي للعلوم الاسلاميه | وإذا المنيا أنشبت أظفارها | The narrow recognition supplied the name of an Islamic encyclopaedia. The widened clip recovers the clause about death fixing its claws and both full transcripts place it after the father's unsuccessful efforts. |
| 11.1 | فان المومنون الا كان للحوادث مروه | — | The figure of the flint continues into the rock at Mina in 11.2. The full recitation supplies للحوادث مروة and the Egyptian text and al-Sukkari give both words. |
| 12.1 | لفضيله الدكتور محمد راتب النابلسي | لابد من تلف مقيم فانتظر | The spoken-sounding name in the narrow recognition is unrelated to the neighbouring poetry. The widened check recovers every word from لا بد to فانتظر. Cairo has the same bayt. |
| 18.1 | فلين بهم فجع الزمان وريبهم | فلئن بهم فجعل الزمان وريبه | A final m would attach “their” to time's vicissitudes. The poet has already supplied the singular antecedent الزمان. The wider transcript ends with وريبه. |
| 19.2 | فراس شاهقه اعز ممنع | في رأس شاهقة أعز ممن عود | في رأس is a preposition and its noun, defining the place of the protected creature. The wider recognition recovers the words. Its echo of a following sound after ممنّع does not form a new word. |
| 20.2 | جون السرات له جدايل اربع | جون السرات له جدائ أربع | السراة takes an audible t when the verse is joined. جدائد is the old term for the four dry jennies. The rare final consonants are imprecise in both clip transcriptions. All the following feminine plurals refer back to them. |
| 22.1 | اكل الجميم وطاوعته سمحجه | — | الجميم is fresh grass and سمحج a long-backed jenny. Both continuous accounts and the separately transmitted verse give this pairing. The hunt starts in plentiful pasture. |
| 22.2 | مثل القناه وازعلته الامر | مثل القناة وأزعلته الأمرع | The narrow clip stops at الأمر. The wider strip completes الأمرع. The rich pastures explain the animal's spirited movement. |
| 27.1 | فافتنهن من السواء وماوهم | فافتنهن من السواء وماؤه | The water is that of السواء, which is singular. Widening recovers ماؤه, continued by the water adjective بثر in 27.2. No plural possessor has been introduced. |
| 31.2 | في الظرباء فوق النظم لا يتدلعون | بئذ ضرباء فوق النظم لا يتتلع | This is a metrically divided word boundary in part of the transmission. The sound at the start belongs to «رابئ الضرباء». Widening also recovers يتتلّع. The ending describes the single star, so no plural verb is called for. |
| 33.2 | شرف الحزاب وريب قروع يقرع | شرف الحجاب ورئب قرع يقرع | The screen الحجاب conceals the hunter and the doubtful knocking is the sound of his bow. Widening supplies both الحجاب and قرع. The Cairo and al-Sukkari texts give them together. |
| 34.2 | في كفه جس اجس واقطع | في كفه جس أجس وأقطع | Both clip recognisers turn the pair of sh sounds into s. The commentators explicitly distinguish جشء, the wood of the bow, and أجشّ, its low sound. جس أجس offers no such pair. The final أقطع is a plural of arrowhead. |
| 36.1 | فرما فانفذ في نجود عايق | — | Both continuous transcripts carry في نجود and the recorded animal term agrees with the Egyptian نجود. Both anthology and diwan have مِن and al-Sukkari's main text مِن نحوص. The word card explains the change of preposition. |
| 37.1 | فبدا له اقرابها ذا رايغا | فبدا له أقرب هذا رائعا | The hunter sees the flanks of this male animal, أقراب هذا. The following participle describes it turning. The apparent هَا is part of the demonstrative and the wider clip restores هذا. |
| 40.2 | والقدره المومنين | كسيت برود بني يزيد الأذرع | The narrow replacement about believers belongs to neither the preceding wound nor the metre. The widened strip recovers the whole comparison with Banu Yazid's mantles, including the forelegs at the rhyme. |
| 42.1 | شعف الكلاب الضاليات فوادهم | شفع الكلا بالضاريات فؤاده | The hounds are الضاريات, trained to the hunt. The heart belongs to the one bull, فؤاده. The wider clip recovers both and the next half explains his fear. |
| 48.2 | والانسان الا ما كانوا يعلمون | بهما من النضح المجدح أي دع | The narrow transcript supplies an unrelated religious phrase. The wider passage recognises the stirred spatter and divides the rare colour word أيدع in two. The commentary identifies the red substance on the horns. |
| 50.2 | موسوعه النابلسي للعلوم الاسلاميه | متترب ولكل جنب مصرع | The widened clip recovers متترب ولكل جنب مصرع in full. It describes the dust after the fall and repeats the father's words from 6.2. The unrelated encyclopaedia title in the narrow pass is discarded. |
| 51.1 | حتي اذا اوتدت واقصد عصبه | حتى إذا روتدت وأقصد عصبة | ارتدت describes the pack drawing back. The continuous reading and the printed verse have that verb. The apparent initial w in one clip comes where the prefixed hamza of connection falls after إذا. |
| 54.1 | فكباك ما يكبو فنيق تارز | فكباك ما يكب فنيق تارز | فكبا كما compares two falls. A boundary placed one consonant later creates the recognisers' فكباك ما. The formula is explicit in the Egyptian diwan and the next word يكبو repeats the same root. |
| 59.1 | موسوعه النابلسي للعلوم الاسلاميه | متفلق أنساؤها عنقانين | The narrow clip inserts the title of an Islamic encyclopaedia. The widened transcript recognises the thigh sinews and runs عن قانئ together. The small red udder in 59.2 completes the description. |
| 61.1 | بين تعنقه الكماه وروضه | بينات عنقه الكماة وروغه | This rider both grapples and wheels away. The widened clip has روغه, his feint or swerve. The narrow روضه would introduce a meadow in the midst of his manoeuvre. |
| 64.2 | والقوم يوم اشنع | ببلائه واليوم يوم أشنع | The wider strip restores ببلائه واليوم يوم أشنع. Prior experience completes كل واثق in the first half. The narrow والقوم loses that complement. |
| 66.2 | في ها سنان كالمناره اصلع | فيها سنان كالمنارة أصلع | فيها is one written preposition with an attached pronoun. The wider check recognises it and the shining spearhead which it introduces. |
| 67.2 | عطبا اذا مس الظريبه يقطعه | — | The Egyptian text and the anthology name الضريبة, anything struck by the blade. Al-Sukkari gives الكريهة and records other diction. The continuous recitation has الضريبة before يقطع. |
| 68.1 | فتخالسا نفسيهما بنوافذه | فتخالسا نفسيهما بنوافذ | بنوافذ is an indefinite plural, “with piercing wounds”. The initial narrow clip adds a possessive ending. The widened strip and the comparison which follows both give نوافذ. |
| 69.2 | وجنا العلاء لون شييا ينفعه / وجنا العلي الون شييا ينفع | وجنى العلا ألوا النشيء ينفعه | The continuous ending is the conditional لو أن شيئاً ينفع. The two short particles are run into the following noun in the clips. Chanting of the final rhyme creates the apparent pronoun. Al-Sukkari's gloss reports this wording beside the other ending. |
