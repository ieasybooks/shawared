# أَيُؤسَرُ القَلبُ

## Recording and text

Khalid al-Sharafi recites the 33 bayts of «أيؤسر القلب» for Alqimmah Studio, released 11 July 2025. The studio credits Dr Husam ibn Abd Allah al-Dosari. The verse cards and performance run in the same order and each half occurs once. The source runs 311.925 seconds. The edit keeps 2.90–309.90 seconds, trimming the silence around the poem. ## Readings and commentary

- **4, 6, 9.** The poet shifts from «شذاً» to «الشَّذو», the spreading of a fragrance, at 0:37. At 0:57 the screen reads «لأياً كففت سنا اللمياء عن حدقي». The lover shields his resolve as well as his eyes. Verse 9 ends «مبصراً وعَمِ» (1:24), carrying the contradiction of sight and blindness into the same gaze. [Verse 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Dx5drB4zq0&t=35s) · [Verse 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Dx5drB4zq0&t=55s) · [Verse 9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Dx5drB4zq0&t=82s)
- **10–13.** Keep the paired commands «قُمِ» and «نَمِ», spoken first by grey hair, then by the lover. The wine at 2:00 comes in «نُغَم», mouthfuls, as the studio gloss confirms. The Cairo Arabic Language Academy also defines نُغْمة as a draught. This makes the last action of 13 another drink from the cup. [Verse 13 and gloss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Dx5drB4zq0&t=117s) · [*Al-Mu'jam al-Wasit*, النُّغْمة](https://www.arabicacademy.gov.eg/ar/محرك-البحث/النُّغْمَةُ?exact_search=true)
- **17, 20–21.** The fire of grey hair recalls Zechariah’s prayer. The monosyllabic stem in «الوَهجُ» gives the metre of 17.2. It scores +4.25 against «الوَهَجُ». Ibn Manzur records وَهْج and وَهَج. At 3:03 the studio has «تُجلي بصائرنا», without a doubled lam (+5.16 against «تُجلّي»). Ibn al-Athir explains المنبتّ as a traveller stranded after exhausting his mount (*al-Nihaya*, vol. 1, p. 92). The following horse is «بَرقٍ جموحٍ» (3:13), a flash of lightning in its speed (+6.66 against «بَرِقٍ»), and there is nothing to check it. [Qur’an 19:4](https://quran.com/19/4) · [*Lisan al-Arab*, وهج](https://www.islamweb.net/ar/library/content/122/9203/وهج) · [Verse 20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Dx5drB4zq0&t=181s) · [Verse 21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Dx5drB4zq0&t=191s) · [Ibn al-Athir, بتت](https://ablibrary.net/book_content/b/4867/112?lang=ar)
- **26.1.** All three transcriptions and the frame at 3:59 have «تَذخَرُها», the simple verb ذَخَرَ, “put away for a time of need”. This restores the last foot and scores +3.58 against the doubled dal. The first working text had the more familiar «تدّخرها». Beside it the studio places «بنات الدهر», the calamities those stored shields fail to turn aside. [Studio text](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Dx5drB4zq0&t=237s) · [*Al-Mu'jam al-Wasit*, ذخر](https://www.arabicacademy.gov.eg/ar/محرك-البحث/ذَخَرَ?exact_search=true)
- **29, 33.** The claim for supplication echoes al-Tirmidhi’s report from Salman, which he classifies as *hasan gharib*. The injunction to take hold of God’s rope in the closing half recalls Qur’an 3:103. Both references belong to the change from a captive heart at the opening to the heart’s choice of what to hold in the last line. [*Jami' al-Tirmidhi*, no. 2139](https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2139) · [Qur’an 3:103](https://quran.com/3/103)
- **Metre and rhyme.** Basit with mim as rhyme consonant. With `–` for long and `u` for short, 1.1 «أيؤسر القلب أم يردى من السقم» scans `u–u– | –u– | ––u– | uu–`. The middle example 15.1 «وعد الغواني كنقع ثار ثم خبا» scans `––u– | –u– | ––u– | uu–`. The reciter carries the final kasra on the mim through the rhyme.

## Verification

The first Cohere pass flagged 19 of 66 halves (35 of 360 words). After «تَذخَرُها» was restored from the studio card and recording, a fresh alignment and transcription cleared 26.1. The final pass flags 18 halves (34 of 360 words). The second backend was Audar with a wider clip around each flag. The full transcript and the studio’s written line resolve words on which the clip transcriptions diverge.

- **1.2, 11.2, 13.1.** The short clips add final consonants to «دمي»، «نَمِ» and «نُسكٌ». The sustained vowel at the end of «نَمِ» closes the lover’s reply, and the last sound of «نُسكٌ» is tanwin before «بعد».
- **2.2, 5.1, 6.1.** The studio shows the repeated fa in «لففنه», the separate negation in «ما راعهنّ», and the opening «لأياً كففت» with «اللمياء». Short transcriptions substitute a consonant, fuse words or move a word boundary. The whole transcript recovers the first two phrases and the wider Audar clip distinguishes the two words at the start of 6.1.
- **9.1–2, 10.2.** The image reads «مادت بأفئدةٍ» and closes «مبصراً وعَمِ». Compare the invented ending «وعميرا» with Audar’s «وعمي». The card in 10.2 separates the prohibition «لا تصبوَنَّ» from the command «قُمِ», which the transcribers join.
- **18.1–2, 20.1.** The boundary is «فاحذر تلوُّنه», across the two words. The first backend returned an empty clip for the chameleon at 18.2. The full transcript recovers «حرباء ختل يشوب الجرم باللمم» and Audar confirms its ending. The card at 3:03 separates «وعى حِكَماً», joined in both clip transcriptions.
- **22.2, 24.1, 28.1.** The full recording and card give «صدى الكَلِم» in the verse about praise and lampoon, and the negative command «لا تركننَّ» in 24.1. For 28.1 Audar recovers «فما أتاك فحكم», split incorrectly by Cohere.
- **29.2, 30.2, 32.2.** Cohere substituted an unrelated broadcaster’s name for 29.2. Audar recovers «إلا الدعاء … بالله من نقم» and the studio gives the intervening «فعُذ». The studio’s contrasts between sufficiency and want, then pride and perishing, confirm «تغني» and «الزَّهو» in the other two halves. Wide Audar also recovers «الزهو».

The poet’s full name and field of medicine come from his credited diabetes recitation. [Studio credits](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cnpSc1a5hfg)
