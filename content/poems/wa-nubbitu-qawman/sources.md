# ونبّئت قوماً بهم جنّة

The reference count is 30 in Muhammad al-Tahir ibn Ashur’s reconstruction. Osama al-Nahari performs its 1–14 followed by 16–30, giving 29 text bayts and 58 different half-line entries. After the address to Safra' he goes directly from «ولكنه نصب هم وغم» to «أقول لها حين قل الثراء». The missing bayt is «صببت هواك على قلبه / فضاق وأعلن ما قد كتم». There is no repeat or internal audio splice.

## Editions, order and interpretation

- Muhammad al-Tahir ibn Ashur, ed., *Diwan Bashshar ibn Burd*, IV, pp. 156–161. His note at the opening says that he ordered quotations from different books according to their sense. The praise of Umar ibn al-Ala is explicit. Pp. 156–158 give the boast, the girls’ circling and the intermediary Abu Mijlaz. P. 159 gives Safra', the omitted bayt and Umar’s career. Pp. 160–161 interpret the caliph’s appeal, the revenge and the closing mirage. The line on p. 162 is printed separately with doubt about its connection and is excluded from the 30. [pp. 156–161](https://archive.org/details/diwan-ibn-bord_4/page/n163/mode/2up) · [p. 162](https://archive.org/details/diwan-ibn-bord_4/page/n169/mode/1up)
- Badr al-Din al-Alawi, ed., *Diwan shi'r Bashshar*, no. 335, pp. 214–218. P. 215 explains why, although the laudatory and boastful passages were transmitted together in some books, he was obliged to construct an order. He opens with our 5 «وجارية خلقت وحدها», places Safra' at his 6 and our 1–4 at his 29–32. His additional 16 «وعي الفعال كعي المقال / وفي الصمت عي كعي الكلم» and 21 «يطوف العفاة بأبوابه / طواف الحجيج ببيت الحرم» are unperformed. His 7 transmits Ibn Ashur’s omitted 15. He puts «إذا ما غزا» before «فتى لا ينام». [pp. 214–218](https://archive.org/details/a949n/page/n213/mode/2up)
- *Hamasat al-Qurashi*, pp. 127–128, quotes our 1–4 together with «جاهلاً», «أنف الكرم» and «وإني». Abd al-Rahim al-Abbasi, *Ma'ahid al-tansis*, I, pp. 290–291, gives «جاهلاً» and «أنف» in the discussion of Bashshar. These form independent checks on the incipit and the competing wordings of the boast. [*Hamasa*, p. 127](https://shamela.ws/book/2512/44) · [p. 128](https://shamela.ws/book/2512/45) · [*Ma'ahid*, p. 290](https://shamela.ws/book/6951/284)

At 2 Ibn Ashur prints «جاهداً … أنف» and al-Alawi «جاهلاً … إلف». The first makes the pretended stranger insistent in his questioning, the second makes him ignorant. The second noun is respectively the foremost feature and a familiar companion of liberality. The reading of this performance is «جاهداً … أنف». At 4 the two-point acoustic preference for «إني» alone does not resolve the consonant attached to the previous cadence. The continuous Cohere transcription has the initial fāʾ, as does Ibn Ashur. The reciter has «فإني … وأصبي … فما», against al-Alawi’s «وإني … وأصمي … فلا». Our 8 has a metrically divided word «الشبا / بِ», as printed by Ibn Ashur. Actual lexical alternatives in al-Alawi’s running text include «بالذي ذكروا / لأحمد» in our 18, «الحاسد» in 19, «نصيحاً» in 23, and «دمنة» in 25. Ibn Ashur and the recording have «الذي زعموا / لأمدح», «الطالب», «نصوحاً» and «ثأره». At 29 both editions print «عند كم». Ibn Ashur expressly calls the expression obscure and proposes «عند لَمّ» or a related correction. The continuous recitation has intelligible second-person «عندكم». Its bilingual card explains the editorial choice.

## Meter and rhyme

Mutaqarib, mim in a bound rhyme. At 1.1 «وَنُبْبِئْ / تُ قَوْمَنْ / بِهِمْ جِنْ / نَتُنْ» gives فعولن فعولن فعولن فعول. At 16.1 «إِذَا مَفْ / تَقَرْتُ / فَأُحْيِسْ / سُرَى» gives فعولن فعولُ فعولن فعول. In 18.2 «شمّ» and 20.2 «ثمّ» the final mim is doubled and paused on. The tashkeel linter asks about shadda without a vowel in both places. The pausal notation deliberately retains the doubling and supplies no audible case or adverbial vowel.
