# سَنامُ العُمرِ

## Recording and text

Alqimmah Studio published Khalid al-Sharafi’s recitation on 11 April 2026 and credits the words to Dr Husam ibn Abd Allah al-Dosari. The title, 33 verse cards and final rhyme give the extent of this edition. The reciter follows the cards from «ها قد بلغت» through «أردتكما العلل», without omissions or repeated halves. The 344.811-second source was kept from 2.35 to 342.90 seconds, trimming the surrounding silence. Gain −7.9 dB, output −20.0 LUFS, duration 340.55 seconds. [Studio edition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4)

The primary written witness is the credited studio video, which sets both halves on screen and adds smaller glosses below them. A separate printed edition could not be located. The Arabic was set from the cards against the whole performance, starting with the WebM intake transcript. The time-labelled card sheets and enlargements of the glosses are in `media/work/727afuDk_z4/editor-frames/`. Three-second stills from the original video are in `media/research/video/727afuDk_z4/`. Times below refer to the original, before the trim.

## Readings and commentary

- **2–8.** The caption at 0:21 explains «وفى» as coming to completion. At 0:30 the fa of «الأُفْقُ» carries a sukun, which gives the metrical reading of 3.2 (+3.46 against «الأُفُقُ»). The camp vocabulary in 6–8 supplies the logic of the comparison. The marks of the cooking place and the curve of sand can be revisited, while lost youth leaves no site that a traveller can find. [Verse 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=18s) · [Verse 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=28s) · [Verse 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=59s)
- **9, 15–18.** Verse 9 distinguishes the friendship of youth, «خُلّته», from the plural «الخُلَل». Verse 15 has «بطِلعٍ دونه جُلَلُ». The studio explains a high place giving a view, and the plural of a great ordeal. Verse 16 makes the world a grey-haired woman whose friendship is insincere. Al-Maydani, *Majma' al-Amthal*, no. 3538, vol. 2, p. 220, quotes the whole second half of 18 from al-Ra'i. The proverbial camels deny the speaker any stake in the affair. The studio opens the possessive ya in «لا ناقةٌ لِيَ في هذا», which the rhythm confirms. The acoustic difference between the open and long ya is only −0.22. [Verse 9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=89s) · [Verse 15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=151s) · [Verse 18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=182s) · [Al-Maydani, no. 3538](https://shamela.ws/book/12929/3627)
- **23, 26, 29.** At 3:55 the screen pairs «يمّم يميناً» with «الشُّمُل». Ibn Manzur gives this plural under شِمال, the opposite of the right hand. At 4:24 the studio glosses «تياسروا» as sharing a thing out, which makes the hoard in the preceding half the owner’s time. At 4:56 the caption explains «قَلوب» as constantly turning. The Arabic and English explanations keep the connection with «قلب» audible in the same half. [Verse 23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=233s) · [*Lisan al-Arab*, شمل](https://islamweb.net/ar/library/content/122/4399/شمل) · [Verse 26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=263s) · [Verse 29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=727afuDk_z4&t=295s)
- **31–33.** The «مضغة» guarded like a territory recalls the report of al-Nu'man ibn Bashir on the piece of flesh whose soundness or corruption affects the entire body. The report also speaks of protected ground, which gives a second point of contact with the poet’s «في حماها». [Al-Bukhari, *Sahih*, no. 52](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:52)
- **Metre and rhyme.** Basit with lam as its rhyme consonant. With `–` for long and `u` for short, 1.1 «ها قد بلغت سنام العمر يا رجل» scans `––u– | uu– | ––u– | uu–`, and 17.1 «لعلّ نفسك بعد الصحو من ثمل» gives `u–u– | uu– | ––u– | uu–`. Both halves of the opening rhyme. Later verbal endings such as «وصلوا» and «اقتتلوا» keep the lam and its prolonged final vowel.

## Verification

The first clip pass flagged nine of 66 halves. The flagged audio was transcribed again with Audar at two margins and compared with the original subtitle cards.

- **3.1, 9.1.** The first clips returned «تحالك» and «تضرب». Audar recovers «فحالك» and the imperative «فاضرب». At 3.1 the subtitle reads «كالجاثي», the man kneeling on the mountain.
- **5.1, 23.1.** The short clips add a plural ending to «تطلبه» and «مثالبه». The objects are singular, respectively youth and the man whose faults are hidden. The full transcription and cards support the endings. The wider clip also recovers «تطلبه».
- **11.2, 12.2.** «ثوى فيه» becomes «ثوافيه» across the boundary in the first pass. Audar restores the two words. Both short transcribers merge or split «ما اعوجّ» incorrectly. The card keeps the conditional across the break after «فمتى» in 12.1.
- **20.1, 21.1.** The short clips render «مجادلةٍ» as «مجادلته» and add a final ta to «وجُز». Audar and the cards give an indefinite noun in the first passage and an imperative to continue the journey in the second.
- **23.2.** Cohere adds an initial mim to «يمِّم». Both clip transcripts struggle with the repeated labial sounds. The full transcript and the glossed card at 3:55 give the imperative and the answering «يميناً».

The poet’s full name and professional specialism in the biography come from the credits of his teaching poem on diabetes. The three dated studio releases establish the works mentioned there.
