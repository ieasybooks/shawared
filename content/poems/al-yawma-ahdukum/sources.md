## Text and commentary

- Al-Wahidi, *Sharh Diwan al-Mutanabbi*, ed. Friedrich Dieterici, Berlin, 1861, printed pp. 72–79. The dedication names Shujaʿ ibn Muhammad al-Ta’i of Manbij. The recitation occupies pp. 72–74. The Berlin scan runs backwards in digital leaf numbers. The count reaches bayt 40 on p. 79. [Opening and dedication](https://archive.org/details/carminacumcommen00muta/page/n828/mode/1up) · [Bayts 8–11](https://archive.org/details/carminacumcommen00muta/page/n826/mode/1up) · [Ending](https://archive.org/details/carminacumcommen00muta/page/n821/mode/1up) · [Searchable commentary](https://shamela.ws/book/754/38)
- *Al-Tibyan fi sharh al-diwan*, transmitted under al-Ukbari’s name, ed. Mustafa al-Saqqa, Ibrahim al-Abyari and Abd al-Hafiz Shalabi, vol. I, poem 65, printed pp. 327–340. The numbers above the commentary supply the reference sequence. The recording has 1–10 of 40. It stops before «فله بنو عبد العزيز بن الرضا» and omits the entire panegyric through «يفنى الكلام ولا يحيط بوصفكم». [Opening](https://archive.org/details/20240104_20240104_1855/page/n352/mode/1up) · [Last recited page](https://archive.org/details/20240104_20240104_1855/page/n355/mode/1up) · [Transition](https://archive.org/details/20240104_20240104_1855/page/n356/mode/1up) · [Bayts 39–40](https://archive.org/details/20240104_20240104_1855/page/n365/mode/1up) · [Electronic commentary](https://shamela.ws/book/6908/303)

The note on «عهد» follows al-Wahidi’s gloss «اللقاء». His interpretations of 5–6 connect the woman’s pallor to fear of discovery and distinguish the gold of her colour, the moon of her face and the branch of her swaying figure. On 9 he takes Time’s fetters to increase the weight of its tread. Ibn Jinni takes their short steps as an image of gradual change. On 10 al-Wahidi transmits Ibn Furraja’s interpretation, in which the lover’s doctor and his visitors themselves fall sick. The *Tibyan* also records the alternative explanations and analyses the object «لوني». [Al-Wahidi, pp. 73–74](https://archive.org/details/carminacumcommen00muta/page/n827/mode/1up) · [Al-Tibyan, pp. 329–331](https://archive.org/details/20240104_20240104_1855/page/n354/mode/1up)

## Metre
