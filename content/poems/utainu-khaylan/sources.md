# أطاعن خيلاً من فوارسها الدهر

## Recording and extent

Osama al-Nahari recites the 41 bayts of al-Mutanabbi’s praise of Ali ibn Ahmad ibn Amir of Antioch. The first word begins about 1.26 s into the original 397.502 s recording. The last rhyme, «عذر», subsides at about 387.76 s. The master keeps 0.75–388.35 s on that clock and runs 387.60 s. Both continuous transcripts run from the encounter with Time to the reconciliation with the days. All 82 halves occur once in the diwan’s order. The intervals between the mountains, the rain and the arrival at the patron belong to the recitation. [Recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9rn8KLl9EF8)

The metre is tawil and the rawiy is ra, with final u in the performance. The first sadr divides «أُطاعِ | نُ خَيْلَنْ مِنْ | فَوارِ | سِهادْدَهْرُو», فعولُ مفاعيلن فعولُ مفاعيلن. Its rhyme answers «الصبر» in the ajuz. In 21.1 «فَتَنْ لا | يَضُمُّ الْقَلْـ | بُ هِمْما | تِ قَلْبِهِي» gives فعولن مفاعيلن فعولن مفاعلن. The last foot of the opening sadr is longer because the two opening halves rhyme.

## Decisions in the text

At 4.1 both commentaries explain «الأتيّ» as the incoming torrent. The forced model prefers the familiar «الآتي» by 3.85, but the free phones on the original have a y sound after i, and the full Audar pass writes «الأتي». At 7.1 al-Wahidi has «أعناق الرجال وأن ترى». Both *Mu'jiz Ahmad* and *al-Lami'* give «أعناق الملوك وأن ترى». The pictured text at 61 s and both full transcripts give «الملوك وهامها». The two-word sequence scores −17.91 against −47.24 for «الرجال وأن». The reciter makes the kings’ heads a second object of striking, then begins the description of the dust and army as a new clause. [Al-Wahidi, p. 144](https://shamela.ws/book/754/144) · [Al-Ma'arri, p. 533](https://shamela.ws/book/29591/528) · [Recording at 61 s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9rn8KLl9EF8&t=61s)

At 12.2 al-Wahidi and the reciter have feminine «تشتهى» with «الخمر». It leads the masculine «يشتهى» in the digital *Mu'jiz Ahmad* by 6.72. At 19.2 the digital al-Wahidi drops «لو». The Berlin page, *Mu'jiz Ahmad* and both continuous passes preserve it. At 21.2 al-Wahidi’s «ضمّه» makes the heart itself too great for a chest. *Mu'jiz Ahmad* prints «ضمّها», referring back to the ambitions. The score favours «ضمّه» by just 1.96, but both full passes retain the masculine, and the free phones end in h-u. In 22.1 «سخاؤه» is nominative after لولا. At 23.2 the sword and victory are the two subjects of «يتلاقى» and the nisba «الهندوانيّ» is nominative. The forced nominative and accusative scores differ by 0.12. At 24.1 the two ancestors together bring forth the patron. The two scores for «فجاءا» and «فجاء» coincide, and both full passes spell the word as singular. The caption at 225 s spells out the dual «فجاءا», and the final a is sustained in the original window. *Mu'jiz Ahmad*, *al-Lami'* and the electronic al-Wahidi agree. The Berlin verse is set «فجاء به», though the preceding gloss speaks of the two ancestors meeting. [Berlin, p. 287](https://archive.org/download/carminacumcommen00muta/carminacumcommen00muta.pdf#page=614) · [Berlin, p. 288](https://archive.org/download/carminacumcommen00muta/carminacumcommen00muta.pdf#page=613) · [Al-Wahidi, p. 145](https://shamela.ws/book/754/145) · [Al-Ma'arri, p. 540](https://shamela.ws/book/29591/535) · [Recording at 225 s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9rn8KLl9EF8&t=225s)

At 25.1 all three commentaries give «سميدع» with dal. The source caption at 235 s clearly places a dot above the dental. The forced score favours dal by 3.88. Both clip decoders also write dal. Free recognition of the original, however, gives s-a-m-a-i-ð-a at 234.14–234.80 s. The alternative is a documented form of the same word. In *Sahm al-alhaz*, no. 84, Ibn al-Hanbali explicitly specifies an open sin and a dotted dental for the generous lord, and the editor’s note gives the undotted form from *al-Fasih*, *al-Sihah* and *Lisan al-arab*. The text and both word cards therefore retain «سَمَيذَعاً». [Lexical entry and note, p. 50](https://shamela.ws/book/6921/28) · [Poem commentary, p. 540](https://shamela.ws/book/29591/535) · [Recording at 235 s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9rn8KLl9EF8&t=235s)

At 26.2 «يسايرني» leads «يسافرني» by 5.73 and occurs in both full transcripts. At 28.2 the performance and *Mu'jiz Ahmad* give «كل ما لقيت» where al-Wahidi’s digital text has «كل مما لقيت». The Berlin text joins «كلما» and has no extra mim. The aligned «ما» leads «مما» by 8.57. At 33.1 Berlin prints «قلتَ» and remarks on the quality of the patron’s poetry. *Mu'jiz Ahmad* records both «قلتُ» and «قلتَ» and prefers the first person. On the original the final t-u occurs at 305.02–305.08 s, and the source caption at 306 s vowels the ta with damma. The forced scores for the two persons are virtually tied (−18.61 and −18.67). This takes the light-filled verse as al-Mutanabbi’s own praise, following the reference to his host’s poetry in 32. At 35.2 the word score barely favours *Mu'jiz Ahmad*’s «يقتضي» by 0.64. Al-Wahidi’s «يقتضيني» is clear in the continuous Cohere transcription. The vulture is demanding its due from the speaker. Both full passes start 36 with «وإني», which leads al-Wahidi’s «فإني» by 4.35. For 37.2 the plural «أَوُدّ» leads the alternative «أَوُدّي» by 3.36. The sharh follows Ibn Jinni’s account of friendship between the corresponding faculties and mentions al-Arudi’s interpretation of the patron as teacher. At 40.1 both commentaries give «ولو». The first full transcription and the original consonants support «وإن», ahead by 5.79. At 41.1 «عتبي» leads «عتبى» by 3.35 and the full Cohere pass has the first-person possessive. Free recognition of the original gives a-t-b-i at 380.00–380.48 s. The caption vowels the first letter with fatha and leaves the last ya undotted. The fingertip card at 8.2 uses the root ن م ل, under which Ibn Manzur lists «أنملة». Al-Zabidi also registers the lord of 25.1 under سمذع and describes the disagreement over the dot in the dictionaries. ## Final clip decisions

The final narrow Cohere pass reports 12 differences among 82 halves. Each row compares its disputed wording with the widened Audar window. The original-clock phone sequences supply an additional check where the two decoders disagree. The separate edge report flags 41.1.

| Half | Narrow Cohere | Widened Audar | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 7.2 | العسكر المجرور | العسكر المجر | «المجر» means a very large army in both commentaries. The original phones end j-r-u, with no second r, and the wider window gives the recited word. |
| 12.1 | والإنسان يوم الأرض, twice | يدير بأطراف الرماح عليهم | Both complete passes recover the spearpoints and the men. The wider cut agrees word for word. For the opening verb «يدير» leads «يدور» by 4.00. The object, the cups, comes in 12.2. |
| 13.1 | Stops at أنني | Stops at أنني | The free phones place l at 121.82 s, after the vowel of «أنني» and before the new half. The following noun begins «جبال». Al-Ma'arri describes precisely this division of its article. |
| 17.2 | دزنه | دجنه | The free window gives a z-like consonant at 162.40 s. Both complete transcripts recognise «دجنه», and the two forced scores differ by 1.26 in its favour. All the commentaries identify the dark cloud that clothes the sky. The word card records the consonant. |
| 23.2 | الهند وني | الهندواني | The reciter joins the nisba to the word for India. The wider cut and the full Cohere pass give the single word for the Indian blade. Al-Ma'arri uses it in his explanation of the two allies. |
| 25.1 | سميدعا | سميد عنه | The widened cut breaks the word at its last syllable. The original interdental and the explicit lexical witness support the performed «سميذعاً» as detailed above. |
| 25.2 | مع له | ما له | «ما» leads «مع» by 7.46 and is recovered by the wider and both full passes. It negates the ebb of the tide. |
| 28.2 | بكل وات | بكل وآت | Both cut transcriptions hear t because «وآةٍ» is in liaison. The free sequence ends t-i-n before «كلّ», and the two spellings score equally. Al-Ma'arri explicitly glosses the feminine camel noun. |
| 32.1 | الحجاب | الحجى | The wide and both complete passes recover the third quality, judgement, alongside knowledge and forbearance. The b added in the narrow clip has no support in either commentary. |
| 37.2 | دسمها | ذ اسمها | At 347.52 s the original gives ð-a followed by s-m. The demonstrative «ذا» runs into the initial wasl of «اسمها». Both commentaries quote and discuss these words separately. |
| 39.2 | نحو كالبشر | نحو كلب شر | The full Cohere transcript reads «نحوك البشر». The original gives the k at the end of «نحوك» followed by l-b-i, the beginning of «البشر». Its smiling face turns towards the patron. |
| 40.1 | وإن التسماء | وإني إلتسما | The full Cohere pass separates «وإن نلت السماء». At 370.28–370.88 s the original preserves l-t-a followed by the two s sounds of the next word. The conditional variant is documented above. |

The narrow edge pass adds «لو» before 41.1. The end of 40.2 is «يوجب القدر», and both full passes continue directly «أزالت بك الأيام». The source caption and the aligned opening of the last bayt confirm that transition. There is no extra conditional clause or repeated half at this point.
