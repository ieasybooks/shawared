## Attribution

Every source read gives the poem to al-Khansa': Tha'lab's recension, Cheikho's *Anis al-Julasa*, the Beirut printing, al-Aghani, al-Mubarrad's *al-Ta'azi* and *al-Kamil* (which names no brother), Ibn Abd Rabbih's *al-Iqd*. Whom it mourns is disputed:

- **Mu'awiya ibn Amr.** Tha'lab's heading (p. 78): «وقالت لمعاوية أخيها، وقتله بنو مرّة على غدير قَلَهى», and Cheikho's (*Anis*, p. 201): «وقالت ترثي أخاها معاوية وقتله بنو مرّة على غدير قلهى». Tha'lab's commentary names Mu'awiya in a gloss of bayt 2 («تريد أنّ معاوية كان ثقيلاً على الأرض», p. 79) and in one of his 13, our 23 («تقول: ما كان «معاوية» أدنى لها ممّن جناها», p. 90). Its second gloss of bayt 2, «حلّت أي زيّنت مَنْ في بطنها حين جاورهم معاوية» (p. 79), reads «صخر» in two of its manuscripts and in Cheikho's text: «دار وبغ: «صخر»» (Abu Suwaylim's note, printed as (3) but belonging to the text's (4), p. 79) and «حين جاور[هم] صخر» (*Anis*, p. 202). Al-Aghani 15/57 quotes five of its abyat after Sakhr's burial at Asib, then: «قال السُّلَميّ: ليست هذه في صخر، هذه إنّما رثت بها معاويةَ أخاها، وبنو مُرّة قتلته». The poem itself, bayt 18: «فإن تكُ مُرّةُ أودت به»; and al-Bakri (*Mu'jam ma Ista'jam*, p. 1194, quoted in Abu Suwaylim's note, p. 82 n. 1) puts al-Mahw of bayt 11 in the land of Murra, where Hashim and Durayd, sons of Harmala, killed Mu'awiya. Al-Aghani 15/62–63 tells how they killed him when he raided Murra and Fazara.
- **Sakhr ibn Amr.** Abu Ubayda, as Abu Suwaylim reports him (p. 78 n. 5: «وزعم أبو عبيدة أنها قالت هذا الشعر في أخيها صخر لمّا دُفن بأرض بني سُليم عند جبل عسيب»); al-Aghani's placing of the five abyat; the Beirut printing's heading («قالت ترثي أخاها صخراً، لما مات ودفن في جبل عسيب … وقيل بل قالت هذا في أخيها معاوية لما قتله بنو مرة»); al-Mubarrad, *al-Ta'azi* p. 121 («وقالت أيضاً ترثي صخراً»); *al-Iqd* 3/224, which gives five of its abyat right after an elegy for Sakhr («وقالت أيضا») and whose second bayt reads «أمن بعد صخرٍ من آل الشريد»; the reciter's video description («ترثي أخاها صخر»). Abu Suwaylim adds (p. 107 n. 1) that bayt 15 «يزيدنا ثقة بأنّ هذه القصيدة قيلت في صخر؛ لأنه كان شاعراً».
- **Abyat given to others:** Abu Ubayda gave bayt 2 to Mayya bint Dirar al-Dabbiyya mourning her brother Qabisa (Abu Suwaylim p. 79, the note on that bayt, from al-Aghani, Dar al-Kutub, 13/143); bayt 11 is hers in Berlin 1 and for Abu Ubayda (al-Aghani 13/143), and al-Bakri reports the attribution and doubts it (Abu Suwaylim pp. 82 n. 1, 83 n. 3); Ibn al-A'rabi gave bayt 13 to Amir ibn Juwayn al-Ta'i (Tha'lab p. 104); Abu Suwaylim's notes give al-Asma'i's version of 26 with a following bayt that some ascribe to Amir ibn Juwayn too (p. 93 n. 7).

The intro and sharh follow Tha'lab and al-Sulami (Mu'awiya), and give Abu Ubayda's view and al-Aghani's placing. The second reader keeps that choice: the heading of Tha'lab's recension (in Abu Suwaylim's text and in Cheikho's), al-Sulami (by his nisba a man of her own tribe), and the poem's own words (18, Murra; 11, al-Mahw in Murra's land) point to Mu'awiya, while the case for Sakhr rests on reports about the poem (Abu Ubayda, the anthologies, the Beirut heading) and on Abu Suwaylim's inference from 15. It is left open for the owner. The title is the first sadr: no source names the poem; «إما عليها وإما لها» is only the Beirut printing's heading.

## Editions

- **Tha'lab's recension:** ديوان الخنساء، شرح ثعلب أبي العباس أحمد بن يحيى، تحقيق أنور أبو سويلم (Dar Ammar, Amman, 1st ed. 1409/1988, with Mu'ta University): archive.org `KnSSa_TH` (the same PDF as `3318pdf_202001`), poem 4, pp. 78–109 (leaves 77–108; leaf = page − 1), read as page images (`thalab.md`, with the commentary taken down page by page). Voweled, 38 abyat, with Abu Suwaylim's apparatus: the Berlin manuscripts of Ibn al-Sikkit's recension (برلين ١، ٢), the Dar al-Kutub manuscript with al-Asimi's and al-Kirmani's marginalia (دار), بغ, and the printed «أنيس» (Cheikho, *Anis al-Julasa*), «م أنيس», «كرم» and «الحوفي». The recited text departs from it often (table below). Nine of its abyat are not in the Beirut printing, so not recited: 9 «فنفسي الفداء له من فقيد», 17–19, 30, 33, 34, 36 and 37 (Abu Suwaylim notes 17, 30, 34, 36 and 37 as missing from Karam and al-Hawfi, 17 also from Ibn al-Sikkit's recension, and «تقدّ الذؤابة» standing in Karam and al-Hawfi where Tha'lab has 9). Two recited abyat are not in Tha'lab's text: 8, which al-Aghani has, and 19, which Abu Suwaylim gives from Ibn al-Sikkit's recension, Karam and al-Hawfi (p. 109 n. 3).
- **The Beirut printing:** ديوان الخنساء (Dar Sadir, Beirut, n.d.; no editor named): archive.org `AAlexandrina-109874`, leaves 123–126, and the sharper scan of the same printing `diwan-al-khansa`, leaves 119–122; pp. 120–123, read as page images (`beirut.md`). Voweled, 31 abyat with short notes, headed «إما عليها وإما لها». Its page numbers are the ones Abu Suwaylim cites as «كرم» («كرم: ص ١٢٠», «كرم: ص ١٢٢»), Karam al-Bustani's edition, and so are most of its readings, but not all: Abu Suwaylim cites Karam with «إعوالها» in 16.2 (p. 86 n. 5; the scan has «أوعالها»), «بَيْنَهُ» in 24.1 (p. 92 n. 3; the scan «بَيْنُهُ») and «الصِّيَاح» in 26.1 (p. 93 n. 7; both scans print «الصُّباحِ», one dot under the tooth and a damma over the ص). Either he used another printing or he cites loosely; the readings below credit the scan for what it prints. Many of its departures from Tha'lab and its order of 11–13 are those of Cheikho's manuscripts ح and مم, and its notes on 3, 28 and 31 are taken from *Anis* (see below). **The reciter follows it**: its 31 abyat, its order, its wording, except 5.2 «يُجاري» (it prints «يُجازي»), 26.1 «الصُّياحِ» (it prints «الصُّباحِ») and the vowels discussed below.
- **Cheikho's *Anis al-Julasa fi Sharh Diwan al-Khansa'*** (Beirut, 1896): archive.org `McGillLibrary-rbsc_isl_anis-al-julasa_PJ7696K5Z581896-15687`, poem pp. 201–216 (leaf = page + 33), the pages bearing on the doubts read as page images (201–206, 212–214, 216). Tha'lab's text and commentary from Cheikho's manuscripts (his sigla ب, ح, م, مم), voweled, with his footnotes. Read directly, not through Abu Suwaylim: 5.2 «يُجاري المقارضَ أمثالَها» is his text (p. 206; his n. a: «أرادت بالمقارض الغزوات وكرّات العدو»); ح and مم have 9.1 «نُهين» and 10.1 «ونَعلم» (p. 216); ح has 12.2 «عليها المضاعف أمثالها» (p. 214); ح and مم have 29.2 «كان اكلالها» (p. 212); ح, ب and مم put «ورجراجة» and «ككرفئة» right after «لتجر المنية» (p. 214), the Beirut order of 11–13; the Beirut notes on 28 («أعيت فتركتها هنالك», p. 212) and 31 (p. 213) are ح's commentary, and its note on 3 is the end of Cheikho's n. d (p. 201). For 10.2 Cheikho reports «ح، مم * رويا: ونَعلمُ. وهما يرويان: حيث يُحكى (ولعلّه يُملى) لها» (p. 216): his manuscripts have «يُحكى», not the Beirut «يُحلى», and his «يُملى» is a guess at that.
- **al-Aghani:** Abu al-Faraj al-Isfahani, *Kitab al-Aghani*, ed. Ihsan Abbas, Ibrahim al-Sa'afin and Bakr Abbas (Dar Sadir, Beirut, 3rd printing 1429/2008), vol. 15: archive.org `al-aghani-15`, section 282 «نسب الخنساء وخبرها وخبر مقتل أخويها صخر ومعاوية», pp. 54–63 (leaves 52–61), read as page images (`aghani.md`). Abyat 1, 2, 18, 7 and 8 at p. 57, voweled, with «لقد» in 1.2, «سأحمل نفسي على خُطّةٍ», and «غنّى فيه ابن سُرَيج خفيفَ رملٍ بالبنصر».
- **al-Mubarrad, *al-Ta'azi wa-l-Marathi*:** shamela 21554/83–84, pp. 121–122 (`taazi.txt`): 15 abyat with glosses, «ترثي صخراً».
- **al-Mubarrad, *al-Kamil*:** shamela 8505/1210, vol. 4, p. 43 (`kamil.txt`): six abyat with glosses («لعمر أبيه لنعم الفتى / إذا النفس أعجبها ما لها»).
- **Ibn Abd Rabbih, *al-Iqd al-Farid*:** shamela 23789/957, vol. 3, p. 224 (`iqd.txt`): five abyat («ألا ما لعيني ألا ما لها», «أمن بعد صخر», «فآليت آسى … وأسأل باكية», «وهمّت بنفسي كلّ الهموم», «سأحمل نفسي على خطّة»).
- **The Abu Dhabi encyclopedia text** (old shamela 4394, pp. 102–104, `encyclopedia.txt`): the Beirut printing's 31 abyat and order, with its own slips («الصُّيا», cut short, in 26.1; «تَطاعِنُها»); not an independent witness. It prints «بَيْنَهُ» in 24.1, and its «الصُّيا» has the ي the reciter says with the damma the Dar Sadir scan prints.

`abyat_in_full` is 31, the Beirut printing's count (Tha'lab has 38). The voweled text was set from the Beirut printing and Tha'lab and put in the house style (`scripts/tashkeel.ts --fix` dropped the kasra before the long ya of «العين»).

## Meter

Mutaqarib (فعولن ×4), rawiy ل (لَها throughout); the opening bayt is muṣarraʿ, its sadr ending on the rhyme («ما لَها»). `scan.py` passes 51 of 62 halves; the 11 it flags were scanned by hand:

- **2.1** «مِنَ آلِ»: the hamza of آل is dropped and its fatha moved to the nun, «مِنالِ»: ʾa-baʿ-dab | ni-ʿam-rin | mi-nā-lish | sha-rī-di, u-- u-- u-- u-u. This is the ordinary lightening of a hamza after a quiescent letter, not a licence of verse alone (Sibawayh, *al-Kitab*, ed. Harun, vol. 3, p. 545, shamela 23018/1396: «واعلم أنَّ كل همزةٍ متحركة كان قبلها حرفٌ ساكن فأردت أن تخفف حذفتها وألقيت حركتها على الساكن الذي قبلها. وذلك قولك: من بوك»). The Beirut printing writes it «من الِ» and *al-Iqd* «من ال», without the hamza; Tha'lab prints «من آل» unvoweled; cohere hears «منال».
- **Nine ajuz that open on a long syllable** (2.2 «حَلْ-لَت», 9.2 «يَو-مَل», 13.2 «تَر-مِس», 14.2 «نا-زَل», 15.2 «تَب-قى», 28.2 «غا-دَر»: عولن for فعولن; 10.2 «با-لِ», 26.2 «تَك-شِ», 31.2 «آ-نَ»: عولُ for فعولُ). Each of these abyat is mudawwar: the short last syllable of its sadr belongs to the ajuz, and split there, as the sources print them, both halves scan whole. Tha'lab splits 9, 10, 13, 15, 26 and 31 («النفو/سِ», «الرجا/لِ», «الصبيـ(م)ـرِ», «السنا/نِ», «الصبا/حِ», «الإرا/خِ»), al-Aghani 2 («الشريـ/ـدِ»), and al-Mubarrad's *al-Ta'azi* 2, 14 and 28 («الشري/د», «الوعو/ل», «الثمي/ل»). The house text keeps whole words in the sadr, so read as written each of these ajuz opens with khurm (الخرم: the first letter of the opening watad dropped, athlam or athram), which al-Damamini allows at the head of an ajuz, though rarely (*al-'Uyun al-Ghamiza*, p. 32, shamela 5421/32: «إلا الخرم فإنه يقع ابتداء وهو أعم من ابتداء الصدر وابتداء العجز، وإن كان وقوعه في أول العجز قليلاً، وربما أباه بعضهم»). The one khurm no layout removes is in a reading not recited: Tha'lab's «وناجيةٍ نَقِبٍ خُفُّها / غادرتَ», whose sadr ends long. The audio confirms 26.2's «تَكشِفُ» against the regular «تُكَشِّفُ» (−13.4).
- **11.2** «المُغادَرِ»: the bayt is mudawwar (Tha'lab marks it «م»); the article's lam closes the sadr («الفتى ل» = فَتَل), so the ajuz scans mu-ghā-da | ri-bil-maḥ | wi-ʾadh-lā | la-hā.
- 6.1 «بِنَفسِيَ» and 6.2 «لِنَفسِيَ» take the fatha on the ya, as the Beirut printing prints it, without which neither half scans.
- 16.1 «يَذبُلٍ»: the mountain's name is a diptote (a name on the pattern of a verb), given tanwin as both editions print it, a licence of verse (Sibawayh, *al-Kitab*, vol. 1, p. 26, shamela 23018/24: «اعلم أنه يجوز في الشعر ما لا يجوز في الكلام من صرف ما لا ينصرف»); without the tanwin the sadr ends short (bu-li for bu-lin).
