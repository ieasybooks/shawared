## Editions

- **al-Wahidi**, شرح ديوان المتنبي: shamela 754, pp. 331–333 (`wahidi.txt`). Unvoweled, with commentary. The shamela text has 24 abyat: it lacks 14 («جزاء كل قريب منكم ملل»), which the other three have, and misprints 4.2 «يريد» for «يرد». The heading: «وبلغ أبا الطيب أن قوما نعوه في مجلس سيف الدولة بحلب فقال سنة ٣٤٨». The print of 11.1 drops «قبل», which his gloss has («قبل قولهم يريد قول الناعين»).
- **al-Ukbari** (attributed), التبيان في شرح الديوان, ed. Mustafa al-Saqqa, Ibrahim al-Abyari and Abd al-Hafiz Shalabi (Dar al-Ma'rifa, Beirut): page images, archive.org `20240104_20240104_1855`, leaves 1437–1443 = vol. 4, pp. 233–239, poem 273. Fully voweled, 25 abyat. The heading: «وبلغ أبا الطيب أن قوما نعوه في مجلس سيف الدولة بحلب وهو بمصر». The commentary without the verses is shamela 6908/1361–1366 (`ukbari.txt`, `ukbari2.txt`). This is the starting text, put in the house style.
- **al-Barquqi**, شرح ديوان المتنبي (Hindawi Foundation, Cairo, 2014): page images, archive.org `20201020_20201020_1638`, pp. 1495–1496 (the text, fully voweled) and pp. 1555–1561 (his notes 237–261). 25 abyat.
- **Ibn Jinni**, الفسر, ed. Rida Rajab (Dar al-Yanabi', Damascus, 1st ed., 2004, as its imprint page says: `alfasr`, leaf 3): page images, archive.org `DO-alfasr`, file `alfasr3`, leaves 704–712 = vol. 4, pp. 705–713, poem 275. Voweled, 25 abyat, with Ibn Jinni's commentary and the editor's notes on his manuscripts. The heading: «وقال بمصر، وقد بلغه أنّ ذكره جرى في مجلس سيف الدولة، وأنه هُتف بموته». Ibn Jinni's note on bayt 7 is also quoted in al-Zawzani, قشر الفسر, vol. 2, p. 351 (shamela 37491/346, `qishr.txt`).
- **al-Ma'arri**, اللامع العزيزي: shamela 29591, pp. 1414–1419 (`maarri.txt`). Notes on most abyat.
- **al-Sahib ibn Abbad**, الأمثال السائرة من شعر المتنبي: shamela 37460, p. 63 (`sahib.txt`). Abyat 4, 9 and 12.

`abyat_in_full` is 25, the count in al-Tibyan, al-Barquqi and al-Fasr.

## Meter

- **Ishba':** the mim of 6.1 «عُيونُهُمُ» and of 14.1 and 14.2 «مِنكُمُ» is long (-humū, -kumū), or the basit breaks. Al-Tibyan and al-Barquqi print 6.1 «عيونُهُمْ» with a sukun; al-Fasr prints «عُيونُهُمُ», and al-Tibyan «مِنكُمُ» in 14; the text writes the damma, as it does for every plural mim the meter lengthens (the half-final mims of 5.1, 10.1, 11.1, 13.1, 15.1, 16.1, 20.1, 21.1 and 22.1 too).
- **6.1 «وَأَنفُسُهُم»:** the half-final mim here is silent (-fusuhum, the last فَعِلُن); with a damma the half has fifteen syllables. All three voweled editions print the sukun, and the clip transcript writes «وَأَنْفُسُهُمْ».
- **25.1 «وَلَكِنّي»:** wa-lā-kin-nī, the long a unwritten as the house style spells لَكِن.
- **22.2 «العُذرُ»:** the plural of عِذار is عُذُر; al-Tibyan and al-Barquqi print it with a silent ذ, and the meter needs it so.
- **5.2 «هَووا»:** hawū, from هَوِيَ (to love), as al-Tibyan prints it («هَوُوا»); the house style leaves the damma off before the long waw.
- Every half was scanned (`scan.py`, basit). It flags 6.1, 14.1, 14.2 and 25.1 only because it reads every plural mim short and doesn't know وَلَكِنّي; with the ishba' and lākin written out, every half scans.

## Commentary drawn on

- **Meaning, abyat 1–25:** al-Wahidi throughout; al-Ukbari, al-Ma'arri and Ibn Jinni where they add to him or differ:
  - **2:** Ibn Jinni's reading (time would choose to be all spring), quoted by al-Ukbari and al-Ma'arri and in al-Fasr, and al-Wahidi's (steadiness from changing seasons, night and day).
  - **3:** al-Ukbari's «من كلام الحكيم».
  - **5:** al-Wahidi takes the lovers for lovers of the world; al-Barquqi (note 241) calls that «تخصيص لا معنى له» and reads it of all lovers.
  - **7:** Ibn Jinni, «هذا نسيب من قد أضمر في نفسه عتبًا» (al-Fasr; al-Wahidi quotes it as «تشبيب»).
  - **8:** al-Ma'arri, the bayt as the explanation of the claim in bayt 7 («ثم شرح ما ادعاه في هذا البيت بقوله»; «يقول: سلوت عنكم»).
  - **10:** al-Ukbari, «تعريضًا لسيف الدولة».
  - **11:** al-Ma'arri, «هذا البيت فيه تهزؤ بالناعين له».
  - **13:** al-Wahidi, «وهذا من أوجع الهجاء»; al-Ma'arri, the milk as a figure for the good that comes from them.
  - **14:** al-Barquqi (note 249, on 13), «وكل هذا تعريض لسيف الدولة وهجاء مر له».
  - **15:** al-Wahidi, «وهذا كله تعريض لسيف الدولة»; al-Ma'arri's second reading of «يعاقبه» («ويجوز أن يكون يعاقبه من تعاقب الراكبين على الدابة … ولكن التنغيص يجيء ولا رفد معه»).
  - **16:** al-Wahidi and al-Ukbari read a wish for distance («يدعو بالبعد»); Ibn Jinni, al-Ma'arri and al-Barquqi (note 252, «يذكر شدة إبعاده في الرحيل») a statement.
  - **17:** Ibn Jinni and al-Ukbari, the question as a figure («ولا سؤال هنالك في الحقيقة»).
  - **18:** al-Find al-Zimmani's half-line, as al-Wahidi and al-Ukbari quote it.
  - **21:** al-Wahidi, «وهذا تعريض بالأسود»; al-Ma'arri, «بليت بقوم ليس ودهم بالصحيح»; Ibn Jinni, al-Fasr p. 711: «حُكي أنّ سيف الدولة لمّا سمع هذا البيت قال: سار وحقِّ أبي». The clause on what Sayf al-Dawla understood by it is ours, hedged with «كأنه».
  - **22:** Ibn Jinni, «طاب مقامي عند غيركم؛ لأنه أكرمني، ولم يملّ مثواي عنده».
  - **23:** Ibn Jinni, «غلا في القول، وحاشا لله أن يكون كذلك».
  - **24:** al-Barquqi (note 260), «يشير إلى ما وعده به من خطة الولاية»; al-Wahidi on the next bayt as the excuse.
- **Glosses:** al-Ukbari's and al-Barquqi's «الغريب» (الوطن والنديم والسكن، الناجية، الهودج والمهجة، الناعون، العرض، الضغن، الرفد والمنن، اليهماء، الرواسم والرسيم والثفن، الدرن، المرير وارعوى والوسن، الودّ وقمن، الأجلة والعذر والفسطاط والرسن، الهمام وأبو المسك ومضر الحمراء، وهن يهن، الابتلاء)، al-Ma'arri's (اكترث، يدِرّ ويدُرّ، حبا، المهر، قمن، الأجلة جمع جلال، يمتحن)، Ibn Jinni's (قمن، المرير، الأجلة جمع جلال)، al-Ukbari's and al-Barquqi's on «بِمَ», al-Wahidi's «السفن يعني أهلها», and al-Barquqi's variant «بعض نائله» for 24.1.
- **The grammar notes** on 12.1 «كلُّ» follow al-Wahidi, al-Ukbari and Ibn Jinni; on 24.2 «تأخّرُ» and 11 «كان … جماعةٌ» they are the plain reading of the line.
- **The occasion and the date:** the headings above; the date is al-Wahidi's.
- **The story in the intro:** Ibn Jinni, al-Fasr, vol. 4, p. 710 («وحدّثني المتنبي، قال: حدّثني فلانٌ الهاشمي، قد أسماه، من أهل حرّان بمصر…»); al-Ukbari tells it of «رجل» writing from Egypt to his wife in Baghdad.
- **The proverbs:** al-Sahib ibn Abbad, as above.

## Review

A second reader checked the text against the page images of al-Tibyan, al-Barquqi and al-Fasr and against al-Wahidi, scanned every half, tested the doubtful vowels on the audio with controls, and checked the sharh, notes and intro against the commentaries (2026-11-17). Fixed after the review:

- **Text:** 6.1 «وَأَنفُسُهُم», the half-final mim silent as all three editions print it (with the damma the half had a syllable too many); 21.1 «مِثلَ» as he recites it, with a note giving the editions' «مِثلِ».
- **Meaning:** 21 quotes al-Wahidi exactly («وهذا تعريض بالأسود», not a paraphrase inside the quotation marks) and adds Ibn Jinni's report of Sayf al-Dawla's «سار وحقِّ أبي»; 24 takes the promise from al-Barquqi's note on this bayt (a governorship), not from a line of another poem; the English translation of 15.2 has bitterness, not spite, for التنغيص.
- **Sharh:** 3, 4 and 8 put in the third person; 8 (al-Ma'arri), 14 (al-Barquqi, for a filler sentence), 15 (al-Ma'arri's second reading of «يعاقبه»), 16 (al-Barquqi) and 23 (Ibn Jinni's whole remark) deepened; the English sharh of 14, 15, 18, 19, 21, 23 and 24 deepened, and 19 no longer repeats its note.
- **Notes:** 19.2.6 head «دَرَن» (not «الدَّرَن») and the noun against the editions' adjective explained; 22.1.2 «الأَجِلَّة» the plural of جِلال, as Ibn Jinni and al-Ma'arri say; 8.2.2 its head and the editions' forms; 1.1.1, 12.2.6, 21.1.4, 24.1.5 (al-Barquqi's variant «بعض نائله»), 25.1.2 and 25.2.4 added; the English 7.1.2 without an ellipsis.
- **English:** the intro's closing test is of the love the poet professed, not "his loyalty".
- **sources.md:** 18.2 al-Tibyan prints «جَبَنُ», not «جُبُنُ»; 4.1 «لعل الأظهر» is the verdict of «بعضهم» whom al-Barquqi quotes, not his own; al-Fasr's publisher and date from its imprint page; 19.1, 21.1, 21.2 and 23.2 scores added; the meter notes put right.

After the review, the drafter dropped from the sharh of 21, in both languages, a clause reading Sayf al-Dawla's «سارَ وحقِّ أبي» as his understanding that the poet would leave Kafur too: Ibn Jinni reports the words without a gloss. The al-Barquqi note on 24 («يشير إلى ما وعده به من خطة الولاية») and its «يروى: بعض نائله» were checked on the page image (p. 1561).
