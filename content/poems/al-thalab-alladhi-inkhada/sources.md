# الثَّعلَبُ الَّذي اِنخَدَعَ

## Recording and extent

Alqimmah Studio published «حكايات أحمد شوقي» on 3 March 2023, crediting Osama Al-Waaedh with recitation and editing. The source lasts 966.844 seconds. Chapter 6 keeps **549.87–607.88 seconds of the original compilation** in one continuous cut. The source voice measurements place the first and last pitched sound of this chapter at approximately 550.41 and 606.49 seconds. The cut admits the entry and the decay of the final rhyme. [Compilation, chapter 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r6VpT0Lid0E&t=550s)

All eight couplets occur. The last is performed twice. It first begins near 593.85 seconds and begins again near 600.11, both on the original source clock. The independent source-end clip recovers the close of the first «إذ ربما ينخدع الثعلب» and the entire new «فلا تثق يوماً بذي حيلة / إذ ربما ينخدع الثعلب». This accounts for 18 halves, even though the long Audar transcription condenses the ending. There is no ninth printed couplet.

## Printed text and interpretation

- Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, part IV, «الحكايات», Dar al-Awda, Beirut, 1988. The references follow the pagination of part IV. [p. 180](https://archive.org/details/BBib-Alex-02915/page/n375/mode/1up)
- Ahmad Shawqi, *al-Shawqiyyat*, «الحكايات», Hindawi Foundation for Education and Culture, Cairo, deposit 19938/2012, ISBN 978-977-719-166-1. The page images and the electronic chapter were compared. [p. 899](https://archive.org/details/alshawkeyat/page/n899/mode/1up) · [Electronic text](https://www.safahat.org/books/70706142/143/)

The first edition of *al-Shawqiyyat* (part I, Cairo, Matbaʿat al-Adab wa-l-Muʾayyad, 1898) provides an earlier text. Cairo begins 6.1 with «فقصد القوم» after the fox imagines his reception. The two later printings have «وقصد». Both independent transcriptions of the continuous recording give the wāw, also present in the 574–599-second source clip. On the aligned half (31.48–34.20 seconds of the app track) «وقصد القوم وحياهم» scores −51.47 against −57.47 for «فقصد القوم وحياهم». The eighth couplet appears once on the page and twice in the recording. [p. 195](https://books.google.com/books?id=QIyl-Nj9R6UC&pg=RA1-PA195)

Beirut and Hindawi put the whole fable on a single page. The fox hears a human being addressed «يا ثعلب» before he imagines a village fête arranged for the animal himself. «الديك أو الأرنب» lets an appetite intrude into the fantasy. Once the villagers act, «أُخذ» and «أُعطي» pass the visitor from hand to hand. That grammatical reversal informs the commentary and the translation. Arslan discusses the light diction of Shawqi’s fables and quotes a *different* fox poem, the story set aboard Noah’s ship. [Arslan, §8](https://www.safahat.org/books/96809026/24/)

The two printings transmit the same diwan tradition. The page reproductions and the word-by-word output from `scripts/collate.py` are in `media/research/al-thalab-alladhi-inkhada/`. The studio captions are performance evidence. The glosses and bilingual commentary are editorial close readings of the cited text.

## Metre and rhyme

Sariʿ, with مستفعلن مستفعلن فاعلن as the underlying sequence. In 1, *qad-sa-mi-ʿath | thaʿ-la-bu-ah | lal-qu-rā* scans **– ⏑ ⏑ – | – ⏑ ⏑ – | – ⏑ –**. The two instances of ṭayy give مفتعلن. The answer *yad-ʿū-na-muḥ | tā-lan-bi-yā | thaʿ-la-bū* gives **– – ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | – ⏑ –**. In 5, *la-ʿal-la-hum | yuḥ-yū-na-lī | zī-na-tan* gives **⏑ – ⏑ – | – – ⏑ – | – ⏑ –** in liaison. *Yaḥ-ḍu-ru-had | dī-ku-a-wil | ar-na-bū* gives **– ⏑ ⏑ – | – ⏑ ⏑ – | – ⏑ –**. The rhyme returns to bāʾ, whether the following ū is written, as in استغربوا, or sustained in delivery, as in الثعلبُ.

## Readings against the performance

At 5.2 the Hindawi electronic text explicitly points «الديكَ». The Beirut page does not clearly mark a case ending there. The cock and the rabbit are the alternative subjects of «يحضرها», with the pronoun standing for the festivity. The performed half gives the nominative a 3.27-point advantage over the accusative. The free phoneme trace also gives /k u/ at 579.72–579.78 seconds before the onset of «أو», and a second /u/ after the b of «الأرنب» at 580.94. The coordinated noun supplies the other possible subject. The text and word card keep «الديكُ». At 7 the subject of the passive action is the visitor the villagers seized, and the dog gets him as a plaything. The 574–599-second window transcribes the full reception and the first warning.
