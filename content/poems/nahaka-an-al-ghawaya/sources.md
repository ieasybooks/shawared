## The poet

The upload names no poet. The poem is Baha' al-Din Zuhayr's: it is in his diwan in all three texts below (Dar al-Ma'arif, Palmer, the Abu Dhabi encyclopedia), and al-Hashimi quotes eleven of its abyat under his name. The poet id `baha-al-din-zuhayr` belongs to this batch's poem from M9h-RO58ABs («عرف الحبيب مكانه»), which creates the folder; this poem uses the id and doesn't create it.

## Editions

- **ديوان البهاء زهير**, ed. محمد طاهر الجبلاوي and محمد أبو الفضل إبراهيم (Dar al-Ma'arif, Cairo): scan archive.org `3303pdf_202001`, leaves 191–193, printed pp. 192–194 (`maarif.txt`, typed from the page images). Headed «رثاء عزيز»: «وقال يرثي بعض من يعزّ عليه "من الوافر قافية المتواتر"», with a note that the Beirut edition has «يرثي ولده». 32 abyat, lightly voweled, with variants from the sources the editors list on p. 12: ل (a microfilm at the Arab League's manuscript institute of an Alexandria copy written in 996 AH), ح (a Cairo lithograph of 1277 AH), Palmer, and the Beirut edition of 1964.
- **E. H. Palmer, *The Poetical Works of Behá-ed-Dín Zoheir of Egypt***, vol. 1, the Arabic text (Cambridge University Press, 1876; the Cairo editors date it 1875): archive.org `poeticalworksofb01zuha`, image leaves 157, 156 and 155, printed pp. 177–179 (`palmer.txt`, typed from the page images). Headed «وقال يرثي ولده رحمه الله تعالى من الوافر والقافية المتواتر». 32 abyat, voweled. His English verse translation, vol. 2 (1877), pp. 202–205, is headed "An Elegy. [On his son.]" (archive.org `poeticalworksofb02zuha`; `palmer-translation-ocr.txt`).
- **The Abu Dhabi poetry encyclopedia's text**, through old shamela 4508 (ديوان بهاء الدين), pp. 361–364 (`mawsua.txt`): 32 abyat, no heading. One source, not an edition. Wherever Dar al-Ma'arif and Palmer differ (1.1, 3.2, 7.1, 11.2, 19.2, 21.1, 21.2, 26.1), the reciter follows it, and he follows it against both prints in 23.1 and 30.2.
- **أحمد الهاشمي، جواهر الأدب**, vol. 2, p. 390 (shamela 21505/598; `hashimi-extract.txt`): eleven abyat (the diwan's 12–16, 10, 19, 21, 23, 30 and 32), headed «وقال بهاء الدين زهير المتوفى سنة ٦٥٦ هـ», among elegies.
- **Starting text:** the video's description (aldiwan.net-style harakat, 31 abyat, without the diwan's 17), put in the house style. Its «الغوايةَ» in 1.1 is wrong: the noun after «عن» is genitive; Palmer and the encyclopedia print «الغوايةِ»; the recording prefers it (−3.8 for the fatha); and the voweled clip transcript has «عَنِ الْغِوَايَةِ».

`abyat_in_full` is 32, the count in all three texts.

- **8.2:** «ألا ترى» has fewer letters, which the scorer always favours, and it breaks the wafir foot. Both transcribers hear the س and ت of «ألستَ» («ألا سترى», «ألا أستتري»).
- **10.2:** a missing letter, which the scorer can't settle: first tested with the alif of wasl written out, «فكاكا» won by 12.9; written as they would be said, «روحِ نفِكاكا» beats «روحي فِكاكا» by 0.4. Both transcribers write «روح فكاك», with a short i after the ح. That fits «روحي اِنفِكاكا» said with the wasl (rūḥi-nfikākā), the ن hidden before ف; cohere's voweled transcript, «رُوحٍ فِكَاكَ», hears that ن as tanwin. «روحي فكاكا» would keep the long ī. Both scan, so the text keeps the reading of every printed source.
- **15.1–15.2:** the gaps are small because the aligner scores ذ badly; both transcribers hear «عذرا … يعذر» in both halves. The sharh gives the other reading.
- **13.2, 20.1:** the lexicons give وُداد, وِداد and وَداد. Palmer puts a kasra under the و in both (Dar al-Ma'arif's kasra in 13.2 is on the second د, the case ending), and the voweled transcript has «وِدَادِي».
- **19.2:** «حِلاكا» scores better (+3.4 on the half, +2.0 on the word alone), but under 5, and Lisan gives both حُلًى and حِلًى as the plural of حِلية. The text keeps the damma of both prints.
- **22.2:** «بَلاكا» (Dar al-Ma'arif's fatha) wins by 5.8 on the half and 4.4 on the last two words, against Palmer's «بِلاكا». Lisan (vol. 14, p. 85; `lisan-bala.txt`) gives بَلاء as a form of بِلًى, decay («بَلِيَ الثوبُ يَبْلَى بِلًى وبَلاء»), so the word carries both senses; the note says so.
- **18.2, 23.2:** the reciter departs from every source; both transcribers hear «عن مكانك» and «وحق الله». The word notes give the diwan's readings. The video's description also has «وحق الله».
- **26.2:** «مِن نَواكا», your return from the place you set out for; Palmer translates it so ("From this long journey thou didst make").
- **30.2:** «ذُراكا» is a coin toss by score. The text keeps the editions' damma, which the voweled transcript also has («ذُرَاكَ»).
- **Meter:** 17.2 «كُلُّهُمُ» has its mim long (kullu-humū), or the second foot is a syllable short.

**Against the starting text:** «ياقلب», «ماقد», «لاتطيق» and «ماحاولت» separated; 23.2 «وَحَقِّ» (the oath takes the genitive; the description has «وَحَقَّ»); 30.2 «يَرِفُّ» with its shadda (the description has «يَرُفُ»).

## Commentary drawn on

- There is no classical commentary on the diwan. The sharh is our own reading, checked against:
  - Palmer's verse translation (vol. 2, pp. 202–205), which reads 15 as a reproach ("Thou canst not make excuses — no! All others may, but never thou") and 26.2 as a return from a journey;
  - Dar al-Ma'arif's gloss of التراقي (p. 193 n. 2);
  - *al-Mu'jam al-Wasit* (shamela 7028; `wasit.txt`) for الصبابة, ضني, قلى, هتن, الحلية, النوى, رفّ and التراقي;
  - Ibn Manzur, *Lisan al-Arab*, vol. 14, p. 85 (shamela 1687/7209; `lisan-bala.txt`), for بَلاء as a form of بِلًى (22.2), and vol. 14, p. 284 (shamela 1687/7408; `lisan-dhura.txt`), for الذُّرى, the plural of ذِروة, the top of a thing;
  - al-Maydani, *Majma' al-Amthal*, vol. 2, p. 3, no. 2382 (shamela 12929/2451; `maydani.txt`), for the proverb «عند الصباح يحمد القوم السُّرى» behind 2;
  - the Qur'an: 22:10 (5), 75:26 (9), 20:78 (16).
- **The occasion:** the intro gives both headings, Palmer's and the Beirut edition's «يرثي ولده» and Dar al-Ma'arif's «يرثي بعض من يعزّ عليه». Nothing found names the dead or dates the poem.
- **Al-Hashimi's choice** of eleven abyat for his elegies is the intro's last sentence.

## Review

A second reader checked the text letter by letter against the page images of Dar al-Ma'arif (pp. 192–194) and Palmer (pp. 177–179), the encyclopedia's text and al-Hashimi, scanned every half by hand, scored nine more readings on the audio, and checked the sharh, notes, intro and sources against the editions and lexicons (2026-11-17). Fixed after the review:

- **Text:** no change. The reciter's text stands: 4.2 «تَبَيَّنَ», 17.1 «لِضُعفِ», 19.2 «حُلاكا», 22.2 «بَلاكا» and 13.2/20.1 «وِداد» were tested against their other vowels (table above). Every half scans as wafir (15.1 and 16.1 with the hidden alif of اللَّه and لَكِن, 17.2 with «كُلُّهُمُ» long, 21.1 «فَوا أَسَفي» as mufa'alatun).
- **Meaning:** 9.2 «نَظَرَت بِهِ عَيني الهَلاكا» is that he faced death himself because of the beloved, not that he watched the beloved die (both languages); 17.2 is part of the wish (that all the people had been his ransom), not «ولو افتداه الناس»; 22.2 «بَلاكا» also means the decay of 21.1, after Lisan.
- **Sharh:** Arabic deepened in 2, 6, 10, 11, 19, 20, 22–25 and 27 (median 107 letters to 118.5, none now under 100); 2's «ما يُحمد عليه» (praised for it) corrected to his not praising the journey.
- **Notes:** 30.2 «يَرِفُّ» now gives the reading of both prints, «يَرِقُّ»; 22.2 «بَلاكا» gives Palmer's «بِلاكا» and the sense of decay (both languages); added 12.2 «قَبلُ», 20.2 «وَلَيسَ يَزالُ», 29.1 «وَإِلّا», and an English note for 18.1 «يَعِزُّ عَلَيَّ».
- **Intro:** the Cairo heading «وقال يرثي بعض من يعزّ عليه» is the heading in their edition, not a verdict of the editors (both languages).
- **sources.md:** the encyclopedia's agreements with the reciter listed correctly (23.1 and 30.2 are against both prints, not where they differ); وِداد credited to Palmer, not Dar al-Ma'arif; Palmer's damma in 7.1 added; the new scores added.
