## Attribution

The poem is one of the most disputed in the Hamasa. Every claim found, with where it was read:

- **«بعض بني قيس بن ثعلبة»** (a man of Qays ibn Tha'laba, of Bakr ibn Wa'il): Abu Tammam's heading, «قال بعض بني قيس بن ثعلبة», in al-Marzuqi (p. 100), al-Tibrizi (p. 78), al-Farisi (vol. 2, p. 100), al-Ma'arri (attributed; p. 80, «رجل من بني قيس») and al-Shantamari (p. 366). Al-Namari gave it to «رجل من بني قيس بن ثعلبة» (as al-Tibrizi quotes him, p. 84).
- **Bashama ibn Hazn al-Nahshali**, of Nahshal ibn Darim of Tamim:
  - the Hamasa commentaries' second name: «ويقال إنها لبشامة بن جزء النهشلي» (al-Marzuqi, p. 100; the editors' note: «جزء» in both their manuscripts, «حزن» in al-Tibrizi, al-Mubarrad and al-Baghdadi, and in Ibn Jinni's *al-Mubhij*); «ويقال: إنها لبشامة بن حزن النهشلي» (al-Tibrizi p. 78, al-Farisi p. 100, al-Ma'arri p. 80, which adds «جاهلي»). Al-Marzuqi himself calls bayt 8 «بيت بشامة» (p. 108);
  - al-Shantamari (p. 366) lists the names and settles this one: «وقيل بشامة بن حرّي، وقيل ابن جُزَيء، والأصحّ ابن حزن»;
  - al-Amidi, *al-Mu'talif wa-l-Mukhtalif*, pp. 80–81 (shamela 10913/74–75): «ومنهم بشامة بن حزن النهشلي بن دارم وهو القائل», with five abyat (our 3, 4, 6, the omitted Hamasa 8, and our 8);
  - *Lisan al-Arab* quotes bayt 2 as «قول بشامة بن حزن النهشلي» (ج ل ل, vol. 11, p. 116) and bayt 9 for «بشامة بن حرى النهشلي» (ظ ب ا, vol. 15, p. 22);
  - al-Baghdadi, *Khizanat al-Adab*, vol. 8, p. 311 (shamela 12732/3849): «وهذه الأبيات قد اختلف في قائلها والصحيح أنها لبشامة بن حزن النهشلي. وعليه الآمدي في كتابه المؤتلف والمختلف»; and on p. 312, after a quoted list of opinions: Ibn al-Sayyid al-Batalyawsi on *al-Kamil* gives them to Bashama ibn Hazn, al-Sukkari calls him «بشامة بن حري», «والأول قول أبي رياش».
- **Abu Makhzum al-Nahshali:** al-Mubarrad, *al-Kamil*, vol. 1, pp. 95–98 (shamela 8505/124–127): «وقال رجل يكنى أبا مخزوم، من بني نهشل بن دارم», ten abyat: our 3, 4, 5, the omitted Hamasa 8, our 8, 10, 6, 9 and two more («فرض على مكثرينا…», «إني ومن كأبي يحيى…»). The editor's note: «زيادات ر: هو بشامة بن حزن النهشلى»; Naqsha (al-Ma'arri's editor, p. 84) cites the same gloss from another print of *al-Kamil* (1/66) with «عن أبي رياش». So Abu Makhzum and Bashama are one man for Abu Riyash; al-Shantamari and al-Baghdadi list the two names side by side without saying so.
- **Nahshal ibn Harri al-Darimi** (a *mukhadram* who fought for Ali at Siffin and died about 45 AH (665 CE): al-Zirikli, *al-A'lam*, vol. 8, p. 49, which does not mention this poem): Ibn Qutayba, *al-Shi'r wa-l-Shu'ara'*, vol. 2, pp. 622–623 (shamela 23785/613–614), six abyat (our 3, 4, 7, the omitted Hamasa 8, our 8, 5); Ibn Tabataba, *Iyar al-Shi'r*, pp. 104–105 (shamela 6911/100–101), all twelve in the Hamasa's order, «نهشل بن حرّي المازني»; Ibn Sa'id, *Nashwat al-Tarab*, p. 456 (shamela 28174/399), who adds «ورواه الآمدي لبشامة النهشلي»; al-Shantamari's «ويقال».
- **al-Muraqqish al-Akbar** (of Qays ibn Tha'laba, died about 75 BH (550 CE): *al-A'lam*, vol. 5, p. 95): al-Mufaddal al-Dabbi, *al-Mufaddaliyyat*, no. 128, p. 431 (shamela 6904/405), four abyat: «يا ذاتَ أَجوارِنا قومي فحيّينا» with our 1.2, our 2 with «سَراةَ خِيارِ الناس», «شعث مقادمنا نهبى مراجلنا» and «المطعمون إذا هبت شآمية». Al-Baghdadi (vol. 8, p. 301) adds Ibn al-A'rabi's *Nawadir* and Abu Muhammad al-A'rabi for this; al-Farisi (p. 100): «وقال البرقي: إنها للمرقش».
- **Two poems run together:** Abu Muhammad al-A'rabi, answering al-Namari (al-Tibrizi, pp. 84–85; al-Baghdadi, vol. 8, p. 312): al-Namari did not tell Nahshal (Mudar) from Qays ibn Tha'laba (Rabi'a), «فلزّهما في قرن»; «والبيت الذي فيه «إنّا بني نهشل» لبشامة بن حزن النهشلي، والأبيات الأخر لمرقش الأكبر». He also reports Abu al-Nada's verdict that «بيض مفارقنا تغلي مراجلنا» is a weak reading of «شُعثٌ مقادمُنا نُهبى مراجلُنا».
- **Others:** Hujr ibn Khalid ibn Mahmud of Qays ibn Tha'laba (Ibn al-A'rabi, as quoted by al-Baghdadi, vol. 8, p. 312; al-Shantamari, p. 366); «ابن غلفاء التميمي», which the same quoted list ascribes to Ibn Qutayba and al-Baghdadi corrects to Nahshal ibn Harri. Al-Tibrizi (p. 79) and al-Shantamari (p. 368) note that those who gave it to the Qaysi read «إنّا بني مالك».
- **The person:** al-Zirikli's *al-A'lam* has no entry for Bashama ibn Hazn or for Abu Makhzum (shamela full-text search of book 12286; its only Bashamas are Ibn Amr and Ibn al-Ghadir). Al-Baghdadi (vol. 8, p. 314): al-Amidi gives no more lineage than «بشامة بن حزن النهشلي نهشل بن دارم»; «ولم أر له ترجمة»; «والظاهر أنه إسلامي. وكذا أبو مخزوم النهشلي كما يظهر من شرح المبرد لأبياته». Al-Marzuqi's editors explain this as al-Mubarrad's showing that some of the ideas come from Islamic poets (p. 100, note 1); al-Mubarrad names Abu al-Tamahan al-Qayni (bayt 5), Ka'b ibn Malik (bayt 9) and al-Ajda' al-Hamdani and al-Qattal al-Kilabi (bayt 6). Al-Ma'arri's commentary calls him «جاهلي».

**Decision.** The poet is `bashama-ibn-hazn`. The upload's Abu Makhzum al-Nahshali is al-Mubarrad's name for the author of the Nahshali lines, and the gloss in *al-Kamil*, on Abu Riyash's authority, makes him Bashama ibn Hazn; the four Hamasa commentaries give Bashama as the alternative to their anonymous Qaysi, al-Amidi and *Lisan* give him the lines, and al-Baghdadi calls it the correct attribution. Of the eleven abyat recited, 3–10 are in one or more of the Nahshali versions (al-Mubarrad, al-Amidi, Ibn Qutayba); 1, 2 and a different form of 7 are in al-Muraqqish's Mufaddaliyya; 11 is in the Hamasa tradition only (its commentaries, Ibn Tabataba, al-Baghdadi). The intro and the poet's bio give the dispute.

## Meter

Basit (*al-basit al-tamm*, «الأول من البسيط» in al-Farisi), rawiy ن with the long ī before it and the alif of *itlaq* (ـينا) in every ajuz and in the muṣarraʿ opening half. `python3 -I /home/alifadel/.cache/shw-tmp/b4/tools/scan.py` scans all 22 halves with nothing to check by hand. Licences: 9.1 «يُصيبَهُمُ» ends on the plural mim said long (bahumū, the last فَعِلُن), as al-Marzuqi's «يَنالَهُمُ» is printed; the sadr endings carry the editions' tanwin. The opening bayt and 6 were also scanned by hand: «إنْنا مُحَيْ | يوكِ يا | سَلْمى فَحَيْ | يينا» (مستفعلن فاعلن مستفعلن فَعْلن) and «إنْنا لِنُرْ | خِصُ يَوْ | مَرْرَوْعِ أَنْ | فُسَنا» (مستفعلن فَعِلن مستفعلن فَعِلن).
